## 3. Company values — "ByteStyle" (official, current list = 6)

Sources: https://joinbytedance.com/culture and https://joinbytedance.com/philosophy (footer "© 2012-2025 ByteDance", no version date), summarized on https://www.bytedance.com/en/ ("ByteStyles ... help guide how we work together"). Mission: **"Inspire Creativity, Enrich Life."** TikTok's FAQ: "our culture is deeply connected with our values/workstyles, called ByteStyle" and job postings quote "Always Day 1".
1. **Always Day 1** — "Always maintain an entrepreneurial mindset. Keep pioneering and innovating instead of relying on resources or past achievements. Stay agile and pursue efficiency and simplicity. Reduce unnecessary procedures. Reject complacency. Stay open and humble."
2. **Champion Diversity and Inclusion** — "Value individual differences, and focus on people's unique strengths. Think global. Understand and celebrate different cultures, views, and experiences. Facilitate effective collaboration by assuming good intent and trusting by default."
3. **Be Candid and Clear** — "Speak your mind. Expose problems candidly. Avoid 'managing up'. Be accurate, concise, and straightforward. Avoid overusing jargon or elaborate terms. Drive communication and form conclusions with data and facts, instead of assumptions or emotions."
4. **Seek Truth and Be Pragmatic** — "Be an independent thinker. Get to the bottom of things. Distill ideas down to their fundamental truths. Dive deep into facts. Seek direct experience and first-hand data and information. Be grounded and focus on real impact."
5. **Be Courageous and Aim for the Highest** — "Dare to take calculated risks for bigger gains, with a focus on return on investment. Explore alternative solutions in a larger scope for the optimal result. Insist on high standards. Don't just get the job done; execute with excellence."
6. **Grow Together** — "Live and be driven by our mission and vision. Show patience and resilience in the face of short-term fluctuations. Solve problems together. Keep learning and keep pushing boundaries. Pursue mutual growth with the organization."

Where the task text and the official page differ: "Champion **Diverse Perspectives**" (task text) = "Champion **Diversity and Inclusion**" (official); "Seek Truth and Be **Grounded**" = "Seek Truth and Be **Pragmatic**" (the word "grounded" appears inside the description); "Have Courage to Think Big" was not found on any source (closest official match: "Be Courageous and Aim for the Highest"); "Be Open and Humble" belongs to an older list (search-result summary of a 2020 culture-code article: Aim for the Highest, Be Grounded and Courageous, Be Open and Humble, Be Candid and Clear, Always Day 1, Champion Diversity and Inclusion; an earlier 2018 version existed) and survives today as the phrase "Stay open and humble" inside Always Day 1. The current official joinbytedance.com list has six values. A web-search result also reported a later revision with "Be curious and grounded" and "Be courageous and aim for the highest" (from a trade news item about a post-UK-backlash culture update; page returned 403, **unverified**). Treat joinbytedance.com as current as of the 2025 footer.

## 6. Engineering stack (official engineering publications; dates matter)

- **Go as the main language; CloudWeGo.** Hertz (HTTP) blog 2022-06-21: "largest HTTP framework inside ByteDance, with more than 10,000 online services and a peak QPS of more than 40 million". Kitex (RPC) blog 2022-09-30: "as of August this year Kitex has provided support for over 60,000 internal services ... peak QPS in the hundreds of millions". 2023 CloudWeGo article: Go is "main development language ... tens of thousands of Go microservices". URLs: https://www.cloudwego.io/blog/2022/06/21/hertz-an-ultra-large-scale-enterprise-level-microservice-http-framework-is-now-officially-open-source , https://www.cloudwego.io/blog/2022/09/30/kitex-unifying-open-source-practice-for-a-high-performance-rpc-framework/ , https://www.cloudwego.io/blog/2023/06/15/cloudwego-a-leading-practice-for-building-enterprise-cloud-native-middleware/ . (Numbers are from 2022; no newer official count found.)
- **ByteGraph** (VLDB 2022, https://vldb.org/pvldb/vol15/p3306-li.pdf): "Most products at ByteDance, e.g., TikTok, Douyin, and Toutiao, naturally generate massive amounts of graph data"; graphs of "tens of billions of vertices and trillions of edges"; two-tier (durable storage + cache), edge-trees for adjacency lists, geo-replication. Follow-ups: BG3 (SIGMOD 2024), ByteGraph-Dione (SIGMOD Companion 2026).
- **ByteHTAP** (VLDB 2022, https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol15/p3411-chen.pdf): separate-engine, shared-storage HTAP on ByteDance's own OLTP system **ByteNDB** + Apache Flink; "less than one second" data freshness, global timestamps. (The paper does not say ByteDance uses TiDB or MySQL; TiDB appears only in related work. Whether TiDB is used at ByteDance is **not confirmed by any source I found**.)
- **ByteHouse** (arXiv 2602.08226, Feb 2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08226): ByteDance's cloud-native data warehouse (ClickHouse lineage; open-source sibling ByConity), "real-time multimodal data analytics"; internal KV store "ByteKV" mentioned in passing.
- **Streaming:** ByteMQ/BMQ, a Kafka-API-compatible cloud-native layer (SoCC 2024, DOI 10.1145/3698038.3698536) and Flink at scale (StreamShield, arXiv 2602.03189, Feb 2026: "over 70,000 concurrent streaming jobs", "more than 11 million resource slots"). Figures reached through a secondary summary (https://factorhouse.io/articles/bytedance-kafka-architecture/: "tens of TB/s peak consume", "99.76%" of Kafka workloads migrated, "about 70%" lower resource cost) — **verify against the papers before quoting**.
- **Monolith** (arXiv 2209.07663, 2022): real-time recommendation training with a collisionless (cuckoo-hash) embedding table, expirable embeddings, online training with parameter synchronization; Kafka action/feature queues + Flink joiner (as described by secondary summaries).
- Job-post stack hints (nodeflair/Muse snippets, low): TikTok Shop transaction teams list Java, Go, Redis, MySQL, Linux; "Global LIVE Wallet" and "TikTok Live Revenue" backend teams exist (gifting/revenue features).
