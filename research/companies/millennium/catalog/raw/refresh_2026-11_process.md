## 6. Synthesis: team round

- No source describes a LEaD team round. Most likely format (by inference): **one or two 30-60 min video conversations with the hiring SWE manager/tech lead and 1-2 engineers on the receiving team, mixing project deep dive and resume with behavioural/fit questions, plus possibly one more practical coding or code-reading task (HackerRank/Webex)**. Sure: about 55% that it is conversation-heavy (project + behavioural, matching Miami 2026-02 "mostly behavioural", the 1p3a 2026-11 "project Q&A" interview, QuantVault "pod fit"); about 45% that it contains another coding or code-reading segment (Glassdoor Miami: 2 technical + 1 behavioural onsite; 1p3a Java read-the-output); under 25% it includes real system design. Round count: 2-4 total after screen; timeline per candidate reports: roughly weeks, Miami one case March apply -> July offer (slow), others quick between rounds.
- Format varies by team (pod/platform), so ask the recruiter on 30 November: who, how long, coding or not, language.
