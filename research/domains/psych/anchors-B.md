# Group B: 34 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 重新评估与压抑（reappraisal vs suppression）

- node: `self.emotion.reappraisal` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-reappraisal.md`
- 一句话：换一种解释来看同一件事，比忍着不表现出来有效，代价也小。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gross 1998 JPSP (disgust films: suppression hid expression but raised sympathetic arousal; reappraisal lowered experienced disgust). Gross & John 2003: habitual reappraisers have more positive emotion and better relationships. Aldao, Nolen-Hoeksema, Schweizer 2010 meta (114 studies).

### 给情绪命名（affect labeling）

- node: `self.emotion.affect-labeling` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-affect-labeling.md`
- 一句话：把正在感受的情绪用词说出来，它的强度会降下来一点。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Lieberman 2007 Psychological Science: labelling emotional faces lowered amygdala response and raised right ventrolateral prefrontal activity (n=30). Kircanski, Lieberman, Craske 2012: spider-fearful people who described their fear during exposure had lower skin conductance a week later than reappraisal or distraction groups.

### 自我抽离（self-distancing）

- node: `self.emotion.self-distancing` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-self-distancing.md`
- 一句话：用第三人称、用旁观者的位置想自己的事，更冷静也更明智。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Kross, Ayduk, Mischel 2005. Kross et al. 2014 JPSP: using one's own name in self-talk before a speech reduced anxiety and rumination. Grossmann & Kross 2014 Psychological Science 'Solomon's paradox': people reason more wisely about a friend's problem than their own; distancing closes the gap.

### 反刍（rumination）

- node: `self.emotion.rumination` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-rumination.md`
- 一句话：反复想「为什么是我、哪里不对」不解决问题，只把低落拉长。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Nolen-Hoeksema 1991 response styles theory. Nolen-Hoeksema & Morrow 1991 JPSP: students measured before the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake; ruminators were more depressed 10 days and 7 weeks after. Lyubomirsky & Nolen-Hoeksema 1995: rumination makes problem solving worse and more pessimistic; 8 minutes of distraction lifts mood in dysphoric people.

### 认知歪曲（cognitive distortions）

- node: `self.emotion.cognitive-distortions` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-cognitive-distortions.md`
- 一句话：低落时的想法有固定的歪法：非黑即白、灾难化、读心、以偏概全；认出来再对着证据检查。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Beck 1963, 1979; Burns 1980 list. CBT for depression: Cuijpers et al. 2023 World Psychiatry meta (409 trials, n≈52,700): g=0.79 vs control; comparable to medication in the short term, better at 6–12 months.

### 行为激活（behavioral activation）

- node: `self.emotion.behavioral-activation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-behavioral-activation.md`
- 一句话：低落时不等有心情再做事；先按计划去做，情绪跟在行动后面。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Jacobson et al. 1996 component analysis: activation alone did as well as full cognitive therapy. Dimidjian et al. 2006 (n=241): for severe depression, activation matched antidepressants and beat cognitive therapy. Richards et al. 2016 Lancet COBRA trial (n=440): activation by junior mental-health workers non-inferior to CBT and cheaper.

### 回避与暴露（avoidance, exposure）

- node: `self.emotion.avoidance-exposure` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-avoidance-exposure.md`
- 一句话：躲开害怕的事，当下轻松，害怕却被养大；一点点去接触，它才会变小。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Mowrer 1947 two-factor theory: avoidance is reinforced by relief. Exposure therapy is the most effective treatment for phobias and social anxiety (Wolitzky-Taylor 2008 meta; Carpenter 2018 meta of CBT for anxiety). Craske 2014 inhibitory learning: the point is to violate the expectation, not to feel calm.

### 自我关怀（self-compassion）

- node: `self.emotion.self-compassion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-self-compassion.md`
- 一句话：失败后像对朋友那样对自己，下一次反而更肯努力；自责不是动力。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Neff 2003 scale. Leary et al. 2007 JPSP five studies. Breines & Chen 2012 PSPB: after a failed test, the self-compassion group studied longer for the retest than the self-esteem and control groups. MacBeth & Gumley 2012 meta: r=-0.54 with depression, anxiety, stress. Mostly self-report; causal evidence is thinner.

### 正念的证据（mindfulness）

- node: `self.emotion.mindfulness` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-mindfulness.md`
- 一句话：八周正念训练对焦虑和抑郁有中等大小的效果，和其他正规疗法差不多，不是万能药。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Goyal et al. 2014 JAMA Internal Medicine meta (47 trials, n=3,515): anxiety effect size 0.38, depression 0.30 at 8 weeks; no evidence it beats exercise or CBT. Kuyken et al. 2016 JAMA Psychiatry: MBCT cut depressive relapse, hazard ratio 0.69. Van Dam et al. 2018 'Mind the Hype'.

### 负面偏差（negativity bias）

- node: `self.emotion.negativity-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-negativity-bias.md`
- 一句话：同样强度的坏事比好事影响大、记得久；一次批评要好几次肯定才抵得掉。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Baumeister, Bratslavsky, Finkenauer, Vohs 2001 'Bad Is Stronger Than Good'; Rozin & Royzman 2001. Gottman's 5:1 ratio in stable couples (observational). The 'critical positivity ratio 2.9013' (Fredrickson & Losada 2005) was mathematically refuted (Brown, Sokal, Friedman 2013).

### 现时偏好（present bias, hyperbolic discounting）

- node: `self.control.present-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-present-bias.md`
- 一句话：为下周的自己选健康和努力，为今天的自己选舒服；同一个人，偏好随时间远近翻转。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Read & van Leeuwen 1998: choosing a snack for next week, 74% chose fruit; choosing for now, 70% chose chocolate. Thaler 1981; Laibson 1997. O'Donoghue & Rabin 1999: naive vs sophisticated agents. DellaVigna & Malmendier 2006 AER 'Paying Not to Go to the Gym': members on a $70 monthly contract went 4.3 times a month, paying about $17 a visit when a pass cost $10.

### 承诺机制（commitment device）

- node: `self.control.commitment-device` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-commitment-device.md`
- 一句话：趁冷静时给将来的自己设限或设罚，让到时候想反悔也难。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Ariely & Wertenbroch 2002: students who set their own binding deadlines did better than those with one final deadline, worse than evenly spaced imposed ones. Giné, Karlan, Zinman 2010 (CARES, Philippines): smokers who staked their own money were 3 percentage points more likely to pass a nicotine test at 12 months. Ashraf, Karlan, Yin 2006 SEED: locked savings balances up 81% after a year.

### 执行意图（implementation intention）

- node: `self.control.implementation-intention` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-implementation-intention.md`
- 一句话：把目标写成「如果到了某个时间地点，我就做某个动作」，做到的概率明显变高。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gollwitzer 1999. Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 meta: 94 studies, d=0.65. Milkman et al. 2011 PNAS: prompting employees to write the date and time of their flu shot raised vaccination 4.2 points. Nickerson & Rogers 2010: a voting-plan phone script raised turnout 4.1 points.

### 心理对比（WOOP）

- node: `self.control.mental-contrasting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-mental-contrasting.md`
- 一句话：只想象成功会让人松劲；想完成功再想最大的那个障碍，并配上应对动作，才推动行动。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Oettingen & Mayer 2002 JPSP: graduates with more positive fantasies about their job search sent fewer applications, got fewer offers and lower pay two years later. Kappes & Oettingen 2011: positive fantasy lowered systolic blood pressure (energy). Duckworth, Kirby, Gollwitzer, Oettingen 2013: WOOP improved grades and attendance in disadvantaged middle-schoolers.

### 习惯靠情境触发（habit）

- node: `self.control.habit` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-habit.md`
- 一句话：每天近一半的行为是在固定情境下自动发生的；养成它靠同一个情境里重复，时间比你想的长。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Wood, Quinn, Kashy 2002: about 43% of daily actions are done habitually while thinking about something else. Lally et al. 2010 EJSP (n=96): automaticity plateaued after a median of 66 days, range 18 to 254; missing one day did not matter. Neal, Wood et al. 2011: habitual cinema popcorn eaters ate stale popcorn as much as fresh. '21 days' comes from Maltz 1960, a surgeon's impression.

### 摩擦与环境设计（friction, choice architecture）

- node: `self.control.friction` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-friction.md`
- 一句话：把想做的事变得顺手、不想做的事多隔几步，比下决心管用。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Thorndike et al. 2012 AJPH, Massachusetts General Hospital cafeteria: traffic-light labels plus moving water to eye level; bottled-water sales up 25.8%, sugary drinks down. Rozin et al. 2011: making a salad-bar item slightly harder to reach cut intake 8–16%. Avoid Wansink's studies (retracted).

### 间歇强化（variable-ratio reinforcement）

- node: `self.control.variable-reward` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-variable-reward.md`
- 一句话：不知道下一次有没有奖励的行为最难停：老虎机、短视频、反复刷新邮件都是这个结构。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Ferster & Skinner 1957 'Schedules of Reinforcement': pigeons on variable-ratio schedules pecked fastest and kept pecking longest after rewards stopped; partial reinforcement extinction effect (Humphreys 1939). Slot machines: Schüll 2012 'Addiction by Design'. Infinite feeds and pull-to-refresh share the schedule. Allcott, Gentzkow, Song 2022 AER 'Digital Addiction' (about 2,000 US adults): self-control problems account for about 31% of social media use; a screen-time limit tool with commitment cut use (check).

### 新起点效应（fresh start effect）

- node: `self.control.fresh-start` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-fresh-start.md`
- 一句话：周一、月初、生日这种时间界标之后，人更愿意开始新目标；它帮你开头，不保证坚持。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Dai, Milkman, Riis 2014 Management Science: Google searches for 'diet', gym visits and commitment contracts rise at the start of the week, month, year and after birthdays (gym visits up 33% at week start, 47% at semester start; check). Beshears et al. 2021: fresh-start framing did not raise retirement savings.

### 破罐破摔效应（what-the-hell effect）

- node: `self.control.what-the-hell` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-what-the-hell.md`
- 一句话：一旦破了戒，人容易想「反正已经破了」，然后彻底放开。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Herman & Mack 1975: dieters who were made to drink milkshakes first then ate more ice cream, non-dieters ate less. Polivy & Herman 1985. Marlatt's abstinence violation effect in relapse. Counter: treat a lapse as one data point; flexible rather than rigid restraint (Westenhoefer 1999).

### 冷热共情鸿沟（hot–cold empathy gap）

- node: `self.control.hot-cold-gap` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-hot-cold-gap.md`
- 一句话：冷静时想象不出自己在饿、困、生气、有冲动时会怎么做，所以总高估自己的自控。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Loewenstein 1996. Ariely & Loewenstein 2006. Nordgren, van Harreveld, van der Pligt 2009 'restraint bias': smokers who rated their impulse control highest exposed themselves to more temptation and relapsed more four months later.

### 拖延是情绪问题（procrastination）

- node: `self.control.procrastination` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-procrastination.md`
- 一句话：拖的不是时间，是那件事带来的不舒服；先处理情绪，或把任务切到不吓人。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Steel 2007 Psychological Bulletin meta (691 correlations): strongest predictors are task aversiveness, delay of reward, low self-efficacy, impulsiveness. Tice, Bratslavsky, Baumeister 2001: mood repair takes priority. Sirois & Pychyl 2013. Wohl, Pychyl, Bennett 2010: students who forgave themselves for procrastinating before the first exam procrastinated less on the second.

### 目标设定理论（goal-setting theory）

- node: `self.control.goal-setting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-goal-setting.md`
- 一句话：具体而有难度的目标，比「尽力而为」带来更高的表现；前提是有反馈、人认这个目标。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Locke & Latham 2002 American Psychologist, 35 years of research: effect sizes d=0.42–0.80. Latham & Baldes 1975: logging-truck drivers given a goal of 94% of legal load went from about 60% to 90%. Ordóñez et al. 2009 'Goals Gone Wild': Sears auto repair 1990s, Ford Pinto.

### 目标梯度与小进展（goal gradient, progress principle）

- node: `self.control.small-wins` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-small-wins.md`
- 一句话：离终点越近越卖力；看得见的小进展是最强的日常动力。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Kivetz, Urminsky, Zheng 2006: coffee-card holders bought faster as they neared the free cup; a 12-stamp card with 2 pre-stamped beat a 10-stamp card. Nunes & Drèze 2006 car wash: 34% vs 19% completion. Amabile & Kramer 2011: 12,000 diary entries from 238 knowledge workers; best days were days of progress.

### 自我监测（self-monitoring）

- node: `self.control.self-monitoring` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-self-monitoring.md`
- 一句话：把行为记下来这件事本身就会改变行为，公开或写下来效果更大。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Harkin et al. 2016 Psychological Bulletin meta (138 studies, n=19,951): monitoring goal progress d=0.40; larger when progress is reported publicly or physically recorded. Burke, Wang, Sevick 2011 review: self-monitoring consistently linked to weight loss.

### 自我决定论（self-determination theory）

- node: `self.motivation.self-determination` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-self-determination.md`
- 一句话：持久的动力来自三样：自己选的（自主）、做得来（胜任）、和人有连接（关系）；外部奖励会挤掉原有的兴趣。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Deci 1971 Soma puzzles. Lepper, Greene, Nisbett 1973: preschoolers promised a reward for drawing later drew about half as much in free time. Deci, Koestner, Ryan 1999 meta (128 studies): expected tangible rewards undermine intrinsic motivation, d=-0.34 for free-choice behaviour (check). Ryan & Deci 2000.

### 自我效能（self-efficacy）

- node: `self.motivation.self-efficacy` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-self-efficacy.md`
- 一句话：相信自己做得成这件具体的事，这个信念主要来自亲手做成过。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Bandura 1977; four sources: mastery experience, seeing similar others succeed, persuasion, bodily state. Bandura's snake-phobia guided mastery studies. Stajkovic & Luthans 1998 meta (114 studies): r=0.38 with work performance.

### 习得性无助及其修正（learned helplessness）

- node: `self.motivation.learned-helplessness` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-learned-helplessness.md`
- 一句话：被动不是学来的，是面对长期逆境的默认反应；要学的是「我能控制」。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Seligman & Maier 1967: dogs given inescapable shocks later failed to escape. Hiroto 1974 in humans. Maier & Seligman 2016 Psychological Review 'Learned Helplessness at Fifty': neuroscience showed passivity is the default; detecting control (ventromedial prefrontal cortex inhibiting the dorsal raphe) is what is learned and it immunises.

### 有条件的自我价值（contingent self-worth）

- node: `self.motivation.contingent-self-worth` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-contingent-self-worth.md`
- 一句话：把「我值不值」押在成绩上，成功只是松口气，失败却是对整个人的否定。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Crocker & Wolfe 2001. Crocker, Sommers, Luhtanen 2002: seniors applying to graduate school; those basing self-worth on academics had larger swings in self-esteem on acceptance and rejection days. Crocker & Park 2004 Psychological Bulletin 'The Costly Pursuit of Self-Esteem'.

### 冒名顶替现象（impostor phenomenon）

- node: `self.motivation.impostor` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-impostor.md`
- 一句话：客观上做得不错，却觉得自己是混进来的、迟早被拆穿；它很常见，而且不随成就消失。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Clance & Imes 1978 (150 high-achieving women). Bravata et al. 2020 Journal of General Internal Medicine review (62 studies, n=14,161): prevalence 9–82% depending on the measure; linked to depression, anxiety, burnout; no trial of a treatment. Not a diagnosis.

### 完美主义（perfectionism）

- node: `self.motivation.perfectionism` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-perfectionism.md`
- 一句话：高标准本身不伤人，伤人的是「达不到就没价值」和「别人要求我完美」。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Hewitt & Flett 1991 three forms. Curran & Hill 2019 Psychological Bulletin (164 samples, n=41,641 students, 1989–2016): socially prescribed perfectionism up 33%. Limburg et al. 2017 meta: perfectionistic concerns linked to depression, anxiety, eating disorders. Strivings vs concerns distinction.

### 社会比较（social comparison）

- node: `self.motivation.social-comparison` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-social-comparison.md`
- 一句话：人靠和别人比来评估自己，而且多半往上比；看得到的又是别人挑过的那一面。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Festinger 1954. Verduyn et al. 2015: passive Facebook use lowered well-being. Hunt et al. 2018 (n=143): limiting social media to 30 min a day for 3 weeks reduced loneliness and depression. Allcott, Braghieri, Eichmeyer, Gentzkow 2020 AER (n=2,743): paid to deactivate Facebook for 4 weeks, well-being up about 0.09 SD.

### 内省错觉（introspection illusion）

- node: `self.identity.introspection-illusion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-introspection-illusion.md`
- 一句话：人常常不知道自己为什么这么选，却能马上给出一个听着合理的理由。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Nisbett & Wilson 1977 Psychological Review: shoppers chose the rightmost of four identical stockings about 4 to 1 and none cited position. Johansson, Hall, Sikström, Olsson 2005 Science, choice blindness: only about 26% of swapped photos were detected, and people explained why they 'chose' the face they had rejected.

### 历史终结错觉（end of history illusion）

- node: `self.identity.end-of-history` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-end-of-history.md`
- 一句话：人承认过去十年自己变了很多，却认为今后十年不会怎么变；每个年龄段都这样。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Quoidbach, Gilbert, Wilson 2013 Science (n>19,000, ages 18–68): at every age people predicted less change in the next decade than people ten years older reported having had. They would pay 61% more to see their current favourite band in ten years than to see their favourite of ten years ago today.

### 人格会随年龄成熟（maturity principle）

- node: `self.identity.personality-change` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/self/psy-self-personality-change.md`
- 一句话：性格不是定死的：成年后尽责性、情绪稳定性普遍上升，20 到 40 岁变得最多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Roberts, Walton, Viechtbauer 2006 Psychological Bulletin meta (92 longitudinal samples): conscientiousness, emotional stability and social dominance rise, most between 20 and 40. Roberts et al. 2017 meta (207 studies): therapy changes traits, mainly neuroticism, within weeks. Hudson & Fraley 2015: people who set weekly behavioural goals shifted traits over 16 weeks.
