# Group F: 17 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### MBTI 是怎么来的

- node: `persona.mbti.origin` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-origin.md`
- 一句话：一对母女从荣格的《心理类型》出发，为二战时帮人找合适工作编的问卷，后来成了商业产品；它不是从数据里归纳出来的。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Katharine Cook Briggs and daughter Isabel Briggs Myers, neither trained in psychology; Jung 1921 Psychologische Typen (English 1923); first form 1943 (check); Educational Testing Service research edition 1962; ETS dropped it and Consulting Psychologists Press took over 1975 (check); now The Myers-Briggs Company, claims about 2 million assessments a year (check). Contrast: the Big Five came bottom-up from data. Do not repeat card psy-evidence-mbti (overview); this card is the history.

### 四个维度与十六型

- node: `persona.mbti.four-dimensions` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-four-dimensions.md`
- 一句话：E/I、S/N、T/F、J/P 四组二选一，组合成十六型；J/P 是迈尔斯母女加的，荣格原著里没有。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Jung had two attitudes (E/I) and four functions (T, F, S, N); Briggs/Myers added J/P to say which function is dominant (check). Each dimension yields a continuous preference score; the type letter is which side of the midpoint you fall on.

### 分数是一座山，不是两座

- node: `persona.mbti.not-bimodal` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-not-bimodal.md`
- 一句话：每个维度上多数人挤在中间、单峰分布；切点附近差一两分就换字母，「内向的人」「外向的人」是两种人的说法没有数据支持。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: McCrae & Costa 1989 Journal of Personality (n=468, ages 19–93): continuous scores, no evidence of types; Pittenger 2005 Consulting Psychology Journal; Arnau, Green, Rosen, Gleaves, Melancon 2003 taxometric analysis: dimensional not categorical (check); Bess & Harvey 2002 on apparent bimodality as a scoring artefact (check).

### 重测换型

- node: `persona.mbti.retest` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-retest.md`
- 一句话：单个维度分数的重测还算稳定，但整体四字母类型几周后就有相当多人变了，越靠近中间越容易变。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Pittenger 1993/2005: as many as 50% get a different type within about five weeks (check exact wording); dimension test-retest correlations roughly .7–.8 (check); Capraro & Capraro 2002 reliability generalization (check).

### MBTI 能不能用来选人

- node: `persona.mbti.prediction` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-prediction.md`
- 一句话：没有证据说明某个类型更适合某个工作；出版方自己的伦理指南也说不应拿 MBTI 做招聘筛选。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: The Myers-Briggs Company ethical guidelines: not for selection or hiring (check page); Gardner & Martinko 1996 Journal of Management review of MBTI and managers (check); Pittenger 2005. Do not repeat the conscientiousness meta-analysis numbers already on psy-evidence-mbti.

### 把 MBTI 换算成大五

- node: `persona.mbti.map-to-big5` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-map-to-big5.md`
- 一句话：四个维度分别对应大五的外向性、开放性、宜人性、尽责性，唯独没有神经质；当连续分数看才有用。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: McCrae & Costa 1989: E–I with Extraversion, S–N with Openness, T–F with Agreeableness, J–P with Conscientiousness, correlations roughly .4–.7 (check each); none with Neuroticism. Furnham 1996 Personality and Individual Differences replication (check).

### 16personalities 不是 MBTI

- node: `persona.mbti.16personalities` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-16personalities.md`
- 一句话：最流行的免费测试多一个 A/T（自信/动荡）维度，模型更接近大五，和官方 MBTI 是两套东西。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: NERIS Analytics site ('Our Framework' or 'Our Theory') says it combines the letters with Big Five-style traits (check wording); the -A/-T identity scale tracks Neuroticism/emotional stability. Story option: the MBTI boom on Chinese social media 2021–2023, MBTI in dating profiles and job ads (check a reliable report before using).

### 荣格《心理类型》的原意

- node: `persona.jung.psychological-types` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-psychological-types.md`
- 一句话：荣格提出内倾/外倾两种态度和思维、情感、感觉、直觉四种功能，来自他的临床观察；他把类型当成辨认方向的工具，反对拿它给人贴标签。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Jung 1921 Psychologische Typen (English 1923); based on clinical observation, not data; find Jung's own cautions, e.g. the 1936 'Psychological Typology' lecture or the foreword to the Argentine edition (check exact source and wording before quoting; paraphrase if not found).

### 八维与功能栈

- node: `persona.jung.eight-functions` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-eight-functions.md`
- 一句话：四种功能各分内外向得到 Ni、Te 等八维；「主导、辅助、第三、劣势」的排列是后人（迈尔斯、Beebe 等）推出来的模型，荣格没有这样排。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Example: INTJ = Ni–Te–Fi–Se in type-dynamics theory; Myers' rules for dominant/auxiliary; John Beebe's eight-function archetypal model (1980s–2000s, check); Lenore Thomson 1998 'Personality Type: An Owner's Manual' popularized the eight functions online (check).

### 功能栈有证据吗

- node: `persona.jung.type-dynamics-evidence` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-type-dynamics-evidence.md`
- 一句话：用 MBTI 数据检验「主导功能」这类类型动力学，大多不支持；网上的八维测试普遍没有公开的信度、效度数据。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Reynierse & Harker 2001 Journal of Psychological Type: additive model fits better than type dynamics (check); Reynierse 2009 'The case against type dynamics' (check); Socionics has no peer-reviewed validation (check).

### 为什么八维怎么说都对

- node: `persona.jung.unfalsifiable` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-unfalsifiable.md`
- 一句话：功能栈能解释任何行为（不像就说是劣势功能在起作用），无法被证伪；这让它适合聊天和自我反思，却不能当科学结论。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Popper's falsifiability; worked example: the same behaviour 'explained' by the tertiary or the inferior function; link to the Barnum effect.

### 人格和情境，谁决定行为

- node: `persona.use.person-situation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-person-situation.md`
- 一句话：一次行为受情境影响很大，人格预测的是平均倾向；看一个人要看很多场合的平均。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Mischel 1968 'personality coefficient' about .30; Epstein 1979 aggregation over occasions raises consistency (check); Fleeson 2001 JPSP: within-person variation in states as large as between-person differences, while each person's average is stable (check).

### 什么类型最配

- node: `persona.use.compatibility` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-compatibility.md`
- 一句话：「INTJ 配 ENFP」这类配对表没有研究支持；伴侣人格相似度对关系满意度几乎没有预测力，各自的情绪稳定性更要紧。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Dyrenforth, Kashy, Donnellan, Lucas 2010 JPSP (three national samples, over 20,000 couples, check): similarity explained about 0.5% of satisfaction (check), each partner's own traits mattered more; Malouff et al. 2010 meta (check).

### 招聘里的人格测试

- node: `persona.use.hiring` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-hiring.md`
- 一句话：合格的人格测评能小幅提升选人，但应聘者会往「理想答案」作答；拿 MBTI 选人既没有效度，也违背出版方规定。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Birkeland, Manson, Kisamore, Brannick, Smith 2006 International Journal of Selection and Assessment: applicants score higher on desirable traits than non-applicants (check); integrity tests Ones, Viswesvaran & Schmidt 1993 (check).

### 九型人格、DISC 与其他类型测试

- node: `persona.use.other-typologies` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-other-typologies.md`
- 一句话：九型人格、DISC 等流行工具大多缺少独立的信度、效度研究，作用接近一张有趣的自我反思清单。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Enneagram: Hook, Hall, Davis, Van Tongeren, Conner 2021 Journal of Clinical Psychology systematic review: limited, mixed evidence (check); DISC from William Moulton Marston's 1928 'Emotions of Normal People'; Marston also worked on an early lie detector and created Wonder Woman (check, good story).

### 星座和血型性格

- node: `persona.use.astrology-bloodtype` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-astrology-bloodtype.md`
- 一句话：双盲检验里占星师猜不中人格；日本和美国上万人的数据里，血型和性格几乎没有关系。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Carlson 1985 Nature 'A double-blind test of astrology' (check design and result); Nawata 2014 Japanese Journal of Psychology, over 10,000 people in Japan and the US, blood type explained under 0.3% of variance (check).

### 怎样用人格结果认识自己

- node: `persona.use.self-knowledge` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-self-knowledge.md`
- 一句话：把结果当成「我在这一维大概排第几」的参考，加上熟人评价，用来挑环境、补短板，而不是给自己贴标签。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Vazire 2010 JPSP self–other knowledge asymmetry: others see evaluative traits better, the self sees internal states better (check); person–environment fit (Kristof-Brown, Zimmerman & Johnson 2005 Personnel Psychology meta, check).
