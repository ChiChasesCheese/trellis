# Group E: 40 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 从众实验（Asch conformity）

- node: `group.conform.asch` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-asch.md`
- 一句话：答案明摆着，约三分之一的回答还是跟着错的多数走；只要有一个人不跟，压力就垮了。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Asch 1951, 1956: on critical trials 37% of answers conformed; 75% of people conformed at least once, 25% never. One dissenting ally cut conformity to about a quarter of its level. Bond & Smith 1996 meta (133 studies, 17 countries): higher in collectivist cultures, declining in the US since the 1950s.

### 服从权威（Milgram obedience）

- node: `group.conform.milgram` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-milgram.md`
- 一句话：在权威一步步的要求下，多数普通人会做出自己认为不该做的事。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Milgram 1963: 26 of 40 (65%) went to 450 volts. Variations: authority absent by phone 21%; two peers refuse 10%. Burger 2009: 70% continued past 150 V. Doliński et al. 2017 Poland: 90%. Perry 2013 on departures from the script and participants who doubted it.

### 旁观者效应（bystander effect）

- node: `group.conform.bystander` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-bystander.md`
- 一句话：在场的人越多，每个人越觉得轮不到自己；但真实的公共冲突里，多数时候有人出手。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Darley & Latané 1968 seizure study: alone 85% helped, with four others 31%. The Kitty Genovese '38 witnesses' story was a newspaper exaggeration (Manning, Levine, Collins 2007). Fischer et al. 2011 meta: weaker in dangerous emergencies. Philpot et al. 2020 American Psychologist: CCTV of 219 street conflicts in three countries, someone intervened in 91%.

### 多数无知（pluralistic ignorance）

- node: `group.conform.pluralistic-ignorance` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-pluralistic-ignorance.md`
- 一句话：每个人私下都不认同，却以为别人都认同，于是都不说。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Prentice & Miller 1993: Princeton students thought the average student was more comfortable with campus drinking than they were. Bursztyn, González, Yanagizawa-Drott 2020 AER: 87% of young Saudi husbands supported women working outside the home and underestimated others' support; told the truth, more wives applied for jobs.

### 羊群效应与信息瀑布（herd behavior, information cascade）

- node: `group.conform.herd` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-herd.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：后来的人看前面人的选择来做判断，早期一点随机的领先会滚成压倒性的流行。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Banerjee 1992; Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer, Welch 1992. Salganik, Dodds, Watts 2006 Science MusicLab (n=14,341): showing download counts made hits bigger and less predictable; the same song could be top or middling in different worlds. Muchnik, Aral, Taylor 2013 Science: one random initial upvote raised final ratings by 25%.

### 社会懈怠（social loafing）

- node: `group.team.social-loafing` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-social-loafing.md`
- 一句话：一起干活时每个人出的力比单干时少，人越多越少；个人贡献看得见就好很多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Ringelmann 1913 rope pulling. Latané, Williams, Harkins 1979: clapping and shouting; in groups of six, each person made about 36–40% of the noise they made alone (check). Karau & Williams 1993 meta (78 studies): reduced when individual output is identifiable and the task matters.

### 群体极化（group polarization）

- node: `group.team.group-polarization` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-group-polarization.md`
- 一句话：观点相近的人讨论之后，整体立场比讨论前更极端。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Stoner 1961 risky shift. Moscovici & Zavalloni 1969. Myers & Bishop 1970. Schkade, Sunstein, Hastie 2007 Colorado experiment: liberals from Boulder and conservatives from Colorado Springs each became more extreme and more uniform after 15 minutes of discussion. Janis's 'groupthink' (1972) rests on case studies with weak experimental support.

### 群体智慧及其条件（wisdom of crowds）

- node: `group.team.wisdom-of-crowds` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-wisdom-of-crowds.md`
- 一句话：很多人各自独立的估计取平均，常比其中的专家准；一旦互相看到答案，就不准了。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Galton 1907 Nature: 787 fairgoers guessed an ox's dressed weight; median 1,207 lb, actual 1,198 lb. Lorenz, Rauhut, Schweitzer, Helbing 2011 PNAS: seeing others' estimates narrowed the spread without improving accuracy. Surowiecki 2004 conditions: diversity, independence, aggregation.

### 头脑风暴不如各自先想（brainstorming）

- node: `group.team.brainstorming` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-brainstorming.md`
- 一句话：同样几个人，各自写完再汇总，点子比坐在一起说更多、更好。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Osborn 1953 claimed groups double output. Taylor, Berry, Block 1958 found the opposite. Diehl & Stroebe 1987: production blocking is the main cause. Mullen, Johnson, Salas 1991 meta.

### 心理安全感（psychological safety）

- node: `group.team.psychological-safety` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-psychological-safety.md`
- 一句话：成员相信提问、认错、唱反调不会被惩罚的团队，学得快，表现也好。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Edmondson 1999 Administrative Science Quarterly (51 teams); her earlier hospital study found better teams reported more errors, because they talked about them. Google Project Aristotle 2015 (180 teams): psychological safety was the top factor. Frazier et al. 2017 meta (136 samples).

### 最小群体范式（minimal group paradigm）

- node: `group.team.minimal-group` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-minimal-group.md`
- 一句话：哪怕按抛硬币分组，人也会偏向自己这一组。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tajfel, Billig, Bundy, Flament 1971: boys grouped by supposed preference for Klee or Kandinsky gave more to their own group and preferred a bigger gap over a bigger total. Sherif 1954 Robbers Cave: conflict from competition, reduced by shared goals; Perry 2018 documents an earlier failed attempt and experimenter steering.

### 弱关系的力量（strength of weak ties）

- node: `group.network.weak-ties` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-weak-ties.md`
- 一句话：新工作的消息更多来自不太熟的人，因为熟人知道的和你知道的差不多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Granovetter 1973, 1974 'Getting a Job' (282 Boston-area professionals): of those who found the job through a contact, 16.7% saw that contact often, 55.6% occasionally, 27.8% rarely. Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov, Brynjolfsson, Aral 2022 Science: randomised 'People You May Know' experiments on 20 million LinkedIn users; moderately weak ties produced the most job moves, an inverted U.

### 结构洞（structural holes）

- node: `group.network.structural-holes` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-structural-holes.md`
- 一句话：站在两个互不来往的圈子之间的人，更早看到两边的信息，好点子和晋升都更多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Burt 1992. Burt 2004 American Journal of Sociology 'Structural Holes and Good Ideas': 673 supply-chain managers at a large US electronics company; those bridging groups had ideas rated more valuable, higher pay and faster promotion.

### 马太效应（Matthew effect, cumulative advantage）

- node: `group.network.matthew` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-matthew.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：早期的一点领先带来更多资源和关注，于是差距越拉越大，哪怕起点的差别几乎是运气。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Merton 1968 Science, named after Matthew 25:29. Bol, de Vaan, van de Rijt 2018 PNAS: Dutch early-career grant applicants just above the cutoff won more than twice as much funding over the next eight years as those just below, with near-identical scores. Salganik 2006 MusicLab. Preferential attachment (Barabási & Albert 1999).

### 成功里的运气（luck in success）

- node: `group.network.luck` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-luck.md`
- 一句话：竞争越激烈、参与的人越多，最顶上那个越可能是又强又走运的人；极端的成绩里运气的成分最大。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Denrell & Liu 2012 PNAS: the most extreme performers are expected to be less skilled than those just below them. Frank 2016 'Success and Luck': simulations where luck counts 2% still mostly crown someone who was not the most skilled. Pluchino, Biondo, Rapisarda 2018 talent-vs-luck model. Relative age effect: Barnsley 1985, about 40% of elite Canadian youth hockey players born January–March.

### 相对剥夺与参照群体（relative deprivation, reference group）

- node: `group.norms.relative-deprivation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-relative-deprivation.md`
- 一句话：满意不满意，取决于和身边可比的人比，不取决于绝对水平。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Stouffer et al. 1949 'The American Soldier': Air Corps men, with fast promotion, were less satisfied with promotion than Military Police with slow promotion. Luttmer 2005 QJE: higher neighbours' earnings, lower own happiness. Card, Mas, Moretti, Saez 2012 AER: University of California staff shown a salary website; those below the median for their unit became less satisfied and more likely to look for a job; those above did not change.

### 描述性规范的反作用（descriptive norms）

- node: `group.norms.descriptive-norms` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-descriptive-norms.md`
- 一句话：「很多人都在这么干坏事」这句劝告，听到的是「大家都这么干」。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Cialdini, Reno, Kallgren 1990 littering. Cialdini 2003 / Cialdini et al. 2006 Petrified Forest: the sign 'many past visitors have removed wood' raised theft of marked wood from 2.92% to 7.92% (check); a sign showing one thief with a prohibition lowered it to 1.67%. Schultz et al. 2007: households below average energy use raised it after learning the average, unless a smiley was added.

### 破窗效应（broken windows）

- node: `group.norms.broken-windows` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-broken-windows.md`
- 一句话：环境里有人违规的痕迹，会让后来的人更容易跟着违规；用它来论证「零容忍执法」则证据不足。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Zimbardo 1969 abandoned cars. Wilson & Kelling 1982 Atlantic essay. Keizer, Lindenberg, Steg 2008 Science: 33% littered a flyer in a clean alley, 69% when the wall had graffiti; theft of an envelope with a visible 5-euro note rose from 13% to 27%. Policing version disputed (Harcourt & Ludwig 2006; Braga 2015 meta: modest, from problem-solving not arrests).

### 地位、控制感与健康（Whitehall studies）

- node: `group.norms.control-status` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/group/psy-group-control-status.md`
- 一句话：职级越低死亡率越高，差距不是穷造成的，很大一部分来自对自己工作的控制感低。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Marmot et al. 1978 Whitehall I (17,530 male British civil servants): the lowest grade had about three times the 10-year mortality of the top grade. Whitehall II (Marmot et al. 1991, 1997 Lancet): low job control explained a large share of the gradient in heart disease. Karasek 1979 demand–control model.

### 囚徒困境（prisoner's dilemma）

- node: `game.theory.prisoners-dilemma` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-prisoners-dilemma.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：两个人各自选对自己最有利的做法，结果比都让一步更差。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Flood & Dresher 1950 at RAND; Albert Tucker gave it the prison story. One-shot lab games: cooperation is far above the zero that pure self-interest predicts; Sally 1995 meta (130 experiments 1958–1992): mean cooperation 47%. Communication before play raises it sharply. Arms races, price wars, doping.

### 重复博弈与以牙还牙（tit for tat）

- node: `game.theory.tit-for-tat` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-tit-for-tat.md`
- 一句话：同一批人反复打交道时，先合作、被坑就回敬、对方改了就原谅，是最稳的策略。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Axelrod 1980 tournaments (14 entries, then 62): Anatol Rapoport's four-line tit for tat won both. Axelrod 1984: be nice, retaliate, forgive, be clear. With noise, generous tit for tat and win-stay lose-shift do better (Nowak & Sigmund 1992, 1993). Dal Bó & Fréchette 2011: cooperation rises with the chance of meeting again.

### 零和偏差与固定馅饼（zero-sum bias, fixed-pie bias）

- node: `game.theory.zero-sum-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-zero-sum-bias.md`
- 一句话：人默认别人多得一分自己就少一分，于是看不见双方都能更好的方案。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Thompson & Hastie 1990: 68% of negotiators assumed the other side's interests were directly opposed when they were not; many left with a worse deal than available. Bazerman's 'mythical fixed pie'. Meegan 2010. Johnson, Zhang, Keil 2022. Davidai & Ongis 2019.

### 公地悲剧与奥斯特罗姆（tragedy of the commons）

- node: `game.theory.commons` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-commons.md`
- 一句话：人人可用的资源会被用垮，但不是必然：能互相监督、自己定规矩的小群体能管好几百年。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Hardin 1968 Science. Ostrom 1990 'Governing the Commons': Swiss alpine pastures (Törbel, rules since 1483), Spanish huertas, Maine lobster fisheries; eight design principles; Nobel Prize in economics 2009. Cox, Arnold, Villamayor Tomás 2010 reviewed 91 studies supporting the principles.

### 公共品博弈与惩罚（public goods game）

- node: `game.theory.public-goods` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-public-goods.md`
- 一句话：没有惩罚时，合作会一轮轮滑向搭便车；允许自己掏钱惩罚搭便车的人，合作就回来。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Fehr & Gächter 2000 AER, 2002 Nature 'Altruistic punishment in humans': contributions decayed without punishment and rose to near full with it; people paid to punish even strangers they would never meet again. Herrmann, Thöni, Gächter 2008 Science (16 societies): in some, cooperators got punished too.

### 最后通牒博弈（ultimatum game）

- node: `game.theory.ultimatum` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-ultimatum.md`
- 一句话：人宁可什么都不要，也要拒绝太不公平的分法。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Güth, Schmittberger, Schwarze 1982. Typical offers 40–50%; offers below about 20% are rejected about half the time (Camerer 2003). Henrich et al. 2001: 15 small-scale societies, mean offers from 26% (Machiguenga) to 58% (Lamalera). Sanfey et al. 2003 Science: unfair offers activate the anterior insula.

### 赢家的诅咒（winner's curse）

- node: `game.theory.winners-curse` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-winners-curse.md`
- 一句话：很多人竞价同一个价值不确定的东西时，赢的往往是估得最高的那个，也就是估过头的那个。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Capen, Clapp, Campbell 1971: oil companies bidding for offshore leases in the Gulf of Mexico. Bazerman & Samuelson 1983: jars of coins worth $8 auctioned in MBA classes; mean estimate $5.13, mean winning bid $10.01. Thaler 1988. Applies to acquisitions, free agents, bidding wars for houses.

### 猜三分之二平均数（k-level thinking）

- node: `game.theory.beauty-contest` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-beauty-contest.md`
- 一句话：赢不靠推到最深，靠猜对别人推了几步；多数人只推一两步。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Keynes 1936 beauty contest. Nagel 1995 AER: guess 2/3 of the average of 0–100; mean about 33–37, most players at one or two steps of reasoning; equilibrium is 0. Financial Times contest 1997 run by Thaler: winning number 13. Politiken 2005 (n=19,196): average 21.6.

### 古德哈特定律（Goodhart's law）

- node: `game.incentive.goodhart` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-goodhart.md`
- 一句话：一个数字一旦被拿来考核，人就会去优化这个数字本身，它也就不再反映原来想衡量的东西。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Goodhart 1975; Campbell 1979. Hanoi 1902: French bounty per rat tail led to tailless rats and rat farming (Vann 2003). Wells Fargo 2016: sales quotas, about 3.5 million accounts opened without consent. Atlanta 2009 test-score cheating: 178 educators implicated. Lines of code, tickets closed, test coverage.

### 罚款成了价格（motivation crowding out）

- node: `game.incentive.crowding-out` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-crowding-out.md`
- 一句话：给本来靠自觉和情面的事标上价，会把自觉挤走，钱一撤也回不来。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Gneezy & Rustichini 2000 Journal of Legal Studies 'A Fine Is a Price': ten Haifa day-care centres; a small fine for late pickup about doubled lateness, and it stayed high after the fine was removed. Gneezy & Rustichini 2000 QJE 'Pay Enough or Don't Pay at All'. Titmuss 1970 on paid blood donation; Mellström & Johannesson 2008.

### 委托代理问题（principal–agent problem）

- node: `game.incentive.principal-agent` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-principal-agent.md`
- 一句话：替你办事的人有自己的利益和你不知道的信息，他的最优做法未必是你的。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Jensen & Meckling 1976. Levitt & Syverson 2008: real-estate agents selling their own homes kept them on the market about 10 days longer and sold for about 3.7% more than comparable client homes. Financial advisers, mechanics, recruiters paid on placement.

### 柠檬市场（market for lemons）

- node: `game.incentive.lemons` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-lemons.md`
- 一句话：卖方比买方更清楚质量时，买方只肯出平均价，好货退出，剩下的越来越差。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Akerlof 1970 QJE (rejected by three journals), Nobel 2001. Fixes: warranties, certification, reputation, third-party inspection. Used cars, insurance, the job market for people changing jobs (Gibbons & Katz 1991: laid-off workers' wage loss is larger than that of workers from plant closings).

### 信号理论（signaling）

- node: `game.incentive.signaling` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-signaling.md`
- 一句话：说自己好没有成本所以没人信；只有差的人学不起、装不像的代价，才是可信的信号。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Spence 1973 QJE job-market signalling, Nobel 2001. Sheepskin effect: Hungerford & Solon 1987, the diploma year pays more than other years of schooling. Zahavi 1975 handicap principle. Bertrand & Mullainathan 2004 AER: résumés with white-sounding names got 50% more callbacks.

### 人识别谎言的准确率

- node: `game.deceive.lie-detection` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-lie-detection.md`
- 一句话：靠观察神态判断对方是否说谎，准确率平均 54%，警察、法官也一样。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Bond & DePaulo 2006 Personality and Social Psychology Review meta (206 documents, 24,483 judges): 54% correct; 47% of lies and 61% of truths identified. DePaulo et al. 2003 Psychological Bulletin (158 cues): gaze aversion and fidgeting are not reliable. US GAO 2013 on the TSA's behaviour-detection programme (about $900 million): no evidence it works.

### 真相默认（truth-default theory）

- node: `game.deceive.truth-default` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-truth-default.md`
- 一句话：人先相信、有疑点才怀疑；这在多数时候划算，骗子利用的正是这一点。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Levine 2014, 2019 'Duped'. Gilbert, Tafarodi, Malone 1993 'You Can't Not Believe Everything You Read': understanding a statement means accepting it first; rejecting takes extra effort that load disrupts. Lies are mostly discovered later through evidence or confession, not at the time through demeanour (Park et al. 2002).

### 巴纳姆效应（Barnum / Forer effect）

- node: `game.deceive.barnum` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-barnum.md`
- 一句话：一段对谁都适用的笼统描述，人会觉得是在精准地说自己。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Forer 1949: 39 students each received the same 'personal' sketch assembled from an astrology book and rated its accuracy 4.26 out of 5. Dickson & Kelly 1985 review: stronger when the description is flattering and said to be made for you. Cold reading, horoscopes, many personality tests.

### 骗局的结构（anatomy of a scam）

- node: `game.deceive.scam-structure` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-scam-structure.md`
- 一句话：长线骗局不靠一句谎话，靠一串步骤：建立信任、给小甜头、制造紧迫、隔开你和能提醒你的人、让你舍不得已经投进去的。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Lea, Fischer, Evans 2009 University of Exeter report for the UK Office of Fair Trading 'The psychology of scams'. Fischer, Lea, Evans 2013. US FTC: reported romance-scam losses $1.3 billion in 2022. Each step is a known effect: reciprocity, foot-in-the-door, scarcity, authority, sunk cost, isolation. Victims are not especially gullible; overconfidence in one's own judgment is a risk factor. Check figures.

### 非注意盲视（inattentional blindness）

- 一句话：注意力盯着一件事时，眼前发生的另一件大事可以完全看不见。

### 谈不成的退路（BATNA）

- node: `game.negotiate.batna` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-batna.md`
- 一句话：谈判里的力量主要来自：谈崩了，你最好的另一条路有多好。

- anchor: Fisher & Ury 1981 'Getting to Yes'. Pinkley, Neale, Bennett 1994: negotiators with a better alternative got better outcomes for themselves and larger joint gains. Magee, Galinsky, Gruenfeld 2007. A second offer in hand changes a salary talk more than any phrasing.

### 先出价与锚（first offer anchoring）

- node: `game.negotiate.first-offer` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-first-offer.md`
- 一句话：谁先报出一个有依据的数字，成交价就往谁那边靠；自己信息少的时候才让对方先开口。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Galinsky & Mussweiler 2001 JPSP: whoever made the first offer got the better outcome; the first offer strongly predicted the final price; thinking about the other side's alternatives or one's own target removed the advantage. Mason, Lee, Wiley, Ames 2013: precise offers ($5,115 rather than $5,000) anchor more strongly.

### 谈薪：开口和不开口的差别

- node: `game.negotiate.salary-negotiation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/game/psy-game-salary-negotiation.md`
- 一句话：多数人接受第一个报价；开口谈的人起薪通常高出几个百分点，并在之后每次调薪里被放大。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Babcock & Laschever 2003: among Carnegie Mellon master's graduates 57% of men and 7% of women negotiated; negotiators' starting salaries were about 7.4% higher (check). Marks & Harold 2011: new hires who negotiated gained about $5,000 on average. Leibbrandt & List 2015 field experiment: stating that pay is negotiable closes the gender gap in asking.
