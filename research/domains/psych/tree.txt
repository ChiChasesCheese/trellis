# Master tree for the `psych` domain. gen_tree.py turns it into skeleton/psych.yaml and the per-group anchor lists.
#
#   #  id | title | summary                      level-1 node
#   ## id | title | summary                      level-2 node (id is the full dotted id)
#   *  slug | title | summary | grade | anchor [| req=a.b.c,d.e.f] [| tag=十日终焉]    leaf in the first 200 (core: true)
#   -  slug | title | summary | grade | anchor [| ...]                                 leaf kept for a later wave
#
# grade: A = replicated or meta-analytic support; B = real but small, conditional or thinly replicated;
#        C = famous and weakened or refuted (the card says what failed).
# anchor: the orchestrator's starting point from memory. Every number in it is re-checked online by the card agent
#         before it goes on a card; where the source disagrees, the source wins and the claims file says so.

# evidence | 先学会看证据 | 一个心理学结论靠不靠得住，看它怎么做出来的、别人重做是否成立、效果有多大；流行说法里有一批已经站不住。
## evidence.method | 研究是怎么出错的 | 复现、效应量、发表偏倚、因果与样本：读任何「研究表明」之前先问的五个问题。
* replication-crisis | 复现危机（replication crisis） | 把已发表的心理学实验原样重做，只有一部分还能得到原结论；单个小样本的惊人发现要打折。 | A | Open Science Collaboration 2015 Science: 100 studies, 97% of originals significant, 36% of replications significant, mean effect size about half. Camerer 2018 (Nature Human Behaviour): 13 of 21 Science/Nature social-science experiments replicated. Many Labs 2 (2018): 14 of 28.
* effect-size | 效应量（effect size） | 「有显著差异」不等于「差得多」；要问效果多大，Cohen's d 0.2 小、0.5 中、0.8 大。 | A | Cohen 1988 benchmarks. Contrast pair for intuition: implementation intentions d=0.65 (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) vs growth-mindset interventions d≈0.08 (Sisk 2018). d=0.2 means 58% chance a random treated person beats a random control (common-language effect size).
* publication-bias | 发表偏倚与 p 值操纵（publication bias, p-hacking） | 只有「有效果」的研究容易发表，研究者又有很多分析自由度，所以文献整体高估效果。 | A | Simmons, Nelson, Simonsohn 2011 Psychological Science 'False-Positive Psychology': with flexible analysis they 'showed' listening to 'When I'm Sixty-Four' made people 1.5 years younger. Rosenthal 1979 file-drawer problem. Kvarven, Strømland, Johannesson 2020 (Nature Human Behaviour): meta-analyses' effects about 3x those of pre-registered multi-lab replications.
* correlation-causation | 相关不等于因果（correlation vs causation） | 两件事一起出现，可能是反向因果、共同原因或选择；随机对照实验和自然实验才回答因果。 | A | Hormone replacement therapy: observational studies suggested lower heart disease, the Women's Health Initiative RCT (2002, n≈16,600) found increased risk; healthy-user bias. Use it as the story.
* weird-samples | WEIRD 样本 | 多数心理学实验的被试是西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕、民主社会的大学生，结论未必适用所有人。 | A | Henrich, Heine, Norenzayan 2010 Behavioral and Brain Sciences: 96% of subjects in top psychology journals from Western industrialised countries holding 12% of world population; Müller-Lyer illusion strength varies by society (Segall 1966); ultimatum game offers vary (Henrich 2001).
- meta-analysis | 元分析与预注册（meta-analysis, preregistration） | 把多项研究合并估计效果；预注册先写下假设和分析再收数据，堵住事后挑结果的路。 | A | Kaplan & Irvin 2015 PLoS ONE: NHLBI trials with a benefit fell from 57% to 8% after registration became mandatory in 2000.
- average-vs-individual | 平均效应不等于对你有效 | 实验给的是一群人的平均差异；个体差异大时，对你是否有效要自己小规模试。 | B | heterogeneity of treatment effects; ecological fallacy (Robinson 1950).
## evidence.myth | 流行但站不住的说法 | 写进畅销书、重做却不成立或被大幅削弱的著名结论；知道它们错在哪，比多背十个效应有用。
* ego-depletion | 自我损耗：意志力像肌肉会用完？ | 「意志力是会耗尽的资源」在多实验室重做中效果接近零。 | C | Baumeister 1998 radishes vs cookies (n=67). Hagger 2016 registered replication: 23 labs, N=2,141, d=0.04. Vohs 2021: 36 labs, N=3,531, d=0.06. Glucose claim (Gailliot 2007) also failed. | req=evidence.method.replication-crisis
* power-pose | 高能量姿势（power pose） | 摆两分钟「强势姿势」改变激素和冒险行为的说法没有复现；只剩「自我感觉更有力」的小效应。 | C | Carney, Cuddy, Yap 2010 (n=42). Ranehill 2015 (n=200): no effect on testosterone, cortisol or risk taking. Carney's 2016 public statement: 'I do not believe that power pose effects are real.' Cuddy's TED talk is among the most viewed. | req=evidence.method.replication-crisis
* marshmallow | 棉花糖实验 | 4 岁能等 15 分钟不等于以后更成功：样本扩大并控制家庭背景后，关联缩小到原来的一小部分。 | C | Mischel 1972; Shoda, Mischel, Peake 1990 follow-up had fewer than 100 children of Stanford faculty/staff. Watts, Duncan, Quan 2018 Psychological Science (n=918): association half the size, reduced by two thirds after controlling for family background and early cognition. Kidd, Palmeri, Aslin 2013: children waited 12 min with a reliable adult vs 3 min with an unreliable one. | req=evidence.method.correlation-causation
* ten-thousand-hours | 一万小时定律 | 一万小时是一组小提琴手的平均数，不是门槛；练习量只解释一部分水平差异，领域越不规则解释得越少。 | C | Ericsson, Krampe, Tesch-Römer 1993: best violinists averaged about 10,000 h by age 20, with wide spread. Gladwell 2008 turned it into a rule; Ericsson objected. Macnamara, Hambrick, Oswald 2014 meta (88 studies): deliberate practice explains 26% of variance in games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, under 1% professions. | req=evidence.method.effect-size
* stanford-prison | 斯坦福监狱实验 | 「普通人一穿上制服就变残暴」不是实验自发出现的：看守被教过怎么做，重做没有得到同样结果。 | C | Zimbardo 1971 (24 students, stopped after 6 days). Le Texier 2019 American Psychologist, from the archives: guards were briefed and coached. Reicher & Haslam 2006 BBC Prison Study: guards did not become tyrannical. | req=evidence.method.replication-crisis
* learning-styles | 学习风格（视觉型、听觉型） | 按「学习风格」配教法并不提高成绩；内容适合什么形式，比人属于什么类型重要。 | C | Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer, Bjork 2008 Psychological Science in the Public Interest: almost no study used the required crossover design, and those that did contradict the meshing hypothesis. Surveys: around 90% of teachers believe it (Dekker 2012; Newton 2020 review 89%).
* mehrabian | 沟通的 7-38-55 定律 | 「语言只占 7%」来自两项只测单个词语情绪色彩的小实验，不能推广到一般沟通。 | C | Mehrabian & Wiener 1967; Mehrabian & Ferris 1967: single words spoken in conflicting tones; applies only to feelings and attitudes when words and tone conflict. Mehrabian's own website says the equation is misapplied.
* maslow | 马斯洛需求层次 | 需求确实分种类，但「先满足低层才追求高层」没有数据支持，金字塔图也不是马斯洛画的。 | C | Maslow 1943 Psychological Review. Bridgman, Cummings, Ballard 2019: the pyramid came from management consultants in the 1960s. Tay & Diener 2011 JPSP (Gallup World Poll, 123 countries, n≈60,865): needs predict well-being largely independently of order. Wahba & Bridwell 1976 review.
* mbti | MBTI 与大五人格 | MBTI 把连续的特质硬切成 16 型，重测常常换型；研究里用的是大五人格（Big Five）。 | C | Pittenger 1993/2005: as many as 50% get a different type on retest within about five weeks; scores are normally distributed, not bimodal. Big Five (Costa & McCrae; Goldberg): conscientiousness predicts job performance (Barrick & Mount 1991 meta).
* folk-laws | 鸡汤定律：手表、木桶、鲶鱼、温水青蛙 | 中文管理读物里的很多「定律」是比喻或编出来的故事，没有研究；温水里的青蛙其实会跳走。 | C | Boiling frog: Goltz 1869 frogs with brains removed stayed; modern biologists (Hutchison, University of Oklahoma; Zug, Smithsonian) say intact frogs jump out as water warms. Catfish effect: no documented Norwegian fishery practice. 手表定律 = Segal's law (a joke attributed to a 1930s newspaper). 木桶 = Liebig's law of the minimum, from agricultural chemistry (Sprengel 1828, Liebig 1840), an analogy when applied to people. | tag=十日终焉
- left-brain-right-brain | 左脑人右脑人、大脑只用了 10% | 脑成像没有发现「整体偏用一侧」的人；10% 的说法没有任何依据。 | C | Nielsen 2013 PLoS ONE (n=1,011 resting fMRI): no global left- or right-dominant network.
- behavioral-priming | 行为启动（priming） | 「看到老年相关词就走得更慢」这类社会启动效应重做失败。 | C | Bargh, Chen, Burrows 1996; Doyen 2012 PLoS ONE failed with automatic timing; Kahneman's 2012 open letter 'train wreck'.
- hawthorne | 霍桑效应 | 「被观察就更努力」的原始照明实验数据并不支持这个结论。 | C | Levitt & List 2011 AEJ: Applied reanalysed the found illumination data. McCambridge 2014 systematic review: participation effects exist but are inconsistent.
- dishonesty-studies | 被撤稿的「诚实」研究 | 「在表格开头签名会更诚实」的数据是伪造的；十诫启动也没复现。 | C | Shu, Mazar, Gino, Ariely, Bazerman 2012 PNAS retracted 2021 after Data Colada found fabricated data; Verschuere 2018 multi-lab failed to replicate Mazar 2008 Ten Commandments.
- backfire-effect | 逆火效应（backfire effect） | 「纠正错误信息反而让人更坚信」基本没有复现；纠正通常有用，只是效果会退。 | C | Nyhan & Reifler 2010; Wood & Porter 2019 (n=10,100, 52 issues): no backfire.
- zeigarnik | 蔡格尼克效应（Zeigarnik effect） | 「没做完的事记得更牢」的原始结果重复起来很不稳定。 | C | Zeigarnik 1927; Van Bergen 1968 review of failed replications; Masicampo & Baumeister 2011: making a plan stops unfinished goals intruding.

# judge | 判断与决策 | 大脑用省力的捷径做判断，错得有规律：怎么估概率、怎么看得失、怎么为自己辩护，以及把纠偏变成流程的办法。
## judge.dual | 两套系统与启发式 | 快而自动的直觉和慢而费力的推理；直觉用容易想到、看着像、先入为主来代替真正的计算。
* two-systems | 系统 1 与系统 2（dual process） | 直觉先给答案，推理通常只是签字放行；觉得「一眼就知道」的题最该停一下。 | A | Kahneman 2011. Frederick 2005 Cognitive Reflection Test (bat and ball $1.10): n=3,428, mean 1.24 of 3 correct; more than half of students at Harvard, MIT, Princeton answered 10 cents. The two 'systems' are a description, not two brain parts.
* availability | 可得性启发（availability heuristic） | 越容易想起的事，被判断得越常见、越危险。 | A | Tversky & Kahneman 1973: most people say more English words start with K than have K as third letter (the reverse is true). Lichtenstein, Slovic 1978: people overestimate deaths from tornadoes, underestimate asthma, diabetes. After 9/11 Americans drove instead of flying: Gigerenzer 2004/2006 estimates about 1,500 extra road deaths in the following year.
* base-rate-neglect | 代表性启发与基础比率忽略（base-rate neglect） | 按「像不像」判断概率，忘了这类事本来有多常见。 | A | Kahneman & Tversky 1973 engineer-lawyer. Tversky & Kahneman 1983 Linda: 85% ranked 'bank teller and feminist' above 'bank teller'. Casscells 1978 NEJM: disease prevalence 1/1000, false-positive rate 5%: only 11 of 60 Harvard medical staff and students gave about 2%; most said 95%. Gigerenzer & Hoffrage 1995: natural frequencies fix most of it.
* anchoring | 锚定效应（anchoring） | 先看到的数字，即使无关，也会把之后的估计拉过去。 | A | Tversky & Kahneman 1974: wheel of fortune rigged at 10 or 65; median estimates of African nations in the UN 25% vs 45%. Northcraft & Neale 1987: real-estate agents' appraisals moved with the listing price while they denied using it. Many Labs 1 (2014): anchoring among the strongest replicated effects.
* framing | 框架效应（framing effect） | 同一个选择，说成「救活多少」还是「死多少」，人的偏好会反过来。 | A | Tversky & Kahneman 1981 Science, Asian disease problem: 72% chose the sure option in the gain frame, 78% chose the gamble in the loss frame. Replicated in Many Labs 1. McNeil 1982 NEJM: surgery chosen more when described by survival than by mortality, physicians included.
* normalcy-bias | 正常化偏误（normalcy bias） | 灾难刚开始时，多数人先当它是正常情况，花时间确认、收拾、等别人先动。 | B | Disaster research: NIST 2005 World Trade Center study (Averill et al.): survivors waited about 6 minutes on average before starting to evacuate; many first made calls, shut down computers, gathered belongings. Drabek 1986 on warning response. Ripley 2008 'The Unthinkable' is the popular account. Check the figures. | tag=十日终焉
- affect-heuristic | 情感启发（affect heuristic） | 喜欢的东西被判断为收益大、风险小；讨厌的反过来。 | A | Finucane, Alhakami, Slovic, Johnson 2000; Slovic 2007.
- wysiati | 眼见即全部（WYSIATI）与叙事谬误 | 用手头的信息编出连贯的故事，不问缺了什么；故事越顺越自信。 | B | Kahneman 2011 ch. 7; Brenner, Koehler, Tversky 1996 one-sided evidence.
- conjunction-fallacy | 合取谬误（conjunction fallacy） | 细节越多越像真的，概率却只会更低。 | A | Tversky & Kahneman 1983.
- contrast-effect | 对比效应（contrast effect） | 同一个东西，放在更差的旁边显得好，放在更好的旁边显得差。 | A | Kenrick & Gutierres 1980; Simonsohn & Loewenstein 2006 movers' rents; Bhargava & Fisman 2014 speed dating.
- scope-neglect | 范围忽视（scope neglect） | 愿意为一件事付出多少，几乎不随它的规模变化。 | A | Desvousges et al. 1993: willingness to pay to save 2,000, 20,000 or 200,000 migrating birds from oil ponds was $80, $78, $88; Kahneman 2000.
- action-bias | 行动偏差（action bias） | 压力下人宁可做点什么，哪怕不动才是最好的选择。 | B | Bar-Eli, Azar, Ritov, Keidar-Levin, Schein 2007: 286 penalty kicks; goalkeepers jumped left or right 94% of the time although staying in the centre stopped the most.
## judge.prospect | 得失与风险 | 人按相对于参照点的得失来衡量结果，损失比同样大的收益重，小概率被放大（前景理论）。
* loss-aversion | 损失厌恶（loss aversion） | 丢 100 块的难受，大约要得到 200 块才能抵消。 | A | Kahneman & Tversky 1979 Econometrica (prospect theory); Tversky & Kahneman 1992 λ=2.25. Brown, Imai, Vieider, Camerer 2024 Journal of Economic Literature meta-analysis: 607 estimates from 150 articles, mean λ about 1.96 (check). Critique: Gal & Rucker 2018: weak for small stakes. Chicago Heights teachers (Fryer, Levitt, List, Sadoff 2012/2022): bonus paid upfront and clawed back raised math scores, same bonus paid at year end did not.
* endowment | 禀赋效应（endowment effect） | 东西一旦归你，你要的卖价就比你愿意出的买价高出一倍左右。 | A | Kahneman, Knetsch, Thaler 1990 JPE mugs: median selling price about $7.12 vs buying $2.87 (check). List 2003 QJE: experienced sports-card dealers show little of it. Free trials and 30-day returns use it. | req=judge.prospect.loss-aversion
* sunk-cost | 沉没成本谬误（sunk cost fallacy） | 已经花掉、收不回来的钱和时间不该影响接下来的决定，但它会。 | A | Arkes & Blumer 1985: theatre season-ticket buyers who paid full price ($15) attended more plays in the first half of the season than those given $2 or $7 discounts. Concorde. Staw 1976 escalation of commitment. NBA: Staw & Hoang 1995 high draft picks get more minutes regardless of performance. | req=judge.prospect.loss-aversion
* status-quo-default | 现状偏差与默认选项（status quo bias, default effect） | 多数人不改默认值；谁设默认值，谁就决定了结果。 | A | Samuelson & Zeckhauser 1988. Johnson & Goldstein 2003 Science: organ-donor consent 12% in Germany (opt-in) vs 99.98% in Austria (opt-out). Madrian & Shea 2001 QJE: 401(k) participation of new hires 37% to 86% under automatic enrolment; most stayed at the default 3% rate. | req=judge.prospect.loss-aversion
* mental-accounting | 心理账户（mental accounting） | 钱被分到不同的心理账户里，同样一笔钱在不同账户里花得不一样。 | A | Thaler 1985, 1999. Tversky & Kahneman 1981: lost a $10 ticket: 46% buy another; lost a $10 bill: 88% buy the ticket. Thaler & Johnson 1990 house-money effect. Shefrin & Thaler: windfalls are spent more readily than salary.
* probability-weighting | 小概率被放大（probability weighting） | 人同时买彩票和保险：很小的概率在心里被放大，接近确定的概率被打折。 | A | Allais 1953 paradox; Kahneman & Tversky 1979 certainty effect: 80% of 4,000 vs 3,000 sure: 80% chose sure; fourfold pattern (Tversky & Kahneman 1992).
* regret-action-inaction | 后悔：做了的和没做的 | 短期内更后悔做错的事，拉长到一生，更后悔没做的事。 | B | Gilovich & Medvec 1994 JPSP, 1995 Psychological Review: in surveys of lifetime regrets about 75% were inactions (check). Morrison & Roese 2011 national sample: romance, family, education, career top domains.
* opportunity-cost-neglect | 机会成本忽视（opportunity cost neglect） | 花钱花时间时，人很少自动想到「这些本来还能换什么」。 | B | Frederick, Novemsky, Wang, Dhar, Nowlis 2009 Journal of Consumer Research: adding 'keep the $14.99 for other purchases' cut purchase of a DVD from 75% to 55%.
- disposition-effect | 处置效应（disposition effect） | 投资者急着卖掉赚钱的股票，死拿亏钱的。 | A | Shefrin & Statman 1985; Odean 1998 Journal of Finance (10,000 accounts): 14.8% of gains realised vs 9.8% of losses; sold winners outperformed held losers by 3.4% next year. | req=judge.prospect.loss-aversion
- omission-bias | 不作为偏差（omission bias） | 同样的坏结果，由「做了」造成比由「没做」造成显得更糟。 | A | Ritov & Baron 1990 vaccination; Spranca, Minsk, Baron 1991.
- ikea-effect | 宜家效应（IKEA effect） | 自己动手做的东西，哪怕做得一般，也估价更高。 | B | Norton, Mochon, Ariely 2012: builders bid 63% more for their own plain IKEA box than non-builders; only when the build was completed.
- hyperbolic-risk | 确定性效应之外：模糊厌恶（ambiguity aversion） | 人宁可选已知概率的赌局，也不选概率不明的。 | A | Ellsberg 1961.
## judge.justify | 确认与自我辩护 | 人先有结论再找理由：只看支持自己的证据，事后觉得早就知道，对自己的把握过高。
* confirmation-bias | 确认偏误（confirmation bias） | 人找的、记的、信的，多是支持自己已有看法的证据。 | A | Wason 1960 2-4-6 task: only about 21% found the rule on first announcement, most tested only confirming triples. Lord, Ross, Lepper 1979: both sides of the death-penalty debate became more extreme after reading the same mixed evidence. Nickerson 1998 review.
* hindsight-outcome | 后见之明与结果偏差（hindsight bias, outcome bias） | 知道结果后，觉得当初就该料到，并按结果好坏评价当初的决定。 | A | Fischhoff 1975 (Gurkha war outcomes); Fischhoff & Beyth 1975 Nixon's China trip. Baron & Hershey 1988 outcome bias. Poker players call it 'resulting' (Annie Duke 2018).
* overconfidence | 过度自信与校准（overconfidence, calibration） | 人说「九成把握」的事，实际对的比例低得多。 | A | Alpert & Raiffa 1982: 98% confidence intervals missed the true value about 40% of the time (check). Svenson 1981: 93% of US drivers in the sample rated themselves above the median. Moore & Healy 2008: overestimation, overplacement, overprecision are three different things. Weather forecasters are well calibrated because they get daily feedback (Murphy & Winkler 1977).
* dunning-kruger | 达克效应的真实版本（Dunning–Kruger effect） | 成绩最差的人高估自己最多，但他们并没有觉得自己比高手强；网上那条「愚昧之巅」曲线不在原论文里。 | B | Kruger & Dunning 1999 JPSP: bottom quartile scored at the 12th percentile and estimated themselves at the 62nd. Part of the pattern is regression to the mean plus the better-than-average effect (Krueger & Mueller 2002; Nuhfer 2016; Gignac & Zajenkowski 2020). | req=judge.justify.overconfidence
* planning-fallacy | 计划谬误（planning fallacy） | 估自己的项目要多久，总按最顺利的情况估，哪怕以前次次超时。 | A | Buehler, Griffin, Ross 1994 JPSP: psychology students predicted 33.9 days to finish the thesis, took 55.5; fewer than half finished by the date they were 99% sure of (check). Kahneman's curriculum team: estimated 2 years, the reference class said 7–10 years and 40% never finish; it took 8. Sydney Opera House: planned 1963 for A$7M, opened 1973 for A$102M.
* explanatory-depth | 解释深度错觉（illusion of explanatory depth） | 人以为自己懂拉链、马桶、政策是怎么回事，真要一步步讲出来才发现不懂。 | A | Rozenblit & Keil 2002 Cognitive Science: self-rated understanding dropped after writing a step-by-step explanation. Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, Sloman 2013: explaining how a policy works made political positions more moderate.
* cognitive-dissonance | 认知失调（cognitive dissonance） | 行为和信念对不上时，人常常改信念来配合已经做了的事。 | B | Festinger & Carlsmith 1959: paid $1 to call a boring task fun, people rated it more enjoyable than those paid $20. Festinger 1956 'When Prophecy Fails'. Aronson & Mills 1959 effort justification. Vaidis et al. 2024 (39 labs, N=4,898): attitudes shifted after writing a counter-attitudinal essay, but the classic choice manipulation made no difference.
* bias-blind-spot | 偏见盲点（bias blind spot） | 人看得出别人的偏差，看不出自己的；聪明也不管用。 | A | Pronin, Lin, Ross 2002. West, Meserve, Stanovich 2012 JPSP: higher cognitive ability did not reduce the blind spot, slightly the opposite. Consequence: knowing the names of biases is not protection; procedures are.
- motivated-reasoning | 动机性推理（motivated reasoning） | 想信的结论只要「能不能信」，不想信的结论则要「非信不可吗」。 | A | Kunda 1990; Ditto & Lopez 1992; Kahan 2013 numeracy and polarisation.
- self-serving-bias | 自利归因（self-serving bias） | 成功归自己，失败归环境。 | A | Miller & Ross 1975; Mezulis 2004 meta d=0.96, weaker in East Asia.
- false-consensus | 虚假共识（false consensus effect） | 高估别人和自己想法、做法相同的比例。 | A | Ross, Greene, House 1977 'Eat at Joe's' sandwich board: those who agreed estimated 62% would, those who refused estimated 33%.
- ostrich-effect | 鸵鸟效应（ostrich effect） | 预感是坏消息时，人干脆不去看。 | B | Karlsson, Loewenstein, Seppi 2009: Scandinavian investors logged in to check portfolios less often when markets fell; Sicherman et al. 2016.
- illusion-of-control | 控制错觉（illusion of control） | 人觉得自己能影响纯靠运气的结果。 | A | Langer 1975: people who chose their own lottery ticket asked $8.67 to sell it, those handed one asked $1.96.
- just-world | 公正世界信念（just-world hypothesis） | 人想相信世界是公平的，于是倾向于认为受害者自己有问题。 | A | Lerner & Simmons 1966: observers who could not stop a woman's (staged) electric shocks rated her character lower; Hafer & Bègue 2005 review.
## judge.prob | 概率与统计直觉 | 随机、样本和筛选怎么骗人：极端之后会回落，看到的只是活下来的，小样本最爱出极端值。
* regression-to-mean | 回归均值（regression to the mean） | 一次特别好或特别差的表现之后，下一次多半更接近平常水平，和你做了什么无关。 | A | Galton 1886 heights. Kahneman's Israeli flight instructors: praise seemed to hurt and scolding to help. Sports Illustrated cover jinx; 'sophomore slump'.
* survivorship | 幸存者偏差（survivorship bias） | 只看到通过筛选的样本，就会把筛选的结果当成成功的原因。 | A | Abraham Wald, Statistical Research Group, 1943: armour where returning bombers had no holes (Mangel & Samaniego 1984 reconstruct his memos). Mutual funds: Elton, Gruber, Blake 1996: survivorship inflates average returns by about 0.9% a year. Business books that study only winners.
* small-numbers | 小数定律（law of small numbers） | 小样本更容易出极端结果；人却把小样本当成总体的缩影。 | A | Tversky & Kahneman 1971. Wainer 2007 'The Most Dangerous Equation': the best-scoring US schools were disproportionately small, and so were the worst; the Gates Foundation spent about $1.7B on small schools before backing off. Kidney-cancer rates lowest and highest in rural counties.
* gamblers-hot-hand | 赌徒谬误与热手（gambler's fallacy, hot hand） | 独立事件没有记性；人却觉得连出几次黑就「该出红了」。 | A | Monte Carlo casino, 18 Aug 1913: black 26 times in a row. Chen, Moskowitz, Shue 2016 QJE: asylum judges, loan officers and baseball umpires decide against the previous run. Hot hand: Gilovich, Vallone, Tversky 1985 said none; Miller & Sanjurjo 2018 Econometrica found a counting bias in that analysis; a modest hot hand exists.
* selection-berkson | 选择偏差与伯克森悖论（Berkson's paradox） | 只在被筛选过的人群里看，两个本来无关的特征会显得此消彼长。 | A | Berkson 1946 hospital patients. Ellenberg 2014: if you only date people who are attractive or nice, the two look negatively correlated. Admissions: among admitted students test scores and grades correlate negatively.
* simpson | 辛普森悖论（Simpson's paradox） | 每个分组里都成立的趋势，合在一起可以反过来。 | A | Bickel, Hammel, O'Connell 1975 Science: Berkeley 1973 admissions: 44% of men vs 35% of women overall, yet most departments slightly favoured women; women applied to the harder departments. Kidney-stone treatments (Charig 1986).
* ruin-ergodicity | 期望值为正也会破产（凯利公式与遍历性） | 一连串可以输光的下注里，押多少比押什么重要；平均值好看，不代表你这一条路走得到。 | A | Kelly 1956. Haghani & Dewey 2016: 61 finance students and young professionals, a coin with 60% heads, $25 stake, 30 minutes, capped at $250: 28% went bust and only 21% reached the cap; Kelly says bet 20% each time. Peters 2019 Nature Physics on ergodicity.
* pareto-power-law | 二八定律与幂律（Pareto principle, power law） | 很多结果不是平均分布的：少数原因、少数人、少数作品占了大部分。 | A | Pareto 1896: about 20% of Italians owned 80% of the land. Juran 1951 'vital few'. Microsoft 2002 (Ballmer memo): fixing the top 20% of bugs removed 80% of errors and crashes. Newman 2005 on power laws: city sizes, citations, book sales. The ratio is not always 80/20. | tag=十日终焉
* murphy | 墨菲定律（Murphy's law） | 「会出错的事总会出错」不是心理学定律；站得住的部分是：次数够多，小概率的事必然发生，而人只记得出事的那次。 | B | Origin: Capt. Edward Murphy, Edwards Air Force Base 1949, rocket-sled tests with John Stapp; sensors wired backwards. Matthews 1995 European Journal of Physics: toast lands butter side down for physical reasons (table height, half rotation), Ig Nobel 1996. Engineering use: design so the wrong way is impossible (poka-yoke, Shingo). | tag=十日终焉
- bayes-update | 贝叶斯更新（Bayesian updating） | 新证据应该按它的区分力，把原来的概率往上或往下调一点，不是推倒重来。 | A | Tetlock's superforecasters update often and in small steps.
- butterfly-chaos | 蝴蝶效应（chaos） | 对初始条件敏感的系统长期不可预测；它说的是预测有上限，不是「小事必有大后果」。 | A | Lorenz 1963; 1972 talk title 'Does the flap of a butterfly's wings in Brazil set off a tornado in Texas?'; rounding .506127 to .506. | tag=十日终焉
- lindy | 林迪效应（Lindy effect） | 不会自然老化的东西（书、技术、想法），已经存在得越久，预期还能存在越久。 | B | Goldman 1964; Mandelbrot 1982; Taleb 2012. A heuristic from power-law survival, not an experiment.
## judge.hygiene | 决策卫生 | 知道偏差的名字挡不住偏差，流程才行：先看同类事的统计、先假设失败、列清单、让判断可检验。
* outside-view | 外部视角（reference class forecasting） | 先问「同类的事一般结果如何」，再按这件事的特点微调。 | A | Kahneman & Lovallo 1993; Flyvbjerg 2006: reference class forecasting, used by the UK Treasury and Danish government for infrastructure; his database of 16,000 projects: 8.5% on budget and on time, 0.5% also on benefits (How Big Things Get Done, 2023). | req=judge.justify.planning-fallacy
* premortem | 事前验尸（premortem） | 动手前假设项目已经失败，让每个人写下失败的原因。 | B | Mitchell, Russo, Pennington 1989: imagining an event has already happened (prospective hindsight) raised the number of reasons generated by about 30%. Klein 2007 HBR 'Performing a Project Premortem'. Kahneman called it his favourite debiasing technique. Evidence for outcomes is thin.
* superforecasting | 超级预测者的习惯（superforecasting） | 预测准的人不是更懂行，而是把问题拆开、用概率说话、经常小幅修正、事后对账。 | A | Tetlock 2005: 284 experts, about 28,000 forecasts, barely better than chance; 'foxes' beat 'hedgehogs'. Good Judgment Project (IARPA ACE tournament 2011–2015): top 2% of forecasters beat the control by about 60% in year 1 and were reported to beat intelligence analysts with classified data (check). Mellers 2014: one hour of training improved accuracy about 10%.
* noise | 噪声（noise） | 同一个案子交给不同的人，甚至同一个人换个时间，判出来差很多；这种随机差异和偏差一样伤。 | A | Kahneman, Sibony, Sunstein 2021. Insurance underwriters: executives expected about 10% difference between two underwriters' premiums, the median was 55%. Sentencing: Clancy 1981 study of 208 federal judges on 16 identical cases. Fix: structured criteria, independent judgments averaged.
* checklist | 清单（checklist） | 把必做的几步写下来逐条核对，能挡住熟练的人因为熟练而漏掉的步骤。 | B | Haynes et al. 2009 NEJM, WHO Surgical Safety Checklist in 8 hospitals: deaths 1.5% to 0.8%, complications 11.0% to 7.0%. Pronovost 2006 NEJM Michigan ICUs: central-line infections median to 0 over 18 months. Boeing B-17 1935 crash as the origin story. Urbach 2014 Ontario: mandated checklist without culture change showed no effect.
* satisficing | 够好就行（satisficing vs maximizing） | 总想挑到最好的人，挑到的客观上略好，主观上更不满意。 | B | Simon 1956. Schwartz et al. 2002 JPSP maximization scale: maximizers report less happiness, more regret. Iyengar, Wells, Schwartz 2006: maximizing graduates got 20% higher starting salaries and were less satisfied with them.
* choice-overload | 选择过载（choice overload） | 选项太多有时让人干脆不选，但这个效应不稳定，取决于选项是否难比、人是否已有偏好。 | B | Iyengar & Lepper 2000 jam study: 24 jams 3% bought, 6 jams 30%. Scheibehenne, Greifeneder, Todd 2010 meta (50 studies): mean effect about zero. Chernev, Böckenholt, Goodman 2015: appears with complex choices, hard-to-compare options, unclear preferences.
- decision-journal | 决策日志 | 做重大决定时写下当时知道什么、预期什么、多大把握，以后才能分清是决策差还是运气差。 | B | Follows from hindsight and outcome bias; practice of investors and of Tetlock's forecasters; no direct trial.
- automation-bias | 自动化偏差（automation bias） | 有自动系统给建议时，人会少查证，系统错的时候跟着错。 | A | Skitka, Mosier, Burdick 1999; Parasuraman & Manzey 2010 review; Goddard, Roudsari, Wyatt 2012 clinical decision support.
- base-rates-for-life | 给人生决定找基础比率 | 创业、读博、换城市之前，先查这类决定的统计结果。 | B | e.g. US BLS: about 20% of new businesses fail in year 1, about half by year 5.
- red-team | 唱反调的人与红队 | 指定人专门找漏洞，比泛泛地「欢迎提意见」有效。 | B | Schweiger, Sandberg, Ragan 1986 devil's advocacy vs consensus; Nemeth 2001: authentic dissent beats role-played.

# self | 自我：情绪、动机与自控 | 情绪怎么调、内耗怎么停、事情怎么开始做并坚持下去，以及人对自己的了解有多不准。
## self.emotion | 情绪调节 | 情绪不能直接命令，但能通过怎么解释、怎么行动、离多远看来改变；压下去和反复想是最常用也最没用的两招。
* reappraisal | 重新评估与压抑（reappraisal vs suppression） | 换一种解释来看同一件事，比忍着不表现出来有效，代价也小。 | A | Gross 1998 JPSP (disgust films: suppression hid expression but raised sympathetic arousal; reappraisal lowered experienced disgust). Gross & John 2003: habitual reappraisers have more positive emotion and better relationships. Aldao, Nolen-Hoeksema, Schweizer 2010 meta (114 studies).
* affect-labeling | 给情绪命名（affect labeling） | 把正在感受的情绪用词说出来，它的强度会降下来一点。 | B | Lieberman 2007 Psychological Science: labelling emotional faces lowered amygdala response and raised right ventrolateral prefrontal activity (n=30). Kircanski, Lieberman, Craske 2012: spider-fearful people who described their fear during exposure had lower skin conductance a week later than reappraisal or distraction groups.
* self-distancing | 自我抽离（self-distancing） | 用第三人称、用旁观者的位置想自己的事，更冷静也更明智。 | B | Kross, Ayduk, Mischel 2005. Kross et al. 2014 JPSP: using one's own name in self-talk before a speech reduced anxiety and rumination. Grossmann & Kross 2014 Psychological Science 'Solomon's paradox': people reason more wisely about a friend's problem than their own; distancing closes the gap.
* rumination | 反刍（rumination） | 反复想「为什么是我、哪里不对」不解决问题，只把低落拉长。 | A | Nolen-Hoeksema 1991 response styles theory. Nolen-Hoeksema & Morrow 1991 JPSP: students measured before the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake; ruminators were more depressed 10 days and 7 weeks after. Lyubomirsky & Nolen-Hoeksema 1995: rumination makes problem solving worse and more pessimistic; 8 minutes of distraction lifts mood in dysphoric people.
* cognitive-distortions | 认知歪曲（cognitive distortions） | 低落时的想法有固定的歪法：非黑即白、灾难化、读心、以偏概全；认出来再对着证据检查。 | A | Beck 1963, 1979; Burns 1980 list. CBT for depression: Cuijpers et al. 2023 World Psychiatry meta (409 trials, n≈52,700): g=0.79 vs control; comparable to medication in the short term, better at 6–12 months.
* behavioral-activation | 行为激活（behavioral activation） | 低落时不等有心情再做事；先按计划去做，情绪跟在行动后面。 | A | Jacobson et al. 1996 component analysis: activation alone did as well as full cognitive therapy. Dimidjian et al. 2006 (n=241): for severe depression, activation matched antidepressants and beat cognitive therapy. Richards et al. 2016 Lancet COBRA trial (n=440): activation by junior mental-health workers non-inferior to CBT and cheaper.
* avoidance-exposure | 回避与暴露（avoidance, exposure） | 躲开害怕的事，当下轻松，害怕却被养大；一点点去接触，它才会变小。 | A | Mowrer 1947 two-factor theory: avoidance is reinforced by relief. Exposure therapy is the most effective treatment for phobias and social anxiety (Wolitzky-Taylor 2008 meta; Carpenter 2018 meta of CBT for anxiety). Craske 2014 inhibitory learning: the point is to violate the expectation, not to feel calm.
* self-compassion | 自我关怀（self-compassion） | 失败后像对朋友那样对自己，下一次反而更肯努力；自责不是动力。 | B | Neff 2003 scale. Leary et al. 2007 JPSP five studies. Breines & Chen 2012 PSPB: after a failed test, the self-compassion group studied longer for the retest than the self-esteem and control groups. MacBeth & Gumley 2012 meta: r=-0.54 with depression, anxiety, stress. Mostly self-report; causal evidence is thinner.
* mindfulness | 正念的证据（mindfulness） | 八周正念训练对焦虑和抑郁有中等大小的效果，和其他正规疗法差不多，不是万能药。 | B | Goyal et al. 2014 JAMA Internal Medicine meta (47 trials, n=3,515): anxiety effect size 0.38, depression 0.30 at 8 weeks; no evidence it beats exercise or CBT. Kuyken et al. 2016 JAMA Psychiatry: MBCT cut depressive relapse, hazard ratio 0.69. Van Dam et al. 2018 'Mind the Hype'.
* negativity-bias | 负面偏差（negativity bias） | 同样强度的坏事比好事影响大、记得久；一次批评要好几次肯定才抵得掉。 | A | Baumeister, Bratslavsky, Finkenauer, Vohs 2001 'Bad Is Stronger Than Good'; Rozin & Royzman 2001. Gottman's 5:1 ratio in stable couples (observational). The 'critical positivity ratio 2.9013' (Fredrickson & Losada 2005) was mathematically refuted (Brown, Sokal, Friedman 2013).
- expressive-writing | 表达性书写（expressive writing） | 连续几天每天写 15–20 分钟最烦心的事，对身心有小而真实的好处。 | B | Pennebaker & Beall 1986; Frattaroli 2006 meta (146 studies): d=0.075. Spera, Buhrfeind, Pennebaker 1994: laid-off engineers who wrote found jobs sooner (small sample).
- worry-time | 给担心定个时间（worry postponement） | 把担心推迟到每天固定的半小时，其余时间出现就记下来放一边。 | B | Borkovec et al. 1983 stimulus control; McGowan & Behar 2013.
- mind-wandering | 走神与不快乐（mind-wandering） | 人醒着的时间近一半在想别的事，走神时比专注时不快乐。 | B | Killingsworth & Gilbert 2010 Science: 2,250 adults sampled by iPhone, mind wandering 46.9% of samples.
- defusion | 认知解离（cognitive defusion, ACT） | 把「我不行」换成「我有一个『我不行』的想法」，想法还在，但不再指挥你。 | B | Hayes; Masuda 2004 word repetition; A-Tjak 2015 meta of ACT.
- misattribution-arousal | 唤醒的错误归因 | 心跳加速可能被当成心动；身体的唤醒需要一个解释，解释可以是错的。 | B | Dutton & Aron 1974 Capilano bridge; Schachter & Singer 1962 (replications mixed).
## self.control | 自控与习惯 | 自控靠的不是忍，是把选择提前做掉：改环境、定触发条件、给未来的自己设限。
* present-bias | 现时偏好（present bias, hyperbolic discounting） | 为下周的自己选健康和努力，为今天的自己选舒服；同一个人，偏好随时间远近翻转。 | A | Read & van Leeuwen 1998: choosing a snack for next week, 74% chose fruit; choosing for now, 70% chose chocolate. Thaler 1981; Laibson 1997. O'Donoghue & Rabin 1999: naive vs sophisticated agents. DellaVigna & Malmendier 2006 AER 'Paying Not to Go to the Gym': members on a $70 monthly contract went 4.3 times a month, paying about $17 a visit when a pass cost $10.
* commitment-device | 承诺机制（commitment device） | 趁冷静时给将来的自己设限或设罚，让到时候想反悔也难。 | B | Ariely & Wertenbroch 2002: students who set their own binding deadlines did better than those with one final deadline, worse than evenly spaced imposed ones. Giné, Karlan, Zinman 2010 (CARES, Philippines): smokers who staked their own money were 3 percentage points more likely to pass a nicotine test at 12 months. Ashraf, Karlan, Yin 2006 SEED: locked savings balances up 81% after a year. | req=self.control.present-bias
* implementation-intention | 执行意图（implementation intention） | 把目标写成「如果到了某个时间地点，我就做某个动作」，做到的概率明显变高。 | A | Gollwitzer 1999. Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006 meta: 94 studies, d=0.65. Milkman et al. 2011 PNAS: prompting employees to write the date and time of their flu shot raised vaccination 4.2 points. Nickerson & Rogers 2010: a voting-plan phone script raised turnout 4.1 points.
* mental-contrasting | 心理对比（WOOP） | 只想象成功会让人松劲；想完成功再想最大的那个障碍，并配上应对动作，才推动行动。 | B | Oettingen & Mayer 2002 JPSP: graduates with more positive fantasies about their job search sent fewer applications, got fewer offers and lower pay two years later. Kappes & Oettingen 2011: positive fantasy lowered systolic blood pressure (energy). Duckworth, Kirby, Gollwitzer, Oettingen 2013: WOOP improved grades and attendance in disadvantaged middle-schoolers.
* habit | 习惯靠情境触发（habit） | 每天近一半的行为是在固定情境下自动发生的；养成它靠同一个情境里重复，时间比你想的长。 | A | Wood, Quinn, Kashy 2002: about 43% of daily actions are done habitually while thinking about something else. Lally et al. 2010 EJSP (n=96): automaticity plateaued after a median of 66 days, range 18 to 254; missing one day did not matter. Neal, Wood et al. 2011: habitual cinema popcorn eaters ate stale popcorn as much as fresh. '21 days' comes from Maltz 1960, a surgeon's impression.
* friction | 摩擦与环境设计（friction, choice architecture） | 把想做的事变得顺手、不想做的事多隔几步，比下决心管用。 | B | Thorndike et al. 2012 AJPH, Massachusetts General Hospital cafeteria: traffic-light labels plus moving water to eye level; bottled-water sales up 25.8%, sugary drinks down. Rozin et al. 2011: making a salad-bar item slightly harder to reach cut intake 8–16%. Avoid Wansink's studies (retracted).
* variable-reward | 间歇强化（variable-ratio reinforcement） | 不知道下一次有没有奖励的行为最难停：老虎机、短视频、反复刷新邮件都是这个结构。 | A | Ferster & Skinner 1957 'Schedules of Reinforcement': pigeons on variable-ratio schedules pecked fastest and kept pecking longest after rewards stopped; partial reinforcement extinction effect (Humphreys 1939). Slot machines: Schüll 2012 'Addiction by Design'. Infinite feeds and pull-to-refresh share the schedule. Allcott, Gentzkow, Song 2022 AER 'Digital Addiction' (about 2,000 US adults): self-control problems account for about 31% of social media use; a screen-time limit tool with commitment cut use (check).
- temptation-bundling | 诱惑捆绑（temptation bundling） | 把想拖的事和只在那时才允许的享受绑在一起。 | B | Milkman, Minson, Volpp 2014 Management Science (n=226): audiobooks available only at the gym raised visits 51% at first; the effect faded after a holiday break. Kirgios et al. 2020: a 24 Hour Fitness megastudy version raised weekly workouts 10–14%.
* fresh-start | 新起点效应（fresh start effect） | 周一、月初、生日这种时间界标之后，人更愿意开始新目标；它帮你开头，不保证坚持。 | B | Dai, Milkman, Riis 2014 Management Science: Google searches for 'diet', gym visits and commitment contracts rise at the start of the week, month, year and after birthdays (gym visits up 33% at week start, 47% at semester start; check). Beshears et al. 2021: fresh-start framing did not raise retirement savings.
* what-the-hell | 破罐破摔效应（what-the-hell effect） | 一旦破了戒，人容易想「反正已经破了」，然后彻底放开。 | B | Herman & Mack 1975: dieters who were made to drink milkshakes first then ate more ice cream, non-dieters ate less. Polivy & Herman 1985. Marlatt's abstinence violation effect in relapse. Counter: treat a lapse as one data point; flexible rather than rigid restraint (Westenhoefer 1999).
* hot-cold-gap | 冷热共情鸿沟（hot–cold empathy gap） | 冷静时想象不出自己在饿、困、生气、有冲动时会怎么做，所以总高估自己的自控。 | A | Loewenstein 1996. Ariely & Loewenstein 2006. Nordgren, van Harreveld, van der Pligt 2009 'restraint bias': smokers who rated their impulse control highest exposed themselves to more temptation and relapsed more four months later.
* procrastination | 拖延是情绪问题（procrastination） | 拖的不是时间，是那件事带来的不舒服；先处理情绪，或把任务切到不吓人。 | A | Steel 2007 Psychological Bulletin meta (691 correlations): strongest predictors are task aversiveness, delay of reward, low self-efficacy, impulsiveness. Tice, Bratslavsky, Baumeister 2001: mood repair takes priority. Sirois & Pychyl 2013. Wohl, Pychyl, Bennett 2010: students who forgave themselves for procrastinating before the first exam procrastinated less on the second.
* goal-setting | 目标设定理论（goal-setting theory） | 具体而有难度的目标，比「尽力而为」带来更高的表现；前提是有反馈、人认这个目标。 | A | Locke & Latham 2002 American Psychologist, 35 years of research: effect sizes d=0.42–0.80. Latham & Baldes 1975: logging-truck drivers given a goal of 94% of legal load went from about 60% to 90%. Ordóñez et al. 2009 'Goals Gone Wild': Sears auto repair 1990s, Ford Pinto.
* small-wins | 目标梯度与小进展（goal gradient, progress principle） | 离终点越近越卖力；看得见的小进展是最强的日常动力。 | B | Kivetz, Urminsky, Zheng 2006: coffee-card holders bought faster as they neared the free cup; a 12-stamp card with 2 pre-stamped beat a 10-stamp card. Nunes & Drèze 2006 car wash: 34% vs 19% completion. Amabile & Kramer 2011: 12,000 diary entries from 238 knowledge workers; best days were days of progress.
* self-monitoring | 自我监测（self-monitoring） | 把行为记下来这件事本身就会改变行为，公开或写下来效果更大。 | A | Harkin et al. 2016 Psychological Bulletin meta (138 studies, n=19,951): monitoring goal progress d=0.40; larger when progress is reported publicly or physically recorded. Burke, Wang, Sevick 2011 review: self-monitoring consistently linked to weight loss.
- moral-licensing | 道德许可（moral licensing） | 做了一件「好事」之后，人会给自己放纵一下的许可。 | B | Monin & Miller 2001; Blanken, van de Ven, Zeelenberg 2015 meta d=0.31 with signs of publication bias.
- urge-surfing | 冲动冲浪（urge surfing） | 冲动像浪，不满足它也会在几十分钟内自己退下去；看着它过去。 | B | Bowen & Marlatt 2009 smokers: fewer cigarettes over 7 days.
- planning-buffer | 展开任务（unpacking） | 把任务拆成步骤再估时间，估计会更接近实际。 | B | Kruger & Evans 2004.
- deadline-effect | 截止日期效应 | 期限越远，开始得越晚；短期限的完成率更高。 | B | Shu & Gneezy 2010 gift certificates: 3-week expiry redeemed 31%, 2-month 6%. Tversky & Shafir 1992.
## self.motivation | 动机与自我价值 | 人为什么肯做事、为什么放弃，以及把自我价值押在成绩上要付的代价。
* self-determination | 自我决定论（self-determination theory） | 持久的动力来自三样：自己选的（自主）、做得来（胜任）、和人有连接（关系）；外部奖励会挤掉原有的兴趣。 | A | Deci 1971 Soma puzzles. Lepper, Greene, Nisbett 1973: preschoolers promised a reward for drawing later drew about half as much in free time. Deci, Koestner, Ryan 1999 meta (128 studies): expected tangible rewards undermine intrinsic motivation, d=-0.34 for free-choice behaviour (check). Ryan & Deci 2000.
* self-efficacy | 自我效能（self-efficacy） | 相信自己做得成这件具体的事，这个信念主要来自亲手做成过。 | A | Bandura 1977; four sources: mastery experience, seeing similar others succeed, persuasion, bodily state. Bandura's snake-phobia guided mastery studies. Stajkovic & Luthans 1998 meta (114 studies): r=0.38 with work performance.
* learned-helplessness | 习得性无助及其修正（learned helplessness） | 被动不是学来的，是面对长期逆境的默认反应；要学的是「我能控制」。 | B | Seligman & Maier 1967: dogs given inescapable shocks later failed to escape. Hiroto 1974 in humans. Maier & Seligman 2016 Psychological Review 'Learned Helplessness at Fifty': neuroscience showed passivity is the default; detecting control (ventromedial prefrontal cortex inhibiting the dorsal raphe) is what is learned and it immunises.
* contingent-self-worth | 有条件的自我价值（contingent self-worth） | 把「我值不值」押在成绩上，成功只是松口气，失败却是对整个人的否定。 | B | Crocker & Wolfe 2001. Crocker, Sommers, Luhtanen 2002: seniors applying to graduate school; those basing self-worth on academics had larger swings in self-esteem on acceptance and rejection days. Crocker & Park 2004 Psychological Bulletin 'The Costly Pursuit of Self-Esteem'.
* impostor | 冒名顶替现象（impostor phenomenon） | 客观上做得不错，却觉得自己是混进来的、迟早被拆穿；它很常见，而且不随成就消失。 | B | Clance & Imes 1978 (150 high-achieving women). Bravata et al. 2020 Journal of General Internal Medicine review (62 studies, n=14,161): prevalence 9–82% depending on the measure; linked to depression, anxiety, burnout; no trial of a treatment. Not a diagnosis.
* perfectionism | 完美主义（perfectionism） | 高标准本身不伤人，伤人的是「达不到就没价值」和「别人要求我完美」。 | A | Hewitt & Flett 1991 three forms. Curran & Hill 2019 Psychological Bulletin (164 samples, n=41,641 students, 1989–2016): socially prescribed perfectionism up 33%. Limburg et al. 2017 meta: perfectionistic concerns linked to depression, anxiety, eating disorders. Strivings vs concerns distinction.
* social-comparison | 社会比较（social comparison） | 人靠和别人比来评估自己，而且多半往上比；看得到的又是别人挑过的那一面。 | A | Festinger 1954. Verduyn et al. 2015: passive Facebook use lowered well-being. Hunt et al. 2018 (n=143): limiting social media to 30 min a day for 3 weeks reduced loneliness and depression. Allcott, Braghieri, Eichmeyer, Gentzkow 2020 AER (n=2,743): paid to deactivate Facebook for 4 weeks, well-being up about 0.09 SD.
- explanatory-style | 解释风格（explanatory style） | 把坏事解释成暂时的、局部的、可改的人，更能扛挫折。 | B | Seligman & Schulman 1986 Met Life agents: optimists sold 37% more in the first two years (check); pessimists quit at twice the rate.
- flow | 心流（flow） | 难度略高于能力、目标清楚、反馈即时，人会全神贯注到忘记时间。 | B | Csikszentmihalyi 1975, 1990, experience sampling; Fong 2015 meta challenge–skill balance r≈0.24.
- yerkes-dodson | 耶克斯–多德森定律 | 压力和表现的倒 U 关系在教科书里被讲得比原始数据（1908 年的小鼠）强得多。 | C | Yerkes & Dodson 1908 mice; Teigen 1994 on how the 'law' was constructed later.
- locus-of-control | 控制点（locus of control） | 相信结果主要取决于自己的人，健康、收入和工作满意度都略好。 | B | Rotter 1966; Ng, Sorensen, Eby 2006 meta.
## self.identity | 认识自己 | 人对自己的行为原因、未来的变化和性格的了解，比自己以为的差。
* introspection-illusion | 内省错觉（introspection illusion） | 人常常不知道自己为什么这么选，却能马上给出一个听着合理的理由。 | A | Nisbett & Wilson 1977 Psychological Review: shoppers chose the rightmost of four identical stockings about 4 to 1 and none cited position. Johansson, Hall, Sikström, Olsson 2005 Science, choice blindness: only about 26% of swapped photos were detected, and people explained why they 'chose' the face they had rejected.
* end-of-history | 历史终结错觉（end of history illusion） | 人承认过去十年自己变了很多，却认为今后十年不会怎么变；每个年龄段都这样。 | B | Quoidbach, Gilbert, Wilson 2013 Science (n>19,000, ages 18–68): at every age people predicted less change in the next decade than people ten years older reported having had. They would pay 61% more to see their current favourite band in ten years than to see their favourite of ten years ago today.
* personality-change | 人格会随年龄成熟（maturity principle） | 性格不是定死的：成年后尽责性、情绪稳定性普遍上升，20 到 40 岁变得最多。 | A | Roberts, Walton, Viechtbauer 2006 Psychological Bulletin meta (92 longitudinal samples): conscientiousness, emotional stability and social dominance rise, most between 20 and 40. Roberts et al. 2017 meta (207 studies): therapy changes traits, mainly neuroticism, within weeks. Hudson & Fraley 2015: people who set weekly behavioural goals shifted traits over 16 weeks.
- narrative-identity | 叙事认同（narrative identity） | 人怎么讲自己的故事（比如把坏事讲成转折）和心理健康相关。 | B | McAdams 2001 redemption sequences; Adler 2016 review.
- self-concept-clarity | 自我概念清晰度 | 对「我是谁」的回答清楚而稳定的人，情绪更稳。 | B | Campbell et al. 1996.
- self-verification | 自我验证（self-verification） | 人想被别人按自己的自我看法对待，哪怕那个看法是负面的。 | B | Swann 1983; Swann, Stein-Seroussi, Giesler 1992.

# learn | 学习与成长 | 怎么学才留得住、用得上：从记忆规律到技能训练，再到把输入变成产出。
## learn.memory | 记得住 | 感觉学得顺不等于学会了；让提取变难一点的做法（自测、间隔、交错、自己产出）才留得久。
* testing-effect | 提取练习（testing effect） | 合上书回忆一遍，比再读一遍记得久得多，尽管当时感觉更差。 | A | Roediger & Karpicke 2006 Psychological Science: after one week, study-study-study-study group recalled 40%, study-test-test-test 61%; the rereaders had predicted the opposite. Karpicke & Blunt 2011 Science: retrieval practice beat concept mapping. Dunlosky et al. 2013: practice testing rated high utility.
* forgetting-curve | 遗忘曲线（forgetting curve） | 新学的东西头一天忘得最快，之后变慢；没有意义联系的材料忘得尤其快。 | A | Ebbinghaus 1885, nonsense syllables, savings: 58% after 20 minutes, 44% after 1 hour, 34% after a day, 21% after 31 days. Murre & Dros 2015 PLoS ONE replicated the curve. Meaningful, connected material is forgotten far more slowly.
* spacing | 间隔效应（spacing effect） | 同样的总时间，分开几次学比一次学完记得牢；间隔拉到快忘了再复习。 | A | Cepeda et al. 2006 meta (317 experiments). Cepeda et al. 2008: the best gap is roughly 10–20% of the time you need to remember it for. Bahrick et al. 1993: 13 sessions 56 days apart matched 26 sessions 14 days apart for foreign vocabulary after years. | req=learn.memory.forgetting-curve
* interleaving | 交错练习（interleaving） | 几类题混着练，比一类练完再练下一类考得好，因为你被迫先判断「这是哪一类」。 | A | Rohrer & Taylor 2007: geometry volumes, a week later 63% (interleaved) vs 20% (blocked), though blocked did better during practice. Kornell & Bjork 2008: painters' styles; 78% of learners still believed blocking worked better. Brunmair & Richter 2019 meta g=0.42.
* fluency-illusion | 流畅错觉与合意困难（illusion of fluency, desirable difficulties） | 重读、划线、看答案让材料变眼熟，眼熟被误当成会了。 | A | Bjork 1994 desirable difficulties. Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan, Willingham 2013: rereading, highlighting, summarising rated low utility. Koriat & Bjork 2005 foresight bias: with the answer in view people overestimate later recall. | req=learn.memory.testing-effect
* generation-effect | 生成效应（generation effect） | 自己想出来、写出来的内容，比读现成的记得牢。 | A | Slamecka & Graf 1978: generating a word from a cue (rapid–f___) beat reading the pair. Bertsch, Pesta, Wiscott, McDaniel 2007 meta (86 studies): d=0.40. Kornell, Hays, Bjork 2009: even a failed attempt before seeing the answer improves later memory.
* ai-offloading | 把思考外包给 AI（cognitive offloading） | 用 AI 直接要答案，当场做得更好，脱离 AI 后比没用过的人差；让它提示而不是代做，就没有这个代价。 | B | Bastani et al. 2025 PNAS 'Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning' (about 1,000 Turkish high-school maths students): plain GPT-4 raised practice scores 48% but exam scores fell 17% vs no AI; a tutor version with hints raised practice 127% with no exam penalty. Risko & Gilbert 2016 cognitive offloading. Sparrow, Liu, Wegner 2011 'Google effect' (part failed to replicate). MIT 'Your Brain on ChatGPT' 2025 is a small preprint (n=54): mention only as weak. Check everything here against the papers. | req=learn.memory.generation-effect
* self-explanation | 自我解释（self-explanation） | 学一步就问自己「这一步为什么成立」，比只看懂步骤学得深。 | A | Chi, Bassok, Lewis, Reimann, Glaser 1989: good students explained worked physics examples to themselves far more. Chi et al. 1994: prompted eighth-graders gained 32% vs 22%. Bisra et al. 2018 meta (64 studies): g=0.55.
* learning-by-teaching | 以教为学（learning by teaching） | 准备讲给别人听、真的不看稿讲一遍，学得更好，起作用的是提取和组织。 | B | Nestojko, Bui, Kornell, Bjork 2014: expecting to teach improved recall and organisation. Fiorella & Mayer 2013. Koh, Lee, Lim 2018: teaching without notes beat teaching from a script, as much as retrieval practice did.
* transfer-appropriate | 怎么考就怎么练（transfer-appropriate processing） | 记忆在和练习时相似的条件下最容易取出来；练的形式要像将来用的形式。 | A | Morris, Bransford, Franks 1977. Godden & Baddeley 1975: divers recalled about 40% more words when learning and recall were both on land or both under water. Implication: reading solutions prepares you to recognise solutions; a whiteboard interview needs spoken, timed production.
- chunking | 组块（chunking） | 工作记忆只装得下四个左右的块；专家的优势是每个块更大。 | A | Miller 1956; Cowan 2001 (about 4); Chase & Simon 1973.
- cognitive-load | 认知负荷（cognitive load theory） | 同时要处理的新东西太多就学不进；先减负，再加难度。 | A | Sweller 1988.
- sleep-memory | 睡眠巩固记忆 | 学完睡一觉，比同样时间醒着记得多。 | A | Jenkins & Dallenbach 1924; Stickgold 2005; Gais 2006.
- pretesting | 先考后学（pretesting effect） | 学之前先猜一遍答案，哪怕猜错，之后学得更牢。 | B | Richland, Kornell, Kao 2009.
- note-taking | 手写与键盘笔记 | 「手写笔记比打字学得好」重做后没有稳定优势。 | C | Mueller & Oppenheimer 2014; Urry et al. 2021 direct replication found no advantage.
## learn.skill | 变强 | 技能靠有反馈的、针对弱点的练习长出来；学到的东西不会自动搬到新情境。
* deliberate-practice | 刻意练习（deliberate practice） | 让人变强的不是做的时间，是盯着一个具体弱点、马上得到反馈、在够不着的边上重复。 | B | Ericsson, Krampe, Tesch-Römer 1993: the best violinists did more solitary practice, not more playing. Components: a specific goal, full attention, immediate feedback, the edge of ability, ideally a coach. Macnamara 2014: explains much in chess and music, little where the rules are unstable. Ericsson & Pool 2016.
* transfer | 迁移（transfer of learning） | 学会一件事，不会自动用到表面不一样的另一件事上；要自己把共同的结构说出来。 | A | Gick & Holyoak 1980: Duncker's radiation problem; about 10% solved it cold, about 30% after reading an analogous story, about 75–90% once told to use the story (check). Gick & Holyoak 1983: comparing two analogues builds the schema. Sala & Gobet 2017: chess, music and working-memory training show near-zero far transfer. Barnett & Ceci 2002.
* feedback | 反馈会帮倒忙（feedback interventions） | 反馈平均有用，但约三分之一的情况让表现变差：当它指向「你这个人」而不是任务时。 | A | Kluger & DeNisi 1996 Psychological Bulletin meta (607 effect sizes, 23,663 observations): average d=0.41, but over one third of effects were negative; feedback that draws attention to the self hurts. Hattie & Timperley 2007: task- and process-level feedback works, praise of the person does not.
* expert-intuition | 专家直觉什么时候可信（expert intuition） | 直觉是对见过的模式的识别；只有规律稳定、反馈又快又明确的领域，直觉才值得信。 | A | Chase & Simon 1973: chess masters recalled real positions far better than novices, random positions no better. Klein 1998 fireground commanders. Kahneman & Klein 2009 American Psychologist 'A Failure to Disagree': skilled intuition needs a regular environment and prolonged practice with feedback; stock pickers and long-range political forecasters lack both.
* worked-example | 样例效应与专长反转（worked example effect） | 新手先看带讲解的例题，比硬解学得快；有底子之后反过来，自己解才有长进。 | A | Sweller & Cooper 1985 algebra: studying worked examples beat solving the same problems, in less time. Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, Sweller 2003 expertise reversal effect. Renkl: fading, removing one solution step at a time. Kapur 2008 productive failure is the contrasting case.
* growth-mindset | 成长型思维：真的，但很小（growth mindset） | 相信能力可以练出来有一点帮助，主要对成绩差、处境不利的学生；它不是万能钥匙。 | B | Dweck. Mueller & Dweck 1998 praise for effort vs intelligence. Yeager et al. 2019 Nature (n=12,490 US ninth-graders): a one-hour online intervention raised GPA 0.10 points among lower-achieving students. Sisk et al. 2018 meta: interventions d=0.08. Macnamara & Burgoyne 2023 meta: effects near zero in the best-designed studies. | req=evidence.method.effect-size
* grit | 坚毅（grit） | 「坚毅」和成就的相关不高，而且和大五里的尽责性几乎是一回事；有用的是其中「坚持努力」那一半。 | B | Duckworth, Peterson, Matthews, Kelly 2007 (West Point, spelling bee). Credé, Tynan, Harms 2017 JPSP meta (88 samples, n=66,807): grit–performance ρ=0.18; grit–conscientiousness ρ=0.84; perseverance of effort predicts better than consistency of interest. | req=evidence.method.effect-size
- plateau | 平台期（OK plateau） | 技能够用之后就自动化，不再长进；要继续长就得回到费劲的练法。 | B | Fitts & Posner 1967 three stages; Ericsson 2006.
- sampling-period | 先多试再专精 | 世界级运动员少年时练的项目更多、定主项更晚；过早专精赢在青少年组。 | B | Güllich, Macnamara, Hambrick 2022 Perspectives on Psychological Science meta (51 reports, n=6,096).
- mental-models-analogy | 类比与比较学习 | 把两个例子放一起比，才看得出共同的结构。 | A | Gentner, Loewenstein, Thompson 2003: comparing two negotiation cases tripled transfer.
## learn.create | 从输入到产出 | 好作品出在多产里；影响力大的那一个出现在第几个，事先看不出来。
* equal-odds | 等概率法则（equal-odds rule） | 一个人最好的作品落在他哪一件作品上近乎随机，所以产出越多，出好东西的机会越多。 | B | Simonton 1997 Psychological Review. Sinatra, Wang, Deville, Song, Barabási 2016 Science 'random impact rule': a scientist's highest-impact paper is equally likely to be any paper in the sequence; what differs between people is a stable factor Q. Picasso produced an estimated 20,000+ works.
* hot-streak | 连胜期（hot streak） | 多数人的职业生涯里有一段成果扎堆的时期，它可以出现在任何年龄，往往跟在一段广泛尝试之后。 | B | Liu, Wang, Sinatra, Giles, Song, Wang 2018 Nature: artists, film directors, scientists; about 90% have at least one hot streak, lasting around 4–5 years, timing random within the career. Liu et al. 2021 Nature Communications: exploration followed by exploitation tends to precede it.
- brainwriting | 先各自想再讨论 | 一起头脑风暴产生的点子比同样人数各自想的少。 | A | Diehl & Stroebe 1987; Mullen, Johnson, Salas 1991 meta.
- incubation | 酝酿效应（incubation） | 卡住时放一放再回来，对发散类问题有小的帮助。 | B | Sio & Ormerod 2009 meta.
- constraints-creativity | 限制促进创造 | 适度的限制让人更有创造性。 | B | Acar, Tarakci, van Knippenberg 2019 review (145 studies).

# social | 人际 | 人怎么形成印象、怎么变亲近、怎么被说服，以及高估别人在注意自己这件事。
## social.perceive | 看人与被看 | 对别人下判断又快又偏：看行为就定性格，一个优点盖住全部；而别人其实没那么注意你。
* attribution-error | 基本归因错误（fundamental attribution error） | 解释别人的行为时，高估性格、低估处境。 | A | Jones & Harris 1967: essays for or against Castro; readers inferred the writer's real view even when told the side was assigned. Ross, Amabile, Steinmetz 1977 quiz game: questioners were rated more knowledgeable. Darley & Batson 1973 Good Samaritan: seminarians in a hurry helped 10%, unhurried 63%. Weaker in East Asian samples (Morris & Peng 1994).
* halo | 光环效应（halo effect） | 一个突出的优点（长相、名校、口才）会把对这个人其他方面的评价一起抬高。 | A | Thorndike 1920 officer ratings. Nisbett & Wilson 1977: the same lecturer, warm or cold; students rated his accent and looks differently and denied the influence. Landy & Sigall 1974 essay grading with author photos. Hamermesh & Biddle 1994: beauty premium in earnings of roughly 5–10%.
* first-impression | 第一印象与薄片判断（thin slices） | 人在零点几秒内形成印象，之后的信息多用来确认它。 | A | Willis & Todorov 2006: judgments after 100 ms correlate highly with unhurried ones. Ambady & Rosenthal 1993: ratings of 30-second silent clips of teachers correlated 0.76 with end-of-term evaluations. Todorov et al. 2005 Science: competence judged from faces predicted about 70% of US Senate races. Fast is not the same as accurate.
* warmth-competence | 温暖与能力（warmth–competence） | 人评价他人主要看两条：对我有没有善意，有没有本事；善意先被判断。 | A | Fiske, Cuddy, Glick, Xu 2002 stereotype content model; Fiske, Cuddy, Glick 2007 Trends in Cognitive Sciences: the two dimensions account for most of the variance in impressions; warmth is judged first and weighs more.
* spotlight | 聚光灯效应（spotlight effect） | 人高估别人注意到自己的程度，大约高估一倍。 | A | Gilovich, Medvec, Savitsky 2000 JPSP: students wearing a Barry Manilow T-shirt guessed about half the room would notice; about a quarter did (check: 46% vs 23%).
* transparency-illusion | 透明度错觉（illusion of transparency） | 人以为自己的紧张、说谎、不高兴别人都看得出来，其实漏出去的很少。 | A | Gilovich, Savitsky, Medvec 1998. Savitsky & Gilovich 2003: speakers told about the illusion gave speeches rated better by themselves and by observers.
* liking-gap | 喜欢差距（liking gap） | 聊完天，人普遍低估对方喜欢自己的程度。 | B | Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom, Clark 2018 Psychological Science: after conversations of any length strangers underestimated how much the other liked them; among dorm suitemates the gap lasted most of the year. Wolf et al. 2021 found it in work teams.
* pygmalion | 期望效应（Pygmalion effect） | 别人对你的期望会通过对待你的方式变成一部分现实，但效果比传说的小得多。 | B | Rosenthal & Jacobson 1968 Oak School: 'bloomers' picked at random; first-graders gained 27 IQ points vs 12. Raudenbush 1984 meta: effect near zero once teachers had known pupils for more than two weeks. Jussim & Harber 2005 review: real, small (r about 0.1–0.2), larger for stigmatised groups. Eden's military studies.
- implicit-bias | 内隐联想测验（IAT）测到了什么 | IAT 分数和实际歧视行为的相关很弱，改变分数也不改变行为。 | C | Oswald et al. 2013 meta r≈0.14; Forscher et al. 2019 meta (492 studies).
- naive-realism | 朴素实在论（naive realism） | 人觉得自己看到的就是事实，不同意的人不是没信息就是有偏见。 | A | Ross & Ward 1996; Hastorf & Cantril 1954 'They Saw a Game'.
- physiognomy-limits | 以貌取人的准确率 | 从脸判断性格、可信度的一致性很高，准确性接近没有。 | A | Todorov 2017; Olivola & Todorov 2010.
## social.connect | 亲近与关系 | 关系是见得多、离得近、互相多说一点长出来的；人系统性地低估别人愿意靠近的程度。
* mere-exposure | 曝光效应（mere exposure effect） | 见得越多越有好感，哪怕没有任何交流。 | A | Zajonc 1968. Moreland & Beach 1992: four women attended a lecture course 0, 5, 10 or 15 times without speaking; students liked the most-seen one best. Bornstein 1989 meta: r=0.26. Reverses if the first reaction is dislike; saturates.
* propinquity | 邻近效应（propinquity effect） | 物理距离是友谊最强的预测因素之一：隔几个门，交朋友的概率就掉一大截。 | A | Festinger, Schachter, Back 1950, Westgate housing at MIT: 41% of next-door neighbours were named as close friends, 22% two doors away, 10% at the end of the hall. Segal 1974: police trainees befriended those next in alphabetical order.
* self-disclosure | 自我暴露（self-disclosure） | 亲近感来自轮流、逐步加深地说自己的事，不是来自共处的时间长短。 | B | Aron, Melinat, Aron, Vallone, Bator 1997: 36 escalating questions in 45 minutes produced more closeness than small talk. Collins & Miller 1994 meta: people like those who disclose to them and like those they disclose to.
* undersociality | 人低估和陌生人说话的好处（undersociality） | 人预计和陌生人聊天会尴尬，实际更愉快；深一点的话题也比预想的顺。 | B | Epley & Schroeder 2014 JEP: General: Chicago commuters told to talk to a stranger had the most pleasant commute; they had predicted the opposite. Sandstrom & Dunn 2014: more weak-tie chats, more belonging that day. Kardas, Kumar, Epley 2022: deep conversations felt less awkward than expected. Sandstrom, Boothby, Cooney 2022: a week of daily conversations with strangers cut fear of rejection.
* asking-help | 人低估别人答应的概率 | 开口求助、要内推、请人帮忙，被答应的概率大约是自己估计的两倍。 | A | Flynn & Lake 2008 JPSP: people estimated they would need to ask about 20 strangers to get 5 to fill in a questionnaire; they needed about 10. Bohns 2016 review across 14,000 requests. Helpers also see the asker as more competent (Brooks, Gino, Schweitzer 2015 on advice seeking).
* loneliness-loop | 孤独的自我强化循环（loneliness loop） | 孤独让人对社交威胁更敏感，更容易把中性信号读成拒绝，于是更退缩。 | A | Cacioppo & Hawkley 2009 Trends in Cognitive Sciences. Holt-Lunstad, Smith, Layton 2010 PLoS Medicine meta (148 studies, n=308,849): stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival, comparable to quitting smoking. Masi, Chen, Hawkley, Cacioppo 2011 meta: interventions that correct maladaptive social thinking worked best, better than social-skills training or simply more contact.
* friendship-hours | 交朋友需要的小时数 | 从认识到普通朋友约 50 小时，到朋友约 90 小时，到好朋友要 200 小时以上，而且得是一起闲着的时间。 | B | Hall 2019 Journal of Social and Personal Relationships (two studies: adults who had moved; first-year students). Time at work or in class counted for little; leisure time together counted.
* capitalization | 怎么回应别人的好消息（capitalization） | 对方分享好事时，热情地追问细节，比对方倒霉时安慰他更能预测关系好坏。 | B | Gable, Reis, Impett, Asher 2004 JPSP. Gable, Gonzaga, Strachman 2006: only active-constructive responses were linked to satisfaction; passive or deflating ones predicted breakup two months later.
* attachment | 成人依恋（adult attachment） | 人在亲密关系里有稳定的倾向：安全、焦虑（怕被丢下）或回避（怕靠太近）；它会变，不是判决。 | B | Bowlby; Ainsworth 1978 Strange Situation. Hazan & Shaver 1987: 56% secure, 25% avoidant, 19% anxious. Fraley 2002 meta: moderate stability from infancy to adulthood (r about 0.39). Fraley & Roisman 2019: early experience predicts weakly. Styles are dimensions, not boxes.
* gottman | 关系里的四种危险信号（Gottman's four horsemen） | 批评人格、蔑视、辩解、冷战这四种互动方式预示关系变坏，蔑视最毒；「预测准确率九成」则是高估。 | B | Gottman & Levenson 1992; Gottman 1994. The 90%+ divorce prediction came from fitting a model to couples whose outcomes were known, without validation on new couples (Heyman & Slep 2001). Stable couples show about 5 positive interactions per negative during conflict.
* stated-preferences | 嘴上的择偶标准不预测心动 | 人说自己看重什么条件，和面对面时被谁吸引，几乎对不上。 | A | Eastwick & Finkel 2008 JPSP speed dating: stated preferences for looks or earning power did not predict whom people actually liked. Joel, Eastwick, Finkel 2017 Psychological Science: machine learning on 100+ self-report measures could not predict which specific pair would click. Eastwick et al. 2014 meta.
- similarity | 相似相吸 | 人喜欢和自己态度相近的人；「互补相吸」没有证据。 | A | Byrne 1961; Montoya, Horton, Kirchner 2008 meta.
- ben-franklin | 富兰克林效应（Ben Franklin effect） | 请别人帮一个小忙，对方之后可能更喜欢你，证据不多。 | B | Jecker & Landy 1969 (small); Niiya 2016.
- pratfall | 出丑效应（pratfall effect） | 能力强的人出个小丑更讨喜，能力一般的人出丑更减分。 | B | Aronson, Willerman, Floyd 1966.
- dunbar | 邓巴数与关系圈层（Dunbar's number） | 一个人能维持的稳定关系有上限，按亲近程度分层：约 5、15、50、150；150 这个数字本身误差很大。 | B | Dunbar 1992; Lindenfors, Wartel, Lind 2021: confidence interval from the same method spans 2 to 520.
- investment-model | 投入模型（investment model） | 留在一段关系里取决于满意度、已投入多少、有没有更好的选择。 | A | Rusbult 1980; Le & Agnew 2003 meta.
- assortative-mating | 同类婚配（assortative mating） | 人多半和教育、收入、年龄相近的人结婚。 | A | Schwartz & Mare 2005; Greenwood et al. 2014; Luo 2017 review.
## social.influence | 说服与影响 | 让人说「好」的几种固定办法：先给、先要个小的、告诉他别人都这样、说快没了、重复。
* reciprocity | 互惠（reciprocity） | 先收到好处的人会觉得欠着，回报常常比收到的大。 | A | Regan 1971: a confederate who bought the subject a Coke sold twice as many raffle tickets. Strohmetz et al. 2002: mints with the bill raised tips, most when given personally. Falk 2007 Econometrica: 10,000 charity letters; a small gift raised donations 17%, a large one 75%.
* sequential-requests | 登门槛与留面子（foot-in-the-door, door-in-the-face） | 先让人答应一个小请求，或先提一个会被拒绝的大请求，后面的真请求更容易成。 | B | Freedman & Fraser 1966: 17% agreed to a large 'Drive Carefully' sign on their lawn; 76% of those who had earlier accepted a small sticker. Cialdini et al. 1975: 17% agreed to chaperone juvenile offenders at the zoo; 50% after first refusing a two-year commitment. Meta-analyses: both real and modest (r about 0.15).
* social-proof | 社会认同（social proof） | 不确定该怎么做时，人看别人怎么做，尤其是和自己处境相似的人。 | A | Goldstein, Cialdini, Griskevicius 2008: towel-reuse signs: environmental appeal 35%, 'most guests reuse' 44%, 'most guests in this room' 49%. Allcott 2011: Opower home energy reports comparing households to neighbours cut use about 2%. Milgram, Bickman, Berkowitz 1969: a crowd of 5 looking up made 80% of passers-by look up.
* scarcity | 稀缺（scarcity） | 「快没了」「只剩今天」让东西显得更值钱，也让人来不及想。 | B | Worchel, Lee, Adewole 1975: identical cookies rated more desirable from a jar of 2 than a jar of 10, most when the jar had just been depleted. Cialdini 1984. Booking sites' 'only 1 room left'. Barton, Zlatevska, Oppewal 2022 meta.
* illusory-truth | 真相错觉（illusory truth effect） | 一句话听得多了就更像真的，即使你本来知道它是错的。 | A | Hasher, Goldstein, Toppino 1977. Fazio, Brashier, Payne, Marsh 2015: repetition raised belief even for statements people knew to be false. Pennycook, Cannon, Rand 2018: one prior exposure made fake-news headlines seem more accurate.
- decoy | 诱饵效应（decoy effect） | 加一个明显更差的第三选项，会把人推向它旁边那个。 | B | Huber, Payne, Puto 1982; Frederick, Lee, Baskin 2014 on its limits with real products.
- inoculation | 预防接种（inoculation theory） | 先让人见一个弱化版的误导手法，之后更不容易上当。 | B | McGuire 1961; Roozenbeek et al. 2022 Science Advances prebunking videos on YouTube.
- identifiable-victim | 可识别受害者效应 | 一个有名字的人比一组统计数字更能让人掏钱，但效果很小。 | B | Small, Loewenstein, Slovic 2007; Lee & Feeley 2016 meta r=0.05.
- authority-cue | 权威线索 | 头衔、制服、专业术语本身就让人更服从。 | A | Hofling 1966: 21 of 22 nurses prepared to give a double dose on a phone order from an unknown 'doctor'; Bickman 1974 uniforms.
## social.talk | 沟通与冲突 | 你知道的对方不知道，你想表达的对方没收到；越想逼人同意，对方越往后退。
* curse-of-knowledge | 知识的诅咒（curse of knowledge） | 一旦懂了某件事，就很难想象不懂的人听到的是什么。 | A | Newton 1990 Stanford dissertation: tappers predicted listeners would name 50% of songs; they got 3 of 120 (2.5%). Camerer, Loewenstein, Weber 1989 named it. Kruger, Epley, Parker, Ng 2005: email writers expected sarcasm to be read correctly 78% of the time; it was 56%.
* reactance | 心理抗拒（reactance） | 感到选择被人拿走时，人会偏要选被禁止的那个。 | A | Brehm 1966. Pennebaker & Sanders 1976: restroom signs 'Do NOT write on these walls under any circumstances' drew more graffiti than 'Please don't write on these walls'. Rains 2013 meta. Motivational interviewing avoids it by asking for the person's own reasons (Miller & Rollnick).
* perspective-getting | 换位思考不如直接问（perspective getting） | 努力想象对方的想法并不让你猜得更准，只让你更自信；问出来才准。 | B | Eyal, Steffel, Epley 2018 JPSP, 25 experiments: perspective taking did not improve accuracy at predicting others' thoughts, feelings or preferences; asking them did. Married couples overestimated how well they knew their partner.
- active-listening | 被听见的感觉 | 追问型的问题让人更喜欢你。 | B | Huang, Yeomans, Brooks, Minson, Gino 2017: follow-up questions raised liking; speed daters who asked more got more second dates.
- conversational-receptiveness | 接纳式表达 | 用「我理解你的意思是」「有时候」这类措辞表达分歧，对方更愿意继续谈。 | B | Yeomans, Minson, Collins, Chen, Gino 2020.
- apology | 有效的道歉 | 承认责任和提出补救是道歉里最起作用的两部分。 | B | Lewicki, Polin, Lount 2016.

# group | 群体与社会 | 人在群体里会跟随、服从、偷懒；机会沿着关系网流动；起点的小差异被放大成大差距。
## group.conform | 从众与服从 | 多数人的做法和权威的要求对个人判断的压力，以及什么条件下压力会破掉。
* asch | 从众实验（Asch conformity） | 答案明摆着，约三分之一的回答还是跟着错的多数走；只要有一个人不跟，压力就垮了。 | A | Asch 1951, 1956: on critical trials 37% of answers conformed; 75% of people conformed at least once, 25% never. One dissenting ally cut conformity to about a quarter of its level. Bond & Smith 1996 meta (133 studies, 17 countries): higher in collectivist cultures, declining in the US since the 1950s.
* milgram | 服从权威（Milgram obedience） | 在权威一步步的要求下，多数普通人会做出自己认为不该做的事。 | A | Milgram 1963: 26 of 40 (65%) went to 450 volts. Variations: authority absent by phone 21%; two peers refuse 10%. Burger 2009: 70% continued past 150 V. Doliński et al. 2017 Poland: 90%. Perry 2013 on departures from the script and participants who doubted it.
* bystander | 旁观者效应（bystander effect） | 在场的人越多，每个人越觉得轮不到自己；但真实的公共冲突里，多数时候有人出手。 | B | Darley & Latané 1968 seizure study: alone 85% helped, with four others 31%. The Kitty Genovese '38 witnesses' story was a newspaper exaggeration (Manning, Levine, Collins 2007). Fischer et al. 2011 meta: weaker in dangerous emergencies. Philpot et al. 2020 American Psychologist: CCTV of 219 street conflicts in three countries, someone intervened in 91%.
* pluralistic-ignorance | 多数无知（pluralistic ignorance） | 每个人私下都不认同，却以为别人都认同，于是都不说。 | A | Prentice & Miller 1993: Princeton students thought the average student was more comfortable with campus drinking than they were. Bursztyn, González, Yanagizawa-Drott 2020 AER: 87% of young Saudi husbands supported women working outside the home and underestimated others' support; told the truth, more wives applied for jobs.
* herd | 羊群效应与信息瀑布（herd behavior, information cascade） | 后来的人看前面人的选择来做判断，早期一点随机的领先会滚成压倒性的流行。 | A | Banerjee 1992; Bikhchandani, Hirshleifer, Welch 1992. Salganik, Dodds, Watts 2006 Science MusicLab (n=14,341): showing download counts made hits bigger and less predictable; the same song could be top or middling in different worlds. Muchnik, Aral, Taylor 2013 Science: one random initial upvote raised final ratings by 25%. | tag=十日终焉
- deindividuation | 去个体化 | 匿名和人群让人更按当下群体的规范行事，不一定更坏。 | B | Postmes & Spears 1998 meta.
- minority-influence | 少数派影响 | 少数人只要一直坚持同一个立场，就能改变多数人私下的看法。 | B | Moscovici, Lage, Naffrechoux 1969 blue-green slides; Wood et al. 1994 meta.
- spiral-of-silence | 沉默的螺旋 | 觉得自己是少数的人更不愿意开口，于是显得更少。 | B | Noelle-Neumann 1974; Matthes, Knoll, von Sikorski 2018 meta r≈0.10.
## group.team | 团队里的人 | 人一多，个人出力变少、意见变极端；团队判断好不好，取决于成员是否各自独立地想、敢不敢说。
* social-loafing | 社会懈怠（social loafing） | 一起干活时每个人出的力比单干时少，人越多越少；个人贡献看得见就好很多。 | A | Ringelmann 1913 rope pulling. Latané, Williams, Harkins 1979: clapping and shouting; in groups of six, each person made about 36–40% of the noise they made alone (check). Karau & Williams 1993 meta (78 studies): reduced when individual output is identifiable and the task matters.
* group-polarization | 群体极化（group polarization） | 观点相近的人讨论之后，整体立场比讨论前更极端。 | A | Stoner 1961 risky shift. Moscovici & Zavalloni 1969. Myers & Bishop 1970. Schkade, Sunstein, Hastie 2007 Colorado experiment: liberals from Boulder and conservatives from Colorado Springs each became more extreme and more uniform after 15 minutes of discussion. Janis's 'groupthink' (1972) rests on case studies with weak experimental support.
* wisdom-of-crowds | 群体智慧及其条件（wisdom of crowds） | 很多人各自独立的估计取平均，常比其中的专家准；一旦互相看到答案，就不准了。 | A | Galton 1907 Nature: 787 fairgoers guessed an ox's dressed weight; median 1,207 lb, actual 1,198 lb. Lorenz, Rauhut, Schweitzer, Helbing 2011 PNAS: seeing others' estimates narrowed the spread without improving accuracy. Surowiecki 2004 conditions: diversity, independence, aggregation.
* brainstorming | 头脑风暴不如各自先想（brainstorming） | 同样几个人，各自写完再汇总，点子比坐在一起说更多、更好。 | A | Osborn 1953 claimed groups double output. Taylor, Berry, Block 1958 found the opposite. Diehl & Stroebe 1987: production blocking is the main cause. Mullen, Johnson, Salas 1991 meta.
* psychological-safety | 心理安全感（psychological safety） | 成员相信提问、认错、唱反调不会被惩罚的团队，学得快，表现也好。 | B | Edmondson 1999 Administrative Science Quarterly (51 teams); her earlier hospital study found better teams reported more errors, because they talked about them. Google Project Aristotle 2015 (180 teams): psychological safety was the top factor. Frazier et al. 2017 meta (136 samples).
* minimal-group | 最小群体范式（minimal group paradigm） | 哪怕按抛硬币分组，人也会偏向自己这一组。 | A | Tajfel, Billig, Bundy, Flament 1971: boys grouped by supposed preference for Klee or Kandinsky gave more to their own group and preferred a bigger gap over a bigger total. Sherif 1954 Robbers Cave: conflict from competition, reduced by shared goals; Perry 2018 documents an earlier failed attempt and experimenter steering.
- contact-hypothesis | 接触假说 | 平等地位下的合作接触能减少群体间的偏见。 | A | Allport 1954; Pettigrew & Tropp 2006 meta (515 studies) r=-0.21; Mousa 2020 Science Iraq football leagues.
- shared-information | 共享信息偏差 | 团队讨论的大多是大家都已经知道的信息，只有一个人知道的关键信息很少被提出来。 | A | Stasser & Titus 1985 hidden profiles; Lu, Yuan, McLeod 2012 meta.
- ringelmann-meeting | 会议人数与决策质量 | 团队越大，协调成本涨得比人手快。 | B | Hackman & Vidmar 1970 optimum about 4–5; Steiner 1972 process loss.
## group.network | 网络与机会 | 新信息和机会多半从不太熟的人、从两个圈子之间的位置过来；领先一点会越滚越大。
* weak-ties | 弱关系的力量（strength of weak ties） | 新工作的消息更多来自不太熟的人，因为熟人知道的和你知道的差不多。 | A | Granovetter 1973, 1974 'Getting a Job' (282 Boston-area professionals): of those who found the job through a contact, 16.7% saw that contact often, 55.6% occasionally, 27.8% rarely. Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov, Brynjolfsson, Aral 2022 Science: randomised 'People You May Know' experiments on 20 million LinkedIn users; moderately weak ties produced the most job moves, an inverted U.
* structural-holes | 结构洞（structural holes） | 站在两个互不来往的圈子之间的人，更早看到两边的信息，好点子和晋升都更多。 | A | Burt 1992. Burt 2004 American Journal of Sociology 'Structural Holes and Good Ideas': 673 supply-chain managers at a large US electronics company; those bridging groups had ideas rated more valuable, higher pay and faster promotion. | req=group.network.weak-ties
* matthew | 马太效应（Matthew effect, cumulative advantage） | 早期的一点领先带来更多资源和关注，于是差距越拉越大，哪怕起点的差别几乎是运气。 | A | Merton 1968 Science, named after Matthew 25:29. Bol, de Vaan, van de Rijt 2018 PNAS: Dutch early-career grant applicants just above the cutoff won more than twice as much funding over the next eight years as those just below, with near-identical scores. Salganik 2006 MusicLab. Preferential attachment (Barabási & Albert 1999). | tag=十日终焉
* luck | 成功里的运气（luck in success） | 竞争越激烈、参与的人越多，最顶上那个越可能是又强又走运的人；极端的成绩里运气的成分最大。 | B | Denrell & Liu 2012 PNAS: the most extreme performers are expected to be less skilled than those just below them. Frank 2016 'Success and Luck': simulations where luck counts 2% still mostly crown someone who was not the most skilled. Pluchino, Biondo, Rapisarda 2018 talent-vs-luck model. Relative age effect: Barnsley 1985, about 40% of elite Canadian youth hockey players born January–March. | req=group.network.matthew
- small-world | 六度分隔（small world） | 任意两个人之间隔着的中间人平均只有五六个，但多数传递链会断掉。 | A | Milgram 1967; Travers & Milgram 1969: 64 of 296 chains completed; Dodds, Muhamad, Watts 2003: 384 of 24,163; Facebook 2016: 3.57.
- homophily | 同质性（homophily） | 人和与自己相似的人交往，所以圈子里听到的多是自己已经同意的。 | A | McPherson, Smith-Lovin, Cook 2001 Annual Review of Sociology.
- social-contagion | 肥胖、快乐会沿着网络传染？ | 「朋友的朋友胖了你也更容易胖」的结论，方法上分不清传染和物以类聚。 | C | Christakis & Fowler 2007 NEJM; Cohen-Cole & Fletcher 2008 found 'contagion' of height and acne with the same method; Lyons 2011; Shalizi & Thomas 2011.
- relative-age | 相对年龄效应（relative age effect） | 同一年级里年龄大几个月的孩子，早期被选拔的机会明显更多。 | A | Barnsley, Thompson, Barnsley 1985; Bedard & Dhuey 2006 QJE.
## group.norms | 地位、规范与比较 | 人满不满意取决于和谁比；规范靠「大家都这样」维持，也会被一句话无意中带坏。
* relative-deprivation | 相对剥夺与参照群体（relative deprivation, reference group） | 满意不满意，取决于和身边可比的人比，不取决于绝对水平。 | A | Stouffer et al. 1949 'The American Soldier': Air Corps men, with fast promotion, were less satisfied with promotion than Military Police with slow promotion. Luttmer 2005 QJE: higher neighbours' earnings, lower own happiness. Card, Mas, Moretti, Saez 2012 AER: University of California staff shown a salary website; those below the median for their unit became less satisfied and more likely to look for a job; those above did not change.
* descriptive-norms | 描述性规范的反作用（descriptive norms） | 「很多人都在这么干坏事」这句劝告，听到的是「大家都这么干」。 | A | Cialdini, Reno, Kallgren 1990 littering. Cialdini 2003 / Cialdini et al. 2006 Petrified Forest: the sign 'many past visitors have removed wood' raised theft of marked wood from 2.92% to 7.92% (check); a sign showing one thief with a prohibition lowered it to 1.67%. Schultz et al. 2007: households below average energy use raised it after learning the average, unless a smiley was added.
* broken-windows | 破窗效应（broken windows） | 环境里有人违规的痕迹，会让后来的人更容易跟着违规；用它来论证「零容忍执法」则证据不足。 | B | Zimbardo 1969 abandoned cars. Wilson & Kelling 1982 Atlantic essay. Keizer, Lindenberg, Steg 2008 Science: 33% littered a flyer in a clean alley, 69% when the wall had graffiti; theft of an envelope with a visible 5-euro note rose from 13% to 27%. Policing version disputed (Harcourt & Ludwig 2006; Braga 2015 meta: modest, from problem-solving not arrests).
* control-status | 地位、控制感与健康（Whitehall studies） | 职级越低死亡率越高，差距不是穷造成的，很大一部分来自对自己工作的控制感低。 | A | Marmot et al. 1978 Whitehall I (17,530 male British civil servants): the lowest grade had about three times the 10-year mortality of the top grade. Whitehall II (Marmot et al. 1991, 1997 Lancet): low job control explained a large share of the gradient in heart disease. Karasek 1979 demand–control model.
- cultural-tightness | 文化松紧与个人主义—集体主义 | 不同社会对违反规范的容忍度差别很大，和历史上面对的威胁有关。 | B | Gelfand et al. 2011 Science (33 nations); Hofstede 1980; Talhelm et al. 2014 Science rice theory.
- status-signals | 地位信号与炫耀性消费 | 人花钱让别人看见自己的位置。 | B | Veblen 1899; Bursztyn et al. 2018 QJE platinum cards.
- zero-sum-society | 社会流动与起点 | 出生的邮编在很大程度上预测成年后的收入。 | A | Chetty, Hendren, Kline, Saez 2014 QJE; Chetty et al. 2022 Nature economic connectedness.
- self-fulfilling-prophecy | 自证预言（self-fulfilling prophecy） | 一个本来错误的判断，因为大家照着它行动而变成真的。 | A | Merton 1948 (the run on a solvent bank); Thomas theorem 1928.
- big-fish-little-pond | 大鱼小池效应（big-fish–little-pond effect） | 同样的能力，放在更强的同伴里，对自己的评价更低。 | A | Marsh 1987; Marsh & Hau 2003 (26 countries, n=103,558); Davis 1966 'frog pond'.
- cultural-capital | 文化资本（cultural capital） | 说话方式、品味、见过的世面这些看不见的资本，影响学校和职场里的待遇。 | B | Bourdieu 1986; Lareau 2003 'Unequal Childhoods'; Rivera 2012 hiring as cultural matching at elite firms.

# game | 博弈、激励与识骗 | 结果取决于别人也在怎么想的时候：合作为什么难、指标为什么被玩坏、骗局为什么得手、谈判怎么不吃亏。
## game.theory | 博弈的基本型 | 几个反复出现的局：各自理性却两败俱伤，重复打交道才有合作，出价最高的人往往买贵了。
* prisoners-dilemma | 囚徒困境（prisoner's dilemma） | 两个人各自选对自己最有利的做法，结果比都让一步更差。 | A | Flood & Dresher 1950 at RAND; Albert Tucker gave it the prison story. One-shot lab games: cooperation is far above the zero that pure self-interest predicts; Sally 1995 meta (130 experiments 1958–1992): mean cooperation 47%. Communication before play raises it sharply. Arms races, price wars, doping. | tag=十日终焉
* tit-for-tat | 重复博弈与以牙还牙（tit for tat） | 同一批人反复打交道时，先合作、被坑就回敬、对方改了就原谅，是最稳的策略。 | A | Axelrod 1980 tournaments (14 entries, then 62): Anatol Rapoport's four-line tit for tat won both. Axelrod 1984: be nice, retaliate, forgive, be clear. With noise, generous tit for tat and win-stay lose-shift do better (Nowak & Sigmund 1992, 1993). Dal Bó & Fréchette 2011: cooperation rises with the chance of meeting again. | req=game.theory.prisoners-dilemma
* zero-sum-bias | 零和偏差与固定馅饼（zero-sum bias, fixed-pie bias） | 人默认别人多得一分自己就少一分，于是看不见双方都能更好的方案。 | B | Thompson & Hastie 1990: 68% of negotiators assumed the other side's interests were directly opposed when they were not; many left with a worse deal than available. Bazerman's 'mythical fixed pie'. Meegan 2010. Johnson, Zhang, Keil 2022. Davidai & Ongis 2019.
* commons | 公地悲剧与奥斯特罗姆（tragedy of the commons） | 人人可用的资源会被用垮，但不是必然：能互相监督、自己定规矩的小群体能管好几百年。 | A | Hardin 1968 Science. Ostrom 1990 'Governing the Commons': Swiss alpine pastures (Törbel, rules since 1483), Spanish huertas, Maine lobster fisheries; eight design principles; Nobel Prize in economics 2009. Cox, Arnold, Villamayor Tomás 2010 reviewed 91 studies supporting the principles. | req=game.theory.prisoners-dilemma
* public-goods | 公共品博弈与惩罚（public goods game） | 没有惩罚时，合作会一轮轮滑向搭便车；允许自己掏钱惩罚搭便车的人，合作就回来。 | A | Fehr & Gächter 2000 AER, 2002 Nature 'Altruistic punishment in humans': contributions decayed without punishment and rose to near full with it; people paid to punish even strangers they would never meet again. Herrmann, Thöni, Gächter 2008 Science (16 societies): in some, cooperators got punished too. | req=game.theory.prisoners-dilemma
* ultimatum | 最后通牒博弈（ultimatum game） | 人宁可什么都不要，也要拒绝太不公平的分法。 | A | Güth, Schmittberger, Schwarze 1982. Typical offers 40–50%; offers below about 20% are rejected about half the time (Camerer 2003). Henrich et al. 2001: 15 small-scale societies, mean offers from 26% (Machiguenga) to 58% (Lamalera). Sanfey et al. 2003 Science: unfair offers activate the anterior insula.
* winners-curse | 赢家的诅咒（winner's curse） | 很多人竞价同一个价值不确定的东西时，赢的往往是估得最高的那个，也就是估过头的那个。 | A | Capen, Clapp, Campbell 1971: oil companies bidding for offshore leases in the Gulf of Mexico. Bazerman & Samuelson 1983: jars of coins worth $8 auctioned in MBA classes; mean estimate $5.13, mean winning bid $10.01. Thaler 1988. Applies to acquisitions, free agents, bidding wars for houses.
* beauty-contest | 猜三分之二平均数（k-level thinking） | 赢不靠推到最深，靠猜对别人推了几步；多数人只推一两步。 | A | Keynes 1936 beauty contest. Nagel 1995 AER: guess 2/3 of the average of 0–100; mean about 33–37, most players at one or two steps of reasoning; equilibrium is 0. Financial Times contest 1997 run by Thaler: winning number 13. Politiken 2005 (n=19,196): average 21.6.
- schelling-point | 聚焦点（Schelling point） | 没法商量的时候，人会选那个「大家都会想到」的显眼答案来碰头。 | A | Schelling 1960 'The Strategy of Conflict': asked to meet a stranger in New York City with no communication, most students chose Grand Central Terminal at noon. Mehta, Starmer, Sugden 1994 experiments. Round numbers, existing borders and precedent work this way in negotiation.
- credible-commitment | 可信承诺（credible commitment） | 主动砍掉自己的退路，对方才信你不会退。 | A | Schelling 1960. Cortés 1519 scuttled (not burned) his ships.
- chicken | 胆小鬼博弈（game of chicken） | 双方都不让就一起完蛋，先让的人吃亏；谁先让对方相信自己不会让，谁赢。 | A | Rapoport & Chammah 1966; Cuban missile crisis as the standard case.
- catch-22 | 第二十二条军规与双重束缚（catch-22, double bind） | 规则互相咬死，怎么选都不满足条件。 | B | Heller 1961; Bateson 1956 double bind (the schizophrenia theory built on it is discredited). | tag=十日终焉
- stag-hunt | 猎鹿博弈（stag hunt） | 合作收益更大，但只要怀疑别人不来，自己单干更安全；难的是信任，不是利益冲突。 | A | Rousseau; Skyrms 2004.
## game.incentive | 激励与信息 | 人对激励有反应，但反应的常常是指标本身；一方知道得多时，市场和合作都会变形。
* goodhart | 古德哈特定律（Goodhart's law） | 一个数字一旦被拿来考核，人就会去优化这个数字本身，它也就不再反映原来想衡量的东西。 | A | Goodhart 1975; Campbell 1979. Hanoi 1902: French bounty per rat tail led to tailless rats and rat farming (Vann 2003). Wells Fargo 2016: sales quotas, about 3.5 million accounts opened without consent. Atlanta 2009 test-score cheating: 178 educators implicated. Lines of code, tickets closed, test coverage.
* crowding-out | 罚款成了价格（motivation crowding out） | 给本来靠自觉和情面的事标上价，会把自觉挤走，钱一撤也回不来。 | B | Gneezy & Rustichini 2000 Journal of Legal Studies 'A Fine Is a Price': ten Haifa day-care centres; a small fine for late pickup about doubled lateness, and it stayed high after the fine was removed. Gneezy & Rustichini 2000 QJE 'Pay Enough or Don't Pay at All'. Titmuss 1970 on paid blood donation; Mellström & Johannesson 2008.
* principal-agent | 委托代理问题（principal–agent problem） | 替你办事的人有自己的利益和你不知道的信息，他的最优做法未必是你的。 | A | Jensen & Meckling 1976. Levitt & Syverson 2008: real-estate agents selling their own homes kept them on the market about 10 days longer and sold for about 3.7% more than comparable client homes. Financial advisers, mechanics, recruiters paid on placement.
* lemons | 柠檬市场（market for lemons） | 卖方比买方更清楚质量时，买方只肯出平均价，好货退出，剩下的越来越差。 | A | Akerlof 1970 QJE (rejected by three journals), Nobel 2001. Fixes: warranties, certification, reputation, third-party inspection. Used cars, insurance, the job market for people changing jobs (Gibbons & Katz 1991: laid-off workers' wage loss is larger than that of workers from plant closings).
* signaling | 信号理论（signaling） | 说自己好没有成本所以没人信；只有差的人学不起、装不像的代价，才是可信的信号。 | A | Spence 1973 QJE job-market signalling, Nobel 2001. Sheepskin effect: Hungerford & Solon 1987, the diploma year pays more than other years of schooling. Zahavi 1975 handicap principle. Bertrand & Mullainathan 2004 AER: résumés with white-sounding names got 50% more callbacks. | req=game.incentive.lemons
- moral-hazard | 道德风险（moral hazard） | 后果有人兜底时，人会多冒险。 | A | Arrow 1963; RAND Health Insurance Experiment (Manning et al. 1987).
- skin-in-game | 利益攸关 | 让做决定的人自己承担后果，决定会更谨慎。 | B | Taleb 2018; an argument more than a study.
- tournament-pay | 锦标赛与赢家通吃 | 报酬按名次而不是按绝对产出发的时候，第一和第二差一点，回报差很多。 | A | Lazear & Rosen 1981; Frank & Cook 1995.
- cobra-perverse | 有悖初衷的激励 | 激励设计错了会让问题更严重。 | B | Delhi cobra bounty is an undocumented anecdote; Hanoi rats and Fort Benning pig bounty are documented.
## game.deceive | 欺骗与识骗 | 人默认相信别人，看脸识谎的准确率和猜差不多；骗局靠的是一步步让你自己走进去。
* lie-detection | 人识别谎言的准确率 | 靠观察神态判断对方是否说谎，准确率平均 54%，警察、法官也一样。 | A | Bond & DePaulo 2006 Personality and Social Psychology Review meta (206 documents, 24,483 judges): 54% correct; 47% of lies and 61% of truths identified. DePaulo et al. 2003 Psychological Bulletin (158 cues): gaze aversion and fidgeting are not reliable. US GAO 2013 on the TSA's behaviour-detection programme (about $900 million): no evidence it works.
* truth-default | 真相默认（truth-default theory） | 人先相信、有疑点才怀疑；这在多数时候划算，骗子利用的正是这一点。 | A | Levine 2014, 2019 'Duped'. Gilbert, Tafarodi, Malone 1993 'You Can't Not Believe Everything You Read': understanding a statement means accepting it first; rejecting takes extra effort that load disrupts. Lies are mostly discovered later through evidence or confession, not at the time through demeanour (Park et al. 2002).
* barnum | 巴纳姆效应（Barnum / Forer effect） | 一段对谁都适用的笼统描述，人会觉得是在精准地说自己。 | A | Forer 1949: 39 students each received the same 'personal' sketch assembled from an astrology book and rated its accuracy 4.26 out of 5. Dickson & Kelly 1985 review: stronger when the description is flattering and said to be made for you. Cold reading, horoscopes, many personality tests.
* scam-structure | 骗局的结构（anatomy of a scam） | 长线骗局不靠一句谎话，靠一串步骤：建立信任、给小甜头、制造紧迫、隔开你和能提醒你的人、让你舍不得已经投进去的。 | B | Lea, Fischer, Evans 2009 University of Exeter report for the UK Office of Fair Trading 'The psychology of scams'. Fischer, Lea, Evans 2013. US FTC: reported romance-scam losses $1.3 billion in 2022. Each step is a known effect: reciprocity, foot-in-the-door, scarcity, authority, sunk cost, isolation. Victims are not especially gullible; overconfidence in one's own judgment is a risk factor. Check figures.
* inattentional-blindness | 非注意盲视（inattentional blindness） | 注意力盯着一件事时，眼前发生的另一件大事可以完全看不见。 | A | Simons & Chabris 1999: about half of viewers counting basketball passes missed a person in a gorilla suit walking through for 9 seconds. Drew, Võ, Wolfe 2013: 83% of radiologists missed a gorilla 48 times the size of a nodule inserted in a lung CT. Magicians' misdirection (Kuhn & Tatler 2005).
- cognitive-load-lying | 增加认知负担来识谎 | 让对方倒着讲一遍经过，说谎的人更容易露破绽。 | B | Vrij et al. 2008; Vrij, Fisher, Blank 2017 meta.
- change-blindness | 变化盲视（change blindness） | 眼前的人被换成另一个人，一半的人没发现。 | A | Simons & Levin 1998 door study.
- bullshit-receptivity | 对空话的接受度 | 听着深刻、其实没内容的句子，有人会评为很有深度。 | B | Pennycook et al. 2015.
- gaslighting-limits | 记忆可以被植入 | 反复的暗示能让一部分人「记起」没发生过的事。 | A | Loftus & Pickrell 1995 lost in the mall (about 25%); Loftus & Palmer 1974 'smashed' vs 'hit'.
## game.negotiate | 谈判 | 底气来自谈不成时你还有什么；先报的数字会成为锚；多数人根本没开口谈。
* batna | 谈不成的退路（BATNA） | 谈判里的力量主要来自：谈崩了，你最好的另一条路有多好。 | B | Fisher & Ury 1981 'Getting to Yes'. Pinkley, Neale, Bennett 1994: negotiators with a better alternative got better outcomes for themselves and larger joint gains. Magee, Galinsky, Gruenfeld 2007. A second offer in hand changes a salary talk more than any phrasing.
* first-offer | 先出价与锚（first offer anchoring） | 谁先报出一个有依据的数字，成交价就往谁那边靠；自己信息少的时候才让对方先开口。 | A | Galinsky & Mussweiler 2001 JPSP: whoever made the first offer got the better outcome; the first offer strongly predicted the final price; thinking about the other side's alternatives or one's own target removed the advantage. Mason, Lee, Wiley, Ames 2013: precise offers ($5,115 rather than $5,000) anchor more strongly. | req=judge.dual.anchoring
* salary-negotiation | 谈薪：开口和不开口的差别 | 多数人接受第一个报价；开口谈的人起薪通常高出几个百分点，并在之后每次调薪里被放大。 | B | Babcock & Laschever 2003: among Carnegie Mellon master's graduates 57% of men and 7% of women negotiated; negotiators' starting salaries were about 7.4% higher (check). Marks & Harold 2011: new hires who negotiated gained about $5,000 on average. Leibbrandt & List 2015 field experiment: stating that pay is negotiable closes the gender gap in asking.
- integrative | 把饼做大（integrative negotiation） | 双方看重的东西不一样时，各自在自己不在乎的项上让步，两边都更好。 | A | Follett's orange; Thompson 1991: asking about priorities raised joint gains; Pruitt 1981 logrolling.
- mesos | 同时给出多个等价方案 | 一次给两三个对你等值的方案，对方的选择会告诉你他看重什么。 | B | Leonardelli, Gu, McRuer, Medvec, Galinsky 2019.

# career | 职业与组织 | 职业轨迹的统计规律：什么时候出成果、换工作值不值、晋升看什么，以及组织里反复出现的失灵。
## career.path | 轨迹与选择 | 关于年龄、跳槽和重大职业决定，数据说的和焦虑时想的不一样。
* founder-age | 出成果的年龄（age and achievement） | 最成功的创业者创业时平均四十出头；多数领域的高峰比「三十岁前」的说法晚得多。 | A | Azoulay, Jones, Kim, Miranda 2020 AER: Insights (2.7 million US founders, Census data): mean age at founding 41.9; for the top 0.1% fastest-growing firms 45.0; a 50-year-old founder is 1.8 times as likely as a 30-year-old to build a top-growth firm. Jones 2010: age at Nobel-winning work has risen, now mostly late 30s to 40s.
* job-switching | 跳槽与留任的工资增长 | 职业早期的工资增长里，大约三分之一来自换雇主；换工作的人涨薪中位数长期高于留任的人。 | A | Topel & Ward 1992 QJE: a typical US man holds seven jobs in his first ten years, and job changes account for about a third of early-career wage growth. Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta Wage Growth Tracker: job switchers' median wage growth runs above job stayers' in most years (look up the current gap and the 2022 peak).
* coin-flip | 犹豫不决时偏向改变 | 在重大决定上拿不定主意的人，最后选了改变的，半年后比没改的更快乐。 | B | Levitt 2021 Review of Economic Studies 'Heads or Tails': about 20,000 people undecided about a major change (quit a job, end a relationship) flipped a coin on his website; those told by the coin to change were more likely to do it, and those who changed reported being substantially happier at two and six months. Self-selected sample, self-reports. | req=judge.prospect.status-quo-default
* explore-exploit | 探索与利用（explore–exploit, 37% rule） | 选择还多、时间还长的时候多试；试到一定数量之后，遇到比之前都好的就定下来。 | B | Secretary problem: with n candidates seen in sequence, pass on the first 37% (1/e), then take the first one better than all so far; picks the best 37% of the time (Gardner 1960; Ferguson 1989). In experiments people stop too early (Seale & Rapoport 1997). Gittins index: exploring is worth more the longer the horizon. Liu et al. 2021: hot streaks follow exploration then exploitation.
* peter-principle | 彼得原理（Peter principle） | 按当前岗位的业绩提拔人，会把最好的执行者变成不称职的管理者。 | B | Peter & Hull 1969, written as satire. Benson, Li, Shue 2019 QJE (53,035 sales workers, 214 US firms): doubling a salesperson's sales raised the chance of promotion about 15%, and predicted a 7.5% drop in their new subordinates' sales; firms promoted the best sellers, not the best future managers.
- planned-happenstance | 计划内的偶然（planned happenstance） | 多数职业转折来自没计划到的机会；能做的是提高遇到和接住机会的概率。 | B | Mitchell, Levin, Krumboltz 1999; Bright, Pryor, Harpham 2005: about 70% report a chance event shaped their career.
- specialist-generalist | 专才与通才 | 环境规则稳定时专才占优；规则多变、问题没见过时，跨领域的人更常解出来。 | B | Epstein 2019; Jeppesen & Lakhani 2010 InnoCentive outsiders; Teodoridis, Bikard, Vakili 2019.
- sunk-career | 职业里的沉没成本 | 已经在一个方向上投入的年头，让人更难离开一条不再合适的路。 | B | Applies sunk cost; Staw 1976.
## career.perform | 被看见与被评价 | 业绩不会自己说话：评价里有很大一部分是评价者自己，晋升看得见的人，面试测到的东西有限。
* visibility | 做得好不等于被看见（visibility） | 同样的产出，被看见的人晋升更快；远程办公的人业绩更高，晋升却更少。 | B | Bloom, Liang, Roberts, Ying 2015 QJE (Ctrip, 249 call-centre staff randomised): home workers were 13% more productive, yet their promotion rate conditional on performance fell by about half. Ng, Eby, Sorensen, Feldman 2005 meta: career sponsorship and political knowledge predict salary and promotion alongside human capital. Elsbach, Cable, Sherman 2010 'passive face time'.
* rater-bias | 绩效评分里有多少是评分人（idiosyncratic rater effect） | 一份绩效评分的差异，一多半来自打分的人自己的习惯，只有约五分之一来自被评的人的实际表现。 | A | Scullen, Mount, Goff 2000 Journal of Applied Psychology (4,492 managers rated by bosses, peers, subordinates): 62% of rating variance was idiosyncratic rater effects, 21% actual performance (check). Mount et al. 1998. Buckingham & Goodall 2015 HBR on Deloitte's redesign.
* interview-validity | 面试到底测到什么（selection validity） | 随意聊的面试预测工作表现的能力很差；有固定问题和评分标准的结构化面试、实际工作样本好得多。 | A | Schmidt & Hunter 1998 Psychological Bulletin (85 years of research): work samples 0.54, general mental ability 0.51, structured interviews 0.51, unstructured 0.38, years of experience 0.18. Sackett, Zhang, Berry, Lievens 2022 revision: structured interviews 0.42 at the top, ability lowered to 0.31, unstructured 0.19. Dana, Dawes, Peterson 2013: unstructured interviews can make predictions worse.
* confidence-status | 自信被当成能力（overconfidence and status） | 表现得比实际更有把握的人，会被同伴当成更有能力，得到更高的地位，被拆穿后也没怎么掉。 | B | Anderson, Brion, Moore, Kennedy 2012 JPSP (six studies): overconfident people were rated more competent and gained status in groups. Kennedy, Anderson, Moore 2013: status held even after real performance was revealed. Chamorro-Premuzic 2013 argues this is why incompetent leaders get picked.
* sponsor | 导师与保荐人（mentoring, sponsorship） | 有人指点对薪水和晋升的帮助是真的但不大；有人在你不在场的会议上替你说话，作用更直接。 | B | Allen, Eby, Poteet, Lentz, Lima 2004 meta: mentored people have slightly higher pay and more promotions; small effects. Eby et al. 2008 meta. Ng et al. 2005: sponsorship r about 0.2 with salary. Ibarra, Carter, Silva 2010 HBR: women are over-mentored and under-sponsored (survey).
- self-promotion | 自我推销的代价 | 直接说自己的成绩有效但招人烦；装谦虚的炫耀最差。 | B | Sezer, Gino, Norton 2018 humblebragging; Scopelliti, Loewenstein, Vosgerau 2015.
- recency-review | 近因效应与评估周期 | 评估前最近几个月的表现被过分加权。 | B | serial-position effects in appraisal; Steiner & Rain 1989.
## career.org | 组织里的规律 | 组织会按可预测的方式低效：工作填满时间，权力让人看不见别人。
* parkinson | 帕金森定律（Parkinson's law） | 工作会膨胀到占满给它的时间；它是一篇讽刺文章，但「时间越宽裕做得越慢」有实验支持。 | B | Parkinson 1955 in The Economist: British Admiralty officials rose 78% between 1914 and 1928 while ships in commission fell 67%. Aronson & Gerard 1966; Bryan & Locke 1967: people given more time than needed took longer and then kept the slower pace.
- power | 权力让人少换位思考（power and perspective-taking） | 感到自己有权的人，更少去想别人怎么看、怎么感受。 | B | Galinsky, Magee, Inesi, Gruenfeld 2006 Psychological Science: asked to draw an E on their forehead, 33% of high-power participants drew it so that others could not read it, vs 12% of low-power. Keltner, Gruenfeld, Anderson 2003. Priming-based power studies have replicated unevenly; say so.
- brooks-law | 布鲁克斯法则（Brooks's law） | 给延期的软件项目加人，会让它更延期。 | B | Brooks 1975 'The Mythical Man-Month'.
- organizational-silence | 组织沉默 | 员工知道问题却不说，因为觉得说了没用或有风险。 | B | Morrison & Milliken 2000; Detert & Edmondson 2011 implicit voice theories.
- bikeshedding | 鸡毛蒜皮定律（bikeshedding） | 会议花在一件事上的时间，和这件事的重要性成反比。 | C | Parkinson 1957, satire; no systematic study.
## career.work | 工作、精力与倦怠 | 加班的产出很快封顶，切换任务有残留成本，倦怠来自要求高而掌控少。
* burnout | 倦怠（burnout） | 倦怠有三个部分：耗竭、对工作变冷漠、觉得自己没用；它主要由工作条件造成，不是个人脆弱。 | A | Maslach & Jackson 1981 inventory; Maslach, Schaufeli, Leiter 2001 Annual Review. Six mismatches: workload, control, reward, community, fairness, values. Demerouti et al. 2001 job demands–resources model. WHO ICD-11 (2019): an occupational phenomenon, not a medical condition. Panagioti et al. 2017 meta: organisation-level changes help more than individual ones.
* attention-residue | 注意力残留与切换成本（attention residue） | 从一件没做完的事切到另一件，脑子有一部分还留在前一件上。 | B | Leroy 2009 Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes. Rubinstein, Meyer, Evans 2001: switching costs grow with task complexity. Mark, Gudith, Klocke 2008: interrupted workers finished faster but with more stress and frustration. Mark, González, Harris 2005: about 23 minutes to return to an interrupted task (field observation).
* overwork | 长工时的产出封顶 | 每周工作超过约 50 小时后，每多一小时的产出急剧下降；70 小时做出来的几乎不比 55 小时多。 | B | Pencavel 2015 Economic Journal: British munitions workers in the First World War; output rose proportionally up to about 49 hours a week, then flattened; output at 70 hours was barely above 56. Kivimäki et al. 2015 Lancet meta (603,838 people): 55+ hours a week, 33% higher stroke risk.
* job-crafting | 工作重塑（job crafting） | 不换岗位，也能自己调整任务的范围、和谁打交道、怎么理解这份工作，让它更合自己。 | B | Wrzesniewski & Dutton 2001: hospital cleaners who saw themselves as part of patient care did extra tasks and found the work meaningful. Wrzesniewski et al. 1997 job, career, calling. Rudolph, Katz, Lavigne, Zacher 2017 meta (122 samples): crafting linked to engagement and performance. Oprea et al. 2019 meta of interventions: small effects.
- remote-work | 远程办公的证据 | 混合办公不降低产出和晋升，离职率更低。 | A | Bloom, Han, Liang 2024 Nature (Trip.com, 1,612 staff randomised): hybrid cut quit rates by a third with no effect on performance reviews or promotion.
- pay-for-performance | 计件工资 | 产出容易量的工作，按件付酬能明显提高产量。 | A | Lazear 2000 AER Safelite: output per worker up 44%, half from incentives and half from who stayed.
- meaning-work | 工作的意义感 | 看到自己的工作帮到了谁，人会干得更多。 | B | Grant et al. 2007: fundraising callers who met a scholarship recipient for five minutes spent 142% more time on the phone and raised 171% more.

# life | 幸福、健康与人生阶段 | 什么让人长期过得好：对幸福的预测为什么总错，钱起多大作用，身体怎么托住情绪，人到中年在乎的东西怎么变。
## life.happy | 幸福感 | 人会适应大多数好事和坏事，预测自己将来的感受时总是估过头；关系比收入更能预测晚年。
* hedonic-adaptation | 享乐适应（hedonic adaptation） | 升职、加薪、买房带来的高兴会退回到原来的水平；有几样坏事（失业、长期通勤、噪音）却适应不了。 | B | Brickman, Coates, Janoff-Bulman 1978: 22 lottery winners were not happier than controls; 29 accident victims were less happy but above the midpoint. Small samples. Lucas, Clark, Georgellis, Diener 2003 (German panel, n=24,000): the marriage boost fades in about two years. Lucas 2007: unemployment and disability leave lasting drops. Stutzer & Frey 2008: people do not adapt to commuting.
* affective-forecasting | 情感预测偏差（affective forecasting, impact bias） | 人高估未来的好事坏事会让自己高兴或难受多久，因为只盯着那一件事，也低估了自己的恢复力。 | A | Gilbert, Pinel, Wilson, Blumberg, Wheatley 1998 JPSP: assistant professors expected tenure decisions to shape their happiness for years; those denied were about as happy as those granted. Wilson & Gilbert 2005. Schkade & Kahneman 1998: Midwesterners and Californians both thought Californians were happier; they were not.
* income-happiness | 钱和幸福（income and well-being） | 收入越高，日常情绪平均越好，没有封顶；只有本来就最不快乐的那一小部分人，过了约十万美元后钱不再有用。 | A | Kahneman & Deaton 2010 PNAS: emotional well-being flat above about $75,000. Killingsworth 2021 PNAS (33,391 employed US adults, 1.7 million experience samples): no plateau. Killingsworth, Kahneman, Mellers 2023 PNAS adversarial collaboration: plateau only for the unhappiest 15–20%, at about $100,000; for the rest it keeps rising with log income. | req=life.happy.hedonic-adaptation
* peak-end | 峰终定律（peak–end rule） | 人对一段经历的记忆由最强烈的一刻和结尾决定，和持续了多久几乎无关。 | A | Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber, Redelmeier 1993: one hand in 14°C water for 60 s; the other for 60 s plus 30 s at 15°C; 69% chose to repeat the longer one. Redelmeier & Kahneman 1996 colonoscopy. Redelmeier, Katz, Kahneman 2003 (682 patients randomised): leaving the scope in for a milder final minute improved remembered pain and return rates. | tag=十日终焉
* relationships-health | 关系是晚年健康最强的预测因素 | 跟踪八十多年的研究里，50 岁时对关系的满意度，比胆固醇更能预测 80 岁时的健康。 | A | Harvard Study of Adult Development, begun 1938 (268 Harvard sophomores plus 456 Boston inner-city boys); Vaillant 2012 'Triumphs of Experience'; Waldinger & Schulz 2023 'The Good Life'. Holt-Lunstad 2010 meta: 50% higher odds of survival with stronger relationships. Observational: healthier people may also keep relationships more easily.
* time-vs-money | 花钱买时间（time vs money） | 把钱花在省掉讨厌的杂事上的人更满意；多数人有钱也不这么花。 | B | Whillans, Dunn, Smeets, Bekkers, Norton 2017 PNAS (n=6,271 in four countries): buying time linked to higher life satisfaction at every income level; in an experiment, $40 spent on time-saving beat $40 spent on a material purchase. Whillans, Weidman, Dunn 2016: people who value time over money are happier.
- experiential-purchases | 体验比物品更值 | 花在体验上的钱带来的满足比花在物品上的更持久，差别不大。 | B | Van Boven & Gilovich 2003; Weidman & Dunn 2016: material purchases give more frequent small happiness.
- prosocial-spending | 为别人花钱 | 给别人花钱比给自己花钱略微更快乐。 | B | Dunn, Aknin, Norton 2008 Science; Aknin et al. 2020 pre-registered replication (n>7,000): small.
- gratitude | 感恩练习 | 每周写下几件值得感谢的事，对情绪有小的正面作用。 | B | Emmons & McCullough 2003; Cregg & Cheavens 2021 meta: g=-0.29 for depression, weaker against active controls.
- focusing-illusion | 聚焦错觉（focusing illusion） | 你正在想的那件事，没有你想的时候以为的那么重要。 | A | Schkade & Kahneman 1998; Kahneman et al. 2006 Science.
- midlife-dip | 中年低谷（U-shape of happiness） | 很多国家的截面数据里，生活满意度在四十多岁最低，之后回升；跟踪同一批人时没这么清楚。 | B | Blanchflower 2021 (132 countries, minimum about age 48); Galambos et al. 2020 critique.
- diderot-effect | 狄德罗效应（鸟笼效应） | 买了一件新东西，周围的旧东西显得不配，于是接着换下去。 | B | Diderot 1769 'Regrets on Parting with My Old Dressing Gown'; McCracken 1988 named it; a description of consumption, not an experiment.
## life.health | 身体是地基 | 睡眠、运动和酒精直接改变情绪和判断力，而且当事人自己常常感觉不到。
* sleep-debt | 睡眠不足而不自知（sleep restriction） | 连续两周每晚只睡六小时，反应和注意力掉到相当于两夜没睡，自己却只觉得「有点困」。 | A | Van Dongen, Maislin, Mullington, Dinges 2003 Sleep (48 adults, 14 days): six hours a night produced deficits equal to two nights of total sleep deprivation on the psychomotor vigilance task; four hours, three nights; self-rated sleepiness levelled off after a few days. Belenky et al. 2003. Williamson & Feyer 2000: 17–19 hours awake is like a blood alcohol level of 0.05%.
* exercise-mood | 运动对抑郁的效果（exercise and depression） | 走路、慢跑、力量训练、瑜伽对抑郁的效果，和心理治疗、药物在同一个量级，强度大一点效果更好。 | B | Noetel et al. 2024 BMJ network meta-analysis (218 trials, 14,170 participants): walking or jogging g=-0.62, yoga -0.55, strength training -0.49; effects grew with intensity. Singh et al. 2023 British Journal of Sports Medicine umbrella review (97 reviews). Many trials are small and have a risk of bias; the direction is consistent.
* alcohol | 酒精、睡眠与情绪（alcohol） | 酒让人更快睡着，却把后半夜的睡眠打碎，压掉快速眼动睡眠；长期喝还把抑郁的风险翻倍。 | A | Ebrahim, Shapiro, Williams, Fenwick 2013 review (27 studies): alcohol shortens sleep onset, deepens the first half, disrupts the second half and reduces REM at moderate and high doses. Boden & Fergusson 2011 Addiction: alcohol use disorder about doubles the risk of major depression, most plausibly causally in that direction. GBD 2016 Alcohol Collaborators 2018 Lancet: the level of drinking that minimises health loss is zero.
- social-jetlag | 社交时差（social jetlag） | 工作日和休息日的作息差两小时以上，相当于每周飞一次跨时区。 | B | Wittmann, Dinnich, Merrow, Roenneberg 2006; Roenneberg et al. 2012 Current Biology (n≈65,000): each hour raises odds of overweight 33%.
- stress-mindset | 怎么看压力（stress mindset） | 把压力反应理解成身体在帮自己准备，表现略好；「相信压力有害的人死得更多」来自一项相关研究。 | B | Keller et al. 2012 (n=28,753); Crum, Salovey, Achor 2013; Jamieson et al. 2010 GRE reappraisal.
- nature-exposure | 接触自然 | 在自然环境里走一段，反刍减少。 | B | Bratman et al. 2015 PNAS (90-minute walk, n=38); White et al. 2019: 120 minutes a week.
- weight-maintenance | 减重后怎么不反弹 | 长期保持住的人的共同点是高活动量、持续称重记录、饮食规律。 | B | US National Weight Control Registry (Wing & Phelan 2005).
## life.stage | 人生阶段与成熟 | 所谓四十岁的心态，在研究里是几样具体的变化：把时间看成有限的，看重有情感分量的人和事，能容下不确定和别人的立场。
* social-clock | 社会时钟（social clock） | 「几岁该干什么」是一套文化里的时间表；觉得自己落后于它会带来真实的压力，但这张表本身一直在往后挪。 | B | Neugarten, Moore, Lowe 1965 age norms; Neugarten 1976 on-time and off-time events. Rook, Catalano, Dooley 1989: off-time events are more distressing. Arnett 2000 emerging adulthood: ages 18–29 have become a distinct phase of exploration. US Census: median age at first marriage in 2023 about 30 for men and 28 for women, up from 23 and 21 in 1970 (check).
* socioemotional-selectivity | 社会情绪选择理论（socioemotional selectivity theory） | 觉得时间还很多的人追求新信息和新关系；觉得时间有限的人把精力给最有情感分量的少数人和事。起作用的是时间感，不是年龄。 | A | Carstensen, Isaacowitz, Charles 1999 American Psychologist; Carstensen 2006 Science. Fredrickson & Carstensen 1990: young people imagining an imminent move chose familiar close partners, like the old. Young men with HIV in the 1990s chose like 80-year-olds. Carstensen et al. 2011: emotional well-being improves from early adulthood into the 60s.
* wise-reasoning | 智慧推理（wise reasoning） | 研究里的「智慧」是几样可测的做法：承认自己知道的有限，看到别人的立场，预期事情会变，找折中；它预测生活过得好，智商不预测。 | B | Grossmann et al. 2010 PNAS: older adults used more of these when reasoning about social conflicts. Grossmann, Na, Varnum, Kitayama, Nisbett 2013 JEP: General: wise reasoning predicted well-being, relationship quality and even longevity; intelligence did not. Grossmann 2017: it varies more across situations than across people, and self-distancing raises it. | req=self.emotion.self-distancing
- emerging-adulthood | 成年初显期（emerging adulthood） | 18 到 29 岁换工作、换城市、换伴侣是这个阶段的常态，不是失败。 | B | Arnett 2000 American Psychologist.
- purpose | 人生目标感与寿命 | 觉得生活有方向的人死亡风险更低。 | B | Hill & Turiano 2014 Psychological Science (MIDUS, n=6,163, 14 years): hazard ratio 0.85 per SD.
- post-traumatic-growth | 创伤后成长 | 很多人自述在重大打击后有成长，但自述的成长和实际测到的变化对不上。 | C | Tedeschi & Calhoun 1996; Frazier et al. 2009 Psychological Science.
- reminiscence-bump | 怀旧高峰（reminiscence bump） | 人一生记得最多的是 15 到 30 岁之间的事。 | A | Rubin, Wetzler, Nebes 1986.

# persona | 人格：类型与特质 | MBTI、荣格八维和大五人格分别从哪来、量的是什么、哪些说法经得起数据，以及怎样用人格知识而不被它框住。
## persona.mbti | MBTI：从哪来、量什么 | 一对母女从荣格的理论出发编的商业问卷：四个维度、十六种类型，以及数据怎么看它。
* origin | MBTI 是怎么来的 | 一对母女从荣格的《心理类型》出发，为二战时帮人找合适工作编的问卷，后来成了商业产品；它不是从数据里归纳出来的。 | B | Katharine Cook Briggs and daughter Isabel Briggs Myers, neither trained in psychology; Jung 1921 Psychologische Typen (English 1923); first form 1943 (check); Educational Testing Service research edition 1962; ETS dropped it and Consulting Psychologists Press took over 1975 (check); now The Myers-Briggs Company, claims about 2 million assessments a year (check). Contrast: the Big Five came bottom-up from data. Do not repeat card psy-evidence-mbti (overview); this card is the history.
* four-dimensions | 四个维度与十六型 | E/I、S/N、T/F、J/P 四组二选一，组合成十六型；J/P 是迈尔斯母女加的，荣格原著里没有。 | B | Jung had two attitudes (E/I) and four functions (T, F, S, N); Briggs/Myers added J/P to say which function is dominant (check). Each dimension yields a continuous preference score; the type letter is which side of the midpoint you fall on.
* not-bimodal | 分数是一座山，不是两座 | 每个维度上多数人挤在中间、单峰分布；切点附近差一两分就换字母，「内向的人」「外向的人」是两种人的说法没有数据支持。 | C | McCrae & Costa 1989 Journal of Personality (n=468, ages 19–93): continuous scores, no evidence of types; Pittenger 2005 Consulting Psychology Journal; Arnau, Green, Rosen, Gleaves, Melancon 2003 taxometric analysis: dimensional not categorical (check); Bess & Harvey 2002 on apparent bimodality as a scoring artefact (check). | req=evidence.myth.mbti
* retest | 重测换型 | 单个维度分数的重测还算稳定，但整体四字母类型几周后就有相当多人变了，越靠近中间越容易变。 | C | Pittenger 1993/2005: as many as 50% get a different type within about five weeks (check exact wording); dimension test-retest correlations roughly .7–.8 (check); Capraro & Capraro 2002 reliability generalization (check). | req=persona.mbti.not-bimodal
* prediction | MBTI 能不能用来选人 | 没有证据说明某个类型更适合某个工作；出版方自己的伦理指南也说不应拿 MBTI 做招聘筛选。 | C | The Myers-Briggs Company ethical guidelines: not for selection or hiring (check page); Gardner & Martinko 1996 Journal of Management review of MBTI and managers (check); Pittenger 2005. Do not repeat the conscientiousness meta-analysis numbers already on psy-evidence-mbti.
* map-to-big5 | 把 MBTI 换算成大五 | 四个维度分别对应大五的外向性、开放性、宜人性、尽责性，唯独没有神经质；当连续分数看才有用。 | A | McCrae & Costa 1989: E–I with Extraversion, S–N with Openness, T–F with Agreeableness, J–P with Conscientiousness, correlations roughly .4–.7 (check each); none with Neuroticism. Furnham 1996 Personality and Individual Differences replication (check).
* 16personalities | 16personalities 不是 MBTI | 最流行的免费测试多一个 A/T（自信/动荡）维度，模型更接近大五，和官方 MBTI 是两套东西。 | B | NERIS Analytics site ('Our Framework' or 'Our Theory') says it combines the letters with Big Five-style traits (check wording); the -A/-T identity scale tracks Neuroticism/emotional stability. Story option: the MBTI boom on Chinese social media 2021–2023, MBTI in dating profiles and job ads (check a reliable report before using).
## persona.jung | 荣格八维：认知功能与功能栈 | 荣格的原始理论、后人搭出来的八维功能栈，以及数据对它说了什么。
* psychological-types | 荣格《心理类型》的原意 | 荣格提出内倾/外倾两种态度和思维、情感、感觉、直觉四种功能，来自他的临床观察；他把类型当成辨认方向的工具，反对拿它给人贴标签。 | B | Jung 1921 Psychologische Typen (English 1923); based on clinical observation, not data; find Jung's own cautions, e.g. the 1936 'Psychological Typology' lecture or the foreword to the Argentine edition (check exact source and wording before quoting; paraphrase if not found).
* eight-functions | 八维与功能栈 | 四种功能各分内外向得到 Ni、Te 等八维；「主导、辅助、第三、劣势」的排列是后人（迈尔斯、Beebe 等）推出来的模型，荣格没有这样排。 | C | Example: INTJ = Ni–Te–Fi–Se in type-dynamics theory; Myers' rules for dominant/auxiliary; John Beebe's eight-function archetypal model (1980s–2000s, check); Lenore Thomson 1998 'Personality Type: An Owner's Manual' popularized the eight functions online (check). | req=persona.jung.psychological-types
* type-dynamics-evidence | 功能栈有证据吗 | 用 MBTI 数据检验「主导功能」这类类型动力学，大多不支持；网上的八维测试普遍没有公开的信度、效度数据。 | C | Reynierse & Harker 2001 Journal of Psychological Type: additive model fits better than type dynamics (check); Reynierse 2009 'The case against type dynamics' (check); Socionics has no peer-reviewed validation (check). | req=persona.jung.eight-functions
* unfalsifiable | 为什么八维怎么说都对 | 功能栈能解释任何行为（不像就说是劣势功能在起作用），无法被证伪；这让它适合聊天和自我反思，却不能当科学结论。 | C | Popper's falsifiability; worked example: the same behaviour 'explained' by the tertiary or the inferior function; link to the Barnum effect. | req=persona.jung.type-dynamics-evidence,game.deceive.barnum
## persona.big5 | 大五人格：研究里用的那一套 | 从词典里归纳出的五个连续维度：怎么来的、怎么测、多少来自遗传、能预测什么、能不能改。
* lexical | 大五是怎么从词典里找出来的 | 重要的性格差异会进入语言：从词典里挑出描述人的词做因素分析，几十年里反复得到五个大维度。 | A | Allport & Odbert 1936: about 18,000 person-descriptive words, about 4,500 stable traits (check); Cattell's 16PF; Tupes & Christal 1961 US Air Force data found five factors; Norman 1963; Goldberg 1990 named the 'Big Five'; Costa & McCrae NEO-PI 1985/1992.
* ocean | 五个维度各是什么 | 开放性、尽责性、外向性、宜人性、神经质；每一维都是连续分数、大致正态分布，下面还分更细的侧面（facet）。 | A | NEO-PI-R: 30 facets, 6 per domain; BFI-2 (Soto & John 2017 JPSP): 15 facets, 3 per domain. One concrete behavioural example per trait. | req=persona.big5.lexical
* measure | 怎样测一份靠谱的大五 | 用公开、有信效度数据的量表（BFI-2、IPIP-NEO），看自己在常模里的百分位，再请熟人评一次。 | A | BFI-2: 60 items, domain alphas roughly .83–.90 (check); IPIP public-domain items (Goldberg 1999; Goldberg et al. 2006, check); Johnson's IPIP-NEO-120 free online (check); observer reports add validity (Connelly & Ones 2010 meta, check). | req=persona.big5.ocean
* heritability | 人格有多少来自遗传 | 双生子研究汇总：人格差异约四成和基因有关；兄弟姐妹共同的家庭环境几乎不起作用，剩下的是各自独有的经历。 | A | Vukasović & Bratko 2015 Psychological Bulletin meta-analysis: heritability about .40 (check study count and N); Plomin & Daniels 1987 'Why are children in the same family so different from one another?' (shared environment near zero); Turkheimer 2000 three laws of behavior genetics (check).
* stability | 人格稳不稳定 | 人和人之间的相对排序随年龄越来越稳，五十岁以后很稳；但每个人的平均水平一生都在慢慢变。 | A | Roberts & DelVecchio 2000 Psychological Bulletin: rank-order consistency about .31 in childhood, .54 in college, .64 at 30, .74 at 50–70 (check). Mean-level change is on card psy-self-personality-change: link, do not repeat. | req=self.identity.personality-change
* life-outcomes | 人格能预测人生结果吗 | 尽责性、神经质、宜人性对寿命、离婚、职业成就的预测力，和家庭经济地位、智商在同一量级。 | A | Roberts, Kuncel, Shiner, Caspi, Goldberg 2007 Perspectives on Psychological Science 'The Power of Personality'; Soto 2019 Psychological Science replication of 78 trait–outcome associations: 87% replicated, effects about 77% of the originals (check).
* job-performance | 什么人格和工作表现有关 | 尽责性对几乎所有职业都有稳定但不大的预测力；外向性只在销售、管理这类和人打交道的岗位有用。 | A | Barrick & Mount 1991 (117 studies, check N); Barrick, Mount & Judge 2001 second-order meta-analysis (check); Sackett, Zhang, Berry, Lievens 2022 Journal of Applied Psychology revised validity estimates (check conscientiousness value). Do not repeat the .20–.23 line already on psy-evidence-mbti; focus on which trait for which job. | req=persona.big5.life-outcomes
* neuroticism | 神经质：最值得关心的一维 | 神经质高的人更容易焦虑、抑郁和身体不适，社会代价很大；它也是干预最容易改变的一维。 | A | Lahey 2009 American Psychologist 'Public health significance of neuroticism'; Cuijpers et al. 2010 Archives of General Psychiatry: economic costs of neuroticism (check); Roberts 2017 intervention meta: emotional stability changed most (check value). | req=persona.big5.ocean
