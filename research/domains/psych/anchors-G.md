# Group G: 11 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 大五是怎么从词典里找出来的

- node: `persona.big5.lexical` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-lexical.md`
- 一句话：重要的性格差异会进入语言：从词典里挑出描述人的词做因素分析，几十年里反复得到五个大维度。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Allport & Odbert 1936: about 18,000 person-descriptive words, about 4,500 stable traits (check); Cattell's 16PF; Tupes & Christal 1961 US Air Force data found five factors; Norman 1963; Goldberg 1990 named the 'Big Five'; Costa & McCrae NEO-PI 1985/1992.

### 五个维度各是什么

- node: `persona.big5.ocean` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-ocean.md`
- 一句话：开放性、尽责性、外向性、宜人性、神经质；每一维都是连续分数、大致正态分布，下面还分更细的侧面（facet）。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: NEO-PI-R: 30 facets, 6 per domain; BFI-2 (Soto & John 2017 JPSP): 15 facets, 3 per domain. One concrete behavioural example per trait.

### 怎样测一份靠谱的大五

- node: `persona.big5.measure` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-measure.md`
- 一句话：用公开、有信效度数据的量表（BFI-2、IPIP-NEO），看自己在常模里的百分位，再请熟人评一次。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: BFI-2: 60 items, domain alphas roughly .83–.90 (check); IPIP public-domain items (Goldberg 1999; Goldberg et al. 2006, check); Johnson's IPIP-NEO-120 free online (check); observer reports add validity (Connelly & Ones 2010 meta, check).

### 人格有多少来自遗传

- node: `persona.big5.heritability` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-heritability.md`
- 一句话：双生子研究汇总：人格差异约四成和基因有关；兄弟姐妹共同的家庭环境几乎不起作用，剩下的是各自独有的经历。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Vukasović & Bratko 2015 Psychological Bulletin meta-analysis: heritability about .40 (check study count and N); Plomin & Daniels 1987 'Why are children in the same family so different from one another?' (shared environment near zero); Turkheimer 2000 three laws of behavior genetics (check).

### 人格稳不稳定

- node: `persona.big5.stability` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-stability.md`
- 一句话：人和人之间的相对排序随年龄越来越稳，五十岁以后很稳；但每个人的平均水平一生都在慢慢变。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Roberts & DelVecchio 2000 Psychological Bulletin: rank-order consistency about .31 in childhood, .54 in college, .64 at 30, .74 at 50–70 (check). Mean-level change is on card psy-self-personality-change: link, do not repeat.

### 人格能预测人生结果吗

- node: `persona.big5.life-outcomes` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-life-outcomes.md`
- 一句话：尽责性、神经质、宜人性对寿命、离婚、职业成就的预测力，和家庭经济地位、智商在同一量级。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Roberts, Kuncel, Shiner, Caspi, Goldberg 2007 Perspectives on Psychological Science 'The Power of Personality'; Soto 2019 Psychological Science replication of 78 trait–outcome associations: 87% replicated, effects about 77% of the originals (check).

### 什么人格和工作表现有关

- node: `persona.big5.job-performance` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-job-performance.md`
- 一句话：尽责性对几乎所有职业都有稳定但不大的预测力；外向性只在销售、管理这类和人打交道的岗位有用。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Barrick & Mount 1991 (117 studies, check N); Barrick, Mount & Judge 2001 second-order meta-analysis (check); Sackett, Zhang, Berry, Lievens 2022 Journal of Applied Psychology revised validity estimates (check conscientiousness value). Do not repeat the .20–.23 line already on psy-evidence-mbti; focus on which trait for which job.

### 神经质：最值得关心的一维

- node: `persona.big5.neuroticism` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-neuroticism.md`
- 一句话：神经质高的人更容易焦虑、抑郁和身体不适，社会代价很大；它也是干预最容易改变的一维。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Lahey 2009 American Psychologist 'Public health significance of neuroticism'; Cuijpers et al. 2010 Archives of General Psychiatry: economic costs of neuroticism (check); Roberts 2017 intervention meta: emotional stability changed most (check value).

### 想改人格能做到吗

- node: `persona.big5.change` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-change.md`
- 一句话：有目标、落到具体行为的练习能在几个月里让特质小幅移动；做了才有用，只有完成的练习才改变特质。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Roberts, Luo, Briley, Chow, Su, Hill 2017 Psychological Bulletin (207 studies, about 20,000 people, check): about d=.37 over a median 24 weeks (check); Hudson & Fraley 2015 JPSP volitional personality change; Hudson, Briley, Chopik, Derringer 2019 JPSP 'You have to follow through' (check).

### 性别与文化差异

- node: `persona.big5.gender-culture` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-gender-culture.md`
- 一句话：男女在大五上的平均差异不大但稳定（女性的神经质、宜人性偏高）；反直觉的是，越平等富裕的国家差异越大。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Costa, Terracciano & McCrae 2001 JPSP (26 cultures, check N); Schmitt, Realo, Voracek, Allik 2008 JPSP (55 nations, check N). Explanations are debated: say so.

### HEXACO：第六个维度「诚实—谦逊」

- node: `persona.big5.hexaco` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/persona/psy-persona-hexaco.md`
