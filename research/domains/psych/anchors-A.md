# Group A: 43 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 复现危机（replication crisis）

- node: `evidence.method.replication-crisis` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-replication-crisis.md`
- 一句话：把已发表的心理学实验原样重做，只有一部分还能得到原结论；单个小样本的惊人发现要打折。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Open Science Collaboration 2015 Science: 100 studies, 97% of originals significant, 36% of replications significant, mean effect size about half. Camerer 2018 (Nature Human Behaviour): 13 of 21 Science/Nature social-science experiments replicated. Many Labs 2 (2018): 14 of 28.

### 效应量（effect size）

- node: `evidence.method.effect-size` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-effect-size.md`
- 一句话：「有显著差异」不等于「差得多」；要问效果多大，Cohen's d 0.2 小、0.5 中、0.8 大。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Cohen 1988 benchmarks. Contrast pair for intuition: implementation intentions d=0.65 (Gollwitzer & Sheeran 2006) vs growth-mindset interventions d≈0.08 (Sisk 2018). d=0.2 means 58% chance a random treated person beats a random control (common-language effect size).

### 发表偏倚与 p 值操纵（publication bias, p-hacking）

- node: `evidence.method.publication-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-publication-bias.md`
- 一句话：只有「有效果」的研究容易发表，研究者又有很多分析自由度，所以文献整体高估效果。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Simmons, Nelson, Simonsohn 2011 Psychological Science 'False-Positive Psychology': with flexible analysis they 'showed' listening to 'When I'm Sixty-Four' made people 1.5 years younger. Rosenthal 1979 file-drawer problem. Kvarven, Strømland, Johannesson 2020 (Nature Human Behaviour): meta-analyses' effects about 3x those of pre-registered multi-lab replications.

### 相关不等于因果（correlation vs causation）

- node: `evidence.method.correlation-causation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-correlation-causation.md`
- 一句话：两件事一起出现，可能是反向因果、共同原因或选择；随机对照实验和自然实验才回答因果。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Hormone replacement therapy: observational studies suggested lower heart disease, the Women's Health Initiative RCT (2002, n≈16,600) found increased risk; healthy-user bias. Use it as the story.

### WEIRD 样本

- node: `evidence.method.weird-samples` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-weird-samples.md`
- 一句话：多数心理学实验的被试是西方、受过教育、工业化、富裕、民主社会的大学生，结论未必适用所有人。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Henrich, Heine, Norenzayan 2010 Behavioral and Brain Sciences: 96% of subjects in top psychology journals from Western industrialised countries holding 12% of world population; Müller-Lyer illusion strength varies by society (Segall 1966); ultimatum game offers vary (Henrich 2001).

### 系统 1 与系统 2（dual process）

- node: `judge.dual.two-systems` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-two-systems.md`
- 一句话：直觉先给答案，推理通常只是签字放行；觉得「一眼就知道」的题最该停一下。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman 2011. Frederick 2005 Cognitive Reflection Test (bat and ball $1.10): n=3,428, mean 1.24 of 3 correct; more than half of students at Harvard, MIT, Princeton answered 10 cents. The two 'systems' are a description, not two brain parts.

### 可得性启发（availability heuristic）

- node: `judge.dual.availability` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-availability.md`
- 一句话：越容易想起的事，被判断得越常见、越危险。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tversky & Kahneman 1973: most people say more English words start with K than have K as third letter (the reverse is true). Lichtenstein, Slovic 1978: people overestimate deaths from tornadoes, underestimate asthma, diabetes. After 9/11 Americans drove instead of flying: Gigerenzer 2004/2006 estimates about 1,500 extra road deaths in the following year.

### 代表性启发与基础比率忽略（base-rate neglect）

- node: `judge.dual.base-rate-neglect` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-base-rate-neglect.md`
- 一句话：按「像不像」判断概率，忘了这类事本来有多常见。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman & Tversky 1973 engineer-lawyer. Tversky & Kahneman 1983 Linda: 85% ranked 'bank teller and feminist' above 'bank teller'. Casscells 1978 NEJM: disease prevalence 1/1000, false-positive rate 5%: only 11 of 60 Harvard medical staff and students gave about 2%; most said 95%. Gigerenzer & Hoffrage 1995: natural frequencies fix most of it.

### 锚定效应（anchoring）

- node: `judge.dual.anchoring` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-anchoring.md`
- 一句话：先看到的数字，即使无关，也会把之后的估计拉过去。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tversky & Kahneman 1974: wheel of fortune rigged at 10 or 65; median estimates of African nations in the UN 25% vs 45%. Northcraft & Neale 1987: real-estate agents' appraisals moved with the listing price while they denied using it. Many Labs 1 (2014): anchoring among the strongest replicated effects.

### 框架效应（framing effect）

- node: `judge.dual.framing` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-framing.md`
- 一句话：同一个选择，说成「救活多少」还是「死多少」，人的偏好会反过来。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tversky & Kahneman 1981 Science, Asian disease problem: 72% chose the sure option in the gain frame, 78% chose the gamble in the loss frame. Replicated in Many Labs 1. McNeil 1982 NEJM: surgery chosen more when described by survival than by mortality, physicians included.

### 正常化偏误（normalcy bias）

- node: `judge.dual.normalcy-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-normalcy-bias.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：灾难刚开始时，多数人先当它是正常情况，花时间确认、收拾、等别人先动。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Disaster research: NIST 2005 World Trade Center study (Averill et al.): survivors waited about 6 minutes on average before starting to evacuate; many first made calls, shut down computers, gathered belongings. Drabek 1986 on warning response. Ripley 2008 'The Unthinkable' is the popular account. Check the figures.

### 损失厌恶（loss aversion）

- node: `judge.prospect.loss-aversion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-loss-aversion.md`
- 一句话：丢 100 块的难受，大约要得到 200 块才能抵消。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman & Tversky 1979 Econometrica (prospect theory); Tversky & Kahneman 1992 λ=2.25. Brown, Imai, Vieider, Camerer 2024 Journal of Economic Literature meta-analysis: 607 estimates from 150 articles, mean λ about 1.96 (check). Critique: Gal & Rucker 2018: weak for small stakes. Chicago Heights teachers (Fryer, Levitt, List, Sadoff 2012/2022): bonus paid upfront and clawed back raised math scores, same bonus paid at year end did not.

### 禀赋效应（endowment effect）

- node: `judge.prospect.endowment` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-endowment.md`
- 一句话：东西一旦归你，你要的卖价就比你愿意出的买价高出一倍左右。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman, Knetsch, Thaler 1990 JPE mugs: median selling price about $7.12 vs buying $2.87 (check). List 2003 QJE: experienced sports-card dealers show little of it. Free trials and 30-day returns use it.

### 沉没成本谬误（sunk cost fallacy）

- node: `judge.prospect.sunk-cost` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-sunk-cost.md`
- 一句话：已经花掉、收不回来的钱和时间不该影响接下来的决定，但它会。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Arkes & Blumer 1985: theatre season-ticket buyers who paid full price ($15) attended more plays in the first half of the season than those given $2 or $7 discounts. Concorde. Staw 1976 escalation of commitment. NBA: Staw & Hoang 1995 high draft picks get more minutes regardless of performance.

### 现状偏差与默认选项（status quo bias, default effect）

- node: `judge.prospect.status-quo-default` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-status-quo-default.md`
- 一句话：多数人不改默认值；谁设默认值，谁就决定了结果。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Samuelson & Zeckhauser 1988. Johnson & Goldstein 2003 Science: organ-donor consent 12% in Germany (opt-in) vs 99.98% in Austria (opt-out). Madrian & Shea 2001 QJE: 401(k) participation of new hires 37% to 86% under automatic enrolment; most stayed at the default 3% rate.

### 心理账户（mental accounting）

- node: `judge.prospect.mental-accounting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-mental-accounting.md`
- 一句话：钱被分到不同的心理账户里，同样一笔钱在不同账户里花得不一样。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Thaler 1985, 1999. Tversky & Kahneman 1981: lost a $10 ticket: 46% buy another; lost a $10 bill: 88% buy the ticket. Thaler & Johnson 1990 house-money effect. Shefrin & Thaler: windfalls are spent more readily than salary.

### 小概率被放大（probability weighting）

- node: `judge.prospect.probability-weighting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-probability-weighting.md`
- 一句话：人同时买彩票和保险：很小的概率在心里被放大，接近确定的概率被打折。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Allais 1953 paradox; Kahneman & Tversky 1979 certainty effect: 80% of 4,000 vs 3,000 sure: 80% chose sure; fourfold pattern (Tversky & Kahneman 1992).

### 后悔：做了的和没做的

- node: `judge.prospect.regret-action-inaction` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-regret-action-inaction.md`
- 一句话：短期内更后悔做错的事，拉长到一生，更后悔没做的事。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Gilovich & Medvec 1994 JPSP, 1995 Psychological Review: in surveys of lifetime regrets about 75% were inactions (check). Morrison & Roese 2011 national sample: romance, family, education, career top domains.

### 机会成本忽视（opportunity cost neglect）

- node: `judge.prospect.opportunity-cost-neglect` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-opportunity-cost-neglect.md`
- 一句话：花钱花时间时，人很少自动想到「这些本来还能换什么」。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Frederick, Novemsky, Wang, Dhar, Nowlis 2009 Journal of Consumer Research: adding 'keep the $14.99 for other purchases' cut purchase of a DVD from 75% to 55%.

### 确认偏误（confirmation bias）

- node: `judge.justify.confirmation-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-confirmation-bias.md`
- 一句话：人找的、记的、信的，多是支持自己已有看法的证据。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Wason 1960 2-4-6 task: only about 21% found the rule on first announcement, most tested only confirming triples. Lord, Ross, Lepper 1979: both sides of the death-penalty debate became more extreme after reading the same mixed evidence. Nickerson 1998 review.

### 后见之明与结果偏差（hindsight bias, outcome bias）

- node: `judge.justify.hindsight-outcome` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-hindsight-outcome.md`
- 一句话：知道结果后，觉得当初就该料到，并按结果好坏评价当初的决定。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Fischhoff 1975 (Gurkha war outcomes); Fischhoff & Beyth 1975 Nixon's China trip. Baron & Hershey 1988 outcome bias. Poker players call it 'resulting' (Annie Duke 2018).

### 过度自信与校准（overconfidence, calibration）

- node: `judge.justify.overconfidence` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-overconfidence.md`
- 一句话：人说「九成把握」的事，实际对的比例低得多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Alpert & Raiffa 1982: 98% confidence intervals missed the true value about 40% of the time (check). Svenson 1981: 93% of US drivers in the sample rated themselves above the median. Moore & Healy 2008: overestimation, overplacement, overprecision are three different things. Weather forecasters are well calibrated because they get daily feedback (Murphy & Winkler 1977).

### 达克效应的真实版本（Dunning–Kruger effect）

- node: `judge.justify.dunning-kruger` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-dunning-kruger.md`
- 一句话：成绩最差的人高估自己最多，但他们并没有觉得自己比高手强；网上那条「愚昧之巅」曲线不在原论文里。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Kruger & Dunning 1999 JPSP: bottom quartile scored at the 12th percentile and estimated themselves at the 62nd. Part of the pattern is regression to the mean plus the better-than-average effect (Krueger & Mueller 2002; Nuhfer 2016; Gignac & Zajenkowski 2020).

### 计划谬误（planning fallacy）

- node: `judge.justify.planning-fallacy` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-planning-fallacy.md`
- 一句话：估自己的项目要多久，总按最顺利的情况估，哪怕以前次次超时。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Buehler, Griffin, Ross 1994 JPSP: psychology students predicted 33.9 days to finish the thesis, took 55.5; fewer than half finished by the date they were 99% sure of (check). Kahneman's curriculum team: estimated 2 years, the reference class said 7–10 years and 40% never finish; it took 8. Sydney Opera House: planned 1963 for A$7M, opened 1973 for A$102M.

### 解释深度错觉（illusion of explanatory depth）

- node: `judge.justify.explanatory-depth` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-explanatory-depth.md`
- 一句话：人以为自己懂拉链、马桶、政策是怎么回事，真要一步步讲出来才发现不懂。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Rozenblit & Keil 2002 Cognitive Science: self-rated understanding dropped after writing a step-by-step explanation. Fernbach, Rogers, Fox, Sloman 2013: explaining how a policy works made political positions more moderate.

### 认知失调（cognitive dissonance）

- node: `judge.justify.cognitive-dissonance` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-cognitive-dissonance.md`
- 一句话：行为和信念对不上时，人常常改信念来配合已经做了的事。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Festinger & Carlsmith 1959: paid $1 to call a boring task fun, people rated it more enjoyable than those paid $20. Festinger 1956 'When Prophecy Fails'. Aronson & Mills 1959 effort justification. Vaidis et al. 2024 (39 labs, N=4,898): attitudes shifted after writing a counter-attitudinal essay, but the classic choice manipulation made no difference.

### 偏见盲点（bias blind spot）

- node: `judge.justify.bias-blind-spot` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-bias-blind-spot.md`
- 一句话：人看得出别人的偏差，看不出自己的；聪明也不管用。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Pronin, Lin, Ross 2002. West, Meserve, Stanovich 2012 JPSP: higher cognitive ability did not reduce the blind spot, slightly the opposite. Consequence: knowing the names of biases is not protection; procedures are.

### 回归均值（regression to the mean）

- node: `judge.prob.regression-to-mean` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-regression-to-mean.md`
- 一句话：一次特别好或特别差的表现之后，下一次多半更接近平常水平，和你做了什么无关。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Galton 1886 heights. Kahneman's Israeli flight instructors: praise seemed to hurt and scolding to help. Sports Illustrated cover jinx; 'sophomore slump'.

### 幸存者偏差（survivorship bias）

- node: `judge.prob.survivorship` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-survivorship.md`
- 一句话：只看到通过筛选的样本，就会把筛选的结果当成成功的原因。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Abraham Wald, Statistical Research Group, 1943: armour where returning bombers had no holes (Mangel & Samaniego 1984 reconstruct his memos). Mutual funds: Elton, Gruber, Blake 1996: survivorship inflates average returns by about 0.9% a year. Business books that study only winners.

### 小数定律（law of small numbers）

- node: `judge.prob.small-numbers` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-small-numbers.md`
- 一句话：小样本更容易出极端结果；人却把小样本当成总体的缩影。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tversky & Kahneman 1971. Wainer 2007 'The Most Dangerous Equation': the best-scoring US schools were disproportionately small, and so were the worst; the Gates Foundation spent about $1.7B on small schools before backing off. Kidney-cancer rates lowest and highest in rural counties.

### 赌徒谬误与热手（gambler's fallacy, hot hand）

- node: `judge.prob.gamblers-hot-hand` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-gamblers-hot-hand.md`
- 一句话：独立事件没有记性；人却觉得连出几次黑就「该出红了」。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Monte Carlo casino, 18 Aug 1913: black 26 times in a row. Chen, Moskowitz, Shue 2016 QJE: asylum judges, loan officers and baseball umpires decide against the previous run. Hot hand: Gilovich, Vallone, Tversky 1985 said none; Miller & Sanjurjo 2018 Econometrica found a counting bias in that analysis; a modest hot hand exists.

### 选择偏差与伯克森悖论（Berkson's paradox）

- node: `judge.prob.selection-berkson` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-selection-berkson.md`
- 一句话：只在被筛选过的人群里看，两个本来无关的特征会显得此消彼长。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Berkson 1946 hospital patients. Ellenberg 2014: if you only date people who are attractive or nice, the two look negatively correlated. Admissions: among admitted students test scores and grades correlate negatively.

### 辛普森悖论（Simpson's paradox）

- node: `judge.prob.simpson` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-simpson.md`
- 一句话：每个分组里都成立的趋势，合在一起可以反过来。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Bickel, Hammel, O'Connell 1975 Science: Berkeley 1973 admissions: 44% of men vs 35% of women overall, yet most departments slightly favoured women; women applied to the harder departments. Kidney-stone treatments (Charig 1986).

### 期望值为正也会破产（凯利公式与遍历性）

- node: `judge.prob.ruin-ergodicity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-ruin-ergodicity.md`
- 一句话：一连串可以输光的下注里，押多少比押什么重要；平均值好看，不代表你这一条路走得到。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kelly 1956. Haghani & Dewey 2016: 61 finance students and young professionals, a coin with 60% heads, $25 stake, 30 minutes, capped at $250: 28% went bust and only 21% reached the cap; Kelly says bet 20% each time. Peters 2019 Nature Physics on ergodicity.

### 二八定律与幂律（Pareto principle, power law）

- node: `judge.prob.pareto-power-law` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-pareto-power-law.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：很多结果不是平均分布的：少数原因、少数人、少数作品占了大部分。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Pareto 1896: about 20% of Italians owned 80% of the land. Juran 1951 'vital few'. Microsoft 2002 (Ballmer memo): fixing the top 20% of bugs removed 80% of errors and crashes. Newman 2005 on power laws: city sizes, citations, book sales. The ratio is not always 80/20.

### 墨菲定律（Murphy's law）

- node: `judge.prob.murphy` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-murphy.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：「会出错的事总会出错」不是心理学定律；站得住的部分是：次数够多，小概率的事必然发生，而人只记得出事的那次。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Origin: Capt. Edward Murphy, Edwards Air Force Base 1949, rocket-sled tests with John Stapp; sensors wired backwards. Matthews 1995 European Journal of Physics: toast lands butter side down for physical reasons (table height, half rotation), Ig Nobel 1996. Engineering use: design so the wrong way is impossible (poka-yoke, Shingo).

### 外部视角（reference class forecasting）

- node: `judge.hygiene.outside-view` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-outside-view.md`
- 一句话：先问「同类的事一般结果如何」，再按这件事的特点微调。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman & Lovallo 1993; Flyvbjerg 2006: reference class forecasting, used by the UK Treasury and Danish government for infrastructure; his database of 16,000 projects: 8.5% on budget and on time, 0.5% also on benefits (How Big Things Get Done, 2023).

### 事前验尸（premortem）

- node: `judge.hygiene.premortem` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-premortem.md`
- 一句话：动手前假设项目已经失败，让每个人写下失败的原因。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Mitchell, Russo, Pennington 1989: imagining an event has already happened (prospective hindsight) raised the number of reasons generated by about 30%. Klein 2007 HBR 'Performing a Project Premortem'. Kahneman called it his favourite debiasing technique. Evidence for outcomes is thin.

### 超级预测者的习惯（superforecasting）

- node: `judge.hygiene.superforecasting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-superforecasting.md`
- 一句话：预测准的人不是更懂行，而是把问题拆开、用概率说话、经常小幅修正、事后对账。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Tetlock 2005: 284 experts, about 28,000 forecasts, barely better than chance; 'foxes' beat 'hedgehogs'. Good Judgment Project (IARPA ACE tournament 2011–2015): top 2% of forecasters beat the control by about 60% in year 1 and were reported to beat intelligence analysts with classified data (check). Mellers 2014: one hour of training improved accuracy about 10%.

### 噪声（noise）

- node: `judge.hygiene.noise` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-noise.md`
- 一句话：同一个案子交给不同的人，甚至同一个人换个时间，判出来差很多；这种随机差异和偏差一样伤。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman, Sibony, Sunstein 2021. Insurance underwriters: executives expected about 10% difference between two underwriters' premiums, the median was 55%. Sentencing: Clancy 1981 study of 208 federal judges on 16 identical cases. Fix: structured criteria, independent judgments averaged.

### 清单（checklist）

- node: `judge.hygiene.checklist` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-checklist.md`
- 一句话：把必做的几步写下来逐条核对，能挡住熟练的人因为熟练而漏掉的步骤。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Haynes et al. 2009 NEJM, WHO Surgical Safety Checklist in 8 hospitals: deaths 1.5% to 0.8%, complications 11.0% to 7.0%. Pronovost 2006 NEJM Michigan ICUs: central-line infections median to 0 over 18 months. Boeing B-17 1935 crash as the origin story. Urbach 2014 Ontario: mandated checklist without culture change showed no effect.

### 够好就行（satisficing vs maximizing）

- node: `judge.hygiene.satisficing` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-satisficing.md`
- 一句话：总想挑到最好的人，挑到的客观上略好，主观上更不满意。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Simon 1956. Schwartz et al. 2002 JPSP maximization scale: maximizers report less happiness, more regret. Iyengar, Wells, Schwartz 2006: maximizing graduates got 20% higher starting salaries and were less satisfied with them.

### 选择过载（choice overload）

- node: `judge.hygiene.choice-overload` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/judge/psy-judge-choice-overload.md`
- 一句话：选项太多有时让人干脆不选，但这个效应不稳定，取决于选项是否难比、人是否已有偏好。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Iyengar & Lepper 2000 jam study: 24 jams 3% bought, 6 jams 30%. Scheibehenne, Greifeneder, Todd 2010 meta (50 studies): mean effect about zero. Chernev, Böckenholt, Goodman 2015: appears with complex choices, hard-to-compare options, unclear preferences.
