# Group C: 44 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 自我损耗：意志力像肌肉会用完？

- node: `evidence.myth.ego-depletion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-ego-depletion.md`
- 一句话：「意志力是会耗尽的资源」在多实验室重做中效果接近零。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Baumeister 1998 radishes vs cookies (n=67). Hagger 2016 registered replication: 23 labs, N=2,141, d=0.04. Vohs 2021: 36 labs, N=3,531, d=0.06. Glucose claim (Gailliot 2007) also failed.

### 高能量姿势（power pose）

- node: `evidence.myth.power-pose` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-power-pose.md`
- 一句话：摆两分钟「强势姿势」改变激素和冒险行为的说法没有复现；只剩「自我感觉更有力」的小效应。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Carney, Cuddy, Yap 2010 (n=42). Ranehill 2015 (n=200): no effect on testosterone, cortisol or risk taking. Carney's 2016 public statement: 'I do not believe that power pose effects are real.' Cuddy's TED talk is among the most viewed.

### 棉花糖实验

- node: `evidence.myth.marshmallow` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-marshmallow.md`
- 一句话：4 岁能等 15 分钟不等于以后更成功：样本扩大并控制家庭背景后，关联缩小到原来的一小部分。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Mischel 1972; Shoda, Mischel, Peake 1990 follow-up had fewer than 100 children of Stanford faculty/staff. Watts, Duncan, Quan 2018 Psychological Science (n=918): association half the size, reduced by two thirds after controlling for family background and early cognition. Kidd, Palmeri, Aslin 2013: children waited 12 min with a reliable adult vs 3 min with an unreliable one.

### 一万小时定律

- node: `evidence.myth.ten-thousand-hours` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-ten-thousand-hours.md`
- 一句话：一万小时是一组小提琴手的平均数，不是门槛；练习量只解释一部分水平差异，领域越不规则解释得越少。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Ericsson, Krampe, Tesch-Römer 1993: best violinists averaged about 10,000 h by age 20, with wide spread. Gladwell 2008 turned it into a rule; Ericsson objected. Macnamara, Hambrick, Oswald 2014 meta (88 studies): deliberate practice explains 26% of variance in games, 21% music, 18% sports, 4% education, under 1% professions.

### 斯坦福监狱实验

- node: `evidence.myth.stanford-prison` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-stanford-prison.md`
- 一句话：「普通人一穿上制服就变残暴」不是实验自发出现的：看守被教过怎么做，重做没有得到同样结果。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Zimbardo 1971 (24 students, stopped after 6 days). Le Texier 2019 American Psychologist, from the archives: guards were briefed and coached. Reicher & Haslam 2006 BBC Prison Study: guards did not become tyrannical.

### 学习风格（视觉型、听觉型）

- node: `evidence.myth.learning-styles` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-learning-styles.md`
- 一句话：按「学习风格」配教法并不提高成绩；内容适合什么形式，比人属于什么类型重要。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Pashler, McDaniel, Rohrer, Bjork 2008 Psychological Science in the Public Interest: almost no study used the required crossover design, and those that did contradict the meshing hypothesis. Surveys: around 90% of teachers believe it (Dekker 2012; Newton 2020 review 89%).

### 沟通的 7-38-55 定律

- node: `evidence.myth.mehrabian` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-mehrabian.md`
- 一句话：「语言只占 7%」来自两项只测单个词语情绪色彩的小实验，不能推广到一般沟通。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Mehrabian & Wiener 1967; Mehrabian & Ferris 1967: single words spoken in conflicting tones; applies only to feelings and attitudes when words and tone conflict. Mehrabian's own website says the equation is misapplied.

### 马斯洛需求层次

- node: `evidence.myth.maslow` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-maslow.md`
- 一句话：需求确实分种类，但「先满足低层才追求高层」没有数据支持，金字塔图也不是马斯洛画的。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Maslow 1943 Psychological Review. Bridgman, Cummings, Ballard 2019: the pyramid came from management consultants in the 1960s. Tay & Diener 2011 JPSP (Gallup World Poll, 123 countries, n≈60,865): needs predict well-being largely independently of order. Wahba & Bridwell 1976 review.

### MBTI 与大五人格

- node: `evidence.myth.mbti` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-mbti.md`
- 一句话：MBTI 把连续的特质硬切成 16 型，重测常常换型；研究里用的是大五人格（Big Five）。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Pittenger 1993/2005: as many as 50% get a different type on retest within about five weeks; scores are normally distributed, not bimodal. Big Five (Costa & McCrae; Goldberg): conscientiousness predicts job performance (Barrick & Mount 1991 meta).

### 鸡汤定律：手表、木桶、鲶鱼、温水青蛙

- node: `evidence.myth.folk-laws` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/evidence/psy-evidence-folk-laws.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：中文管理读物里的很多「定律」是比喻或编出来的故事，没有研究；温水里的青蛙其实会跳走。
- 证据等级：C（卡片上写「证据 · 弱」）
- anchor: Boiling frog: Goltz 1869 frogs with brains removed stayed; modern biologists (Hutchison, University of Oklahoma; Zug, Smithsonian) say intact frogs jump out as water warms. Catfish effect: no documented Norwegian fishery practice. 手表定律 = Segal's law (a joke attributed to a 1930s newspaper). 木桶 = Liebig's law of the minimum, from agricultural chemistry (Sprengel 1828, Liebig 1840), an analogy when applied to people.

### 提取练习（testing effect）

- node: `learn.memory.testing-effect` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-testing-effect.md`
- 一句话：合上书回忆一遍，比再读一遍记得久得多，尽管当时感觉更差。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Roediger & Karpicke 2006 Psychological Science: after one week, study-study-study-study group recalled 40%, study-test-test-test 61%; the rereaders had predicted the opposite. Karpicke & Blunt 2011 Science: retrieval practice beat concept mapping. Dunlosky et al. 2013: practice testing rated high utility.

### 遗忘曲线（forgetting curve）

- node: `learn.memory.forgetting-curve` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-forgetting-curve.md`
- 一句话：新学的东西头一天忘得最快，之后变慢；没有意义联系的材料忘得尤其快。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Ebbinghaus 1885, nonsense syllables, savings: 58% after 20 minutes, 44% after 1 hour, 34% after a day, 21% after 31 days. Murre & Dros 2015 PLoS ONE replicated the curve. Meaningful, connected material is forgotten far more slowly.

### 间隔效应（spacing effect）

- node: `learn.memory.spacing` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-spacing.md`
- 一句话：同样的总时间，分开几次学比一次学完记得牢；间隔拉到快忘了再复习。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Cepeda et al. 2006 meta (317 experiments). Cepeda et al. 2008: the best gap is roughly 10–20% of the time you need to remember it for. Bahrick et al. 1993: 13 sessions 56 days apart matched 26 sessions 14 days apart for foreign vocabulary after years.

### 交错练习（interleaving）

- node: `learn.memory.interleaving` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-interleaving.md`
- 一句话：几类题混着练，比一类练完再练下一类考得好，因为你被迫先判断「这是哪一类」。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Rohrer & Taylor 2007: geometry volumes, a week later 63% (interleaved) vs 20% (blocked), though blocked did better during practice. Kornell & Bjork 2008: painters' styles; 78% of learners still believed blocking worked better. Brunmair & Richter 2019 meta g=0.42.

### 流畅错觉与合意困难（illusion of fluency, desirable difficulties）

- node: `learn.memory.fluency-illusion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-fluency-illusion.md`
- 一句话：重读、划线、看答案让材料变眼熟，眼熟被误当成会了。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Bjork 1994 desirable difficulties. Dunlosky, Rawson, Marsh, Nathan, Willingham 2013: rereading, highlighting, summarising rated low utility. Koriat & Bjork 2005 foresight bias: with the answer in view people overestimate later recall.

### 生成效应（generation effect）

- node: `learn.memory.generation-effect` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-generation-effect.md`
- 一句话：自己想出来、写出来的内容，比读现成的记得牢。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Slamecka & Graf 1978: generating a word from a cue (rapid–f___) beat reading the pair. Bertsch, Pesta, Wiscott, McDaniel 2007 meta (86 studies): d=0.40. Kornell, Hays, Bjork 2009: even a failed attempt before seeing the answer improves later memory.

### 把思考外包给 AI（cognitive offloading）

- node: `learn.memory.ai-offloading` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-ai-offloading.md`
- 一句话：用 AI 直接要答案，当场做得更好，脱离 AI 后比没用过的人差；让它提示而不是代做，就没有这个代价。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Bastani et al. 2025 PNAS 'Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning' (about 1,000 Turkish high-school maths students): plain GPT-4 raised practice scores 48% but exam scores fell 17% vs no AI; a tutor version with hints raised practice 127% with no exam penalty. Risko & Gilbert 2016 cognitive offloading. Sparrow, Liu, Wegner 2011 'Google effect' (part failed to replicate). MIT 'Your Brain on ChatGPT' 2025 is a small preprint (n=54): mention only as weak. Check everything here against the papers.

### 自我解释（self-explanation）

- node: `learn.memory.self-explanation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-self-explanation.md`
- 一句话：学一步就问自己「这一步为什么成立」，比只看懂步骤学得深。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Chi, Bassok, Lewis, Reimann, Glaser 1989: good students explained worked physics examples to themselves far more. Chi et al. 1994: prompted eighth-graders gained 32% vs 22%. Bisra et al. 2018 meta (64 studies): g=0.55.

### 以教为学（learning by teaching）

- node: `learn.memory.learning-by-teaching` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-learning-by-teaching.md`
- 一句话：准备讲给别人听、真的不看稿讲一遍，学得更好，起作用的是提取和组织。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Nestojko, Bui, Kornell, Bjork 2014: expecting to teach improved recall and organisation. Fiorella & Mayer 2013. Koh, Lee, Lim 2018: teaching without notes beat teaching from a script, as much as retrieval practice did.

### 怎么考就怎么练（transfer-appropriate processing）

- node: `learn.memory.transfer-appropriate` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-transfer-appropriate.md`
- 一句话：记忆在和练习时相似的条件下最容易取出来；练的形式要像将来用的形式。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Morris, Bransford, Franks 1977. Godden & Baddeley 1975: divers recalled about 40% more words when learning and recall were both on land or both under water. Implication: reading solutions prepares you to recognise solutions; a whiteboard interview needs spoken, timed production.

### 刻意练习（deliberate practice）

- node: `learn.skill.deliberate-practice` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-deliberate-practice.md`
- 一句话：让人变强的不是做的时间，是盯着一个具体弱点、马上得到反馈、在够不着的边上重复。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Ericsson, Krampe, Tesch-Römer 1993: the best violinists did more solitary practice, not more playing. Components: a specific goal, full attention, immediate feedback, the edge of ability, ideally a coach. Macnamara 2014: explains much in chess and music, little where the rules are unstable. Ericsson & Pool 2016.

### 迁移（transfer of learning）

- node: `learn.skill.transfer` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-transfer.md`
- 一句话：学会一件事，不会自动用到表面不一样的另一件事上；要自己把共同的结构说出来。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gick & Holyoak 1980: Duncker's radiation problem; about 10% solved it cold, about 30% after reading an analogous story, about 75–90% once told to use the story (check). Gick & Holyoak 1983: comparing two analogues builds the schema. Sala & Gobet 2017: chess, music and working-memory training show near-zero far transfer. Barnett & Ceci 2002.

### 反馈会帮倒忙（feedback interventions）

- node: `learn.skill.feedback` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-feedback.md`
- 一句话：反馈平均有用，但约三分之一的情况让表现变差：当它指向「你这个人」而不是任务时。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kluger & DeNisi 1996 Psychological Bulletin meta (607 effect sizes, 23,663 observations): average d=0.41, but over one third of effects were negative; feedback that draws attention to the self hurts. Hattie & Timperley 2007: task- and process-level feedback works, praise of the person does not.

### 专家直觉什么时候可信（expert intuition）

- node: `learn.skill.expert-intuition` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-expert-intuition.md`
- 一句话：直觉是对见过的模式的识别；只有规律稳定、反馈又快又明确的领域，直觉才值得信。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Chase & Simon 1973: chess masters recalled real positions far better than novices, random positions no better. Klein 1998 fireground commanders. Kahneman & Klein 2009 American Psychologist 'A Failure to Disagree': skilled intuition needs a regular environment and prolonged practice with feedback; stock pickers and long-range political forecasters lack both.

### 样例效应与专长反转（worked example effect）

- node: `learn.skill.worked-example` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-worked-example.md`
- 一句话：新手先看带讲解的例题，比硬解学得快；有底子之后反过来，自己解才有长进。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Sweller & Cooper 1985 algebra: studying worked examples beat solving the same problems, in less time. Kalyuga, Ayres, Chandler, Sweller 2003 expertise reversal effect. Renkl: fading, removing one solution step at a time. Kapur 2008 productive failure is the contrasting case.

### 成长型思维：真的，但很小（growth mindset）

- node: `learn.skill.growth-mindset` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-growth-mindset.md`
- 一句话：相信能力可以练出来有一点帮助，主要对成绩差、处境不利的学生；它不是万能钥匙。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Dweck. Mueller & Dweck 1998 praise for effort vs intelligence. Yeager et al. 2019 Nature (n=12,490 US ninth-graders): a one-hour online intervention raised GPA 0.10 points among lower-achieving students. Sisk et al. 2018 meta: interventions d=0.08. Macnamara & Burgoyne 2023 meta: effects near zero in the best-designed studies.

### 坚毅（grit）

- node: `learn.skill.grit` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-grit.md`
- 一句话：「坚毅」和成就的相关不高，而且和大五里的尽责性几乎是一回事；有用的是其中「坚持努力」那一半。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Duckworth, Peterson, Matthews, Kelly 2007 (West Point, spelling bee). Credé, Tynan, Harms 2017 JPSP meta (88 samples, n=66,807): grit–performance ρ=0.18; grit–conscientiousness ρ=0.84; perseverance of effort predicts better than consistency of interest.

### 等概率法则（equal-odds rule）

- node: `learn.create.equal-odds` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-equal-odds.md`
- 一句话：一个人最好的作品落在他哪一件作品上近乎随机，所以产出越多，出好东西的机会越多。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Simonton 1997 Psychological Review. Sinatra, Wang, Deville, Song, Barabási 2016 Science 'random impact rule': a scientist's highest-impact paper is equally likely to be any paper in the sequence; what differs between people is a stable factor Q. Picasso produced an estimated 20,000+ works.

### 连胜期（hot streak）

- node: `learn.create.hot-streak` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/learn/psy-learn-hot-streak.md`
- 一句话：多数人的职业生涯里有一段成果扎堆的时期，它可以出现在任何年龄，往往跟在一段广泛尝试之后。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Liu, Wang, Sinatra, Giles, Song, Wang 2018 Nature: artists, film directors, scientists; about 90% have at least one hot streak, lasting around 4–5 years, timing random within the career. Liu et al. 2021 Nature Communications: exploration followed by exploitation tends to precede it.

### 出成果的年龄（age and achievement）

- node: `career.path.founder-age` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-founder-age.md`
- 一句话：最成功的创业者创业时平均四十出头；多数领域的高峰比「三十岁前」的说法晚得多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Azoulay, Jones, Kim, Miranda 2020 AER: Insights (2.7 million US founders, Census data): mean age at founding 41.9; for the top 0.1% fastest-growing firms 45.0; a 50-year-old founder is 1.8 times as likely as a 30-year-old to build a top-growth firm. Jones 2010: age at Nobel-winning work has risen, now mostly late 30s to 40s.

### 跳槽与留任的工资增长

- node: `career.path.job-switching` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-job-switching.md`
- 一句话：职业早期的工资增长里，大约三分之一来自换雇主；换工作的人涨薪中位数长期高于留任的人。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Topel & Ward 1992 QJE: a typical US man holds seven jobs in his first ten years, and job changes account for about a third of early-career wage growth. Federal Reserve Bank of Atlanta Wage Growth Tracker: job switchers' median wage growth runs above job stayers' in most years (look up the current gap and the 2022 peak).

### 犹豫不决时偏向改变

- node: `career.path.coin-flip` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-coin-flip.md`
- 一句话：在重大决定上拿不定主意的人，最后选了改变的，半年后比没改的更快乐。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Levitt 2021 Review of Economic Studies 'Heads or Tails': about 20,000 people undecided about a major change (quit a job, end a relationship) flipped a coin on his website; those told by the coin to change were more likely to do it, and those who changed reported being substantially happier at two and six months. Self-selected sample, self-reports.

### 探索与利用（explore–exploit, 37% rule）

- node: `career.path.explore-exploit` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-explore-exploit.md`
- 一句话：选择还多、时间还长的时候多试；试到一定数量之后，遇到比之前都好的就定下来。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Secretary problem: with n candidates seen in sequence, pass on the first 37% (1/e), then take the first one better than all so far; picks the best 37% of the time (Gardner 1960; Ferguson 1989). In experiments people stop too early (Seale & Rapoport 1997). Gittins index: exploring is worth more the longer the horizon. Liu et al. 2021: hot streaks follow exploration then exploitation.

### 彼得原理（Peter principle）

- node: `career.path.peter-principle` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-peter-principle.md`
- 一句话：按当前岗位的业绩提拔人，会把最好的执行者变成不称职的管理者。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Peter & Hull 1969, written as satire. Benson, Li, Shue 2019 QJE (53,035 sales workers, 214 US firms): doubling a salesperson's sales raised the chance of promotion about 15%, and predicted a 7.5% drop in their new subordinates' sales; firms promoted the best sellers, not the best future managers.

### 做得好不等于被看见（visibility）

- node: `career.perform.visibility` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-visibility.md`
- 一句话：同样的产出，被看见的人晋升更快；远程办公的人业绩更高，晋升却更少。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Bloom, Liang, Roberts, Ying 2015 QJE (Ctrip, 249 call-centre staff randomised): home workers were 13% more productive, yet their promotion rate conditional on performance fell by about half. Ng, Eby, Sorensen, Feldman 2005 meta: career sponsorship and political knowledge predict salary and promotion alongside human capital. Elsbach, Cable, Sherman 2010 'passive face time'.

### 绩效评分里有多少是评分人（idiosyncratic rater effect）

- node: `career.perform.rater-bias` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-rater-bias.md`
- 一句话：一份绩效评分的差异，一多半来自打分的人自己的习惯，只有约五分之一来自被评的人的实际表现。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Scullen, Mount, Goff 2000 Journal of Applied Psychology (4,492 managers rated by bosses, peers, subordinates): 62% of rating variance was idiosyncratic rater effects, 21% actual performance (check). Mount et al. 1998. Buckingham & Goodall 2015 HBR on Deloitte's redesign.

### 面试到底测到什么（selection validity）

- node: `career.perform.interview-validity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-interview-validity.md`
- 一句话：随意聊的面试预测工作表现的能力很差；有固定问题和评分标准的结构化面试、实际工作样本好得多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Schmidt & Hunter 1998 Psychological Bulletin (85 years of research): work samples 0.54, general mental ability 0.51, structured interviews 0.51, unstructured 0.38, years of experience 0.18. Sackett, Zhang, Berry, Lievens 2022 revision: structured interviews 0.42 at the top, ability lowered to 0.31, unstructured 0.19. Dana, Dawes, Peterson 2013: unstructured interviews can make predictions worse.

### 自信被当成能力（overconfidence and status）

- node: `career.perform.confidence-status` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-confidence-status.md`
- 一句话：表现得比实际更有把握的人，会被同伴当成更有能力，得到更高的地位，被拆穿后也没怎么掉。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Anderson, Brion, Moore, Kennedy 2012 JPSP (six studies): overconfident people were rated more competent and gained status in groups. Kennedy, Anderson, Moore 2013: status held even after real performance was revealed. Chamorro-Premuzic 2013 argues this is why incompetent leaders get picked.

### 导师与保荐人（mentoring, sponsorship）

- node: `career.perform.sponsor` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-sponsor.md`
- 一句话：有人指点对薪水和晋升的帮助是真的但不大；有人在你不在场的会议上替你说话，作用更直接。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Allen, Eby, Poteet, Lentz, Lima 2004 meta: mentored people have slightly higher pay and more promotions; small effects. Eby et al. 2008 meta. Ng et al. 2005: sponsorship r about 0.2 with salary. Ibarra, Carter, Silva 2010 HBR: women are over-mentored and under-sponsored (survey).

### 帕金森定律（Parkinson's law）

- node: `career.org.parkinson` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-parkinson.md`
- 一句话：工作会膨胀到占满给它的时间；它是一篇讽刺文章，但「时间越宽裕做得越慢」有实验支持。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Parkinson 1955 in The Economist: British Admiralty officials rose 78% between 1914 and 1928 while ships in commission fell 67%. Aronson & Gerard 1966; Bryan & Locke 1967: people given more time than needed took longer and then kept the slower pace.

### 倦怠（burnout）

- node: `career.work.burnout` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-burnout.md`
- 一句话：倦怠有三个部分：耗竭、对工作变冷漠、觉得自己没用；它主要由工作条件造成，不是个人脆弱。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Maslach & Jackson 1981 inventory; Maslach, Schaufeli, Leiter 2001 Annual Review. Six mismatches: workload, control, reward, community, fairness, values. Demerouti et al. 2001 job demands–resources model. WHO ICD-11 (2019): an occupational phenomenon, not a medical condition. Panagioti et al. 2017 meta: organisation-level changes help more than individual ones.

### 注意力残留与切换成本（attention residue）

- node: `career.work.attention-residue` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-attention-residue.md`
- 一句话：从一件没做完的事切到另一件，脑子有一部分还留在前一件上。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Leroy 2009 Organizational Behavior and Human Decision Processes. Rubinstein, Meyer, Evans 2001: switching costs grow with task complexity. Mark, Gudith, Klocke 2008: interrupted workers finished faster but with more stress and frustration. Mark, González, Harris 2005: about 23 minutes to return to an interrupted task (field observation).

### 长工时的产出封顶

- node: `career.work.overwork` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-overwork.md`
- 一句话：每周工作超过约 50 小时后，每多一小时的产出急剧下降；70 小时做出来的几乎不比 55 小时多。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Pencavel 2015 Economic Journal: British munitions workers in the First World War; output rose proportionally up to about 49 hours a week, then flattened; output at 70 hours was barely above 56. Kivimäki et al. 2015 Lancet meta (603,838 people): 55+ hours a week, 33% higher stroke risk.

### 工作重塑（job crafting）

- node: `career.work.job-crafting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/career/psy-career-job-crafting.md`
- 一句话：不换岗位，也能自己调整任务的范围、和谁打交道、怎么理解这份工作，让它更合自己。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Wrzesniewski & Dutton 2001: hospital cleaners who saw themselves as part of patient care did extra tasks and found the work meaningful. Wrzesniewski et al. 1997 job, career, calling. Rudolph, Katz, Lavigne, Zacher 2017 meta (122 samples): crafting linked to engagement and performance. Oprea et al. 2019 meta of interventions: small effects.
