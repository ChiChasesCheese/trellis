# Group D: 39 cards

One card per entry, in this order. The anchor is a starting point from memory: check every number against the paper or a reliable summary before it goes on a card.

### 基本归因错误（fundamental attribution error）

- node: `social.perceive.attribution-error` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-attribution-error.md`
- 一句话：解释别人的行为时，高估性格、低估处境。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Jones & Harris 1967: essays for or against Castro; readers inferred the writer's real view even when told the side was assigned. Ross, Amabile, Steinmetz 1977 quiz game: questioners were rated more knowledgeable. Darley & Batson 1973 Good Samaritan: seminarians in a hurry helped 10%, unhurried 63%. Weaker in East Asian samples (Morris & Peng 1994).

### 光环效应（halo effect）

- node: `social.perceive.halo` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-halo.md`
- 一句话：一个突出的优点（长相、名校、口才）会把对这个人其他方面的评价一起抬高。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Thorndike 1920 officer ratings. Nisbett & Wilson 1977: the same lecturer, warm or cold; students rated his accent and looks differently and denied the influence. Landy & Sigall 1974 essay grading with author photos. Hamermesh & Biddle 1994: beauty premium in earnings of roughly 5–10%.

### 第一印象与薄片判断（thin slices）

- node: `social.perceive.first-impression` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-first-impression.md`
- 一句话：人在零点几秒内形成印象，之后的信息多用来确认它。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Willis & Todorov 2006: judgments after 100 ms correlate highly with unhurried ones. Ambady & Rosenthal 1993: ratings of 30-second silent clips of teachers correlated 0.76 with end-of-term evaluations. Todorov et al. 2005 Science: competence judged from faces predicted about 70% of US Senate races. Fast is not the same as accurate.

### 温暖与能力（warmth–competence）

- node: `social.perceive.warmth-competence` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-warmth-competence.md`
- 一句话：人评价他人主要看两条：对我有没有善意，有没有本事；善意先被判断。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Fiske, Cuddy, Glick, Xu 2002 stereotype content model; Fiske, Cuddy, Glick 2007 Trends in Cognitive Sciences: the two dimensions account for most of the variance in impressions; warmth is judged first and weighs more.

### 聚光灯效应（spotlight effect）

- node: `social.perceive.spotlight` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-spotlight.md`
- 一句话：人高估别人注意到自己的程度，大约高估一倍。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gilovich, Medvec, Savitsky 2000 JPSP: students wearing a Barry Manilow T-shirt guessed about half the room would notice; about a quarter did (check: 46% vs 23%).

### 透明度错觉（illusion of transparency）

- node: `social.perceive.transparency-illusion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-transparency-illusion.md`
- 一句话：人以为自己的紧张、说谎、不高兴别人都看得出来，其实漏出去的很少。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gilovich, Savitsky, Medvec 1998. Savitsky & Gilovich 2003: speakers told about the illusion gave speeches rated better by themselves and by observers.

### 喜欢差距（liking gap）

- node: `social.perceive.liking-gap` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-liking-gap.md`
- 一句话：聊完天，人普遍低估对方喜欢自己的程度。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Boothby, Cooney, Sandstrom, Clark 2018 Psychological Science: after conversations of any length strangers underestimated how much the other liked them; among dorm suitemates the gap lasted most of the year. Wolf et al. 2021 found it in work teams.

### 期望效应（Pygmalion effect）

- node: `social.perceive.pygmalion` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-pygmalion.md`
- 一句话：别人对你的期望会通过对待你的方式变成一部分现实，但效果比传说的小得多。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Rosenthal & Jacobson 1968 Oak School: 'bloomers' picked at random; first-graders gained 27 IQ points vs 12. Raudenbush 1984 meta: effect near zero once teachers had known pupils for more than two weeks. Jussim & Harber 2005 review: real, small (r about 0.1–0.2), larger for stigmatised groups. Eden's military studies.

### 曝光效应（mere exposure effect）

- node: `social.connect.mere-exposure` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-mere-exposure.md`
- 一句话：见得越多越有好感，哪怕没有任何交流。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Zajonc 1968. Moreland & Beach 1992: four women attended a lecture course 0, 5, 10 or 15 times without speaking; students liked the most-seen one best. Bornstein 1989 meta: r=0.26. Reverses if the first reaction is dislike; saturates.

### 邻近效应（propinquity effect）

- node: `social.connect.propinquity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-propinquity.md`
- 一句话：物理距离是友谊最强的预测因素之一：隔几个门，交朋友的概率就掉一大截。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Festinger, Schachter, Back 1950, Westgate housing at MIT: 41% of next-door neighbours were named as close friends, 22% two doors away, 10% at the end of the hall. Segal 1974: police trainees befriended those next in alphabetical order.

### 自我暴露（self-disclosure）

- node: `social.connect.self-disclosure` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-self-disclosure.md`
- 一句话：亲近感来自轮流、逐步加深地说自己的事，不是来自共处的时间长短。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Aron, Melinat, Aron, Vallone, Bator 1997: 36 escalating questions in 45 minutes produced more closeness than small talk. Collins & Miller 1994 meta: people like those who disclose to them and like those they disclose to.

### 人低估和陌生人说话的好处（undersociality）

- node: `social.connect.undersociality` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-undersociality.md`
- 一句话：人预计和陌生人聊天会尴尬，实际更愉快；深一点的话题也比预想的顺。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Epley & Schroeder 2014 JEP: General: Chicago commuters told to talk to a stranger had the most pleasant commute; they had predicted the opposite. Sandstrom & Dunn 2014: more weak-tie chats, more belonging that day. Kardas, Kumar, Epley 2022: deep conversations felt less awkward than expected. Sandstrom, Boothby, Cooney 2022: a week of daily conversations with strangers cut fear of rejection.

### 人低估别人答应的概率

- node: `social.connect.asking-help` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-asking-help.md`
- 一句话：开口求助、要内推、请人帮忙，被答应的概率大约是自己估计的两倍。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Flynn & Lake 2008 JPSP: people estimated they would need to ask about 20 strangers to get 5 to fill in a questionnaire; they needed about 10. Bohns 2016 review across 14,000 requests. Helpers also see the asker as more competent (Brooks, Gino, Schweitzer 2015 on advice seeking).

### 孤独的自我强化循环（loneliness loop）

- node: `social.connect.loneliness-loop` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-loneliness-loop.md`
- 一句话：孤独让人对社交威胁更敏感，更容易把中性信号读成拒绝，于是更退缩。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Cacioppo & Hawkley 2009 Trends in Cognitive Sciences. Holt-Lunstad, Smith, Layton 2010 PLoS Medicine meta (148 studies, n=308,849): stronger social relationships, 50% higher odds of survival, comparable to quitting smoking. Masi, Chen, Hawkley, Cacioppo 2011 meta: interventions that correct maladaptive social thinking worked best, better than social-skills training or simply more contact.

### 交朋友需要的小时数

- node: `social.connect.friendship-hours` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-friendship-hours.md`
- 一句话：从认识到普通朋友约 50 小时，到朋友约 90 小时，到好朋友要 200 小时以上，而且得是一起闲着的时间。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Hall 2019 Journal of Social and Personal Relationships (two studies: adults who had moved; first-year students). Time at work or in class counted for little; leisure time together counted.

### 怎么回应别人的好消息（capitalization）

- node: `social.connect.capitalization` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-capitalization.md`
- 一句话：对方分享好事时，热情地追问细节，比对方倒霉时安慰他更能预测关系好坏。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Gable, Reis, Impett, Asher 2004 JPSP. Gable, Gonzaga, Strachman 2006: only active-constructive responses were linked to satisfaction; passive or deflating ones predicted breakup two months later.

### 成人依恋（adult attachment）

- node: `social.connect.attachment` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-attachment.md`
- 一句话：人在亲密关系里有稳定的倾向：安全、焦虑（怕被丢下）或回避（怕靠太近）；它会变，不是判决。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Bowlby; Ainsworth 1978 Strange Situation. Hazan & Shaver 1987: 56% secure, 25% avoidant, 19% anxious. Fraley 2002 meta: moderate stability from infancy to adulthood (r about 0.39). Fraley & Roisman 2019: early experience predicts weakly. Styles are dimensions, not boxes.

### 关系里的四种危险信号（Gottman's four horsemen）

- node: `social.connect.gottman` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-gottman.md`
- 一句话：批评人格、蔑视、辩解、冷战这四种互动方式预示关系变坏，蔑视最毒；「预测准确率九成」则是高估。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Gottman & Levenson 1992; Gottman 1994. The 90%+ divorce prediction came from fitting a model to couples whose outcomes were known, without validation on new couples (Heyman & Slep 2001). Stable couples show about 5 positive interactions per negative during conflict.

### 嘴上的择偶标准不预测心动

- node: `social.connect.stated-preferences` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-stated-preferences.md`
- 一句话：人说自己看重什么条件，和面对面时被谁吸引，几乎对不上。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Eastwick & Finkel 2008 JPSP speed dating: stated preferences for looks or earning power did not predict whom people actually liked. Joel, Eastwick, Finkel 2017 Psychological Science: machine learning on 100+ self-report measures could not predict which specific pair would click. Eastwick et al. 2014 meta.

### 互惠（reciprocity）

- node: `social.influence.reciprocity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-reciprocity.md`
- 一句话：先收到好处的人会觉得欠着，回报常常比收到的大。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Regan 1971: a confederate who bought the subject a Coke sold twice as many raffle tickets. Strohmetz et al. 2002: mints with the bill raised tips, most when given personally. Falk 2007 Econometrica: 10,000 charity letters; a small gift raised donations 17%, a large one 75%.

### 登门槛与留面子（foot-in-the-door, door-in-the-face）

- node: `social.influence.sequential-requests` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-sequential-requests.md`
- 一句话：先让人答应一个小请求，或先提一个会被拒绝的大请求，后面的真请求更容易成。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Freedman & Fraser 1966: 17% agreed to a large 'Drive Carefully' sign on their lawn; 76% of those who had earlier accepted a small sticker. Cialdini et al. 1975: 17% agreed to chaperone juvenile offenders at the zoo; 50% after first refusing a two-year commitment. Meta-analyses: both real and modest (r about 0.15).

### 社会认同（social proof）

- node: `social.influence.social-proof` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-social-proof.md`
- 一句话：不确定该怎么做时，人看别人怎么做，尤其是和自己处境相似的人。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Goldstein, Cialdini, Griskevicius 2008: towel-reuse signs: environmental appeal 35%, 'most guests reuse' 44%, 'most guests in this room' 49%. Allcott 2011: Opower home energy reports comparing households to neighbours cut use about 2%. Milgram, Bickman, Berkowitz 1969: a crowd of 5 looking up made 80% of passers-by look up.

### 稀缺（scarcity）

- node: `social.influence.scarcity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-scarcity.md`
- 一句话：「快没了」「只剩今天」让东西显得更值钱，也让人来不及想。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Worchel, Lee, Adewole 1975: identical cookies rated more desirable from a jar of 2 than a jar of 10, most when the jar had just been depleted. Cialdini 1984. Booking sites' 'only 1 room left'. Barton, Zlatevska, Oppewal 2022 meta.

### 真相错觉（illusory truth effect）

- node: `social.influence.illusory-truth` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-illusory-truth.md`
- 一句话：一句话听得多了就更像真的，即使你本来知道它是错的。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Hasher, Goldstein, Toppino 1977. Fazio, Brashier, Payne, Marsh 2015: repetition raised belief even for statements people knew to be false. Pennycook, Cannon, Rand 2018: one prior exposure made fake-news headlines seem more accurate.

### 知识的诅咒（curse of knowledge）

- node: `social.talk.curse-of-knowledge` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-curse-of-knowledge.md`
- 一句话：一旦懂了某件事，就很难想象不懂的人听到的是什么。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Newton 1990 Stanford dissertation: tappers predicted listeners would name 50% of songs; they got 3 of 120 (2.5%). Camerer, Loewenstein, Weber 1989 named it. Kruger, Epley, Parker, Ng 2005: email writers expected sarcasm to be read correctly 78% of the time; it was 56%.

### 心理抗拒（reactance）

- node: `social.talk.reactance` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-reactance.md`
- 一句话：感到选择被人拿走时，人会偏要选被禁止的那个。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Brehm 1966. Pennebaker & Sanders 1976: restroom signs 'Do NOT write on these walls under any circumstances' drew more graffiti than 'Please don't write on these walls'. Rains 2013 meta. Motivational interviewing avoids it by asking for the person's own reasons (Miller & Rollnick).

### 换位思考不如直接问（perspective getting）

- node: `social.talk.perspective-getting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/social/psy-social-perspective-getting.md`
- 一句话：努力想象对方的想法并不让你猜得更准，只让你更自信；问出来才准。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Eyal, Steffel, Epley 2018 JPSP, 25 experiments: perspective taking did not improve accuracy at predicting others' thoughts, feelings or preferences; asking them did. Married couples overestimated how well they knew their partner.

### 享乐适应（hedonic adaptation）

- node: `life.happy.hedonic-adaptation` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-hedonic-adaptation.md`
- 一句话：升职、加薪、买房带来的高兴会退回到原来的水平；有几样坏事（失业、长期通勤、噪音）却适应不了。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Brickman, Coates, Janoff-Bulman 1978: 22 lottery winners were not happier than controls; 29 accident victims were less happy but above the midpoint. Small samples. Lucas, Clark, Georgellis, Diener 2003 (German panel, n=24,000): the marriage boost fades in about two years. Lucas 2007: unemployment and disability leave lasting drops. Stutzer & Frey 2008: people do not adapt to commuting.

### 情感预测偏差（affective forecasting, impact bias）

- node: `life.happy.affective-forecasting` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-affective-forecasting.md`
- 一句话：人高估未来的好事坏事会让自己高兴或难受多久，因为只盯着那一件事，也低估了自己的恢复力。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Gilbert, Pinel, Wilson, Blumberg, Wheatley 1998 JPSP: assistant professors expected tenure decisions to shape their happiness for years; those denied were about as happy as those granted. Wilson & Gilbert 2005. Schkade & Kahneman 1998: Midwesterners and Californians both thought Californians were happier; they were not.

### 钱和幸福（income and well-being）

- node: `life.happy.income-happiness` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-income-happiness.md`
- 一句话：收入越高，日常情绪平均越好，没有封顶；只有本来就最不快乐的那一小部分人，过了约十万美元后钱不再有用。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman & Deaton 2010 PNAS: emotional well-being flat above about $75,000. Killingsworth 2021 PNAS (33,391 employed US adults, 1.7 million experience samples): no plateau. Killingsworth, Kahneman, Mellers 2023 PNAS adversarial collaboration: plateau only for the unhappiest 15–20%, at about $100,000; for the rest it keeps rising with log income.

### 峰终定律（peak–end rule）

- node: `life.happy.peak-end` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-peak-end.md` · tags: [十日终焉]
- 一句话：人对一段经历的记忆由最强烈的一刻和结尾决定，和持续了多久几乎无关。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Kahneman, Fredrickson, Schreiber, Redelmeier 1993: one hand in 14°C water for 60 s; the other for 60 s plus 30 s at 15°C; 69% chose to repeat the longer one. Redelmeier & Kahneman 1996 colonoscopy. Redelmeier, Katz, Kahneman 2003 (682 patients randomised): leaving the scope in for a milder final minute improved remembered pain and return rates.

### 关系是晚年健康最强的预测因素

- node: `life.happy.relationships-health` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-relationships-health.md`
- 一句话：跟踪八十多年的研究里，50 岁时对关系的满意度，比胆固醇更能预测 80 岁时的健康。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Harvard Study of Adult Development, begun 1938 (268 Harvard sophomores plus 456 Boston inner-city boys); Vaillant 2012 'Triumphs of Experience'; Waldinger & Schulz 2023 'The Good Life'. Holt-Lunstad 2010 meta: 50% higher odds of survival with stronger relationships. Observational: healthier people may also keep relationships more easily.

### 花钱买时间（time vs money）

- node: `life.happy.time-vs-money` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-time-vs-money.md`
- 一句话：把钱花在省掉讨厌的杂事上的人更满意；多数人有钱也不这么花。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Whillans, Dunn, Smeets, Bekkers, Norton 2017 PNAS (n=6,271 in four countries): buying time linked to higher life satisfaction at every income level; in an experiment, $40 spent on time-saving beat $40 spent on a material purchase. Whillans, Weidman, Dunn 2016: people who value time over money are happier.

### 睡眠不足而不自知（sleep restriction）

- node: `life.health.sleep-debt` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-sleep-debt.md`
- 一句话：连续两周每晚只睡六小时，反应和注意力掉到相当于两夜没睡，自己却只觉得「有点困」。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Van Dongen, Maislin, Mullington, Dinges 2003 Sleep (48 adults, 14 days): six hours a night produced deficits equal to two nights of total sleep deprivation on the psychomotor vigilance task; four hours, three nights; self-rated sleepiness levelled off after a few days. Belenky et al. 2003. Williamson & Feyer 2000: 17–19 hours awake is like a blood alcohol level of 0.05%.

### 运动对抑郁的效果（exercise and depression）

- node: `life.health.exercise-mood` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-exercise-mood.md`
- 一句话：走路、慢跑、力量训练、瑜伽对抑郁的效果，和心理治疗、药物在同一个量级，强度大一点效果更好。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Noetel et al. 2024 BMJ network meta-analysis (218 trials, 14,170 participants): walking or jogging g=-0.62, yoga -0.55, strength training -0.49; effects grew with intensity. Singh et al. 2023 British Journal of Sports Medicine umbrella review (97 reviews). Many trials are small and have a risk of bias; the direction is consistent.

### 酒精、睡眠与情绪（alcohol）

- node: `life.health.alcohol` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-alcohol.md`
- 一句话：酒让人更快睡着，却把后半夜的睡眠打碎，压掉快速眼动睡眠；长期喝还把抑郁的风险翻倍。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Ebrahim, Shapiro, Williams, Fenwick 2013 review (27 studies): alcohol shortens sleep onset, deepens the first half, disrupts the second half and reduces REM at moderate and high doses. Boden & Fergusson 2011 Addiction: alcohol use disorder about doubles the risk of major depression, most plausibly causally in that direction. GBD 2016 Alcohol Collaborators 2018 Lancet: the level of drinking that minimises health loss is zero.

### 社会时钟（social clock）

- node: `life.stage.social-clock` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-social-clock.md`
- 一句话：「几岁该干什么」是一套文化里的时间表；觉得自己落后于它会带来真实的压力，但这张表本身一直在往后挪。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Neugarten, Moore, Lowe 1965 age norms; Neugarten 1976 on-time and off-time events. Rook, Catalano, Dooley 1989: off-time events are more distressing. Arnett 2000 emerging adulthood: ages 18–29 have become a distinct phase of exploration. US Census: median age at first marriage in 2023 about 30 for men and 28 for women, up from 23 and 21 in 1970 (check).

### 社会情绪选择理论（socioemotional selectivity theory）

- node: `life.stage.socioemotional-selectivity` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-socioemotional-selectivity.md`
- 一句话：觉得时间还很多的人追求新信息和新关系；觉得时间有限的人把精力给最有情感分量的少数人和事。起作用的是时间感，不是年龄。
- 证据等级：A（卡片上写「证据 · 强」）
- anchor: Carstensen, Isaacowitz, Charles 1999 American Psychologist; Carstensen 2006 Science. Fredrickson & Carstensen 1990: young people imagining an imminent move chose familiar close partners, like the old. Young men with HIV in the 1990s chose like 80-year-olds. Carstensen et al. 2011: emotional well-being improves from early adulthood into the 60s.

### 智慧推理（wise reasoning）

- node: `life.stage.wise-reasoning` · file: `vault/domains/psych/cards/life/psy-life-wise-reasoning.md`
- 一句话：研究里的「智慧」是几样可测的做法：承认自己知道的有限，看到别人的立场，预期事情会变，找折中；它预测生活过得好，智商不预测。
- 证据等级：B（卡片上写「证据 · 中」）
- anchor: Grossmann et al. 2010 PNAS: older adults used more of these when reasoning about social conflicts. Grossmann, Na, Varnum, Kitayama, Nisbett 2013 JEP: General: wise reasoning predicted well-being, relationship quality and even longevity; intelligence did not. Grossmann 2017: it varies more across situations than across people, and self-distancing raises it.
