# Survey brief — new long-term domains (2026-11-20)

Chi asked for long-term domains that make him a versatile senior engineer, not only interview prep. This task is
steps 1–2 of skill `building-study-domains` (read `.claude/skills/building-study-domains/SKILL.md` first): survey the
field, then draft an outline. **No skeleton YAML, no cards, no Anki, no git commits** — the orchestrator reviews and
Chi approves the outline first.

## Output per domain `<d>` (your assignment lists the ids)

1. `research/domains/<d>/BUILD.md` — `## Sources surveyed (step 1)`: one row per source with URL, licence
   (free-online / commercial / paywalled), what it covers. At least **three independent outlines**, one authoritative
   (official docs, a canonical book, a published career framework, a standards body). GitHub first:
   `gh api -X GET search/repositories -f q="awesome <subject>"` and similar; then books, official docs, engineering
   blogs. Prefer sources from 2025–2026 where the field moves (AI, Kubernetes).
2. `research/domains/<d>/outline.md` — the draft tree, in Chinese, English term in full-width parentheses on first use:
   - `# field` → `## theme` → `- leaf`. A leaf is one thing an interviewer or a senior colleague would probe. Each leaf
     line: title, then ` — ` and a one-line summary of what the card will teach.
   - Mark the Pareto core with `*` at the start of the leaf (about 50–60% of leaves). Size: 40–90 leaves per domain.
   - Prerequisites: after a leaf, `(需先: <other leaf title>)` only where understanding truly depends on it.
   - `## 不收（out of scope）` list with one-line reasons.
   - `## 与已有领域的重叠` — check the existing skeletons named in your assignment (`skeleton/<x>.yaml`); list each
     overlapping leaf and say: skip / link / go deeper (and how).
   - `## 卡片格式建议` — 3–5 lines: what each card should contain for this domain.
3. Finish with a short report (your final message): per domain — leaf count, core count, the 3 sources you trust most,
   open questions for Chi, and anything you could not confirm.

## Rules

- **Generic, not personal.** Never map content to Chi's past jobs or stories; no employer names from his history.
  Chi wants principles he can carry anywhere and pick his own stories for at interview time.
- Content Chi reads is Chinese; sources may be English.
- Cap web searches at about 40 per agent (searches share a per-turn limit with other agents).
- Do not spawn agents. Write only inside the `research/domains/<d>/` folders you are assigned (and for agent C,
  `research/career-paths/`).
