# Task: one map of where every card comes from (card-source audit)

Read-only. You change nothing in the repo, the vault or Anki. Read first: `AGENTS.md`, `CONTEXT.md`,
`docs/explanation/study-system.md`, `docs/explanation/architecture.md`, `scripts/deck_audit.py` (reuse it; its output
goes to `sources/local/audit/`), `trellis/anki.py` (`card_id_from_tags`: a built note carries a tag `id::<card id>`).

## Why

Chi wants every card to have one known home and wants to drop decks that are stale or unimportant. While planning the
public Atlas we tripped over these source problems; confirm each with numbers and look for more of the same kinds:

1. The tracked `system-design` domain (856 card files, `vault/domains/system-design`) is not in Anki at all; the deck
   Chi studies, 系统设计, is the merged view `skeleton/sysdesign.yaml` (local, generated by `research/sysdesign/gen.py`)
   over `domains/hello-interview` + `include:` folders.
2. Cards whose node exists only in the merged view (`ov.*` overview cards, `fix.*`, mock cards) make
   `trellis --domain hello-interview validate` report 91 errors.
3. Several LeetCode sources: `leetcode` (interviews/rounds/leetcode, 1,054 cards, 0 studied), `leetcode-tops`,
   `leetcode-daily`, `leetcode-tree` (0x3F tree, the one studied), `tiktok-lc`, `tiktok-lc-drill`, `tiktok-core`,
   `leetcode-mustread` (local), plus problem bodies in `vault/interviews/core/leetcode/problems/`.
4. Test-deck questions (2 算法模式识别 `recog`, 碎片速刷 tap cards) carry no source card id, so their reviews cannot be
   traced to a vault card; LLD/AI test questions can (`card_tests.py`: tag + sha1(source id)).
5. Anki notes with no `id::` tag ("unmapped": code-core had 434, markets 50, quant-infra 85 in September).
6. Domains with readings but 0 cards (`markets`, `quant-infra`).
7. Company sprint decks (6 Millennium, 7 Millennium 力扣) hold borrowed cards; homes are recorded in
   `sources/local/plan/*homes.json`.

## What to produce

Sync Anki first (`{"action":"sync","version":6}` to 127.0.0.1:8765; retry after a few seconds if it says
"Collection lifecycle operation is active"). Then, read-only:

A. **Per skeleton** (`skeleton/*.yaml`, tracked and local): domain, title, vault folder(s) incl. `include`, tracked or
   local, card files on disk (tracked / untracked), card ids found in Anki (by `id::` tag), card files with no Anki
   note, studied cards (any card of the note with reps > 0), last review date (date only, from `cardsInfo` `mod` is not
   a review date — use `getReviewsOfCards` or `cardReviews` if available, else say "unknown"), validate error count
   (`uv run trellis --domain <d> validate`, last line).
B. **Per top-level Anki deck** (`deckNames`, group by the part before the first `::`): card count, notes with an `id::`
   tag and which skeleton those ids belong to, notes with no `id::` tag (and their note types), new / learning / review
   counts, studied in the last 30 days (from `getNumCardsReviewedByDay` you cannot split by deck; use
   `findCards "deck:X rated:30"`), suspended count.
C. **Duplicates and overlaps**: the same card id in two vault files; the same note text in two decks
   (`scripts/deck_audit.py` already finds duplicates — run it and cite its output file); skeletons whose folders overlap.
D. **Findings**: for every problem, one row: what, where, how many cards, evidence (command + number), and a
   recommendation from {keep, merge into X, archive (anki_archive.py), delete from vault (git rm; history keeps it),

   Do not recommend touching Chi's study decks (2 算法模式识别, 3 低层设计测验, 4 碎片速刷, 5 系统设计高频题, AI 测验,
   系统设计, 6/7 Millennium) beyond fixing their sources.

Write `sources/local/audit/card-sources.md` (the tables and findings, Chinese prose, English ids) and
`sources/local/audit/card-sources.json` (the same data). Never copy card text into either file; ids, counts and dates
only. Never print private-domain titles (`vault/private/`, headlands, braintree) — count them as "private" rows.

Budget: about 60 AnkiConnect calls; batch `notesInfo`/`cardsInfo` in chunks of 500. Do not modify anything; do not
