In a senior tech offer, the components are elastic in roughly this order. Most elastic is the {{c1::sign-on bonus}}, because it is one-time, often from a different budget than headcount, and costs nothing in future years. Next is {{c2::equity}}, where senior bands are wide. Least elastic is {{c3::base salary}}, banded tightly by level and most tied to internal-equity policy.

Upstream of all three sits {{c4::the level itself}} — moving up a level changes every component at once and compounds through refreshers, so a level appeal is worth more than any negotiation conducted inside a level.

The component candidates most often forget to ask about is the {{c5::annual refresher grant}}, since a large initial grant with no refresh policy produces a compensation cliff in year 4.
