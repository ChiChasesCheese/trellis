## Q
Why assign an incident commander instead of letting the engineer with the deepest cache knowledge coordinate everything?

## A
The commander owns priorities, decision cadence, roles, and communication so subject-matter experts can investigate and execute mitigations. Separate operations, communications, and note-taking when the incident is large enough. This prevents duplicated risky actions, silent assumptions, and an expert becoming a throughput bottleneck while debugging.
