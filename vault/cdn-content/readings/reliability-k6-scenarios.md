# Grafana k6: Scenarios

Read this to design workloads rather than merely select a virtual-user count. Extract open and closed models, arrival-rate executors, ramps, graceful stop, and independent scenarios for different traffic classes.

Build warm HIT, cold MISS, hot-key expiry, and origin-degradation scenarios around [[reliability-load-open-model]], [[reliability-load-cache-scenarios]], and [[reliability-load-fault-success]]. A scenario passes only against predeclared SLO and protection thresholds.
