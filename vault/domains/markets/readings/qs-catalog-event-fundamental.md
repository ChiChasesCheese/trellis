# Family 7: Event-Driven & Fundamental

Groups strategies around the idea that markets absorb new information gradually rather than instantly: post-earnings-announcement drift (a stock that beats expectations keeps drifting up for weeks), merger arbitrage (buying a takeover target below the deal price collects a spread that is really compensation for deal-collapse risk), forced index-rebalancing flows, buybacks and share issuance, insider-trading signals, and accruals. Notes this family has some of the clearest 'who is on the other side and why' stories of any family, but the cleanest versions require expensive analyst-expectations data, and several depend on a short leg that doesn't exist in short-sale-restricted markets.
