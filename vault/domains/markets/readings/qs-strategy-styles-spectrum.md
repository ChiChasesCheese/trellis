# The Spectrum of Strategy Styles: What Each One Is Actually Betting On

Dissects eight major strategy styles, momentum, short and long reversal, value, quality, carry, statistical arbitrage, event-driven, and microstructure, through one consistent lens for each: what it's actually betting on, who is losing money on the other side and why they keep doing it, what time horizon and regime it likes, how crowded it is, and what shape its return distribution takes. The recurring meta-lesson is that a strategy without an answer to 'who is losing and why do they keep paying' is very likely a backtest illusion rather than a real edge, and that crowding and true signal strength are almost inversely related across the styles surveyed.
