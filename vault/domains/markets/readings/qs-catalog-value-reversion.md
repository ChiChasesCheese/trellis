# Family 2: Value & Mean Reversion

Frames value as the slow-motion cousin of mean reversion: buying assets cheap relative to fundamentals or history and waiting years for the market to agree, versus fast reversal, days, where a price snap-back is compensation for providing liquidity to someone who had to sell in a hurry. Covers equity value ratios, FX value (currencies reverting to a purchasing-power-parity-like fair value), short-horizon reversal, and pairs/statistical arbitrage built on cointegrated price spreads, with a warning about the value trap, cheap because a business is genuinely dying rather than mispriced, and about pegged exchange rates faking mean-reversion until the peg breaks.
