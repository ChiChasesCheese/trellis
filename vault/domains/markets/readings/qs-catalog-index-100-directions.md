# 100 Quant Strategy Directions: A Study Catalogue

The organizing document for a 100-direction strategy catalogue, opening with the field's first-principles claim: any strategy that makes money must be able to name who is losing money on the other side and why they keep accepting the loss, and every real profit source reduces to one of three types — behavioral error, risk premium, or structural constraint. Also states the validation discipline governing the whole catalogue (strict causality, purged out-of-sample testing, the classic backtest traps, deflated-Sharpe multiple-testing correction, and the cost wall) and the central empirical finding that almost no single factor survives these tests alone — the money is in combining several weakly-correlated survivors into a portfolio.
