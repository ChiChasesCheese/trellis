# Family 12: Crypto & DeFi

Treats crypto as the youngest, most retail-dominated, least arbitraged major market, where classical behavioral anomalies run stronger and fresh, and market-specific structural mechanisms (perpetual funding rates, cross-exchange and DEX-CEX price gaps) create genuinely new edge, but insists it should be used only for cross-validation, not as an anchor, because its short history makes the deflated-Sharpe bar hard to clear and any long-only strategy through 2021-2025 looks profitable purely from riding the bull-market beta. Covers cross-sectional crypto momentum, funding-rate carry, basis trades, cross-venue arbitrage, on-chain signals, liquidity provision and impermanent loss, staking, and MEV.
