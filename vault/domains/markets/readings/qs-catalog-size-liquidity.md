# Family 5: Size & Liquidity

Covers the size premium (small stocks have historically outperformed, though unreliably) and the liquidity premium (assets hard to buy and sell trade cheap because someone must be compensated for being unable to exit easily) as related but distinct structural edges: large institutions are mechanically excluded from small or illiquid names because their trades would move the price too much, leaving that segment under-arbitraged. Warns both are capacity-constrained almost by definition, that microcap alpha is a frequent backtest illusion from unrealistic execution assumptions, and that survivorship bias is especially dangerous in this corner of the market.
