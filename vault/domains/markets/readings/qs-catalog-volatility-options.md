# Family 6: Volatility & Options

Frames selling volatility as literally running an insurance business: implied volatility persistently exceeds subsequently realized volatility (the variance risk premium), because people overpay for protection against outcomes they fear most, and the seller collects that overpayment as a steady premium until a crash arrives and gives several years of it back at once. Surveys short-VRP strategies, VRP-based regime timing, dispersion trading, skew and volatility term-structure trades, tail hedging, and using implied volatility as a stock-selection input, repeatedly warning that a suspiciously smooth, high-Sharpe volatility-selling backtest is hiding its risk, not eliminating it.
