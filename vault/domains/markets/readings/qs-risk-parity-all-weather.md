# Risk Parity and All Weather: Allocating by Risk, Not by Dollars

Explains Bridgewater's All Weather approach as a genuinely different paradigm from picking winning bets: instead of predicting which economic environment is coming, it allocates risk, not dollars, equally across four growth/inflation quadrants so that some part of the portfolio is positioned correctly whichever quadrant actually arrives — a philosophy the piece calls beta engineering as opposed to the alpha-factory style of a multi-manager platform. It is honest about the approach's known failure mode (2022, when equities and leveraged long-duration bonds fell together) and about the fact that risk parity is a real, separately learnable portfolio-construction technique, not just a slogan.
