# Family 10: Alternative Data & Machine Learning

Surveys alternative data (news/social sentiment NLP, earnings-call language, satellite and geospatial proxies, supply-chain network effects, search/web-traffic demand signals) and machine learning's proper role in combining many weak factors nonlinearly, alongside a pointed warning: this is the family with the most traps, since alternative datasets are typically short-history and expensive, making the statistical bar nearly impossible to clear, and decay fast once proven useful. States a clear house rule: use ML to combine already-validated factors or optimize execution, never to discover patterns directly from raw price via deep learning.
