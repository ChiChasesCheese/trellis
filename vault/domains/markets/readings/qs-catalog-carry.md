# Family 3: Carry

Defines carry as the return collected just from holding a position while nothing happens, borrowing a low-interest currency to fund a high-interest one, or holding a bond and earning its roll-down, and frames it as compensation for bearing tail risk: carry strategies collect small, steady premiums for long stretches and then give several years of profit back in one crisis, because the spread persists precisely because it doesn't get arbitraged away by the risk it represents. Surveys FX carry, bond term-premium carry, commodity roll yield, volatility carry, credit carry, and cross-asset unification, stressing that carry needs a tail hedge or trend overlay almost by definition.
