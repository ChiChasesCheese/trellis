# Family 4: Low-Risk & Quality

Explains the low-volatility and quality anomalies as a textbook structural-constraint edge: investors who can't use leverage can only reach for higher expected return by buying high-volatility lottery stocks, bidding them up and leaving boring low-volatility names cheap on a risk-adjusted basis. Covers low-volatility investing, betting-against-beta, profitability and quality metrics, and warns specifically that raw ROE is a poor quality proxy because it can be inflated by leverage, a highly leveraged, fragile company can show high ROE, which is exactly the trap this codebase's own small-cap tests fell into before switching to gross profitability.
