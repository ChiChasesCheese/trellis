# Family 8: Microstructure & Intraday

Zooms into the minute-to-second scale where price moves are literally the outcome of individual order flow: order-flow imbalance, intraday reversal and momentum, the overnight-versus-intraday return split (most of a stock's return historically accrues overnight, not during the session), market-making with inventory and adverse-selection risk, and realized-volatility forecasting from high-frequency data. The recurring, hard-won lesson is that this is where the strongest genuinely-real gross signals in the whole catalogue live, and also where they die fastest to transaction costs and to a scarcity of truly independent, as opposed to merely frequent, observations.
