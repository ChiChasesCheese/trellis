# From Idea to Signal: Cross-Sectional, Time-Series, and Signal Engineering

A practical, five-step pipeline for turning a vague idea into a tradeable, testable signal: choosing between a cross-sectional design (rank names against each other) and a time-series design (judge each asset against its own history), two fundamentally different sources of return; enforcing causality; standardizing across the cross-section by rank rather than raw z-score to survive fat-tailed data; neutralizing away unwanted exposures by regression; and choosing a weighting scheme. A worked example shows combining six modestly-performing signals equally can underperform the single best one, because equal weighting has no defense against a sign-flipped or genuinely weak component — a concrete lesson in why naive signal combination is not free diversification.
