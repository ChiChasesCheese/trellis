# The Unified Factor Catalog: Raw Material vs Vetted Research

Distinguishes two very different things that both get called a factor: the research plane (curated, vetted, ledger-confirmed signals worth reading about) and the raw-material plane (a battery of thousands of mechanically generated candidate formulas, template windows, and public-library expressions that have not been tested yet). Describes a unified catalog schema that normalizes candidates from nine different sources into one comparable record with provenance and license, while insisting raw candidate counts are never conflated with vetted discoveries, and that every candidate in a batch is charged as a trial against the batch's shared statistical budget — selection and synthesis priced differently.
