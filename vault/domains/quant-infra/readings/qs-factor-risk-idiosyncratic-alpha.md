# Factor Risk and the Discipline of Only Betting on What's Idiosyncratic

Explains the return = factor exposure + residual decomposition that separates a return stream into common-factor exposure and stock-specific residual, and argues a fund's real edge is only the residual — factor exposure is risk you're carrying, not skill, and you could buy it cheaply as an ETF instead. Covers neutralizing a book against known factors, attributing realized P&L to factor bets versus stock-picking, optimizing a portfolio under a neutrality constraint, and combining many weak signals by their return-stream covariance rather than equally, with a worked example showing a 'working' momentum strategy on liquid large caps is almost entirely a factor bet in disguise.
