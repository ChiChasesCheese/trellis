# Family 11: Portfolio Construction & Risk Management

Argues, with backing from this project's own experience, that the highest-leverage work in quantitative research is not finding a new factor but combining several weak, individually-marginal ones into a well-constructed book, a program that turned an everything-fails single-factor research phase into a portfolio with an out-of-sample Sharpe near 1.0. Walks through risk parity (equalize risk contribution, not dollar allocation), volatility targeting, IC-weighted combination, mean-variance and Black-Litterman optimization, Kelly sizing, and computing portfolio weights only from information available at the time, plus honest treatment of skew and tail-shaping as a portfolio-level, not factor-level, problem.
