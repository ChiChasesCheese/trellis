## Q
30 minutes left and only half the features are built. What do you cut — and what must never be cut?

## A
- **Never cut**: one core flow working end-to-end, demoed with real output.
- **Cut breadth**: secondary features become a stub interface plus one sentence on where they'd plug in.
- Announce the re-scope to the interviewer instead of going silent.

A narrow working demo beats broad code that doesn't run — "runs" is the gate the rest of the rubric sits behind.


## Q zh
30 分钟剩下，只有一半特性被构建。你切什么 — 什么必须永远不切?

## A zh
- **永远不切**: 一个核心流端到端工作，用真实输出演示。
- **切广度**: 次要特性变成一个存根接口加上一句关于它们会在哪里插入的话。
- 而不是沉默，向面试官宣布重新范围。

一个狭窄工作的演示胜过广阔不运行的代码 — "运行"是其余标准坐在后面的门槛。
