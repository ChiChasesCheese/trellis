# Hello Interview: Low-Level Design Guide

The only structured, interviewer-written walkthrough of the LLD round itself —
what the rubric actually rewards and a repeatable requirements→entities→
interfaces→code pipeline, with worked deliveries of classic problems.

**Extract on read:**
- The grading axes (working core flows, extensibility, readability) and how
  much each is worth relative to "finishing everything."
- The requirements-to-objects drill: scope to 3–4 core flows out loud, extract
  entities and invariants, then write interfaces before bodies.
- How to narrate trade-offs while coding so the evaluation happens in your favor.
