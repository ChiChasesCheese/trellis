# Proxy Servers and Tunneling (MDN)

A concise map of forward proxies, reverse proxies, tunnels, and forwarding
metadata. It gives the vocabulary needed to reason about where TLS terminates,
which peer is trusted, and which request is reconstructed for the next hop.

**Extract on read:**
- Distinguish client-side forward proxy from origin-side reverse proxy.
- Explain why forwarding client identity requires a trust boundary.
- Identify when a tunnel relays bytes rather than interpreting HTTP semantics.
