# Introduction: Demand Forecasting and Capacity Planning (Google SRE)

The capacity-planning section gives the production contract: forecast organic
and launch-driven demand, correlate raw resources to service capacity through
load tests, and provision before the acquisition lead time expires.

**Extract on read:**
- Separate demand forecasting from resource provisioning.
- Convert servers, links, and storage into SLO-compliant service capacity.
- Include redundancy and the largest credible failure, not only normal peak load.
