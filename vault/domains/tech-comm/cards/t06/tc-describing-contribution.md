## Q
**Describing your contribution · I vs we**｜惯用说法 · 必会

面试官问："Tell me about a project you worked on." 你一路说 "we did…we decided…"，面试官最后问："那你具体做了什么？" 怎么说清自己的贡献，又不抢团队的功劳？

## A
**可以这么说：**
- 团队背景用 we：「We were migrating the billing system to an event-driven design.」
- 你的部分用 I：「I designed the retry logic and wrote the first prototype.」
- 「I owned the rollout plan, and I coordinated with three teams.」
- 「My part was the data migration. Priya handled the API.」
- 「I led the effort to reduce p99 latency by half.」（有结果就带结果）

**动词强度：**

| 弱 | 强 |
|---|---|
| helped with | designed / built / led |
| was involved in | owned / drove |
| participated in | decided / proposed |
| worked on | implemented / shipped |

**别这么说：**
- 全程 we：对方无法评估你。
- 全程 I，一次也不提队友：显得不合作。
- 夸大：把别人的工作说成自己的。

**语气：** 背景用 we，行动用 I，成果带数字；被追问时给具体例子。
