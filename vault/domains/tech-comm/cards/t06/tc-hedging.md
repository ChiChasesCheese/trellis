## Q
**Hedging · 表达把握程度**｜惯用说法 · 必会

面试官问："Would you use Kafka or a database queue here?" 你的答案取决于吞吐量，没有绝对答案，但也不想显得没立场。英文怎么说？

## A
**可以这么说（给出倾向）：**
- "I'd lean towards Kafka here, mainly because we need replay."
- "It depends on the throughput. If it's under a few hundred messages a second, a database queue is simpler."
- "My understanding is that Kafka keeps messages after they're consumed, but please correct me if I'm wrong."
- "I'm fairly confident about X, less sure about Y."

**别这么说：**
- "Maybe, maybe not." 没有任何倾向。
- 每句都加 "I think maybe probably…" 太多对冲，听不出你的意见。
- "It depends." 只说这一句就停下，没说取决于什么。

**语气：** Hedging 的作用是**标出你有多确定**，不是回避。先给倾向，再说取决于什么，最后说你不确定的部分。
