## Q
**Numbers in English · 数字的读法**｜惯用说法 · 必会

你要在英文面试里说：每天约 3500 万次请求，峰值是平均的三倍多，缓存命中率 95%，延迟下降了一半。这些数字英文怎么读、怎么说？

## A
**读法：**

| 数字 | 英文 |
|---|---|
| 3,500 | three thousand five hundred |
| 35,000,000 | thirty-five million |
| 1,000,000,000 | one billion |
| 0.5 | zero point five |
| 95% | ninety-five percent |

**可以这么说：**
- "We handle roughly 35 million requests a day."
- "Peak traffic is on the order of three times the average."
- "The cache hit rate is around ninety-five percent."
- "Latency dropped by half, from 200 milliseconds to 100."
- "That's about a 3x increase."（三倍）

**别这么说：**
- "Three hundred fifty ten thousand" （按中文万来读）：英文没有"万"，用 thousand、million。
- "increased 200%" 与 "increased by 3x" 混用：先说清起点和终点再说比例。

**语气：** 大数用 roughly、about、on the order of 表示量级，不必精确；重要的数字说明单位和起点（from … to …）。
