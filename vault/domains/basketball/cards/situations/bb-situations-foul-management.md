## Q
Three separate foul decisions in one game: (A) you are down six with 45 seconds left — when do you start fouling and whom? (B) The opponent has one team foul left before the bonus with 3 minutes on the clock — what does that change? (C) You are your team's best defender and you pick up your fourth foul early in the second half.

## A
- **(A) Start fouling immediately, and foul the worst free-throw shooter you can legally reach.** Down six with 45 seconds you need roughly three scores; every second spent defending is a second you cannot get back. Fouling early is almost always better than fouling late — the mistake teams make is waiting until the deficit is mathematically hopeless. Foul on the catch, away from the basket, and get the ball inbounded fast.
- **(B) The foul to give is a free defensive possession.** With one foul before the bonus, you can foul before the offense starts its action — killing a fast break, disrupting a set, or stopping the clock — at no cost in points. Use it deliberately and early in the shot clock; wasting it on a reach at the rim is throwing away a genuine asset.
- **(C) Four fouls is a resource-allocation problem, not a reason to hide.** The question is where your minutes are worth most. Sitting until the last six minutes preserves you for the end of the game but concedes the middle; playing on risks fouling out and losing the end entirely.
- **The practical compromise for (C):** stay in, but change *how* you defend — no help-side charges, no reaching, no contesting on the ground when you can contest straight up, and switch off the opponent's most aggressive driver. You are still on the floor, just not offering the referee decisions.
- **The single rule under all three:** fouls are a currency with a supply. Spend them on possessions you want to buy, not on possessions that happen to you.

## Q zh
一场比赛里的三个不同的犯规决策：(A) 你落后六分，还剩 45 秒——什么时候开始犯规？对谁犯规？(B) 还有 3 分钟时，对手在进入罚球状态（bonus）前还剩一次团队犯规——这改变了什么？(C) 你是球队最好的防守人，下半场早早领到了第四次犯规。

## A zh
- **(A) 立刻开始犯规，并对你能合法够到的罚球最差的人犯规。** 落后六分、还剩 45 秒，你大约需要三次得分；每一秒用来防守，都是拿不回来的一秒。提早犯规几乎总是优于推迟犯规——球队常犯的错是一直等到分差在数学上已经无望。要在接球瞬间、远离篮筐处犯规，然后迅速把球发出来。
- **(B) 那次"可用的犯规"（foul to give）等于一个免费的防守回合。** 在进入 bonus 前还剩一次犯规时，你可以在进攻方启动战术之前就犯规——掐掉一次快攻、打乱一套战术、或者停掉时钟——而不付出任何分数代价。要在进攻时限早段有意识地用掉它；把它浪费在篮下的一次伸手上，等于扔掉一份实实在在的资产。
- **(C) 四次犯规是一个资源分配问题，不是躲起来的理由。** 问题在于你的上场时间在哪一段最值钱。一直坐到最后六分钟能把你留到末段，但把中段拱手让人；继续打则冒着六犯离场、彻底失去末段的风险。
- **(C) 的务实折中：** 留在场上，但改变**防守方式**——不去协防造进攻犯规、不伸手、能垂直起跳干扰就不在地面上纠缠，并把对手最凶的突破手换给别人防。你人还在场上，只是不再给裁判做判罚的机会。
- **贯穿三种情形的唯一规则：** 犯规是一种有限供给的货币。把它花在你**想买下**的回合上，而不是花在碰巧发生在你身上的回合上。
