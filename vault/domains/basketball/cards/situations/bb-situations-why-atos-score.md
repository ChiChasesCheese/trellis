## Q
Possessions coming out of a timeout (**ATO**) convert at a noticeably higher rate than ordinary half-court possessions. Explain the three reasons why, and then explain what a defense should do differently on an ATO because of them.

## A
- **Reason 1 — information asymmetry.** The offense knows exactly what is coming and where every cut goes; the defense is guessing. Even a mediocre action beats a defense that has to read it in real time.
- **Reason 2 — the offense gets to pick the matchup and the action.** A coach with 60 seconds can design directly at the worst defender on the floor, or at a coverage he has watched fail all half. Ordinary possessions take whatever the flow produces.
- **Reason 3 — misdirection is available.** With a drawn play you can spend two players purely on decoying, which is impossible in flow offense. The first cut exists to move a defender, not to score.
- **What the defense does differently:** (1) **switch more than usual** — switching is the coverage that cares least about not knowing the play; (2) **talk earlier and louder**, calling screens before they arrive; (3) **guard the second action**, since the first one is usually a decoy; (4) put your best defender on the inbounder's first read or take away the entry pass entirely.
- **And name the personnel out loud during the timeout.** Most ATO baskets against amateurs come from a defender who did not know who he had when play resumed — a failure that happens before the ball is live.
- **The mirror lesson on offense:** if ATOs work because of preparation and misdirection, then your ordinary offense improves by importing both — a called entry, a decoy cut, and a known second option.

## Q zh
暂停之后的回合（**ATO**）转化率明显高于普通的半场回合。请解释背后的三个原因，然后说明防守方因此在 ATO 上应该有什么不同。

## A zh
- **原因 1——信息不对称。** 进攻方确切知道要发生什么、每一次空切去哪里；防守方只能猜。哪怕一套平庸的战术，也能赢过必须临场读位的防守。
- **原因 2——进攻方可以自选对位和动作。** 有 60 秒的教练可以直接冲着场上最差的防守人设计，或者冲着他整个半场看着失效的那套防守设计。普通回合只能接受流动中偶然产生的机会。
- **原因 3——可以使用声东击西。** 有了画好的战术，你可以让两名球员纯粹去做诱饵，这在流动进攻里做不到。第一次空切的存在是为了调动某名防守人，不是为了得分。
- **防守方要做的不同之处：** (1) **比平时更多地换防**——换防是那种最不在乎"不知道对方要打什么"的防守方式；(2) **更早、更大声地喊**，在掩护到达之前就报出来；(3) **守第二个动作**，因为第一个通常是诱饵；(4) 把你最好的防守人放在发球人的第一选择上，或者干脆切断第一传。
- **并且要在暂停里把对位一个个念出来。** 业余层面大多数被 ATO 打进的球，来自比赛恢复时某名防守人不知道自己该防谁——这个失误在球活起来之前就已经发生了。
- **进攻端的镜像启示：** 如果 ATO 之所以有效是因为准备和声东击西，那么把这两样引入你的普通进攻就能提升它——一个喊出来的启动方式、一次诱饵空切、以及一个大家都知道的第二选择。
