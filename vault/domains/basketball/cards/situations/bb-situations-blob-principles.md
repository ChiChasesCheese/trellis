## Q
You have a baseline out-of-bounds play (**BLOB**) under your own basket. Why is a BLOB the single most efficient half-court possession available to you, and what are the principles that make one work — including what "screening the screener" means and why it beats a simple screen?

## A
- **Why it is efficient:** the defense has to guard five players in a compressed space next to the rim with no help behind them, and they cannot pressure the ball. Any confusion produces a layup, not a jumper — that is why BLOB conversion rates sit well above normal half-court offense.
- **Principle 1 — attack the rim, not the perimeter.** A BLOB that ends in a wing three has wasted the geometry. Design the first option as a layup and let the three be the bail-out.
- **Principle 2 — make defenders move before they can see.** Two actions, not one: an initial cut or screen that turns defenders' heads, then the real action behind it.
- **Screening the screener:** player A sets a screen for player B, and the instant A's defender relaxes — having just done his job — a third player screens A, who is now the open cutter. It works because the screener's defender is the least alert man on the floor: he has already helped on the first action and is watching the ball.
- **Principle 3 — the inbounder is a live threat.** Five defenders guarding four attackers means one man is free. If your inbounder passes and immediately steps in, the defender guarding the ball has to make a second decision, and the play becomes a genuine 5-on-5 instead of 4-on-5.
- **Principle 4 — spacing and a safety.** One player is always the release valve out top; you have five seconds, and a turnover under your own basket is a layup the other way.

## Q zh
你有一次己方篮下的底线发球战术（**BLOB**）。为什么 BLOB 是你能拿到的效率最高的半场回合？让它奏效的原则有哪些——包括 "screening the screener（给掩护人做掩护）" 是什么意思，以及它为什么强于一次普通掩护？

## A zh
- **为什么效率高：** 防守方必须在篮筐旁一片被压缩的空间里守五个人，身后没有协防，而且无法给球施压。任何一点混乱产生的都是上篮而不是跳投——这就是 BLOB 的转化率远高于普通阵地战的原因。
- **原则 1——攻篮筐，不是攻外线。** 以侧翼三分结束的 BLOB 浪费了这套几何。把第一选择设计成上篮，让三分当作退路。
- **原则 2——在防守人看清之前先让他们移动。** 要两个动作而不是一个：先用一次空切或掩护把防守人的头转过去，真正的动作藏在它后面。
- **Screening the screener：** 球员 A 给球员 B 做掩护，而在 A 的防守人刚完成任务、松懈下来的那一瞬间，第三名球员去给 A 做掩护，此时 A 成了空位切入者。它之所以有效，是因为掩护人的防守人是全场警觉性最低的人：他刚刚在第一个动作上协防完，眼睛正盯着球。
- **原则 3——发球人是活的威胁。** 五名防守人守四名进攻人，意味着有一个人是自由的。如果你的发球人传球后立刻步入场内，防球的那名防守人就必须再做一次决定，这个战术也就从 4 打 5 变成真正的 5 打 5。
- **原则 4——空间和一个安全出口。** 永远要有一名球员在弧顶做接应；你只有五秒，而在自家篮下失误就是对方的一次上篮。
