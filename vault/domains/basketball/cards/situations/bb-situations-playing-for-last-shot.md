## Q
Tie game, you have the ball with 40 seconds left and the shot clock is turned off. Your team wants "the last shot." At what point should the offense actually start, and what are the three ways teams butcher this?

## A
- **Start the action with roughly 10 to 12 seconds left, and shoot at about 5.** That leaves your opponent no realistic possession after an offensive rebound, and still leaves you time for a second attempt off your own miss.
- **"Holding for the last shot" is not standing still.** The four seconds before your action begins should be spent creating an advantage cheaply — a dribble handoff, a slow drag screen — so you enter the real action already moving. Standing and then playing one-on-one at 8 seconds is how good defenses win these.
- **Butchery 1 — shooting too early.** Shooting at 15 seconds hands them a full possession and turns your one-shot game into a two-shot game you can lose.
- **Butchery 2 — shooting too late.** Getting the shot up at 2 seconds means no offensive rebound, no put-back, and any deflection ends the game. The rebound is worth more than the two extra seconds of "safety."
- **Butchery 3 — running a set that needs the whole possession.** Late-game offense should reduce to a two-man action with a clear counter, run by whoever is best with the ball, with everyone else spaced and reading. Complexity gets switched, doubled, and burned.
- **Also decide the foul plan in advance:** whether you take a foul on a defensive rebound, and who does not get the ball if the opponent starts fouling. Late-game execution is a plan, not a moment of inspiration.

## Q zh
比分打平，还剩 40 秒球在你手上，进攻时限已关闭。你们想打"最后一攻"。进攻实际应该在什么时候启动？球队通常有哪三种把它搞砸的方式？

## A zh
- **在还剩大约 10 到 12 秒时启动战术，在大约 5 秒时出手。** 这样即使他们抢到防守篮板，也来不及打一个像样的回合；而你自己不中时还有时间做第二次尝试。
- **"拖到最后一攻"不等于站着不动。** 战术启动前的那几秒，应该用来低成本地制造一点优势——一次 dribble handoff（运球交接）、一次慢节奏的 drag screen——好让你带着移动进入真正的动作。站着不动、然后在 8 秒时打一对一，正是好防守赢下这种球的方式。
- **搞砸方式 1——出手太早。** 15 秒就出手，等于送给对方一个完整回合，把只有一次出手的比赛变成你可能输掉的两次出手的比赛。
- **搞砸方式 2——出手太晚。** 剩 2 秒才出手意味着没有前场篮板、没有补篮，任何一次被拨掉都直接结束比赛。那个篮板机会比多出来的两秒"保险"值钱。
- **搞砸方式 3——跑一套需要整个回合才能跑完的战术。** 末段进攻应该简化成一个有明确后手的两人配合，由最擅长持球的人来打，其余人拉开并读位。复杂的东西会被换防、被包夹、被耗光时间。
- **同时要提前定好犯规预案：** 抢到防守篮板后是否要主动犯规，以及如果对方开始犯规，球不能交到谁手上。末段执行力是一份计划，不是灵光一现。
