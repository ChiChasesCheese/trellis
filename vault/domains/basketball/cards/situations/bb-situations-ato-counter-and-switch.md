## Q
You run your after-timeout play, the defense switches the first screen and takes away option one. Your team stands around and someone shoots a contested jumper at 4 on the shot clock. What should have happened instead, and how do you read the switch you just forced?

## A
- **Every drawn play needs a named counter, and everyone has to know it.** A play is not "an option"; it is a first option plus what happens when it is taken away. If your team has to invent the next four seconds, the play only works against a defense that lets it.
- **A switch is information, not a wall.** The instant the defense switches, you have been handed a mismatch and, more importantly, two defenders who have just changed responsibilities and are momentarily unsure who they have.
- **Read it in this order:** (1) is there a **size mismatch** you can attack immediately — a big on a guard in space, or a guard on a big before he can front? Attack it *now*, in the first two seconds, before help can set up; (2) if not, **re-screen** the same action — a switch defense that has to switch twice usually breaks on the second one; (3) if the switch was clean and there is no mismatch, the switch has left a **weak-side defender** in an unfamiliar spot; that is where the second action goes.
- **Speed is the whole point.** A mismatch attacked immediately is a 1-on-1; the same mismatch attacked after four seconds of dribbling is a 1-on-3, because the defense has had time to build help and to switch back on the next screen.
- **Design principle:** the best ATOs contain their own counter — the first action forces the defense to declare (switch, hedge, or drop), and the second action punishes whichever they chose. That is why they score twice: once by surprise, once by design.

## Q zh
你跑了暂停后的战术，防守把第一个掩护换防了，第一选择被掐掉。你们全队站着不动，最后有人在进攻时限剩 4 秒时投出一记被干扰的跳投。本来应该发生什么？你又该如何读刚刚被你逼出来的那次换防？

## A zh
- **每一套画出来的战术都需要一个有名字的后手，而且所有人都要知道它。** 一套战术不是"一个选择"；它是第一选择，加上第一选择被掐掉时会发生什么。如果你的球队必须临场发明接下来的四秒，那么这套战术只对肯让它成立的防守有效。
- **换防是信息，不是墙。** 防守换防的那一瞬间，你拿到了一个错位；更重要的是，两名防守人刚刚交换了责任，此刻并不完全确定自己该防谁。
- **按这个顺序去读：** (1) 有没有可以立刻攻击的**体型错位**——大个在外线守后卫，或者后卫在低位守大个而他还来不及绕前？**现在就打**，在头两秒内，趁协防还没站好；(2) 如果没有，就对同一个动作**再做一次掩护（re-screen）**——被迫连续换两次的换防体系，通常在第二次上崩掉；(3) 如果换防很干净、也没有错位，那么这次换防已经把某名**弱侧防守人**留在了一个陌生的位置；第二个动作就该打向那里。
- **速度就是全部要点。** 立刻攻击的错位是 1 打 1；同样的错位在运了四秒之后再打，就是 1 打 3，因为防守方已经有时间搭好协防，并在下一个掩护上换回去。
- **设计原则：** 最好的 ATO 自带后手——第一个动作逼防守表态（换防、hedge 还是 drop），第二个动作则惩罚他们所选的那一种。这就是它们能得两次分的原因：一次靠意外，一次靠设计。
