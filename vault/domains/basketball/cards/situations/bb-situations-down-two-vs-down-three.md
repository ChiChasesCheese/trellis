## Q
Two scenarios, both with about 25 seconds left and you have the ball, no timeouts, and the opponent is in the bonus. (A) You are down two. (B) You are down three. What is the correct plan in each, and what is the arithmetic behind the difference?

## A
- **(A) Down two — play for the best shot, and prefer a two.** Getting the game to overtime with a good look at the rim is worth more than a low-percentage three, because a two ties it and you still have a second chance if you miss and rebound. Attack the rim, take the foul if it comes, and time the shot so you shoot with roughly **5 seconds** left — enough for an offensive rebound put-back, not enough for them to run a real possession.
- **(B) Down three — you need two possessions or one three.** The two honest options: take a good three now while there is still time to foul afterwards if you miss, or get a quick two and immediately foul to extend the game.
- **The arithmetic that decides it:** a decent three is roughly a 33-35% proposition. The quick-two-then-foul route is a chain of events — make the two, foul quickly, they miss at least one free throw, you rebound, you score again — and each link is below certain, so the compound probability is usually in a similar range but takes far longer and requires them to miss.
- **So the practical rule: with time for only one possession, shoot the three. With 25 or more seconds and a bad three-point team, the two-then-foul route becomes competitive.**
- **Both cases share one rule: know the clock before the catch.** The most common late-game error is not shot choice, it is shooting with 12 seconds left when down two (giving them a full possession) or with 2 seconds left when down three (giving up your rebound).
- **And in both cases, avoid your worst turnover risk.** A live-ball turnover down two with 20 seconds left ends the game as surely as a missed shot with none.

## Q zh
两种情形，都是还剩约 25 秒、球在你手上、没有暂停、对方已进入犯规罚球状态（bonus）。(A) 你落后两分。(B) 你落后三分。每种情形下正确的计划是什么？两者差别背后的算术是什么？

## A zh
- **(A) 落后两分——打最好的一次出手，并优先选两分。** 用一次篮下好机会把比赛拖进加时，价值高于一记低命中率的三分，因为两分就能追平，而且不中还有前场篮板的第二次机会。冲击篮筐，犯规送上门就接住，并把出手时间控制在大约还剩 **5 秒**——够抢一次前场篮板补篮，不够他们打一个像样的回合。
- **(B) 落后三分——你需要两个回合，或者一记三分。** 两个诚实的选项：现在就投一记好三分，万一不中后面还有时间犯规；或者快速拿下两分，立刻犯规把比赛拖长。
- **决定取舍的算术：** 一记像样的三分大约是 33-35% 的事件。而"快速两分再犯规"是一条事件链——两分要进、要迅速犯规、他们至少罚丢一球、你要抢下篮板、你还要再得分——每一环都不确定，所以复合概率通常落在相近的区间，却要花长得多的时间，而且依赖对手罚丢。
- **所以实用规则是：只剩一个回合的时间，就投三分；还有 25 秒以上，而且你们三分很差，那么"两分加犯规"这条路就有竞争力。**
- **两种情形共用一条规则：接球之前就要知道时钟。** 末段最常见的错误不是选择哪种出手，而是落后两分时还剩 12 秒就出手（送给对方一个完整回合），或者落后三分时剩 2 秒才出手（把篮板机会一并放弃）。
- **两种情形都要避开你最大的失误风险。** 落后两分、还剩 20 秒时的一次活球失误，和一次不中且时间归零一样，都直接结束比赛。
