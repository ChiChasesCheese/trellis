## Q
Sideline out-of-bounds (**SLOB**), 2.4 seconds left, you are down one and inbounding at half court. How do the constraints differ from a normal SLOB, what shape do you want, and what is the defense's job against you?

## A
- **The constraint that changes everything: with under about 3 seconds you cannot catch, dribble and shoot.** The play must produce a **catch-and-shoot** or a catch-and-layup. With over 3 seconds a dribble becomes available and the whole menu opens up.
- **Shape: get two options at different depths.** A screen-the-screener action producing a rim cut, with a second man popping to the ball behind it — because in a tie you take the closest available shot, and a rim catch beats a three when you are down one.
- **Passing angle is the real design problem on a SLOB.** Unlike a BLOB, the sideline is behind you and the defense can pressure the ball, so your first job is a passer who can throw over the top and a receiver who comes back *to* the ball rather than away from it.
- **The inbounder as a threat still applies** — and at half court he can also be your best shooter, receiving on a step-in throwback after passing.
- **The defense's job:** switch everything (no one is beaten by a screen if nobody navigates one), deny the direct line, put a big body on the ball to make the pass higher and slower, and never foul a jump shooter. On a made-basket BLOB the inbounder may run the baseline; on a SLOB he may not — so pressuring the ball is far more effective on a sideline play.
- **Both sides' checklist:** know your timeout count, know whether a made basket gives you the baseline, and know the clock does not start until the ball is touched inbounds.

## Q zh
边线发球（**SLOB**），还剩 2.4 秒，你落后一分，在中场附近发球。约束条件与普通 SLOB 有什么不同？你想要什么样的战术形状？防守方的任务是什么？

## A zh
- **改变一切的约束：低于大约 3 秒，你无法完成"接球—运球—出手"。** 战术必须产出一次 **catch-and-shoot（接球即投）** 或接球上篮。超过 3 秒，运球才成为选项，整份菜单才打开。
- **形状：拿到两个不同深度的选项。** 用 screen-the-screener 打出一次篮下切入，后面跟一名球员向球迎出——因为落后一分时你要的是最近的可得出手，篮下接球优于三分。
- **SLOB 真正的设计难题是传球角度。** 和 BLOB 不同，边线在你身后，防守可以给球施压，所以你的首要任务是：一个能从上方吊过去的传球人，以及一个**迎着球**回接、而不是背离球的接球人。
- **发球人是活威胁这一条依然成立**——在中场，他甚至可以是你最好的射手，传球后步入场内接一记回传。
- **防守方的任务：** 全部换防（没人去绕掩护，就没人会被掩护打掉）、切断直线传球路线、用大个身体贴住球让传球更高更慢，以及绝不对跳投的人犯规。投篮命中后的 BLOB 允许发球人沿底线跑动；SLOB 不允许——所以给球施压在边线球上有效得多。
- **两边共用的检查清单：** 知道自己还剩几个暂停、知道对方进球后你是否可以沿底线移动、知道时钟要等球在场内被触碰才启动。
