## Q
You are up three with 8 seconds left, the opponent has the ball and no timeouts. Should you foul before they can shoot a three? Make the case both ways and state the execution rules if you decide to foul.

## A
- **The case for fouling:** you remove the only shot that can tie the game. They make two free throws and are down one, then must foul you and hope; or they miss the first deliberately and need a tip-in. You have converted a roughly one-in-three event into a chain of much less likely ones.
- **The case against:** the risk is concentrated in *execution*. Fouling in the act of shooting a three gives up three free throws — a chance to tie with no time off the clock at all. And a scramble foul can turn into an and-one.
- **The honest answer: fouling is correct if and only if your team can execute it cleanly.** With disciplined players it is the better play; with a team that will foul a jump shooter, playing straight-up defense on the arc is safer.
- **Execution rules if you foul:**
  - Foul **immediately on the catch or on the dribble**, well before anyone rises — around 6 to 7 seconds, never with 2 left.
  - Foul the **ball handler**, and only him, with a clear two-hand wrap — no swipes near a shooter's arms.
  - Foul a player **away from the three-point line** if you can choose, and never a player already in shooting motion.
  - After the free throws, know your inbounds plan and your own bonus situation.
- **The other half nobody rehearses:** do not foul with under about 3 seconds. At that point there is no time for them to complete the miss-and-tip sequence, and the free throws just hand them a chance you did not have to give.

## Q zh
你领先三分，还剩 8 秒，对手持球且没有暂停。你该不该在他们投三分之前先犯规？请给出正反两方的理由，并说明如果决定犯规该怎么执行。

## A zh
- **支持犯规的理由：** 你把唯一能追平比赛的那一种出手直接删掉了。他们两罚全中就是落后一分，然后必须犯规并祈祷；或者故意罚丢第二球去抢补篮。你把一个大约三分之一概率的事件，变成了一串概率低得多的连锁事件。
- **反对犯规的理由：** 风险集中在**执行**上。对正在投三分的人犯规会送出三次罚球——一次不消耗任何时间的追平机会。而混乱中的犯规还可能变成 and-one（打成加罚）。
- **诚实的答案：只有当你的球队能干净利落地执行时，犯规才是对的。** 球员纪律好，它就是更优解；如果这支球队会对跳投的人犯规，那么老老实实在三分线上防守更安全。
- **决定犯规时的执行规则：**
  - **在接球或运球的瞬间**就犯规，远早于任何人起跳——大约在还剩 6 到 7 秒时，绝不要拖到剩 2 秒。
  - 只对**持球人**犯规，用清楚的双手抱住，不要在射手手臂附近去掏。
  - 如果可以选，就对**远离三分线**的球员犯规，绝不对已经进入投篮动作的人犯规。
  - 罚球之后，要清楚你的发球预案和自己这边的犯规罚球状态。
- **没人演练的另一半：** 剩不到大约 3 秒时不要犯规。那时他们已经没有时间完成"故意罚丢再补篮"这套流程，罚球只是白送一个你本不必给的机会。
