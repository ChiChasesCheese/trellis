## Q
Your players never make the extra pass. Compare telling them to pass more with imposing a constraint, and give the test that separates a constraint that teaches from one that just makes the game harder.

## A
**Telling** produces compliance while you are watching and disappears in a game, because the player has an instruction rather than a reason. A **constraint** is a rule change that makes the read you want the *easiest way to succeed*, so the player discovers it and owns it. For the extra pass: a two-dribble limit, or "a basket counts double after three passes", or defending 4-on-3 so the open man is always obvious.

**The test:** does the constraint make the target behaviour the **path of least resistance**, or merely punish its absence?

- Good: two-dribble limit — driving into traffic stops working, so passing early becomes the simple solution.
- Bad: "every missed shot is ten push-ups" — this adds cost without changing which option looks best, so it produces anxiety and slower decisions rather than better ones.

A constraint should be removed once the behaviour survives without it; a constraint you never take off has become a different game.

## Q zh
你的球员从不多传那一下。把"叫他们多传"和"设置一个 constraint（限制）"做对比，并给出区分"能教会东西的限制"和"只是让比赛更难的限制"的判据。

## A zh
**用嘴说**只在你盯着的时候有效，到了比赛就消失，因为球员拿到的是一条指令，不是一个理由。**Constraint** 是一条规则改动，它让你想要的那个 read 变成*最容易成功的方式*，于是球员自己发现它、并且把它变成自己的。针对多传一下：两次运球上限，或者"传满三次之后进球算双倍"，或者打 4 打 3 让空位永远显而易见。

**判据：**这条限制是让目标行为成为**阻力最小的路径**，还是仅仅惩罚它的缺席？

- 好的：两次运球上限 —— 往人堆里冲不再管用，于是早传球成了最简单的解法。
- 坏的："每投失一次做十个俯卧撑" —— 只加了代价，没有改变哪个选项看起来最好，结果是更焦虑、更慢的决策，而不是更好的决策。

行为在没有限制时也能保持后，就应该把限制撤掉；永远不撤的限制，已经变成了另一种运动。
