## Q
You shoot 100 catch-and-shoot threes from the same spot and hit 62. Your friend shoots 100 spread across spots, shot types and cues, and hits 44. Whose session will show up more in Saturday's game, and what is the principle?

## A
**Your friend's**, almost certainly.

The principle is **contextual interference**: practice that varies the task from rep to rep (**random practice**) produces worse performance *during* the session and better retention and transfer *afterwards*, while practice that repeats one task (**blocked practice**) produces the opposite. Blocked reps let you reuse the previous rep's solution; random reps force you to build the solution again each time, which is exactly what a game demands.

The trap is that the feedback is inverted. Blocked practice feels productive because the number is high, and the high number is caused by the very thing that makes it transfer poorly.

**How to use both:** blocked work is genuinely useful when you are changing a pattern and need enough consecutive reps to feel it. As soon as the pattern is stable, switch — and keep the blocked portion the smallest part of the session, not the biggest.

## Q zh
你在同一个点投了 100 记接球三分，进了 62 个。你朋友把 100 次分散在不同位置、不同出手方式和不同信号下，进了 44 个。周六比赛里谁的训练更管用？原则是什么？

## A zh
**你朋友的**，几乎可以肯定。

原则叫 **contextual interference（情境干扰）**：逐次变化任务的训练（**random practice，随机练习**）在训练*当中*表现更差，但在*之后*的保持和迁移上更好；重复同一任务的训练（**blocked practice，块状练习**）则正好相反。块状练习让你复用上一次的解法；随机练习逼你每次重新构建解法，而这正是比赛的要求。

陷阱在于反馈是反的。块状练习感觉很有成效，因为数字很好看，而那个好看的数字恰恰来自让它难以迁移的那个原因。

**两者怎么用：** 当你正在改动作模式、需要足够多的连续重复来找到感觉时，块状训练确实有用。一旦模式稳定，立刻切换 —— 并且让块状部分成为整节课里最小的一块，而不是最大的一块。
