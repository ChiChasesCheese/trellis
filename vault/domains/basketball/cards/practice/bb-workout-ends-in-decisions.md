## Q
Design the shape of a 90-minute solo workout so that it transfers. What goes at the start, what goes at the end, and why is that order not reversible?

## A
**The shape: mechanics → blocked → random → decisions → decisions under fatigue.**

- **Start with mechanics and rhythm** while you are fresh and can feel the pattern. Corrections made tired are corrections to a different movement.
- **A short blocked block** next, only if you are actually changing something. It should be the smallest part of the session.
- **A random block**: vary spot, shot type and start on every rep, so no rep reuses the previous rep's solution.
- **End in decisions**: every rep begins with an external cue you do not control — a timer, a shuffled card, a partner's call — that names the read. Then repeat that block with a conditioning cost attached, because the game's decisions arrive tired.

**Why the order cannot be reversed:** decision reps are the closest thing to a game and therefore the most valuable, so they must land where they still resemble a game — after you are warm, before you are wrecked. And a fresh decision block would be practising an easier version of the problem than the one you actually face in the fourth quarter.

## Q zh
设计一节 90 分钟单人训练的结构，使它能迁移到比赛。开头放什么、结尾放什么？为什么这个顺序不能颠倒？

## A zh
**结构：技术动作 → 块状 → 随机 → 决策 → 疲劳下的决策。**

- **先做动作与节奏**，趁你还清醒、能感觉到模式。累的时候做的修正，修的是另一个动作。
- 接着是**一小段块状训练**，而且只在你确实要改动什么时才做。它应该是整节课里最小的一块。
- **随机段**：每一次重复都换位置、换出手方式、换起手，让任何一次都不能复用上一次的解法。
- **以决策收尾**：每次重复都由一个你控制不了的外部信号启动 —— 计时器、洗过的牌、同伴的口令 —— 由它指定这次要做的 read。然后把这一段带上体能代价再做一遍，因为比赛里的决策都是累着做的。

**为什么不能颠倒：** 决策训练最接近比赛，因而最有价值，所以必须放在它仍然像比赛的位置上 —— 热开之后、垮掉之前。而且状态全满时做的决策段，练的是比第四节实际面对的问题更容易的版本。
