## Q
You have 90 minutes and six players. Argue for spending it on 3-on-3 rather than 5-on-5 half court, in terms of what each player actually gets.

## A
**3-on-3 gives every player far more of the thing you came to train: decisions.**

- **Touches and reads per minute.** With three attackers instead of five, each player is involved in a much larger share of possessions, and each possession contains a decision he has to make rather than watch.
- **Every action is legible.** In 3-on-3 the help defender, the low man and the rotation are visible as individuals — you can see who left, why, and what it cost. In 5-on-5 the same events are buried in traffic.
- **The core two-man game is exposed.** Pick and roll with one spacer is the skeleton of modern offense; 3-on-3 is that skeleton with nothing hiding it.
- **Conditioning comes free** and is specific, since there is nowhere to stand still.

What you give up is genuine: full-side spacing, weak-side help geometry, and the timing of a second rotation. So use 5-on-5 to *test* what 3-on-3 taught, not as the default way to spend the session.

## Q zh
你有 90 分钟和六个人。请从"每名球员实际得到什么"的角度，论证把时间花在 3 打 3 而不是半场 5 打 5 上。

## A zh
**3 打 3 让每个人拿到多得多的、你本来就是为它而来的东西：决策。**

- **每分钟的触球和 read 更多。** 进攻方只有三个人而不是五个，每名球员参与的回合比例大得多，而且每个回合里都有一个必须由他做、而不是由他旁观的决策。
- **每个动作都看得懂。** 3 打 3 里，协防人、low man 和轮转是以个人形态可见的 —— 你能看清谁离开了、为什么、代价是什么。5 打 5 里同样的事件被埋在人堆里。
- **核心的 two-man game 被暴露出来。** 挡拆加一个拉开空间的人，就是现代进攻的骨架；3 打 3 就是这副骨架，没有任何东西遮住它。
- **体能是附赠的**，而且是专项的，因为没有地方可以站着不动。

你放弃的东西是真实的：整侧的 spacing、弱侧协防的几何关系，以及第二次轮转的时机。所以用 5 打 5 去*检验* 3 打 3 教会的东西，而不是把它当成消耗一节课的默认方式。
