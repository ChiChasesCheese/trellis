## Q
"Game speed" reps are supposed to transfer. What makes a rep genuinely game-speed, and why does adding speed alone to a solo drill not achieve it?

## A
A rep is game-speed when it contains the three things a game rep contains, and speed is only one of them:

1. **An external cue you did not choose.** In a game, what you do is decided by a defender. Alone, that must be replaced by something outside your control — a partner's call, a randomized card, a timer, a shot clock. If you decide the move in advance, you have practised execution, not decision.
2. **A real start.** Every game rep begins with a catch on the move, a rebound, or a live dribble against pressure — never a stationary hold at your favourite spot.
3. **A consequence.** A miss costs something (reps, time, score), so the rep carries the arousal a game rep carries.

Speed alone gives you a fast rehearsal of a decision you already made. That is why fast blocked reps still feel great and still fail on Saturday: the missing ingredient is **uncertainty**, not tempo.

## Q zh
"Game speed（比赛速度）"的训练应该能迁移。什么才让一次训练真的算 game-speed？为什么只是把单人训练做快并不能达到？

## A zh
一次训练算 game-speed，是因为它包含比赛回合所包含的三样东西，而速度只是其中之一：

1. **一个不是你选的外部信号。** 比赛里，你做什么是由防守人决定的。一个人练时，必须用你控制不了的东西替代 —— 同伴的口令、随机抽的牌、计时器、shot clock。如果动作是你事先定好的，你练的是执行，不是决策。
2. **一个真实的起点。** 每一次比赛回合都始于跑动中接球、抢下篮板，或者在压迫下的活球运球 —— 从来不是站在你最喜欢的点上握着球。
3. **一个代价。** 投失要付出点什么（组数、时间、比分），这样这次重复才带有比赛回合的那种唤醒水平。

只加速度，得到的是对一个你已经做好的决策的快速排练。这就是为什么又快又整齐的块状训练感觉极好，周六却还是不灵：缺的是 **uncertainty（不确定性）**，不是节奏。
