## Q
You sit down with 40 minutes of your own game footage. Why does watching it "to see how I played" reliably change nothing, and what is the format that does?

## A
Watching without a question turns into highlight-and-lowlight review: you notice the plays with the strongest emotion attached, which are the makes and the embarrassments, and you finish with a mood rather than a finding.

**The format that works:**
1. **Write one question before you press play** — specific enough to be answered from the footage. "Where was the low man on every drive I took?" or "How many of my catches were on the move?" A question you can tally beats a question you can only feel.
2. **Answer it by counting**, possession by possession, including possessions where you never touched the ball.
3. **Pull exactly two clips**: one rep of something you did well under pressure, one rep of the error you repeat most. Two clips get rewatched; a twenty-clip reel does not.
4. **Convert the error into a constraint** for your next session, not into a resolution. "Be more decisive" changes nothing; "two-dribble limit for 20 minutes" changes behaviour.

One question per viewing. A second question means a second viewing.

## Q zh
你坐下来看自己 40 分钟的比赛录像。为什么"看看我打得怎么样"式的观看基本什么都改变不了？什么样的格式才有效？

## A zh
没有问题地看，最后会变成集锦与糗事回顾：你注意到的是情绪最强的那些球，也就是进球和丢人的时刻，看完收获的是一种心情，而不是一个结论。

**有效的格式：**
1. **按下播放前先写下一个问题** —— 具体到可以从画面里回答。"我每次突破时 low man 在哪？"或者"我有多少次接球是在跑动中？"能计数的问题胜过只能体会的问题。
2. **用计数来回答**，一个回合一个回合过，包括那些你根本没碰到球的回合。
3. **只截两段片段**：一段是你在压力下做得好的，一段是你重复最多的那个错误。两段会被反复看；二十段的集锦不会。
4. **把错误变成下一次训练的 constraint**，而不是变成一句决心。"要更果断"什么都不会改变；"接下来 20 分钟两次运球上限"会改变行为。

一次观看只问一个问题。第二个问题意味着第二次观看。
