## Q
You track "makes out of 100" every session. After a bad week you stop logging entirely. What is wrong with that metric, and what should a workout log record so that it survives a bad day?

## A
**What is wrong:** a single make percentage is dominated by day-to-day shooting variance. Over 100 shots the swing from luck alone is large enough to hide any real improvement, so the number mostly reports your mood and the gym's rims. Worse, it is a **verdict**, and people abandon logs that deliver verdicts.

**What to record instead, in this order:**
1. **Inputs you controlled** — sets completed, reps taken, whether the decision block actually happened. This is the part you can be honest about on a bad day.
2. **Decision counts** — how many reps you played the read the cue called for, separately from whether the shot fell.
3. **Shot quality** — how many attempts were on the move, off a real catch, inside your range.
4. **Percentage last**, and read as a trend over weeks, never as a session grade.

A log built this way still has something true to write on your worst day, which is the only property that makes a log last long enough to be useful.

## Q zh
你每节课都记"100 投进了几个"。糟糕的一周之后你干脆不记了。这个指标问题出在哪？训练日志该记什么，才能在状态差的日子里活下来？

## A zh
**问题在于：** 单一的命中率被逐日的投篮波动主导。100 次出手里，仅靠运气造成的摆动就足以盖住任何真实进步，所以这个数字主要反映的是你的心情和球馆的篮筐。更糟的是，它是一个**判决**，而人会抛弃给自己下判决的日志。

**该记的东西，按这个顺序：**
1. **你控制得了的输入** —— 完成了几组、出手多少、决策段到底做没做。这是状态差的日子里你依然能如实写下的部分。
2. **决策计数** —— 有多少次你按信号指定的 read 打了，与球进没进分开记。
3. **出手质量** —— 有多少次是跑动中出手、真实接球后出手、在自己射程之内。
4. **命中率放最后**，而且按周看趋势，绝不当作单节课的评分。

这样构建的日志，在你最糟的一天也仍然有真实的东西可写 —— 而这是让一份日志活得够久、久到有用的唯一属性。
