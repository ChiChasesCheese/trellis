## Q
Same shooter, same spot, one shot in rhythm and one rushed. What physically differs between them, and what do you control *before* the ball arrives?

## A
- **Rhythm means the lower body was already loading when the ball arrived.** The catch happens *inside* the shooting motion, not before it — feet, dip and rise are one continuous sequence.
- **Rushed means the sequence is inverted:** hands catch, *then* the feet try to organize, *then* the shot fires late. The upper body compensates with arm speed, the release point drops, and the shot comes out flat and short. Short-and-flat is the signature of late legs, not of bad aim.
- **Pre-catch control 1 — feet.** Move to the ball and hop or 1-2 *into the shot*, so your footwork on arrival is already the shot's footwork, not a preparation for it.
- **Pre-catch control 2 — hands.** Hands up as a target, ready to receive at shooting height, so the ball does not have to travel up your body.
- **Pre-catch control 3 — the decision.** Know before the pass leaves whether you are shooting it. Deciding after the catch is what produces the extra beat, and that beat is the whole difference.
- **Diagnostic:** if you feel your arms working hard, your legs arrived late. A rhythm shot feels like the arms did almost nothing.

## Q zh
同一个射手、同一个位置，一次是 in rhythm（有节奏）的出手，一次是仓促的出手。两者在身体上差在哪？球到手**之前**你能控制什么？

## A zh
- **有节奏，意味着球到手时下肢已经在蓄力。** 接球发生在投篮动作**内部**，而不是在它之前——脚步、下沉、上升是一条连续的序列。
- **仓促，意味着顺序被颠倒了：** 先用手接住，**然后**脚才开始整理，**然后**才慢半拍出手。上肢用手臂速度去补偿，出手点降低，球飞得又平又短。又平又短是腿到得晚的标志，不是瞄得不准。
- **接球前的控制之一——脚。** 迎着球移动，用 hop 或 1-2 步**踩进投篮**，让你落地时的脚步本身就是投篮的脚步，而不是投篮的准备动作。
- **接球前的控制之二——手。** 手举起来当靶子，在出手高度上迎球，这样球不必再沿着你的身体往上走一趟。
- **接球前的控制之三——决定。** 在传球出手之前就知道自己要不要投。接球之后才决定，正是那多出来的半拍的来源，而这半拍就是全部差别。
- **自检：** 如果你感觉手臂很费劲，说明腿到晚了。有节奏的一投，感觉上手臂几乎没出力。
