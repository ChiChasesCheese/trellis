## Q
Explain the inside-hand rule at the rim, and describe the exact defensive situation for which the floater — not a layup, not a pull-up — is the correct finish.

## A
- **The inside hand is the one farther from the defender.** Driving right along the baseline with a defender on your left shoulder, the right hand is the inside hand.
- Finishing with it puts the ball on the far side of the rim from the shot blocker and turns your torso into a shield. Using the outside hand carries the ball back **across** his contest, which is how good finishers get blocked from behind.
- **The floater exists for one specific gap:** the help defender is between you and the rim but has *not yet committed*. He is too close for a layup — he would meet you at the rim — and too far for a pull-up, because he would close out on it. That band is roughly **5 to 12 feet**.
- It is released early and high, usually off one foot, **before the help defender can rise**. That is why it beats a rim protector who is taller and quicker off the floor than you: you are not competing with his jump, you are beating its timing.
- **The floater is also a decision point, not just a shot.** If the big fully commits to you, the roller or the man in the dunker spot behind him is now open, and the same motion becomes a drop-off pass. Practice both out of the identical gather.

## Q zh
解释篮下的 inside hand（内侧手）原则，并描述在什么样的确切防守情境下，正确的终结是 floater（抛投）——而不是上篮，也不是急停跳投。

## A zh
- **inside hand 是离防守人更远的那只手。** 沿底线右侧突破、防守人在你左肩，那么右手就是内侧手。
- 用它终结，球会落在离盖帽人更远的那一侧篮筐，而你的躯干变成了盾牌。用外侧手则会把球**横穿**过他封盖的路线——这就是终结能力不错的人被人从背后掏掉的原因。
- **floater 只为一种特定缝隙存在：** 协防人挡在你和篮筐之间，但**还没有完全出击**。上篮嫌他太近——他会在篮下等到你；急停跳投嫌他太远——他能扑出来干扰。这个区间大约是 **5 到 12 英尺**。
- 它出手早、弧线高，通常单脚起跳，**赶在协防人起跳之前**。这就是它能打败比你更高、起跳更快的护框人的原因：你不是在和他的弹跳竞争，而是在打败它的时机。
- **floater 同时是一个决策点，不只是一次出手。** 如果内线彻底扑向你，那么他身后的 roller 或站在 dunker spot 的人就空了，同一个动作变成一次 drop-off 传球。用完全相同的收球动作把这两样都练出来。
