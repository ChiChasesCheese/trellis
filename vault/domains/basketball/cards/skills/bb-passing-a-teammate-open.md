## Q
What does "passing a teammate open" mean, and give three concrete forms of it. What is the anti-pattern it is defined against?

## A
- It means **the pass itself creates the advantage.** Where and when you deliver decides whether the receiver catches into a shot or catches into a problem — the same "open man" can be genuinely open or effectively covered depending on the pass.
- **Form 1 — pass to the far hand.** A closeout is coming from one side; put the ball on the hand *away* from that defender. The catch itself moves the ball away from the contest and the receiver can rise or drive without a repositioning dribble.
- **Form 2 — pass ahead of the movement.** Lead a cutter or a drifting shooter to the spot he is going to, not the spot he is in, so he catches without decelerating.
- **Form 3 — pass on time, which nearly always means early.** Throw as the defender turns his head or as he commits to help, not after he arrives. A pass that leaves late arrives into a set defender no matter how accurate it is.
- **The anti-pattern:** the technically correct pass — to a genuinely open man, chest-high, on his inside hand, half a second late. He is open on the film and covered in reality, and the possession dies looking like someone else's missed shot.

## Q zh
"passing a teammate open"（把队友传空）是什么意思？给出三种具体形式。它是针对什么反面模式而定义的？

## A zh
- 它的意思是**传球本身创造了优势。** 你把球送到哪里、什么时候送，决定了接球人是接进一次出手，还是接进一个麻烦——同一个"空位的人"，因传球不同，可能是真的空位，也可能实际上被防死。
- **形式一——传给远侧手。** closeout（扑防）从一侧过来，就把球放在**远离**那名防守人的那只手上。接球这个动作本身就把球带离了干扰，接球人不需要一次调整运球就能起跳或突破。
- **形式二——传到移动的前方。** 领着空切的人或横向拉开的射手，传向他**将要到**的位置，而不是他现在所在的位置，让他不必减速就能接住。
- **形式三——按时传，而按时几乎总是意味着早传。** 在防守人转头的瞬间、或在他决定协防的瞬间出手，而不是等他到位之后。晚出手的传球，无论多准，都会送到一个站好位的防守人面前。
- **反面模式：** 那种技术上完全正确的传球——传给一个确实空位的人，胸口高度，落在他的内侧手上，晚了半秒。他在录像里是空位，在现实里被防死，而这个回合会以"别人投丢了"的样子结束。
