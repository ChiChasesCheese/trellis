## Q
You are cutting up to the wing to receive a pass. What is the difference between catching on your inside foot and your outside foot, which do you want by default, and when is the other one correct?

## A
- "Inside" means toward the middle of the floor, "outside" toward the sideline. The question is **which foot lands first as the ball arrives**, because on a 1-2 catch that foot becomes your pivot.
- **Inside-foot catch (default):** you land already turned toward the basket, with the outside foot free to step. Shot, drive in either direction, and the pass are all in front of you, and the defender cannot pre-commit to one.
- **Outside-foot catch:** your pivot is on the sideline side, so squaring up costs an extra move, and the only comfortable drive is baseline into the short side of the floor. A scouting defender simply sits on that.
- **When outside-foot is right:** when you are deliberately attacking baseline — an empty corner on that side, or a screen arriving from the top — because it lands you already facing the direction you intend to go.
- **When unsure, hop.** Landing on both feet preserves both pivots and costs you only a fraction of the rhythm; the one thing you must never do is catch flat-footed and then organize your feet.

## Q zh
你正向侧翼跑动接球。用 inside foot（内侧脚）接球和用 outside foot（外侧脚）接球有什么区别？默认该用哪个？另一个什么时候才正确？

## A zh
- "内侧"指朝向球场中路，"外侧"指朝向边线。问题在于**球到手时哪只脚先落地**，因为在 1-2 步接球里，那只脚会成为你的中枢脚。
- **内侧脚接球（默认）：** 你落地时身体已经转向篮筐，外侧脚可以自由迈步。投篮、向两个方向突破、传球全都在你面前，防守人无法提前赌任何一样。
- **外侧脚接球：** 中枢脚在靠边线一侧，所以转正面框要多做一个动作，而唯一顺手的突破方向是走底线、冲向场地更窄的那一半。有准备的防守人直接守死这一点就行。
- **什么时候外侧脚才对：** 当你就是要打底线时——那一侧底角是空的，或者掩护从上方过来——因为它让你落地时已经面向你打算去的方向。
- **拿不准就用 hop。** 双脚同时落地保住两个中枢脚，只损失一点点节奏；唯一绝对不能做的，是平脚接球之后再去整理脚步。
