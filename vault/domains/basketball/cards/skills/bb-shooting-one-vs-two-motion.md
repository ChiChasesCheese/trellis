## Q
One-motion and two-motion shooting — what physically differs between them, and which situations does each one fit?

## A
- **Two-motion:** the ball rises to a set point, the legs' lift finishes, and *then* the arms release. The jump and the shot are separable events — the classic high-release jump shot.
- **One-motion:** the ball never stops. Leg drive flows straight through into the arms and the ball is released on the way up, out of a shorter dip.
- **One-motion fits** quick releases off the catch, deep threes, and smaller players who must beat a hard closeout — because the legs are feeding the shot, range costs you nothing extra.
- **Two-motion fits** shooting over a contest and off-balance or contested midrange looks: the higher release point clears a hand, and separating the jump from the release gives you control when you land into the shot with speed.
- **Not a moral choice.** Pick one, keep the lower body and the dip identical every rep, and do not switch mid-season. The real failure mode is a hybrid — a shooter who is one-motion when fresh and two-motion when tired has two different shots and can trust neither.

## Q zh
one-motion（一次发力）和 two-motion（两段发力）投篮——在身体机制上有什么区别？各自适合什么情境？

## A zh
- **two-motion：** 球先升到一个固定的举球点，腿部的上升先结束，**然后**手臂才出手。起跳和出手是可以分开的两件事——经典的高出手点跳投。
- **one-motion：** 球从不停顿。腿部力量直接贯穿到手臂，球在上升过程中就出手，下沉（dip）幅度更短。
- **one-motion 适合**接球快速出手、超远三分，以及必须打破凶狠 closeout（扑防）的小个球员——因为腿在给投篮供能，射程几乎不额外收费。
- **two-motion 适合**在干扰之上出手，以及失衡或被贴防的中距离：更高的出手点能越过封盖的手，而把起跳与出手分开，能让你带速度冲进投篮时仍有控制力。
- **这不是道德选择。** 选一种，让下肢和 dip 每一次都完全相同，赛季中途不要改。真正的失败模式是混合体——体力充沛时是 one-motion、疲劳时变成 two-motion 的射手，等于有两套投篮，哪一套都信不过。
