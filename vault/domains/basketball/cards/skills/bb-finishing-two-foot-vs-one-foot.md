## Q
You are driving into the paint. What decides whether you gather onto two feet or take off from one, and what does each choice buy you?

## A
- **One-foot takeoff converts speed into distance and height.** It gets you past a defender who is behind or beside you, and it is how you finish *before* the help arrives. The cost: you are committed in the air, balance is poor, and contact knocks you off your line.
- **Two-foot takeoff (jump stop) sacrifices speed to buy balance.** It gives you verticality, contact absorption, a pump fake, and the ability to go straight up and finish with either hand.
- **The read is where the help is.** No help committed → one foot, get there first. Help already waiting at the rim → two feet, land into him, go up through the contact and take the foul.
- Two feet also preserves **a legal way to change your mind**: from a jump stop you can still pass out, pivot, or use the up-and-under. From a one-foot takeoff you have already chosen.
- **Rule of thumb:** one foot beats a defender *to* the rim; two feet beats the defender who is *already there*.

## Q zh
你正突入禁区。什么决定你是收球起两只脚，还是单脚起跳？两种选择各自换来什么？

## A zh
- **单脚起跳把速度转化为距离和高度。** 它能让你摆脱身后或身侧的防守人，是在协防到位**之前**完成终结的方式。代价：你在空中已经无法更改，平衡差，一次对抗就把你撞离线路。
- **双脚起跳（jump stop）牺牲速度来换平衡。** 它带来垂直起跳、吸收对抗的能力、假动作，以及原地起跳、左右手皆可的终结。
- **要读的是协防在哪。** 没有协防上来 → 单脚，抢在他们之前到。协防已经在篮下等着 → 双脚，撞进他身上，顶着对抗上去把犯规拿下。
- 双脚还保留了**一条合法的反悔路径**：从 jump stop 你仍然可以分球、转身，或者用假上篮再收（up-and-under）。单脚起跳一旦离地，选择已经做完了。
- **经验法则：** 单脚是**抢在**防守人之前到篮下；双脚是打**已经站在那里**的防守人。
