## Q
A second defender digs at the ball from your blind side while you are dribbling in traffic. What are the mechanical rules that keep the ball, and what is the read that avoids the situation entirely?

## A
- **Dribble low and outside your own foot**, not in front of your body. The ball belongs in the space your torso is already shielding, never on the line between the two defenders.
- **Off-arm up as a bar** — forearm across, not an extended push-off — between the ball and the digger, and turn your shoulders so the side he is reaching through is your back half.
- **Change hands away from pressure, and do it behind or beside you.** A behind-the-back or between-the-legs keeps the ball in shielded space; a front crossover sweeps it across your body, straight through the digger's reach.
- **Keep the dribble alive.** A live dribble is your only escape from a double; picking it up in traffic is exactly how a dig turns into a turnover, because now they can swarm with no consequence.
- **The read that prevents it:** the defender who digs is the one who is a pass away with his head turned toward the ball and his weight on the inside foot. See him before you drive, then either drive away from him or pass to the man he is ignoring.

## Q zh
你在人堆里运球时，第二名防守人从你的视线盲侧来掏球（dig）。哪些力学规则能保住球？什么样的阅读能让这个局面根本不发生？

## A zh
- **把球运得低，并运在自己脚的外侧**，不要运在身体正前方。球应该待在你躯干已经遮蔽住的空间里，绝不能落在两名防守人之间的连线上。
- **非持球手抬起来当横杠**——是前臂横挡，不是伸直了推人——挡在球和掏球者之间，同时转动肩膀，让他伸手的那一侧成为你的背侧。
- **朝远离压力的方向换手，并且在身后或身侧换。** 背后运球或胯下运球把球留在被遮蔽的空间里；体前变向则把球从身前扫过去，正好穿过掏球者的手臂范围。
- **保持活球。** 活球是你摆脱包夹的唯一出路；在人堆里收球，正是 dig 变成失误的方式，因为此时他们可以毫无代价地围上来。
- **能预防它的阅读：** 会来掏球的那个人，是隔一个传球位置、头转向球、重心压在内侧脚上的那名防守人。突破之前先看到他，然后要么朝远离他的方向突，要么把球传给他正在忽视的那个人。
