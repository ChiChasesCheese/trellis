Each pass in the menu is opened by one specific defensive reaction. The {{c1::pocket pass}} — a low bounce delivered past the hip of a big who is hedging or dropping, thrown into the gap before the help commits. The {{c2::skip pass}} — cross-court over the top, opened the moment two or more defenders sink toward the ball side. The {{c3::wrap-around}} — thrown around your own body past a defender's front leg, the answer on a baseline drive when the low man steps up in front of you. The {{c4::lob}} — thrown over a defender who is beneath or fully in front of a roller or a sealing post. The {{c5::drop-off}} — a short pass from under the rim to the dunker spot, opened when the help defender leaves that man to meet your drive.

## zh
传球菜单里的每一种，都由一种特定的防守反应打开。{{c1::pocket pass}}（口袋传球）——低手击地球，从做 hedge（外弹）或 drop（沉退）的内线髋部旁边送出，在协防合拢之前塞进缝隙。{{c2::skip pass}}（跨场转移）——横跨球场、越过头顶的传球，在两名或更多防守人沉向强侧的那一刻打开。{{c3::wrap-around}}（绕身传球）——绕过自己身体、从防守人前腿旁边送出，是底线突破时 low man 上前挡在你面前的答案。{{c4::lob}}（吊传）——越过位于 roller 或落位内线下方、或完全绕前的防守人。{{c5::drop-off}}（篮下分球）——从篮下短距离传给 dunker spot 的人，在协防人离开他来堵你的突破时打开。
