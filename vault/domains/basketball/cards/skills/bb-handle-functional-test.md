## Q
What separates a functional handle from dribbling for its own sake? Give a test you can apply to any dribble you made in a game.

## A
- **The test:** did that dribble move a defender, change an angle, or advance the ball? If none of the three, it was a wasted possession-second and the defense got a free tenth to recover.
- **Functional handle is a small vocabulary executed at game speed with the head up:** change of pace, change of direction, a between-the-legs or behind-the-back to protect the ball while changing hands, and a retreat dribble to reset an angle. That is most of it.
- **Highlight handle chains moves; game handle uses one move and finishes.** Every extra move hands the help another tenth to arrive and adds another chance to lose the ball.
- **Dribbling is also the slowest way to move the ball.** A pass covers fifteen feet in a fraction of the time a dribble does, so a dribble should only be spent on something a pass cannot do — creating an angle, drawing a second defender, or getting to the rim.
- **Consequence for practice:** most of your handle work should be one or two dribbles into a decision (shot, pass, finish), not eight dribbles into nothing. Reps that end in a move rather than a decision train the wrong thing.

## Q zh
什么区分了"有功能的控球"和"为运球而运球"？给出一个可以用来检验你比赛中每一次运球的标准。

## A zh
- **检验标准：** 那次运球是否调动了一名防守人、改变了一个角度，或者推进了球？三者皆无，它就是被浪费掉的一秒回合时间，而防守白得了十分之一秒来回位。
- **有功能的控球是一套很小的词汇，用比赛速度、抬着头完成：** 变速、变向、用胯下或背后在换手时护球，以及用后撤运球重置角度。基本就这些。
- **集锦式控球把动作串起来；比赛式控球用一个动作然后终结。** 每多一个动作，就多给协防十分之一秒到位的时间，也多一次丢球的机会。
- **运球还是移动球最慢的方式。** 传球跑完十五英尺所用的时间，只是运球的零头，所以运球只该花在传球做不到的事情上——创造角度、吸引第二名防守人，或者杀到篮下。
- **对训练的推论：** 你大部分的控球训练应该是一到两次运球接一个决策（投、传、终结），而不是运八下什么也不做。以动作而不是以决策结尾的重复，练的是错误的东西。
