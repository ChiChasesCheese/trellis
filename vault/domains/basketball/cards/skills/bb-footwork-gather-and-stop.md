## Q
You are driving with a live dribble and want to stop and shoot. What is the gather, and how does choosing a 1-2 stop instead of a hop change what you are allowed to do next?

## A
- The **gather** is the moment you stop dribbling and take control of the ball with your hands. It is the boundary between dribbling rules and pivot rules: the step on which you collect the ball, plus two steps after it, is legal.
- Those two steps can be spent two ways. A **1-2 stop** puts them down as separate footfalls; a **hop** (jump stop) lands both feet simultaneously.
- **1-2 stop:** the first foot down becomes your pivot foot. You keep a pivot, so a step-through, a shot-fake-and-go, or a spin is all still available, and you rise into the shot faster because you are already stepping into it. This is the standard for a pull-up.
- **Hop:** both feet land together, so *neither* is the pivot until you lift one — meaning you may pivot on **either** foot. That is the better choice when you do not yet know which side the help came from.
- **The trade-off in one line:** the 1-2 buys rhythm and speed into the shot; the hop buys a choice of pivot foot and a wider, more balanced base against contact.

## Q zh
你正带球突破，想急停出手。gather（收球）是什么？选择 1-2 stop 而不是 hop，会怎样改变你接下来被允许做的事？

## A zh
- **gather** 是你停止运球、用手完全控制住球的那一刻。它是运球规则与中枢脚规则的分界线：收球的那一步，加上之后的两步，是合法的。
- 这两步可以有两种花法。**1-2 stop**（一二步急停）把它们踩成先后两下；**hop**（跳步急停）则两脚同时落地。
- **1-2 stop：** 先落地的那只脚成为中枢脚。你保留了一个中枢，所以跨步（step-through）、投篮假动作再走、转身都还在，而且因为你本来就是踩进去的，起跳出手更快。这是干拔跳投的标准做法。
- **hop：** 两脚同时落地，所以在你抬起一只脚之前**哪只都不是**中枢脚——也就是说你可以用**任意一只**脚做中枢。当你还不知道协防从哪一侧来时，这是更好的选择。
- **一句话权衡：** 1-2 stop 换来的是投篮的节奏和速度；hop 换来的是中枢脚的选择权，以及对抗中更宽、更稳的支撑面。
