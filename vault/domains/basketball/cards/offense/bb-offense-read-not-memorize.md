## Q
Team A runs twelve set plays. Team B runs four rules: pass and cut, fill the open spot, drive when your defender turns his head, and screen away when the ball leaves your side. Both teams have the same players. Why does Team B usually score more against a good defense, and where does Team A's approach genuinely win?

## A
- **Sets are answers to questions the defense has not asked yet.** A play is decided before the possession; a rule is decided during it. Against a defense that switches, traps or simply guards the first option, a play must be abandoned, while a rule-based offense never had a plan to abandon.
- **Rules scale with defensive reaction.** "Drive when your defender turns his head" produces an advantage exactly when one exists, which no scripted cut can promise.
- **Reading is also faster.** The half-second decision window is impossible if you are recalling a diagram. Players in a read offense are looking at defenders; players in a set are looking at spots.
- **Where sets genuinely win:** (1) after timeouts and in inbounds situations, where surprise and preparation are the whole advantage; (2) to manufacture a specific matchup or get one player a specific shot; (3) as a *starting* shape for a team that cannot yet space itself, since rules require judgment that inexperienced players do not have.
- **The mature answer is both:** a called entry that gets you into an advantage, and then rules for what happens next. Most good modern offenses are a set for the first four seconds and a read offense for the remaining eighteen.
- **The tell that a team is memorizing:** the possession dies when the first option is covered, and everyone looks at the coach.

## Q zh
A 队跑十二套固定战术。B 队跑四条规则：传球后就切、填补空出来的位置、防守人转头时就突、球离开你这一侧时就去给远端做掩护。两队球员完全一样。为什么面对好防守时通常是 B 队得分更多？A 队的做法又在哪里确实占优？

## A zh
- **战术套路是在回答防守还没问出口的问题。** 套路在回合开始前就定好了；规则在回合进行中才产生。面对会换防、会包夹、或者只是守住了第一选择的防守，套路必须被放弃，而基于规则的进攻从来就没有可放弃的计划。
- **规则会随着防守反应自动缩放。** "防守人转头时就突"恰好在优势存在时才产出优势，这是任何被编排好的空切都无法保证的。
- **读位也更快。** 如果你还在回忆战术图，那半秒的决定窗口根本不可能达成。打读位进攻的球员在看防守人；跑套路的球员在看位置点。
- **套路确实占优的地方：** (1) 暂停之后和发球球，那里出其不意和事先准备就是全部优势；(2) 用来制造某个特定对位，或给某名球员一个特定的出手；(3) 作为一支还拉不开空间的球队的**起始**形状，因为规则需要判断力，而没经验的球员还不具备。
- **成熟的答案是两者兼有：** 用一个喊出来的启动方式先制造优势，然后用规则决定接下来发生什么。大多数优秀的现代进攻，前四秒是套路，剩下的十八秒是读位进攻。
- **一支球队在背战术的迹象：** 第一选择被守住，回合就死了，然后所有人都去看教练。
