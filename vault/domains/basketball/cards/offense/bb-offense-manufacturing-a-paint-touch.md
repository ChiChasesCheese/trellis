## Q
It is a half-court possession, the defense is set, and nobody can beat their man off the dribble. Name four ways to manufacture a paint touch anyway, and say what each one costs.

## A
- **A cut.** A basket cut or a backdoor from the weak side is the cheapest paint touch in basketball — it needs no screen, no skill advantage, and only a defender who turns his head. **Cost:** nothing, except that a cut must *end* (through to the rim or out to the corner); a cutter who stops in the lane kills the spacing he was supposed to exploit.
- **A post entry or a duck-in.** Getting the ball on the block with a body sealed inside moves the whole defense inward. **Cost:** it is slow, and it lets the defense load up if the post cannot pass out of a double.
- **A dribble handoff or a screen that gets you *downhill*.** The point is not the screen; it is the angle it creates toward the middle of the floor. **Cost:** the ball handler must actually turn the corner rather than settle behind it.
- **A short-roll or a drive off a swing to the weak side.** Reversing the ball twice pulls a defense two rotations sideways, and the third attack goes into a defense that is no longer set. **Cost:** time — this is a possession that ends at about 8 on the shot clock.
- **The frame to remember:** a paint touch does not require a beaten defender; it requires **displacement**. A pass into the lane, a body sealed inside, or a cut behind a distracted defender all count the same as a blow-by.
- **What kills all four:** a full lane. Every one of these needs an empty paint to arrive in, which is why the offensive players standing nearest the rim decide whether any of it is available.

## Q zh
这是一个阵地战回合，防守已经站好，而且没有人能靠运球过掉自己的对位人。请说出四种依然能制造出 paint touch（禁区触球）的方法，以及每一种的代价。

## A zh
- **空切。** 从弱侧的篮下空切或 backdoor（反跑）是篮球里最便宜的 paint touch——不需要掩护、不需要技术优势，只需要一个转头的防守人。**代价：** 几乎没有，只是空切必须**走完**（要么切到篮下，要么切出去到底角）；停在禁区里的切入者会毁掉他本该利用的空间。
- **低位传球或 duck-in（内切要位）。** 把球送到低位方框、有人把防守人封在身后，会让整个防守向内收缩。**代价：** 慢；而且如果低位球员在被包夹时传不出球，防守就可以放心压过来。
- **一次 dribble handoff（运球交接）或一个能让你"downhill（顺势向篮）"的掩护。** 重点不是掩护本身，而是它创造出的朝向球场中路的角度。**代价：** 持球人必须真的转过弯去，而不是停在掩护后面。
- **Short roll，或者把球转移到弱侧后的一次突破。** 把球来回转移两次，会把防守横向拉动两次轮转，第三次进攻面对的就是一支不再站好的防守。**代价：** 时间——这种回合大约在进攻时限剩 8 秒时结束。
- **要记住的框架：** paint touch 不需要有人被过掉，它需要的是**位移**。一记传进禁区的球、一个把人封在身后的身体、或者一次在走神防守人身后的空切，效果都等同于一次生吃。
- **能毁掉这四种方法的东西：** 拥挤的禁区。它们每一种都需要一片空的油漆区可以进入——所以站得离篮筐最近的那些进攻球员，决定了以上是否有任何一条可用。
