## Q
You grab a defensive rebound. In the next second you must decide to push the ball or pull it out and set up. What are you actually reading, and when is pushing the *wrong* decision even though transition is the most efficient offense in the game?

## A
- **Read the numbers first, then the matchups, then the personnel.** How many of them are back versus how many of us are ahead of the ball? Equal or better numbers, push. Fewer, and you are running into a set defense at speed, which is worse than a normal half-court possession, not better.
- **Read who is back.** Two defenders back is often still a push if the two are guards and your big is rim running — a numbers-even break with a size advantage at the rim is a good break.
- **Read your own trailer.** If your big is not running, the "break" is three perimeter players against a set paint, and the trailer option that saves stopped breaks does not exist.
- **When pushing is wrong:** (1) the opponent has all five back and organised; (2) your handler is not the one who grabbed the ball and you would be pushing with a poor decision-maker; (3) late in a close game when you need to consume clock, or when you are protecting a lead and a live-ball turnover costs more than a quick two gains; (4) when your team is in foul trouble or badly out-rebounded — a fast possession gives the ball back fast.
- **The decision is the point, not the speed.** The cheap version of this rule: *push every time it is not clearly wrong*, because the base rate of transition efficiency is so much higher — but push into a **shape** (lanes filled, big running), never into a crowd.

## Q zh
你抢下一个防守篮板。接下来一秒钟里你必须决定：是推快攻，还是把球拉出来打阵地战。你实际在读什么？既然转换是全场效率最高的进攻，什么时候推反而是**错**的？

## A zh
- **先读人数，再读对位，最后读人员。** 他们退回去几个人，我们有几个人跑在球前面？人数持平或占优就推。人数吃亏，你就是在全速撞进一支站好位的防守，那比一个普通阵地战回合更差，而不是更好。
- **读退回去的是谁。** 对方两人退防，往往仍然值得推——只要那两人是后卫，而你的大个在 rim run（直冲篮筐）；人数持平但篮下有身高优势的快攻，就是好快攻。
- **读你自己的 trailer。** 如果你的大个没有在跑，那么这次"快攻"就是三名外线打一个站好的油漆区，而那个能挽救被挡下快攻的 trailer 选项根本不存在。
- **什么时候推是错的：** (1) 对手五个人全部退回并组织好了；(2) 抢到球的不是你的持球人，推起来等于让决策差的人带球；(3) 焦灼比赛的末段你需要消耗时间，或者你在保护领先——此时一次活球失误的代价大于一次快速两分的收益；(4) 你们犯规麻烦缠身，或者篮板被打得很惨——快回合会让球很快回到对方手里。
- **重点是这个决定本身，而不是速度。** 这条规则的简化版：**只要不是明显错误就推**，因为转换进攻的基准效率高出太多——但要推进一个**形状**里（跑道站满、大个在跑），绝不要推进一堆人里。
