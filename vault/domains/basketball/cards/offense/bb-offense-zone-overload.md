## Q
Explain what it means to **overload** a zone, why it works, and what the two players on the weak side must do while it happens. Then name the shot the overload is actually trying to create.

## A
- **Overloading means putting more offensive players on one side of the floor than the zone has defenders assigned to it** — for example four attackers on the right side against a 2-3's two-and-a-half defenders on that side: corner, wing, short corner and high post.
- **Why it works: a zone defends areas with a fixed number of bodies.** Add a body and someone must guard two areas, which is the only failure state a zone has. It also creates short passes between adjacent attackers, so the ball moves faster than the zone can slide.
- **The weak side is not idle.** One player stays high at the top or opposite slot as the **skip target** — the entire overload exists to make the skip pass across the top available, because that pass forces the longest closeout on the floor. The other player is usually the weak-side rebounder, positioned where nobody has boxed him out (zones box out by area, badly).
- **The shot it is trying to create is not on the overload side.** You overload right in order to shoot left. The overload's job is to pull the zone's shape sideways; the payoff is the skip pass to the opposite wing or corner, or the offensive rebound on that side.
- **The second payoff:** after the skip, the zone rotates hard to that side, and now a *second* quick pass or drive back into the middle finds a zone that is scrambling rather than shaped.
- **The most common execution error:** overloading and then shooting on the overload side, which is where the defense is densest — it looks like the offense worked because there is a shot, but you have taken a contested one against the strong part of the zone.

## Q zh
请解释 **overload（堆边）** 联防是什么意思、为什么有效、以及在此期间弱侧那两名球员必须做什么。然后说出 overload 真正想创造的是哪一种出手。

## A zh
- **Overload 的意思是在场上一侧放比联防分配到那一侧更多的进攻球员**——例如面对 2-3 联防在那一侧的两个半名防守人，你在右侧放四名进攻人：底角、侧翼、short corner 和高位。
- **为什么有效：联防用固定数量的身体去守区域。** 你多加一个身体，就必须有人同时守两个区域，而这正是联防唯一的失败状态。它还制造出相邻进攻人之间的短传，让球移动得比联防滑动更快。
- **弱侧并不是闲着。** 一名球员留在弧顶或对侧 slot 做 **skip 的目标点**——整套 overload 存在的意义，就是让那记从弧顶横跨过去的 skip pass 成立，因为那一传逼出全场最长的 closeout 距离。另一名球员通常是弱侧的篮板手，站在没有人卡住他的位置（联防按区域卡位，而且卡得很差）。
- **它想创造的出手并不在 overload 那一侧。** 你堆在右边，是为了在左边出手。Overload 的任务是把联防的形状横向拽走；回报是传向对侧侧翼或底角的那记 skip，或者那一侧的前场篮板。
- **第二重回报：** skip 之后联防会猛地往那一侧轮转，此时**再**来一记快速传球或者回攻中路，面对的就是一支正在混乱救火、而不是保持形状的联防。
- **最常见的执行错误：** 堆完边就在堆边那一侧出手，而那里正是防守最密集的地方——因为有出手，看起来像是进攻奏效了，实际上你是在联防最强的部位投了一记被干扰的球。
