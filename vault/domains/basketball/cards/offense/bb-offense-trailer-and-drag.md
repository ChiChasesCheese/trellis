## Q
Your break is stopped: the defense got three men back and the paint is walled off. The **trailer** — your big arriving late behind the ball — is now the most important player on the floor. Why, and what are his two options?

## A
- **Why: he is the only man the defense has not accounted for.** Transition defenders match up from the rim outward, so the last attacker to arrive is guarded last, usually by a defender who is still turning around.
- **Option 1 — the drag screen.** He sets a ball screen on the move, near the top of the floor, before the defense has settled into its half-court coverage. This is the most valuable ball screen in basketball because the defenders have not communicated a coverage, are out of position, and cannot switch cleanly — you are running a pick-and-roll against a defense that has not decided how it guards pick-and-roll.
- **Option 2 — the trail three or the trail attack.** If the defense has collapsed to wall off the paint, he catches behind the ball at the arc against a defender who is still retreating.
- **What the ball handler owes him: keep the dribble alive and be patient for one beat.** A handler who picks the ball up or shoots at 20 seconds erases the trailer entirely.
- **The wider point: transition does not end when the layup is not there.** The most efficient half-court possessions are the ones that flow directly out of a stopped break, because the defense is still assembling. Pulling the ball out and calling a set surrenders that.

## Q zh
你们的快攻被挡下了：对方有三个人退了回来，油漆区被封死。此时 **trailer**——从球后方晚到的大个——成了场上最重要的球员。为什么？他有哪两个选择？

## A zh
- **为什么：他是防守唯一还没算进去的人。** 转换防守是由内向外去 match up（对位）的，所以最后到达的进攻者最后才被防到，而且通常是被一名还在转身的防守人防。
- **选择 1——drag screen（跟进挡拆）。** 他在移动中、在球场偏上的位置做一个挡拆，此时防守还没落位到半场防守体系里。这是篮球里最有价值的一次挡拆，因为防守人还没沟通好用哪种 coverage、站位不对、也没法干净地换防——你是在对着一支还没决定如何防挡拆的防守打挡拆。
- **选择 2——跟进三分或跟进突破。** 如果防守已经收缩去封油漆区，他就在球后方的弧线外接球，面对一名还在后退的防守人。
- **持球人欠他的：保持活球，并且多耐心一拍。** 一个把球收起来、或者在还剩 20 秒时就出手的持球人，等于把 trailer 整个抹掉。
- **更大的道理：快攻并不在上篮机会消失时结束。** 效率最高的阵地战回合，正是那些从被挡下的快攻里直接流出来的回合，因为防守还在组装当中。把球拉出来重新喊战术，等于把这份优势交还回去。
