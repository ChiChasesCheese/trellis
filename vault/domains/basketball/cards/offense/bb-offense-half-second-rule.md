## Q
Your team's rule is "**0.5**" — shoot, pass or drive within half a second of catching the ball. Why does such an arbitrary-sounding number improve an offense, what does it forbid, and what is the one thing it is not asking for?

## A
- **What it does: it keeps the defense in rotation.** An advantage created by a drive lasts only as long as defenders are out of position. Every extra tenth of a second a catcher spends deciding is time the defense spends recovering — the ball stopping is the single most common way a good possession dies.
- **What it forbids: the catch-and-survey.** Catching, standing up, looking around, then putting the ball on the floor. That sequence tells the defense exactly where the ball is going and hands them a free reset.
- **How you actually achieve it: decide before the catch, not after.** The half second is not thinking time — it is execution time. The read (where the help is, whether your defender is closing out hard) happens while the ball is in the air.
- **What it is not asking for: speed for its own sake.** 0.5 does not mean shoot quickly or force a drive; a **swing pass** counts as a decision. The rule punishes hesitation, not patience — a deliberate possession made of fast decisions is exactly the point.
- **Why "read, don't memorize" belongs to the same idea:** if you are recalling what comes next in a set, you cannot decide in half a second. Systems built on rules and reads are faster than systems built on plays because the answer is in front of you rather than in your memory.
- **The classic failure it fixes:** ball movement without player movement. Five men swinging the ball around the arc while nobody cuts moves the defense sideways, never inward, and produces a contested three at 6 seconds.

## Q zh
你们队的规则是 "**0.5**"——接到球后半秒内必须投、传或突。为什么这样一个听起来很随意的数字能提升进攻？它禁止什么？它**不**是在要求什么？

## A zh
- **它的作用：让防守始终处在轮转中。** 一次突破创造的优势，只在防守人还没归位期间存在。接球人每多花十分之一秒去做决定，就是防守多花十分之一秒回位——球停下来，是好回合最常见的死法。
- **它禁止的：接球后先观望。** 接球、站直、四下张望，然后才把球放到地上。这一串动作等于告诉防守球要去哪里，并白送他们一次重置。
- **实际怎么做到：在接球之前决定，而不是之后。** 这半秒不是思考时间，而是执行时间。读位（协防在哪、你的防守人 closeout 有多凶）要在球还在空中时完成。
- **它不是在要求：为快而快。** 0.5 不等于赶紧投或者硬突；一次 **swing pass（转移球）** 同样算一个决定。这条规则惩罚的是犹豫，不是耐心——一个由快速决定组成的耐心回合，恰恰是它想要的。
- **为什么"读位而不是背战术"属于同一个概念：** 如果你正在回忆战术的下一步，你就不可能在半秒内做决定。基于规则和读位的体系比基于套路的体系更快，因为答案在你眼前，而不在你的记忆里。
- **它修好的经典毛病：** 只有球在动、人不动。五个人沿着三分线把球倒来倒去而没有人空切，只会让防守横向移动、永远不会向内收缩，最后在剩 6 秒时投出一记有干扰的三分。
