## Q
Coaches call the corner the "release valve" on a drive. What makes that specific spot on the floor so valuable to a driver, and what are the two ways players ruin it?

## A
- **Geometrically, the corner is the furthest point of any defensive rotation.** The help defender who stops your drive is usually the one guarding that corner, and the defender who must replace him has the longest sprint on the floor. Longest closeout means most open shot.
- **It is also the shortest three,** and it sits behind the driver's eyes on a baseline drive — a straight, short, low pass along the baseline, rather than a cross-court throw over recovering hands.
- **And it keeps the low man honest.** A shooter in the corner forces the low man to choose between your layup and that three. If the corner is empty, the low man helps for free and your drive was never a threat.
- **Ruin #1 — the corner drifts up toward the wing.** It feels more involved and it destroys the geometry: now the same defender can guard the corner *and* the paint, and your kickout is a shorter closeout.
- **Ruin #2 — the corner catches and holds.** The corner is the *end* of one rotation and the *start* of the next; catching and surveying gives the second rotation time to arrive. Catch on the hop with feet ready, then shoot, drive the closeout, or swing it immediately.
- **The nuance worth knowing:** the corner is a great place to receive and a poor place to live. A player who stands in the corner all possession is easy to guard — the value comes from being there at the moment of the drive, not from being there all day.

## Q zh
教练把底角称为突破时的 "release valve（泄压阀）"。是什么让场上这个特定位置对突破者如此有价值？球员又有哪两种方式会把它毁掉？

## A zh
- **从几何上说，底角是任何一次防守轮转最远的终点。** 挡住你突破的那名协防人，通常正是防那个底角的人；而必须去顶替他的防守人，要跑全场最长的一段距离。最长的 closeout 意味着最空的出手。
- **它同时是最短的三分**，而且在底线突破时它就在突破者的视线方向上——沿底线传出一记又短又低的直线球，而不是从回追的手上方甩一记横传全场。
- **它还能让 low man 不敢乱动。** 底角有射手，就逼 low man 在你的上篮和那记三分之间二选一。底角空着，low man 就可以免费协防，你的突破从来就不是威胁。
- **毁法一——底角往侧翼上飘。** 那样感觉更参与得上，却毁掉了几何：现在同一名防守人既能防底角**又**能防禁区，而你的外传变成了一段更短的 closeout 距离。
- **毁法二——底角接球后停住。** 底角是一次轮转的**终点**，也是下一次轮转的**起点**；接球后再观望，等于给第二次轮转到位的时间。要跳步接球、双脚准备好，然后立刻投、突 closeout，或者立刻转移。
- **值得知道的细节：** 底角是一个绝佳的接球位置，却是一个糟糕的常驻位置。整个回合都站在底角的球员很好防——它的价值来自你在突破发生的那一刻在那里，而不是一直待在那里。
