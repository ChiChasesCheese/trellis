## Q
The opponent goes full-court press. Your best guard tries to dribble through it, gets trapped near the sideline, and turns it over. Explain why beating a press is a spacing and passing problem rather than a dribbling one, and describe the shape you want.

## A
- **A press is a trap machine, and traps need two things: a dribbler and a boundary.** Every time your handler dribbles into pressure he brings a second defender to the ball for free. Passing forces the defense to re-form; dribbling lets it converge.
- **Spacing is the actual weapon.** Traps only work if two defenders can reach the ball and a third can reach the outlet. Spread the inbounder's options so that no two receivers can be covered by one man — and get the ball inbounded quickly, before the press is set.
- **The shape:** two players up the sideline for width, one player **in the middle of the floor** around the free-throw-line-extended area, and one deep behind the press as the safety valve.
- **The middle is where a press dies.** A catch in the middle of the floor lets you see both sidelines, and the defenders behind the ball are now out of the play — one pass into the middle usually turns a press into a 4-on-3 going the other way.
- **The sideline is where a press feeds.** Never turn your back, never pick up your dribble near a sideline, and never dribble into the corner formed by the sideline and half court — that corner is a third defender.
- **Attack the press, do not survive it.** Teams that only try to advance the ball let the press succeed at its real purpose, which is consuming clock. A press beaten with two passes is a layup, which is why good teams *want* to be pressed.

## Q zh
对手打全场紧逼。你最好的后卫想运球突破它，在边线附近被包夹，丢了球。请解释为什么破紧逼是一个空间和传球问题，而不是运球问题，并描述你想要的阵型形状。

## A zh
- **紧逼是一台制造包夹的机器，而包夹需要两样东西：一个运球的人和一条边界。** 你的持球人每次运球撞进压迫里，就等于免费把第二名防守人招到球边。传球会逼防守重新组织；运球则让它得以合围。
- **空间才是真正的武器。** 只有当两名防守人能够到球、第三名能够到接应点时，包夹才成立。把发球人的接应选择拉开，让任意两名接球人无法被同一个人覆盖——并且要在紧逼站好之前迅速把球发进来。
- **阵型形状：** 两名球员沿两条边线拉开宽度，一名球员站在**球场中路**、大约罚球线延长线附近，还有一名深远地站在紧逼阵型身后做安全阀。
- **中路是紧逼的死亡之地。** 在球场中路接到球，你可以同时看到两条边线，而球身后的防守人已经出局了——一记传进中路的球，通常会把紧逼变成反方向的 4 打 3。
- **边线是紧逼的食粮。** 绝不背身，绝不在边线附近收球，绝不把球运进边线与中场线构成的那个夹角——那个角落就是第三名防守人。
- **要进攻紧逼，而不是熬过它。** 只想着把球推过半场的球队，等于让紧逼实现了它真正的目的：消耗时间。用两记传球破掉的紧逼就是一次上篮，这就是为什么好球队**希望**对方来紧逼。
