## Q
A shot goes up. Should your team crash the offensive glass or retreat in transition defense? Explain why this is a **team** decision made before the shot rather than an individual instinct, and what the trade actually costs.

## A
- **The trade:** every extra crasher raises your offensive-rebound chances and simultaneously raises the opponent's transition efficiency, because he is the man who is not back. You are buying second-chance points with points allowed the other way.
- **Why it must be decided as a team:** if two players crash believing the rule is "everyone crashes" and three retreat believing it is "send one," you get the worst of both — not enough bodies to win the ball, and not enough back to stop the break. The cost of *inconsistency* is larger than the cost of either policy.
- **The common modern policy: send one or two designated crashers plus whoever is already inside, and the guards get back.** The shooter almost never crashes; the two farthest perimeter players are the safety.
- **What moves the dial toward crashing:** you have a size advantage, the opponent is a poor transition team, the shot came from an angle you can predict (your crashers are already on the weak side), or you badly need possessions late in a game you are losing.
- **What moves it toward retreating:** the opponent has an elite transition attack, your shot was a long three that will rebound long past your crashers, or you are protecting a lead where a possession you already have is worth more than one you might win.
- **The one universal:** whichever policy you pick, it should be said out loud before the possession, and the crashers should be moving on the shot, not after — a late crash gets neither the rebound nor the retreat.

## Q zh
一记出手起飞了。你的球队该冲抢前场篮板（crash），还是退回去打转换防守？请解释为什么这是一个在出手之前就定好的**球队**决定，而不是个人本能，以及这笔交易实际的代价是什么。

## A zh
- **这笔交易：** 每多一名冲抢的人，都会提高你们抢到前场篮板的概率，同时提高对手转换进攻的效率，因为他就是那个没退回去的人。你是在用"另一头被得的分"去买二次进攻得分。
- **为什么必须作为球队来决定：** 如果两个人按"所有人都冲"去冲，三个人按"只派一个"去退，你会同时拿到两边最差的结果——抢球的人不够多，退防的人也不够多。**不一致**的代价，比任何一种政策本身的代价都大。
- **常见的现代政策：派一到两名指定冲抢者，加上本来就在里面的人，后卫全部退防。** 投篮的人几乎从不去冲抢；位置最远的两名外线负责安全退防。
- **哪些因素让天平偏向冲抢：** 你们有身高优势、对手转换很差、这记出手的角度可预测（你的冲抢者已经在弱侧），或者你正在落后、末段急需球权。
- **哪些因素让天平偏向退防：** 对手拥有顶级转换进攻、你们投的是一记会弹得很远、越过冲抢者的长三分，或者你正在保护领先——此时一个已经到手的球权，比一个可能抢到的球权更值钱。
- **唯一通用的一条：** 无论选哪种政策，都要在这个回合开始前说出来；而且冲抢的人必须在球出手的瞬间就动，而不是之后——晚到的冲抢既拿不到篮板，也回不了防。
