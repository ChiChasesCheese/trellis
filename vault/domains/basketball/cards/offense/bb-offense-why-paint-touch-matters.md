## Q
Your team's rule is "no shot before a **paint touch**" — the ball must get inside the lane once before you take a jumper. Defend that rule: what does a paint touch physically do to each of the five defenders, and why is a perimeter possession without one usually a bad possession?

## A
- **The on-ball defender** is now behind or beside the ball rather than in front of it, and can no longer contest anything he did not start in front of.
- **The two help defenders on the strong side** must collapse a step or two toward the ball, which lengthens their closeout to their own men by exactly that much.
- **The low man** has to commit to the rim or accept a layup, which vacates the weak-side corner — the shot the whole possession is trying to produce.
- **The last defender** is now guarding a paint that has a body in it, which means he cannot leave to help anyone else. Five defenders have all moved toward one point, and every shooter on the floor got more open without doing anything.
- **Why a possession without one is bad:** a jumper taken against a defense that never moved is a contested shot by definition, and it is also the worst offensive-rebounding situation you can have, because every defender is already inside you. The three-point attempt is the same shot — its value depends entirely on whether the defense was displaced first.
- **The counting version of the rule:** track possessions with a paint touch versus without. The gap in points per possession between them is usually large enough that "get one touch inside" is worth more than any set you could call.

## Q zh
你们队的规则是"没有 **paint touch（禁区触球）** 就不许出手"——球必须先进过一次油漆区，你们才能投跳投。请为这条规则辩护：一次 paint touch 在物理上对五名防守人各自做了什么？为什么没有它的外线回合通常是一个糟糕的回合？

## A zh
- **防球的那名防守人**现在处在球的身后或身侧，而不是身前，他再也干扰不了任何一个他没有一开始就站在前面的出手。
- **强侧的两名协防人**必须朝球收缩一两步，而这恰好把他们回防各自对位人的 closeout 距离拉长了同样多。
- **Low man** 必须选择上来护筐，或者接受一次上篮，而这会空出弱侧底角——那正是整个回合想要生产的出手。
- **最后一名防守人**现在守的是一个里面已经有人的油漆区，也就意味着他不能离开去协防别人。五名防守人全都朝同一个点移动了，场上每一名射手什么都没做就变得更空。
- **为什么没有 paint touch 的回合很糟：** 对着一支从未移动过的防守投出的跳投，按定义就是被干扰的出手；而且它还是你能遇到的最差的前场篮板局面，因为每一名防守人都已经在你里侧。三分出手也是同一回事——它的价值完全取决于防守是否先被调动过。
- **这条规则的计数版本：** 分别统计有 paint touch 和没有 paint touch 的回合。两者每回合得分的差距通常大到——"先在里面碰一次球"比你能喊出的任何一套战术都更值钱。
