## Q
You are designated to crash the offensive glass. A shot goes up from the left wing and your defender turns to box you out. What do you do with your body, where do you go, and what does the flight of the ball tell you before it lands?

## A
- **Move on the shot, not on the miss.** Offensive rebounding is won in the first half second, while the defender is turning to find you. Once he has made contact and established position, you are trying to beat a stronger player with worse leverage.
- **Go around, not through.** The fastest route past a boxing-out defender is a hard step to one side — usually the baseline side, where he has less room to slide — followed by a spin or swim past his hip. Pushing into a set box-out is what the box-out is designed to receive.
- **Use the angle the shot gave you.** A left-wing shot most often comes off to the **right** side of the rim, so your path should already be toward the opposite side of the floor from the shooter. Attack the weak side of the shot, not the strong side.
- **Distance matters as much as side.** Long shots produce long rebounds — off a three, the ball frequently comes out past the free-throw line, into space that the defenders under the rim have already conceded by boxing out inside.
- **Go with two hands and go up, not at the ball with one.** A tipped ball you do not control usually goes to the defense; a two-hand catch is the possession.
- **And know your retreat trigger.** A designated crasher who does not get a hand on the ball must be turning and running by the time the ball is secured — the crash and the sprint back are the same job, not two.

## Q zh
你被指定去冲抢前场篮板。左侧翼投出一记球，你的防守人转身来卡你的位。你的身体该怎么做？你该去哪里？球的飞行在落下之前告诉了你什么？

## A zh
- **在出手瞬间就动，不要等打铁才动。** 前场篮板是在最初的半秒里赢下的，趁防守人还在转身找你。等他完成接触、站稳位置，你就是在用更差的杠杆去赢一个更壮的人。
- **绕过去，不要顶过去。** 越过一名正在卡位的防守人最快的路线，是朝一侧硬迈一大步——通常是底线一侧，那里他横移的空间更小——然后从他胯部旁边转身或探臂穿过。往一个已经站稳的 box out 上顶，正是 box out 被设计来承受的东西。
- **利用出手角度给你的信息。** 左侧翼的出手最常弹向篮筐的**右**侧，所以你的路线一开始就该朝远离投篮者的那一侧走。攻击出手的弱侧，而不是强侧。
- **距离和方向同样重要。** 远距离出手产生长篮板——三分不中时，球经常弹到罚球线以外的空间，而篮下那些在里侧卡位的防守人早已把那片区域让了出来。
- **要双手、向上去拿，不要单手去拨。** 一个你没控制住的拨球通常会落到防守方手里；双手抓下来才算球权。
- **并且要知道自己的退防触发点。** 一名没能碰到球的指定冲抢者，必须在球被控制住的那一刻就已经转身开跑——冲抢和回撤是同一份工作，不是两份。
