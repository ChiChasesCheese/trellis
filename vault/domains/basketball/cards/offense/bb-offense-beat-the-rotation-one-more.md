## Q
You drive baseline from the right wing, the weak-side low man steps up to stop you, and you kick to the weak-side corner. The corner shooter is closed out on hard by the next rotating defender. Why did that happen, where is the actually-open man, and what should the corner do?

## A
- **Why it happened: the defense has an answer to the first kick.** The low man helping is rotation one; the defender sprinting down to the corner is rotation two. A well-drilled team has both, so the first pass out of a drive is usually contested by design.
- **Where the open man is: one pass further, behind the second rotation.** The defender who closed out on the corner came from somewhere — usually the weak-side wing or slot. That spot is now empty, and it is where the shot is.
- **So the corner's job is not to shoot into a hard closeout — it is to make the next decision fast.** Catch ready, and either swing it up to the vacated wing/slot immediately ("one more"), or attack the closeout's outside shoulder for a straight-line drive into a defense that is still rotating.
- **The rule: the advantage lives one pass ahead of the ball.** The defense rotates to where the ball *is*; the offense should be passing to where the defense *is not yet*. That is why a drive plus two passes beats a drive plus one against a good team, and why the same second pass is wasteful against a defense that never rotated.
- **What the drive owes this:** you have to actually threaten the rim. A drive that stops at twelve feet does not move the low man, and if the low man does not move, nothing behind him opens up. **No help, no kick.**
- **What the other two players owe it:** stay on the arc, one pass apart, and relocate along it as the ball moves so a passing lane always exists — a shooter standing still behind a rotating defender is not open even when he looks open.

## Q zh
你从右侧翼底线方向突破，弱侧的 low man 上来挡你，你把球分给弱侧底角。底角射手立刻被下一名轮转过来的防守人凶狠地 closeout（封堵）。为什么会这样？真正的空位在哪里？底角该怎么做？

## A zh
- **为什么会这样：防守对第一记外传是有准备的。** Low man 上来协防是第一次轮转；冲下来防底角的那个人是第二次轮转。训练有素的球队两次都有，所以突破后的第一记外传通常按设计就是要被干扰的。
- **真正的空位在哪：再往前一传，在第二次轮转的身后。** 那个冲去封堵底角的防守人是从某个地方来的——通常是弱侧的侧翼或 slot（弧顶两侧）。那个位置现在空了，出手机会就在那里。
- **所以底角的任务不是对着凶狠的 closeout 硬投，而是快速做下一个决定。** 接球即准备好，要么立刻把球往上转移到空出来的侧翼/slot（"one more，再多传一次"），要么攻 closeout 者的外侧肩膀，对着还在轮转的防守打一条直线突破。
- **规则：优势活在球的前面一传。** 防守朝球**所在**的位置轮转；进攻应该把球传向防守**还没到**的位置。这就是为什么面对好球队时"一次突破加两次传球"胜过"突破加一传"，也是为什么面对根本没轮转的防守时，同样的第二传只是浪费。
- **突破这一环欠这套东西的：你必须真的威胁到篮筐。** 停在十二英尺处的突破不会调动 low man，而 low man 不动，他身后就什么都不会打开。**没有协防，就没有外传（no help, no kick）。**
- **另外两名球员欠的：** 留在弧线上，彼此相隔一传的距离，并随着球的移动沿弧线重新定位，让传球路线始终存在——一个站着不动、身前有轮转防守人的射手，即使看起来是空位，也不是空位。
