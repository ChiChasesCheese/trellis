## Q
Your team faces a 2-3 zone and responds by passing the ball around the perimeter — wing, top, wing, corner — until someone shoots a three at 5 seconds. Why does this fail, and what does "attack the gaps, don't pass around" mean in practice?

## A
- **Passing around the outside is exactly what a zone is built to survive.** Each perimeter pass moves the zone one short slide; nobody is ever put in conflict, and the shape is intact when the clock runs out. A zone loses only when **one defender has to guard two people**, and that never happens on the perimeter.
- **Gaps mean stepping into the seams between defenders, with the dribble or with your body.** A dribble into the gap between the top guard and the forward forces one of them to step up — and whichever steps up leaves his area.
- **Pass *through* the zone, not around it.** The passes that hurt a zone are the ones into the middle: the high post at the free-throw line, the short corner along the baseline, and the skip pass across the top to the opposite wing. Every one of them makes multiple defenders move at once.
- **The high post is the key seat.** A catch at the free-throw line has the back line facing the wrong way and can hit either corner or the opposite block. Zones are usually beaten from the inside out, not the outside in.
- **Cutters break zones because a zone has no man to follow.** A cut through the middle of a 2-3, or a flash from the weak side to the high post, arrives in an area whose owner is watching the ball.
- **The tell you are doing it wrong:** the ball never enters the paint and every shot is above the break. The tell you are doing it right: the zone's back line is being pulled apart before the shot goes up, and your offensive rebounders are inside it.

## Q zh
你们面对 2-3 联防，采取的对策是在外线倒球——侧翼、弧顶、侧翼、底角——直到有人在剩 5 秒时投出一记三分。为什么这样会失败？"攻缝，别绕着传"在实践中是什么意思？

## A zh
- **在外围绕着传，恰恰是联防被设计来扛住的东西。** 每一次外线传球只让联防滑动一小步；没有任何人被置于冲突之中，时间走完时阵型完好无损。联防只有在**一名防守人必须同时守两个人**时才会输，而这在外线永远不会发生。
- **攻缝的意思是用运球或身体踏进防守人之间的接缝。** 一次运球切进上线后卫与前锋之间的缝隙，会逼其中一人上前——而不管谁上来，他都离开了自己的区域。
- **要穿过联防传球，而不是绕着它传。** 真正伤害联防的传球是传进中路的那些：罚球线的高位、底线上的 short corner，以及从弧顶横跨到对面侧翼的 skip pass。它们每一记都会同时调动多名防守人。
- **高位是关键的那把椅子。** 在罚球线接到球，会让后排三人全部面朝错误的方向，而你可以传向任一底角或对侧低位。联防通常是被由内向外打穿的，而不是由外向内。
- **空切能打破联防，因为联防没有固定的人可追。** 从 2-3 中路穿过的空切，或者从弱侧闪现到高位（flash），到达的是一个"主人正盯着球"的区域。
- **做错了的迹象：** 球从未进过油漆区，每一记出手都在弧顶。**做对了的迹象：** 出手之前联防的后排已经被扯开，而你的前场篮板手站在它里面。
