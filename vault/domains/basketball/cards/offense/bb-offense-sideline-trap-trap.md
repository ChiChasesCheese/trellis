## Q
Against half-court pressure, your handler crosses half court on the right sideline, two defenders converge, and he is stuck with his dribble alive but nowhere to go. Explain the "sideline trap trap" he walked into, and give the three habits that keep a handler out of it.

## A
- **The trap you walked into is geometric.** The sideline is a defender that never gets tired: once you are within a few feet of it, two live defenders plus the line can cover every direction you can go. Half court adds a second line, so the area just over half court on either sideline is the worst real estate on the floor.
- **The pressure defense's whole design is to push you there** — the on-ball defender angles you toward the sideline, and the trap arrives at the boundary, not before it. If you feel yourself being steered, you are already being trapped; the steering *is* the play.
- **Habit 1 — advance the ball in the middle third of the floor.** Take the ball up the middle by default and only use the sideline deliberately.
- **Habit 2 — never pick up your dribble near a line.** A live dribble is your only escape. The single most common press turnover is a handler stopping his dribble in a trap and then trying to pass over two sets of arms.
- **Habit 3 — retreat dribble before the trap closes.** Backing out of pressure toward the middle is not a defeat; it resets the geometry and costs two seconds.
- **What teammates owe:** the nearest player must **come back to the ball** to shorten the pass, and someone must fill the middle. A trapped handler with all four teammates standing still and away from him has no legal escape — the trap succeeded before it arrived.

## Q zh
面对半场压迫，你的持球人沿右侧边线过了中场，两名防守人合围，他球还活着却无处可去。请解释他撞进去的那个"边线包夹陷阱"，并给出让持球人避开它的三个习惯。

## A zh
- **你撞进去的这个陷阱是几何性的。** 边线是一名永远不会累的防守人：一旦你离它只有几英尺，两名活着的防守人加上这条线，就能覆盖你能去的所有方向。中场线又加了第二条线，所以刚过中场、贴着任一边线的那片区域，是全场最糟糕的地段。
- **压迫防守的整套设计就是把你逼到那里**——防球的人把你的角度往边线赶，而包夹是在边界处才到达，不是提前到达。如果你感觉自己正被驱赶，那你已经在被包夹了；驱赶**就是**这套防守本身。
- **习惯 1——在球场中间三分之一区域推进。** 默认从中路把球带上去，只在有意为之时才使用边线。
- **习惯 2——绝不在靠近任何一条线的地方收球。** 活球是你唯一的逃生通道。紧逼下最常见的失误，就是持球人在包夹里把球收起来，然后试图从两对手臂上方传出去。
- **习惯 3——在包夹合拢之前先用退运（retreat dribble）。** 朝中路退出压迫不是失败，它重置了几何关系，代价只有两秒。
- **队友欠的：** 最近的球员必须**迎着球回来**以缩短传球距离，同时必须有人填补中路。一个被包夹的持球人，如果四名队友都站着不动、又都离他很远，那他就没有合法的出路——包夹在到达之前就已经成功了。
