A transition break is five jobs, not five sprints. The **rebounder or inbounder** {{c1::outlets to the sideline immediately and then trails the play as the last man}}. The **ball handler** {{c2::takes the ball up the middle of the floor, because the middle threatens both sides and keeps two defenders honest}}. The **two wings** {{c3::sprint wide to the corners and stay outside the arc until the defense commits — width is what stops one defender covering two}}. The **first big** {{c4::rim runs: a straight sprint to the front of the rim without looking for the ball, which drags the last defender out of the paint even when no pass comes}}. The **trailer** {{c5::arrives behind the ball for a drag screen or a trail three, and is the reason a stopped break still produces a shot}}. The rule underneath all of it: {{c6::run to a spot, not to the ball — a break dies the moment two players occupy the same lane}}.

## zh
一次转换进攻是五份任务，不是五个人瞎冲。**抢下篮板或发球的人** {{c1::立刻把球一传到边线，然后作为最后一人跟进（trail）}}。**持球推进的人** {{c2::从球场中路把球带上去，因为中路同时威胁两侧，能牵制住两名防守人}}。**两个侧翼** {{c3::拉到最宽处冲向底角，并在防守做出选择之前待在三分弧线外——宽度才是让一名防守人无法兼顾两人的原因}}。**第一个大个** {{c4::rim run：不看球，直线冲向篮筐正前方；即使没有传球过来，这也会把最后一名防守人从油漆区里拽出来}}。**Trailer（跟进者）** {{c5::从球后方赶到，做一个 drag screen 或投一记跟进三分，这正是快攻被挡下后仍能产出出手的原因}}。贯穿这一切的规则：{{c6::跑向一个位置，而不是跑向球——两名球员挤进同一条跑道的那一刻，快攻就死了}}。
