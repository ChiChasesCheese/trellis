## Q
**Gyro step（陀螺步）**｜Giannis Antetokounmpo

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-gyro-step.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `start beyond the arc → long Euro-step first step (not a gather) → two more long strides → finish at the rim`
**Read。** defender cannot predict the step pattern or direction, so he is out of position before the shot goes up.
