## Q
**360 spin layup（360度转身上篮）**｜Victor Wembanyama · Nick Young, Ja Morant

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-360-reverse-layup.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `drive into traffic → plant → full 360 spin → finish at the rim`
**Read。** defender and helper lose track of the ball and the takeoff while you rotate.
