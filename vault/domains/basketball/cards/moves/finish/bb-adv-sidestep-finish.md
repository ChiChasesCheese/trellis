## Q
**Sidestep finish（侧步绕过终结）**｜Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-sidestep-finish.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `drive at the defender → gather → sidestep around his body → finish at the rim`
**Read。** defender cannot reset his feet in time to cut off the new angle.
