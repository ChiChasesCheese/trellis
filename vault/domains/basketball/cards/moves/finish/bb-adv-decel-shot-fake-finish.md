## Q
**Decel step then shot fake finish（急停假投终结）**｜Luka Doncic · Anthony Edwards

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-decel-shot-fake-finish.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `fast drive → decel on the second step → shot fake / hang → defender leaves the floor → rise and finish`
**Read。** a chasing defender either flies by or jumps at the fake, so you go up after he has come down.
**Cue。** brake on the second step and let him jump first.
