## Q
**Wrong-foot layup（错脚上篮）**｜Trae Young · Tyrese Maxey, Tony Parker, Steve Nash

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-wrong-foot-finish.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `drive → gather → take off from the same-side foot as the shooting hand → body shields the ball → layup`
**Read。** a shot blocker times the block for the usual left-right takeoff, so he is early or late on the off-pattern jump.
