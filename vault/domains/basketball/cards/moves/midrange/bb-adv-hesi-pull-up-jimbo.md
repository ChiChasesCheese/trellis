## Q
**Hesi pull-up jimbo（犹豫步急停跳投）**｜Kevin Durant

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-hesi-pull-up-jimbo.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `hesitation dribble (optional between-the-legs) → sell the drive → rise for the pull-up`
**Read。** a defender who sinks to cut off the drive gives a long-armed shooter a clean look over the top.
