## Q
**Double step-back（连续后撤步）**｜James Harden

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-double-stepback.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `dribble into the defender → step-back → re-gather → second step-back → shoot`
**Read。** a defender who follows the first retreat step is left a full extra step behind after the second.
**Cue。** re-gather the ball before the second step: the sequence sits next to a travel call.
