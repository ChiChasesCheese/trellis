## Q
**Jab step into step-back（试探步接后撤步）**｜Stephen Curry

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-jab-stepback.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `jab step with head and shoulder fake → retreat step → step-back shot`
**Read。** a defender who bites on the jab shifts his weight forward and cannot recover to the shot.
