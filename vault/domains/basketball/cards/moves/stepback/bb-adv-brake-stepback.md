## Q
**Brake-and-step-back（急停后撤步）**｜Maxey

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-brake-stepback.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `downhill dribble at speed → sudden stop (brake) → step back far from the defender → rise for the pull-up`
**Read。** a defender who is committed to stopping the drive lunges into the space and cannot recover once the ball handler backs out.
**Cue。** the brake is the sell: speed first, then one hard plant, and the step-back must be long enough to clear his hands.
