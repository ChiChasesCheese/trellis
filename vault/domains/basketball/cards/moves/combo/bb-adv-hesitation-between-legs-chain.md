## Q
**Hesitation into between-the-legs chain（犹豫步接胯下连招）**｜Kyrie Irving

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-hesitation-between-legs-chain.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `hard dribble → hesitation (freeze the pace) → delayed between-the-legs → behind-the-back change of direction → pull-up or finish`
**Read。** a defender who cannot read the timing freezes on the hesitation, then is a step late on the change of direction.
