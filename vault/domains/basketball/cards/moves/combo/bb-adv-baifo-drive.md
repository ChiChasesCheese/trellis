## Q
**Pump-fake drive（拜佛过人）**｜Kyrie Irving · Harden · Curry · DeRozan · Wade

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-baifo-drive.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `hold the dribble → ball up to shooting position with both hands, eyes on the rim → defender jumps → pull the feet in → drive past`
**Read。** a defender who leaves the floor to contest the shot is past his balance point and loses the drive.
