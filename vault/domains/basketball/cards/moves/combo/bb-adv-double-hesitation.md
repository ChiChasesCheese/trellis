## Q
**Double hesitation into jumper（双重犹豫步）**｜Stephen Curry

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-double-hesitation.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `explosive first step → hesitation → second hesitation → pull-up`
**Read。** the defender relaxes after the first pause (flat-footed), so the second hesitation freezes him before the pull-up.
