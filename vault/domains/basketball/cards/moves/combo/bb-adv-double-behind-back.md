## Q
**Double behind-the-back crossover（双背后运球）**｜Jamal Crawford

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-double-behind-back.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `behind-the-back cross → second behind-the-back cross (ball stays wide) → shoulder shimmy → pull-up or drive`
**Read。** a defender who plants his feet and turns his hips for the first cross is wrong-footed by the second.
