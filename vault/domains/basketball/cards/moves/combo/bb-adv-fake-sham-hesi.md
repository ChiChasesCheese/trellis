## Q
**Fake sham into hesitation（假沙姆格德接犹豫步）**｜Will Bynum

## A
![[bb-adv-fake-sham-hesi.mp4]]

**Read。** a defender who anticipates the sham jumps to the snatch side, and the hesitation freezes him again.
