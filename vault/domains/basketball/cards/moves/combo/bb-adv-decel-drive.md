## Q
**Deceleration drive（急刹减速过人）**｜Luka Doncic · Anthony Edwards, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-decel-drive.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `full-speed drive → brake on the last 1-2 steps → defender flies by → finish in the gap`
**Read。** a defender committed to a chase pace overruns the ball handler.
