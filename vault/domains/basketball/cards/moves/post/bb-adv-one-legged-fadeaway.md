## Q
**One-legged fadeaway（单腿后仰）**｜Jokic · Wembanyama · Durant · Dirk · Kobe

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-one-legged-fadeaway.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `catch / hard dribble → pivot away from the defender → hop off the back foot, lean back, other knee up → release over his arms`
**Read。** a defender who sits on the shot has no way to reach the release point: the fade plus the high release point stays over his arms.
