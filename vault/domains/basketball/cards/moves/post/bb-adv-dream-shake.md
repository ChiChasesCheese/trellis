## Q
**Dream Shake（梦幻脚步）**｜Hakeem Olajuwon · Joel Embiid · Victor Wembanyama

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-dream-shake.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `back-down → head/shoulder fake one way → turn the other way → fadeaway or finish (up-and-under as a counter)`
**Read。** the defender flinches on the first fake, then gets turned the wrong way by the reverse.
**Cue。** sell the fake with the head and shoulders, then keep the pivot foot planted through the turn.
