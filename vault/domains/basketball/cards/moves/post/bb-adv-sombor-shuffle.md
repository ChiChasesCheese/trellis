## Q
**Sombor Shuffle（单腿后仰摇摆步）**｜Nikola Jokic

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-sombor-shuffle.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `rhythm dribble → gather → lean back → one-footed fadeaway`, released high from behind the head
**Read。** a defender sagging or playing drop cannot time the odd tempo and release point, and cannot contest the high arc.
