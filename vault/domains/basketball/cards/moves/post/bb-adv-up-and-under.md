## Q
**Up-and-under（上步虚晃接下步）**｜Kevin McHale · Hakeem Olajuwon

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-up-and-under.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `catch on the block → up-fake → defender leaves his feet → step under with the pivot foot planted → finish`
**Read。** a defender who bites on the shot fake and jumps gets passed as he comes down.
**Cue。** keep the pivot foot planted through the fake, or it is a travel.
