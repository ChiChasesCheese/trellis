## Q
**Fake spin / spin hesitation（假转身）**｜Isaiah Thomas · DeMar DeRozan

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-fake-spin.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `dribble at defender → head, shoulder and hip start the spin → stop → continue forward`
**Read。** a defender who shifts his weight to the spin side gets frozen or left behind.
