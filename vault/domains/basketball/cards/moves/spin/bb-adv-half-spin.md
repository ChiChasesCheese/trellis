## Q
**Half spin（半转身）**｜Nightowl 教学演示 · Isaiah Thomas, Jalen Brunson

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-half-spin.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `attack the defender → plant → half turn with the ball shielded → reverse out → drive / pull-up`
**Read。** a defender who relaxes or follows the retreat is a step late on the reversal.
**Cue。** keep the ball on the far hip and the pivot foot planted, or it becomes a travel.
