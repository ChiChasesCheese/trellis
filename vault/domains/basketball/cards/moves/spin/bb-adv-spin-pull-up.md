## Q
**One-dribble spin into pull-up（转身急停跳投）**｜Kobe Bryant（集锦，older footage）· Shai Gilgeous-Alexander

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-spin-pull-up.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `one hard dribble → plant → spin off the pivot foot → rise into the pull-up`
**Read。** a defender braced for the drive turns with the spin and is late on the contest.
