## Q
**Slow-roll then spin（转身误导）**｜Tony Parker

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-lull-and-spin.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `slow-roll dribble → lull the defender → accelerate → spin off the pivot foot → finish at the rim`
**Read。** a defender who settles into the slow pace (on his heels) cannot recover when the direction changes.
**Cue。** keep the pivot foot planted through the spin: lifting it is a travel.
