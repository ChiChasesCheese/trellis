## Q
**Spin-back into repeated half-spins（回转接连续半转身）**｜Anthony Edwards

Sequence 是什么？骗防守什么？

## A
![[bb-adv-spin-back-half-spin-chain.mp4]]

**Sequence。** `spin-back → half-spin → half-spin → step-through (or fake step-through)`
**Read。** the defender is turned in circles, never gets a set stance, and the final step-through is open.
