## Q
You drive baseline, the low man cuts you off, and you pick your dribble up eight feet from the rim with two defenders on you. Do you still have an advantage? How do you tell, and what does the answer tell you to do?

## A
- Ask exactly one question: **is a defender still responsible for two people?** If yes, the advantage is live — it has simply stopped belonging to you.
- Here it is live. Two defenders are on you, so two offensive players are unguarded: the man whose defender left to help, and usually the corner shooter the low man abandoned.
- What died is *your personal* advantage — no dribble, no angle, a contested shot. Forcing a finish converts a live **team** advantage into a bad shot, which is the most expensive mistake in the sequence.
- The read: **find the defender who left, and pass to the man he left.** That is the drop-off to the dunker spot or the kick to the corner, not a fadeaway over two.
- A genuinely *dead* advantage looks different: nobody is guarding two people, all five defenders are matched and set. Then you stop attacking and restart the offense with a new action — a screen, a handoff — rather than a hero drive.

## Q zh
你走底线突破，low man（弱侧最靠近篮筐的协防人）把你堵住了，你在离篮筐八英尺处收球，身上贴着两个防守人。你还有优势吗？怎么判断？判断结果告诉你该做什么？

## A zh
- 只问一个问题：**是否还有一名防守人要负责两个人？** 如果是，优势还活着——只是它不再属于你了。
- 这里它是活的。两名防守人在你身上，说明有两名进攻人无人看管：协防人放掉的那个，通常还有 low man 丢下的底角射手。
- 死掉的是*你个人的*优势——没有运球、没有角度、出手被封。硬终结等于把一个活着的**团队**优势换成一次糟糕的出手，这是整个连锁里代价最高的错误。
- 该读的是：**找出那个离开的防守人，把球传给他丢下的人。** 那是传给 dunker spot 的 drop-off，或者分给底角，而不是在两人头上后仰。
- 真正*死掉*的优势长得不一样：没有人在防两个人，五名防守人都对位站好了。这时你就停止攻击，用一个新动作重启进攻——一个掩护、一次手递手——而不是英雄式强突。
