## Q
The coaching rule of thumb is 12-15 feet between offensive players. Where does that number actually come from, and what does it fail to account for?

## A
- It is the distance at which **one defender can no longer meaningfully guard both men**. Inside about 10 feet he can stand between them and contest either; the two offensive players are effectively one.
- Beyond roughly 15 feet the pass gets long enough to be read and intercepted, or to arrive late enough that the closeout is already there. So 12-15 is the band where the pass still beats the defender.
- 12-15 feet is about **one hard pass**: the ball travels it faster than any defender can cover it, so the pass itself hands the receiver an advantage before he does anything.
- It also sets the **driving gap**. Two properly spaced players leave a lane wide enough to get a shoulder through; bunched players share a defender who is already standing in the lane you wanted.
- **What it misses: threat.** A non-shooter standing a perfect 15 feet away does not space the floor, because his defender is free to ignore him and sit in the gap. Spacing is distance × threat, and only the distance half is coachable by geometry.

## Q zh
教练的经验法则是进攻球员之间保持 12-15 英尺。这个数字究竟从哪来？它又忽略了什么？

## A zh
- 这是**一名防守人再也无法同时有效防住两个人**的距离。在大约 10 英尺以内，他可以站在两人之间、对谁都能干扰；这两名进攻人实际上等于一个人。
- 超过大约 15 英尺，传球变得足够长，容易被预判和抢断，或者到位太慢、closeout（扑防）已经站好了。所以 12-15 英尺是"传球仍然快过防守人"的那个区间。
- 12-15 英尺大约就是**一记直传的距离**：球跑完这段路比任何防守人都快，所以传球本身就在接球人做任何动作之前先给了他优势。
- 它同时决定了**突破的缝隙**。两个间距合理的球员之间留下的通道足够把肩膀挤进去；挤在一起的球员共用一名防守人，而那人已经站在你想走的那条线上了。
- **它没算进去的是威胁度。** 一个不投三分的球员站在完美的 15 英尺外并不拉开空间，因为他的防守人可以放心无视他、坐在缝隙里。spacing（空间）＝距离 × 威胁，而只有距离这一半能靠几何学教会。
