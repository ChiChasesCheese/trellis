## Q
You beat your man toward the middle. A teammate is standing on your side of the floor, about eight feet from the lane line, level with the block. Name exactly what his position costs you — and the one-step fix.

## A
- **His defender is already in the paint on the help line and does not have to move.** You drove into a set help defender instead of forcing a rotation, which is the difference between a broken defense and an inconvenienced one.
- Two defenders' worth of coverage now sits in the space you needed for one, so **the gap closes early** — usually before you reach the second foot of your drive.
- **The pass out is short and inside the defender's reach**: an easy deflection, and if it does get there the receiver catches with a defender already on him rather than in a closeout he can attack.
- **Nobody was pulled out of position**, so no rotation happens anywhere on the floor and there is no third player open behind the help. The advantage you created dies without ever being transported.
- **The fix is one step, not a new play.** He lifts to the slot or drops all the way to the corner. Either removes his defender from your drive lane and puts the next pass on a longer, less guardable line.

## Q zh
你朝中路过掉了防守人。一名队友站在你这一侧，离禁区线大约八英尺，与低位块齐平。准确说出他的站位让你付出了什么代价——以及那个一步就能解决的修正。

## A zh
- **他的防守人已经站在禁区里的协防线上，根本不需要移动。** 你突进的是一个站好位的协防人，而不是逼出一次轮转——这就是"防守被打散"和"防守被打扰了一下"的区别。
- 你只需要一个人的空间，现在却挤着两名防守人的覆盖范围，所以**缝隙提早关闭**——通常在你突破的第二步之前就没了。
- **传出去的球又短又在防守人手臂范围内**：极易被打掉；就算传到了，接球人身上已经贴着防守人，而不是面对一个可以攻击的 closeout（扑防）。
- **没有任何人被调动出位**，所以全场没有发生轮转，协防身后也没有第三个空位人。你创造的优势还没被运输就死了。
- **修正只需要一步，不需要新战术。** 他往上提到 slot（弧顶两侧），或者一路沉到底角。两者都能把他的防守人从你的突破线路上挪走，并让下一次传球走在一条更长、更难防的线上。
