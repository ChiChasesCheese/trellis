## Q
The long two — a jumper from just inside the arc — is the lowest-value shot in basketball. Explain the mechanism, then name the situations in which taking one is still the correct decision.

## A
- **It is priced as a two but shot at nearly the difficulty of a three.** About 40% on a long two is 0.80 points per shot; about 34% from three is 1.02. Stepping back eighteen inches raises the shot's value by roughly 25% for a very small drop in accuracy.
- **It generates almost no free throws**, unlike a drive, and it produces the worst offensive-rebounding position of any shot: a long miss caroms long while your shooter is 20 feet from the glass.
- Still correct when: **(1)** you are a genuinely elite midrange shooter and the alternative is a heavily contested three; **(2)** the shot clock is expiring and it is the only clean look; **(3)** late in a game where two points is enough, or a two-for-one demands a quick attempt; **(4)** a switch has given you a total mismatch and the pull-up over a smaller or slower defender is essentially uncontested.
- Note what those exceptions share: the long two is correct when it is **the best shot currently available**, never when it is the shot the offense was designed to produce.
- The practical rule for your own game: a long two is a signal to review the possession. It usually means an action broke down and nobody moved the ball on.

## Q zh
long two（长两分）——三分线内一点的跳投——是篮球里价值最低的出手。解释它的机制，再说出哪些情况下投它仍然是正确决策。

## A zh
- **它按两分计价，难度却接近三分。** long two 命中率约 40%，即每次出手 0.80 分；三分约 34%，即 1.02 分。往后退十八英寸，出手价值提高约 25%，命中率却只掉一点点。
- 和突破不同，**它几乎不造罚球**，而且是所有出手里进攻篮板位置最差的：长投的长篮板弹得很远，而你的射手站在离篮筐 20 英尺的地方。
- 但在这些情况下仍然正确：**(1)** 你确实是顶级中距离射手，而替代选项是被贴身干扰的三分；**(2)** 进攻时间将尽，这是唯一干净的出手；**(3)** 比赛末段两分就够，或者要打 two-for-one（一攻换两攻）必须快速出手；**(4)** 换防给了你彻底的错位，面对更矮或更慢的防守人几乎无干扰地干拔。
- 注意这些例外的共同点：long two 正确，是因为它是**当下能拿到的最好出手**，而绝不是因为战术就是为它设计的。
- 用在你自己身上的实用准则：投了一记 long two，就该回看这个回合。它通常意味着某个战术打崩了，而且没人把球再转移出去。
