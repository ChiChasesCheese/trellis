## Q
You are standing in the corner behind the three-point line. Name the structural reasons that spot is worth more to an offense than the same three taken from the wing or the top of the arc — and the one cost it carries.

## A
- **It is the shortest three on the floor.** The arc would run off the court, so it is cut flat by the sideline: 22 ft in the NBA versus 23 ft 9 in above the break, and 6.60 m versus 6.75 m in FIBA. Same three points, roughly a foot and a half less distance.
- **Its defender is the furthest from the ball.** Help sent from the corner arrives latest and recovers slowest, so the corner is the last place a defense wants to help from — which is exactly why offenses station a shooter there.
- **The pass to it is straight and short.** A baseline drive kicks to the corner on a direct line; the shooter's feet are easy to set because the sideline and baseline give a fixed reference.
- **It holds the low man.** A corner shooter forces the weakside rim protector to choose between him and the roller — one defender guarding two players.
- **The cost:** no room to relocate along the arc, and the closeout runs you toward the baseline, where the sideline acts as a second defender. A corner shooter who has to put it on the floor is in the worst driving position on the court.

## Q zh
你站在底角三分线外。从结构上说，这个位置对进攻的价值为什么高于同样一记侧翼或弧顶三分？它又有什么代价？

## A zh
- **它是全场最短的三分。** 弧线到这里会跑出边线，所以被边线截平：NBA 底角 22 ft，弧顶（above the break）23 ft 9 in；FIBA 是 6.60 m 对 6.75 m。同样三分，距离少了将近一英尺半。
- **防守它的人离球最远。** 从底角出的协防到得最晚、回防最慢，所以底角是防守最不愿意放的协防来源——这正是进攻把射手摆在那里的原因。
- **传给它的球线又直又短。** 底线突破往底角分球是一条直线；射手的脚也好摆，因为边线和底线给了固定参照。
- **它牵制 low man（弱侧最靠近篮筐的协防人）。** 底角射手迫使护框人在他和顺下的 roller 之间二选一——一个防守人要防两个进攻人。
- **代价：** 沿弧顶没有转移的空间，而且 closeout（扑防）会把你逼向底线，边线相当于第二个防守人。底角射手一旦必须运球，处在全场最差的突破位置。
