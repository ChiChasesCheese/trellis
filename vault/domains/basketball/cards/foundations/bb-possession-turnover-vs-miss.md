## Q
A missed shot and a turnover both end your possession without points. Why is the turnover clearly and quantifiably worse, and what does that imply for a decision on the floor?

## A
- **A miss is not a certain loss of the ball.** Roughly a quarter of missed field goals are rebounded by the offense, so a miss returns you the possession about 25% of the time. A turnover returns it essentially never.
- **A live-ball turnover upgrades the opponent's possession, not just ends yours.** It hands them the ball moving forward with your players out of position, and transition offense is the most efficient basketball there is — comfortably above 1.20 PPP versus roughly 1.00-1.05 in the half court.
- **A miss at least produced a shot**: it drew a contest, it might draw a foul, and it put bodies near the rim for the rebound. A turnover produces nothing at all.
- Rough accounting: a miss costs you your ~1.05 expected points. A live-ball turnover costs that *plus* the gap between the opponent's transition and half-court efficiency — call it another 0.2 to 0.3 points.
- **On-court implication:** a slightly worse shot usually beats a slightly risky pass. "Take the shot rather than force the fourth pass" is turnover arithmetic, not timidity — and it is why a dead-ball turnover (travel, offensive foul) is meaningfully cheaper than a stolen pass.

## Q zh
投丢和失误都让你的回合无功而返。为什么失误明显更糟，而且糟得可以量化？这对场上的决策意味着什么？

## A zh
- **投丢并不等于必然丢掉球权。** 大约四分之一的投失会被进攻方抢下，所以投丢大约有 25% 的概率把回合还给你。失误则基本上是 0%。
- **live-ball turnover（活球失误）不只是结束你的回合，它还升级了对手的回合。** 它把球送到向前推进的对手手里，而你的人站位全乱；transition（快攻转换）是篮球里效率最高的进攻——轻松超过 1.20 PPP，而阵地战大约只有 1.00-1.05。
- **投丢至少产生了一次出手**：它逼出了干扰、可能造犯规，而且把人送到了篮下抢板。失误什么也没产生。
- 粗略算账：投丢让你损失约 1.05 分的期望值。活球失误损失的是这个数**加上**对手 transition 与阵地战效率之差——大约再多 0.2 到 0.3 分。
- **场上的推论：** 稍差一点的出手通常优于稍冒险一点的传球。"宁可投，也别硬塞第四次传球"是失误的算术题，不是保守——这也是为什么 dead-ball turnover（死球失误，如走步、进攻犯规）明显比被断传球便宜。
