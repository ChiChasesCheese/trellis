Four interior landmarks coaches speak in. The {{c1::elbow}} is where the free-throw line meets the lane line — the pivot point for high-post entries and the classic pull-up spot. The {{c2::nail}} is the small mark at the middle of the free-throw line; a help defender stands there because from it he is in two driving gaps at once. The {{c3::short corner}} is the baseline area a few feet outside the lane, below the block and inside the arc — the seam a zone cannot cover and a safe landing spot for a diving big. The {{c4::dunker spot}} sits just off the low block on the baseline side, where a non-shooting big stands to occupy the low man and catch the drop-off pass.

## zh
教练常挂在嘴边的四个内线坐标。{{c1::elbow}}（肘区）是罚球线与禁区线的交点——高位策应的支点，也是经典的急停跳投点。{{c2::nail}}（钉子位）是罚球线正中的小标记；协防人站在那里，因为从那一点他同时卡在两条突破线路上。{{c3::short corner}}（短角）是禁区外侧几英尺、低位以下、三分线以内的底线区域——区域联防补不住的缝隙，也是内线顺下后的安全落点。{{c4::dunker spot}} 位于低位块靠底线一侧，不投三分的内线站在那里，牵制 low man 并接突破分出的 drop-off 球。
