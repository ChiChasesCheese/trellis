## Q
Coaches say most offense is "transporting an advantage, not creating five of them." In concrete terms, what *is* an advantage, and what does transporting one mean?

## A
- An advantage exists the moment **one defender is responsible for more than he can cover**. That is the whole definition; everything else is a form it takes.
- The three currencies it comes in: a **numbers** edge (two defenders on the ball leaves 3 covering 4), an **angle** edge (your shoulder is past his hip, so the driving line is yours), and a **matchup** edge (a switch put a centre on a guard).
- **Transporting** it means the ball and bodies move so that the *same* defensive scramble is still unresolved when a good shot appears: drive, draw the help, pass, and the next player attacks the closeout instead of restarting the possession.
- Creating an advantage is expensive — it takes a screen, a hard drive, or a mismatch hunt, and it can fail. Moving one that already exists costs a pass, which takes a fraction of a second and cannot be guarded.
- Consequence for how you judge a possession: the question is not "did our best player touch it" but **"was the first advantage we created ever converted, or did it die in someone's hands?"**

## Q zh
教练常说，绝大部分进攻是"运输一个优势，而不是创造五个优势"。具体地说，advantage（优势）到底是什么？"运输"它又是什么意思？

## A zh
- 只要**有一个防守人要负责超出他能覆盖的范围**，优势就出现了。这就是全部定义；其余都只是它呈现的形式。
- 它有三种"货币"：**人数**优势（两人夹球，剩下 3 人防 4 人）、**角度**优势（你的肩已过他的胯，突破线路归你）、**错位**优势（换防把中锋换到了后卫身上）。
- **运输**它，是指球和人的移动要让*同一次*防守慌乱在好机会出现之前一直无法解决：突破、吸引协防、分球，接球的人去攻击 closeout（扑防）而不是重新起一次进攻。
- 创造优势很贵——需要一个掩护、一次强突或一次找错位，而且可能失败。转移一个已经存在的优势只需要一次传球，耗时几分之一秒，而且防不住。
- 这决定了你该怎么评价一个回合：问题不是"我们最强的球员碰到球了吗"，而是**"我们创造的第一个优势最终被兑现了，还是死在了某个人手里？"**
