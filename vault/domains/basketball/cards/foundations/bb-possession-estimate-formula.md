A possession lasts until the ball changes teams, so an offensive rebound continues the same possession rather than starting a new one. From a box score it is therefore estimated as {{c1::FGA − ORB + TOV + 0.44 × FTA}}, where the 0.44 coefficient exists because {{c2::free-throw attempts do not map one-to-one onto trips — and-ones, three-shot fouls and technicals}}. Because possession alternates, the two teams in a game play {{c3::almost exactly the same number of possessions}}, which is why per-possession rates and not per-game totals are the only fair way to compare teams playing at different {{c4::pace}}.

## zh
一个 possession（回合）持续到球权易手为止，所以进攻篮板是延续同一个回合，而不是开启新回合。因此从技术统计里可以这样估算：{{c1::FGA − ORB + TOV + 0.44 × FTA}}，其中 0.44 这个系数存在是因为 {{c2::罚球出手数与上罚球线的次数并非一一对应——and-one、三分犯规和技术犯规}}。由于球权是交替的，一场比赛中两队打的回合数 {{c3::几乎完全相同}}——这正是为什么只有每回合的效率、而不是每场的总量，才能公平地比较 {{c4::pace}}（节奏）不同的球队。
