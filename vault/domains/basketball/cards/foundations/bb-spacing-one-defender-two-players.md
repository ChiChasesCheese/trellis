## Q
What does a coach mean by "one defender is guarding two players", and why is producing that situation the entire point of spacing?

## A
- It means geometry has assigned a single defender to cover **two live threats at once** — classically the roller diving to the rim and the shooter standing in the corner behind him.
- That defender (the **low man**) is forced to choose. Whichever one he takes, the other is open. The offense scores from a **decision it forced**, not from a shot it had to manufacture.
- This is the actual mechanism behind the 4-on-3 in a pick and roll: two defenders are attached to the ball, leaving three to cover four, and at least one of those three is inevitably in the bind.
- It only works if **both threats are real and far enough apart that he cannot split the difference.** A corner shooter 15 feet from the roller stretches him past his limit; the same shooter drifting toward the lane lets him cover both with one step.
- The offensive job is therefore to hold him in the bind as long as possible: the roller keeps rolling all the way through, and the corner shooter stays deep in the corner instead of drifting up to "get open".

## Q zh
教练说"一个防守人在防两个人"是什么意思？为什么制造这个局面就是 spacing（空间）的全部目的？

## A zh
- 意思是几何位置迫使**一名防守人同时覆盖两个真实威胁**——最典型的是顺下扑筐的 roller 和站在他身后底角的射手。
- 这名防守人（**low man**，弱侧最靠近篮筐的协防人）被迫二选一。无论他选谁，另一个就是空位。进攻得分来自**它逼出来的一次抉择**，而不是硬造出来的一次出手。
- 这正是 pick and roll（挡拆）里 4 打 3 的真实机制：两名防守人被球黏住，剩下三人防四人，那三人里必然至少有一人陷入这种两难。
- 前提是**两个威胁都真实存在，且距离远到他无法折中站位。** 底角射手离 roller 有 15 英尺，就把他拉过了极限；同一个射手往禁区飘，他一步就能兼顾两人。
- 所以进攻端的任务是把他尽可能长久地困在这个两难里：roller 一路顺到底，底角射手就待在深底角，而不是往上飘去"找空位"。
