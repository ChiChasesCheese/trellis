## Q
You beat your man and the help steps up. What does it mean that "the advantage decays", and what specific behaviour keeps it alive?

## A
- The defense is recovering the entire time you hold the ball. An advantage has a lifespan of roughly **one second**: every tenth you spend, another defender arrives and the numbers even back out.
- Decay is literal arithmetic — 4-on-3 becomes 4-on-3.5 becomes 5-on-5. Nothing bad has to happen to you. You simply stopped, and the defense caught up.
- Keeping it alive means the **0.5 rule**: within about half a second of catching, you shoot, drive, or pass. Sizing up, sweeping the ball, or taking one "settling" dribble spends the advantage on nothing.
- **Direction matters too.** A pass backwards hands the defense free repositioning time; the advantage survives passes that go across the floor or ahead of the rotation, not behind it.
- The player who receives the ball inherits a **smaller** advantage than the one who created it, because the defense has had longer to rotate. So he must attack or move it immediately — he cannot afford to stop and re-create.

## Q zh
你过掉了自己的防守人，协防上来了。"advantage decays（优势衰减）"是什么意思？具体要做什么才能让它活着？

## A zh
- 你持球的每一刻，防守都在回位。一个优势的寿命大约是**一秒**：你每花掉十分之一秒，就有一名防守人到位，人数差被抹平一点。
- 衰减是字面意义上的算术——4 打 3 变成 4 打 3.5，再变成 5 打 5。你不需要犯任何错。你只是停下来了，防守就追上了。
- 让它活着意味着遵守 **0.5 rule（半秒法则）**：接球后大约半秒内，要么投、要么突、要么传。原地试探、大幅拉球、或者多运一次"稳一稳"的球，都是把优势花在了零回报上。
- **方向同样重要。** 往回传等于白送防守重新站位的时间；能让优势存活的是横向转移或走在轮转前面的传球，而不是退回去的传球。
- 接球的人继承到的优势**比创造它的人手里的更小**，因为防守多轮转了一会儿。所以他必须立刻攻击或立刻转移——他没有本钱停下来重新创造。
