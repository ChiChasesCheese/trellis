The shot-value ladder, measured in expected points per attempt. Best is a trip to the free-throw line — two shots for a 78% shooter is {{c1::~1.55 分／趟}}. Then a shot in the restricted area at roughly 65%, worth {{c2::~1.30}}. Then the corner three at roughly 39%, worth {{c3::~1.17}}. Then the above-the-break three at roughly 36%, worth {{c4::~1.07}}. Last and worst is the long two at roughly 40%, worth {{c5::~0.80}}. The whole modern shot chart — rim, line, arc, and nothing in between — falls out of this one ranking.

## zh
按每次出手的期望得分排出的 shot value（出手价值）阶梯。最好的是走上罚球线——78% 的罚球手投两罚，价值 {{c1::~1.55 分／趟}}。其次是 restricted area（禁区弧内）约 65% 命中的出手，价值 {{c2::~1.30}}。再次是底角三分，约 39% 命中，价值 {{c3::~1.17}}。然后是 above-the-break three（弧顶三分），约 36% 命中，价值 {{c4::~1.07}}。最后也最差的是 long two（长两分），约 40% 命中，价值 {{c5::~0.80}}。现代投篮热图的全部形状——篮下、罚球线、三分线，中间空无一物——都是从这一个排序里推出来的。
