## Q
Your team scores 96 in one game and 110 in another. Why can you not conclude the 110 was the better offensive performance, and what number answers the question instead?

## A
- Points per game mixes **how well you played** with **how many chances you got**. A fast team scores more points at identical quality simply by running more possessions.
- The comparison unit is **points per possession (PPP)** = points ÷ possessions. Roughly 1.10 is average in modern pro basketball, 1.20 is elite, below 1.00 is a bad offense.
- Run the two games: 110 points on 105 possessions is 1.05 PPP; 96 points on 80 possessions is 1.20 PPP. The 96-point game was far better offense.
- The same unit compares anything that can be counted: an action, a player, a lineup. "This pick and roll generates 1.02 PPP, this iso 0.88" is how a coach ranks what to run.
- **Pace never changes who is better.** Playing fast adds possessions to both teams, which shrinks the role of luck and makes the better team win more often — it does not make either team more efficient.

## Q zh
你的球队一场得 96 分，另一场得 110 分。为什么不能断定 110 分那场进攻打得更好？该用什么数字来回答这个问题？

## A zh
- 场均得分把**你打得多好**和**你拿到多少机会**混在了一起。节奏快的球队在同等质量下只因为回合数更多就能得更多分。
- 比较的单位是 **points per possession（PPP，每回合得分）**＝得分 ÷ 回合数。现代职业篮球大约 1.10 是平均水平，1.20 是顶级，低于 1.00 是糟糕的进攻。
- 把两场算一遍：110 分打了 105 个回合是 1.05 PPP；96 分打了 80 个回合是 1.20 PPP。96 分那场进攻好得多。
- 同一个单位可以比较任何能计数的东西：一个战术、一名球员、一套阵容。"这个 pick and roll（挡拆）产出 1.02 PPP，这个 iso 只有 0.88"——教练就是这样排序该打什么。
- **节奏（pace）从不改变谁更强。** 打得快给双方都增加回合，压缩运气的作用，让更强的队赢得更频繁——但它不会让任何一方变得更高效。
