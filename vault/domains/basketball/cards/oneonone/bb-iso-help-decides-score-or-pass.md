## Q
You have the ball isolated on the wing. What do you read *before* your first dribble, and how does that read decide whether this is a scoring possession or a playmaking one?

## A
- **Read the off-ball defenders, not your own man.** Their positions have already decided what your drive will produce; your man only decides how hard the first step has to be.
- **Gaps loaded** — two defenders with a foot in the paint, heads swivelling between ball and man? This is a **playmaking** possession. Your drive will draw help and the shot belongs to whoever's defender leaves. Attack *toward* the help, not away from it, so the choice is forced.
- **Gaps empty** — everyone locked onto shooters, high and wide, nobody in the lane? This is a **scoring** possession. You have a genuine 1-on-1: attack the top foot with the finish already chosen.
- **Name two specific defenders before you move:** the **low man** (weakside defender nearest the rim, responsible for the last line) and the **tagger** (the man whose job is to step to your drive). Whichever of them moves is the one whose assignment you pass to.
- **The recurring mistake is choosing the direction from your own preference.** Which is your strong hand matters much less than which side has one defender guarding two people.

## Q zh
你在侧翼单打持球。在第一次运球**之前**你要读什么？这个阅读如何决定这是一个得分回合还是一个组织回合？

## A zh
- **读的是无球端的防守人，不是你自己的对位人。** 他们的站位已经决定了你的突破会产出什么；你的对位人只决定你的第一步要有多狠。
- **缝隙里站满了人**——两名防守人一只脚在禁区里，头在球和人之间来回摆？这是**组织**回合。你的突破会引出协防，出手权属于那个丢下人来协防的防守人所看的人。**朝着**协防突，而不是躲开它，这样才逼出抉择。
- **缝隙是空的**——所有人都黏在射手身上，站得又高又开，禁区里没人？这是**得分**回合。你拿到的是真正的一对一：攻他的 top foot（前脚），终结方式提前选好。
- **在动之前先点名两个具体的防守人：** **low man**（弱侧最靠近篮筐、负责最后一道防线的人）和 **tagger**（负责上前招呼你突破的那个人）。他俩谁动了，你就把球传给谁看的人。
- **反复出现的错误是按自己的偏好选方向。** 你哪只手更强，远不如"哪一侧有一个防守人在防两个人"来得重要。
