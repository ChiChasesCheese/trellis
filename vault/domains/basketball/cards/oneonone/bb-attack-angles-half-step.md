## Q
You have half a step on your man — a real edge, but not a clean blow-by. What turns half a step into a finished drive rather than a stalled one?

## A
- **Treat it as a decaying asset.** Half a step is worth less every tenth of a second, so the drive must be one or two dribbles into a finish or a pass. A third dribble usually gives the half step back.
- **Take the straightest available line to the rim.** The instinct is to arc away from the defender because he feels close; arcing lengthens your path and lets him cut it off *in front*, which turns your advantage into a charge risk.
- **Use your body as the wall:** near shoulder in front of his chest, off arm braced across (not extended into a push-off), so he has to run through you rather than reach across you. Half a step plus body position is a full step of separation.
- **Attack his hips, not the rim.** Running at the spot he must occupy forces him to either concede ground or foul; running at the rim lets him take a straight recovery line to the same place.
- **Choose the finish from the help before you gather:** help committed → drop-off or kick; help hovering at 8-10 feet uncommitted → floater; no help at all → one-foot takeoff and get there first.

## Q zh
你对防守人取得了半步优势——是真优势，但不是干净的过人。什么能把这半步变成一次完成的突破，而不是一次停滞的突破？

## A zh
- **把它当作会衰减的资产。** 半步的价值每十分之一秒都在下降，所以这次突破必须是一到两次运球接终结或传球。第三次运球通常就把这半步还回去了。
- **走能走的最直的那条线冲向篮筐。** 本能是绕开防守人，因为你感觉他很近；但绕弧会拉长路径，让他从**正面**把你截住，把你的优势变成一次带球撞人的风险。
- **用身体当墙：** 近侧肩送到他胸前，非持球臂横撑（是撑住，不是伸直推人），让他必须从你身上穿过去，而不是从你身前伸手够球。半步加上身体位置，等于一整步的分离。
- **攻他的髋，不是攻篮筐。** 朝他必须占据的那个点跑，逼他要么让出空间、要么犯规；朝篮筐跑，等于让他走一条直线回防到同一个点。
- **在收球之前就用协防的位置选好终结方式：** 协防已经扑出来 → drop-off 分球或外传；协防在 8-10 英尺处犹豫未定 → floater（抛投）；完全没有协防 → 单脚起跳，抢在他们之前到。
