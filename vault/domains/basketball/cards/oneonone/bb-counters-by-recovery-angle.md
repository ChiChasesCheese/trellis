Your first move went, and the defender is recovering. Pick the counter from *how* he recovered, not from a script. If he over-runs and his hips fly past you toward the baseline, {{c1::cross back into the space he just vacated}}. If he beats you to the spot and squares up in front of you with his momentum still coming toward you, {{c2::step back or pull up — his weight is moving the wrong way to contest}}. If he recovers onto your back hip and rides you sideways with forearm pressure, {{c3::spin off him, using his own pressure as the pivot point}}. And if he simply gives ground into a balanced stance with his cushion restored, {{c4::there is no advantage left — re-attack the other side, or move the ball and start a new action}}.

## zh
你的第一个动作成功了，防守人正在回位。要根据他**怎么**回位来选择 counter（后手），而不是照剧本走。如果他冲过头、髋部朝底线方向甩了过去，{{c1::体前变向回切到他刚刚让出的那块空间}}。如果他抢先堵到位置、正面对着你、冲力仍朝你而来，{{c2::后撤步或急停跳投——他的重心正朝着错误的方向，干扰不到}}。如果他回到你的后侧髋位、用前臂顶着你横向推着走，{{c3::借他自己的顶劲当作支点，转身摆脱}}。而如果他只是退让出空间、重新站成平衡姿态、距离也恢复了，{{c4::优势已经不存在了——换另一侧重新发起，或者转移球、开启一个新的战术}}。
