## Q
Explain the "decelerate to accelerate" principle, and describe mechanically why a hesitation dribble creates a gap. What makes a hesitation fail?

## A
- **You cannot accelerate hard out of a run at constant speed.** Force is produced against the ground, and that requires a loaded, bent position to push from. Slowing down is what loads the legs — deceleration is the wind-up.
- **The sequence is therefore: sink the hips and shorten the steps** (which simultaneously invites the defender to close his cushion and stand up), then explode out of that loaded position.
- **A hesitation dribble is exactly this compressed into one beat:** a slight rise, the ball held a fraction longer, everything reading as "he is stopping." The defender's weight comes up or drifts forward.
- **The gap comes from two things happening at once:** you got lower while he got higher, so your first step out is faster than his reaction; and he closed the cushion, so that same step covers more of the ground between you.
- **Why it fails:** if you do not actually threaten to stop — eyes up, shoulders squared, shot live, chin over the ball — he does not rise. Then you have simply slowed down in front of a set defender and handed him the exchange.

## Q zh
解释 "decelerate to accelerate"（先减速再加速）原则，并从力学上说明 hesitation（迟疑步）运球为什么能造出缝隙。什么会让 hesitation 失效？

## A zh
- **你无法从匀速奔跑中猛然加速。** 力是对地面产生的，而这需要一个蓄好力、屈曲的姿势去蹬。减速正是给腿蓄力的动作——减速就是引拍。
- **所以顺序是：沉髋、缩短步幅**（这同时在邀请防守人收缩距离并直起身来），然后从这个蓄力姿势中爆发出去。
- **hesitation 运球就是把这一切压缩进一拍：** 身体微微起身，球在手上多停留一瞬，一切都在读作"他要停下来了"。防守人的重心随之上浮或前倾。
- **缝隙来自同时发生的两件事：** 你变低了而他变高了，所以你的第一步快过他的反应；同时他收缩了距离，于是同样一步覆盖了你俩之间更多的地面。
- **它为什么会失效：** 如果你并没有真的做出"要停"的威胁——抬头、双肩正对、投篮还活着、下巴压在球上——他就不会起身。那你只是在一个站稳的防守人面前减了速，把这次交锋白送给他。
