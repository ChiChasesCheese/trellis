## Q
Why do coaches say post play is decided before the catch, and what does "sealing" actually consist of?

## A
- **The catch location determines everything after it.** Catching on the block with a foot in the lane is a scoring position; catching the same pass four feet further out turns the identical move into a contested fadeaway. No footwork recovers those four feet.
- **Sealing is taking and holding the space between the defender and the ball with your body:** wide base, forearm and hip into him, chest and hands turned toward the passer so you present a target he cannot reach.
- **The work is continuous, not a single act.** You re-seal on every pass, because the defender's legal position changes as the passing angle changes. A seal held over from the previous pass is already stale by the time the ball moves.
- **Give the target with the hand away from the defender** and call for the ball where you want it. The pass has to arrive on the side he is not on, or the seal you just won is wasted.
- **If you cannot get the position, do not post.** Step out and re-enter, or convert the possession into a screen. A bad catch is worse than no catch, because it burns shot clock and ends in a low-value shot from a spot you chose badly.

## Q zh
为什么教练说低位单打在接球之前就已经决定了？"seal（封位）"具体包含什么？

## A zh
- **接球的位置决定了之后的一切。** 在低位块、一只脚踩进禁区里接球是得分位置；同一个传球在往外四英尺处接到，同样的动作就变成一次被干扰的后仰。任何脚步都补不回这四英尺。
- **seal 是用身体占据并守住防守人与球之间的那块空间：** 宽站架，前臂和髋部顶住他，胸口和双手转向传球人，给出一个他够不到的目标。
- **这是持续的工作，不是一次性动作。** 每一次传球之后都要重新 seal，因为随着传球角度变化，防守人的合法位置也在变。沿用上一次传球时的 seal，在球一转移的瞬间就已经过期了。
- **用远离防守人的那只手给靶子**，并明确要球的位置。球必须送到他不在的那一侧，否则你刚赢下来的 seal 就白费了。
- **如果拿不到位置，就不要落位。** 拉出去重新要位，或者把这个回合转成一次掩护。糟糕的接球比不接球更糟，因为它烧掉进攻时间，最后以一个你自己选错位置的低价值出手收场。
