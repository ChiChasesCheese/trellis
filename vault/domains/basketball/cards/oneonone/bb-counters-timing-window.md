## Q
Your first move worked, the defender recovered, and your counter got stripped. Assume the counter itself was technically sound. What is the most likely error, and what are the two fixes?

## A
- **Timing.** A counter must fire while the defender is still *moving* from your first move — in the window before he re-establishes a stance and a new top foot. Outside that window it is not a counter, it is a fresh 1-on-1 from a standstill.
- Worse, it is a fresh 1-on-1 in which **you have already spent your dribble and the help has arrived**. That is why late counters get stripped rather than merely contained.
- **Mental fix: plan the counter as a branch, not a rescue.** Before the first move you already know "if he cuts me off high, I go back underneath." Then it fires on *recognition* instead of on the realization that the drive failed — and recognition is far faster than realization.
- **Physical fix: the counter comes off the same rhythm**, one dribble continuous with the first move. A reset dribble followed by a second move is exactly the extra beat that lets him get square.
- **Count the defenders too.** After a first move and a recovery, help is usually in the picture. If two defenders are near you, the correct "counter" is very often a pass, not a second move.

## Q zh
你的第一个动作成功了，防守人回了位，然后你的 counter（后手动作）被断了。假设这个后手动作本身在技术上没问题。最可能的错误是什么？有哪两个修正？

## A zh
- **时机。** 后手必须在防守人仍处于你第一个动作造成的**移动之中**时启动——在他重新站好姿态、确立新的 top foot（前脚）之前的那个窗口。超出这个窗口，它就不是后手，而是一次从静止开始的全新单打。
- 更糟的是，这是一次**你已经花掉了运球、而且协防已经到位**的全新单打。这就是为什么晚了的后手会被断掉，而不只是被限制住。
- **思路上的修正：把后手当作预设分支，而不是救场。** 在第一个动作之前你就已经知道"如果他从上面把我截住，我就从下面回去"。于是它由**识别**触发，而不是由"意识到突破失败了"触发——识别远快过意识到。
- **身体上的修正：后手要接着同一个节奏出来**，一次运球与第一个动作连成一体。先运一下重置、再做第二个动作，正好就是让他站正面的那多余一拍。
- **也要数防守人。** 在一个动作加一次回防之后，协防通常已经进入画面。如果身边有两名防守人，正确的"后手"往往是一次传球，而不是第二个动作。
