## Q
The post defender is either fronting you — fully between you and the ball — or three-quartering you, with an arm and leg across your high side. What is each one conceding, and what do you and the passer do about it?

## A
- **A front concedes the entire area behind him**: he has no body between you and the rim. The answer is the lob over the top, and your job is to **pin** him — seal his back with your body so he cannot retreat — then go get the ball at its highest point.
- **The front only works if the weakside help rotates behind you.** So the read belongs to the passer as much as to you: if the low man is attached to a corner shooter, the lob is free; if he is already dropping to the rim, do not throw it.
- **Three-quarter (topside) concedes the baseline side.** The passer changes the angle — a pass to the corner or a dribble toward the baseline turns his three-quarter into a chase — and you re-seal on the side he is giving up.
- **Against a three-quarter you can also duck in:** as the ball moves, step across his front foot into the lane and demand it immediately, before he can re-establish. The timing is on the flight of the pass, not after it lands.
- **General rule:** fronting is beaten by the pass *over the top* plus a pin; three-quartering is beaten by *moving the ball* to the side he is conceding, then re-sealing there.

## Q zh
低位防守人要么在 front（绕前）你——完全站在你和球之间——要么在 three-quarter（侧前四分之三防守）你，用一条手臂和一条腿卡在你的上侧。这两种各自让出了什么？你和传球人分别该怎么做？

## A zh
- **front 让出了他身后的整片区域**：他和篮筐之间没有他的身体。答案是从头顶吊传（lob），而你的任务是**把他钉住**——用身体封住他的背，让他退不回去——然后去球的最高点把它拿下来。
- **front 只有在弱侧协防能从你背后轮转过来时才成立。** 所以这个阅读既属于你也属于传球人：如果 low man 被底角射手黏住了，这个 lob 是白给的；如果他已经在往篮筐沉，就别传。
- **three-quarter（上侧防守）让出的是底线一侧。** 传球人改变角度——把球传到底角，或者朝底线方向运一步，都会把他的侧前防守变成追防——而你则在他让出的那一侧重新 seal。
- **面对 three-quarter 你还可以 duck in（切入要位）：** 在球转移的过程中，跨过他的前脚踩进禁区，立刻要球，赶在他重新站位之前。时机在传球飞行途中，不是球落地之后。
- **通用准则：** 破 front 靠**从头顶传**加上钉住；破 three-quarter 靠**转移球**到他让出的那一侧，然后在那里重新封位。
