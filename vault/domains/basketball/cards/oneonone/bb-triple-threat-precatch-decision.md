## Q
Coaches say "the first move happens before the catch." What are you actually doing while the ball is in the air, and why does deciding after the catch fail?

## A
- While the pass is travelling you are reading three things: **your defender's stance and cushion**, **where the nearest help defender is standing**, and **whether the pass is arriving on time or late**.
- From those you have already chosen a first option — shoot it, attack his top foot, or move it on — so that the catch is the *start of the action*, not the start of the thinking.
- **Deciding after the catch costs the half second in which the closeout arrives and his feet get set.** The advantage a good pass handed you has a lifespan; sizing him up spends it and gives it back.
- It also changes what the defense does. A player who catches and immediately threatens forces the closeout to be short and off-balance; a player who catches and holds lets all five defenders reset into position.
- **The physical half of the pre-catch decision is footwork:** catch on the inside foot already facing the rim, feet loaded, hands up as a target — so that the option you chose needs no extra step to execute.

## Q zh
教练说"第一个动作发生在接球之前"。球在空中的时候你到底在做什么？为什么接球之后再决定就晚了？

## A zh
- 在传球飞行的过程中，你在读三样东西：**你防守人的站姿和距离**、**最近的协防人站在哪**，以及**这次传球是准时到还是迟到**。
- 由此你已经选好了第一选项——出手、攻击他的 top foot（前脚），或者把球转移出去——于是接球是*动作的开始*，而不是思考的开始。
- **接球之后才决定，代价是 closeout（扑防）到位、他双脚站稳的那半秒。** 一次好传球交到你手里的优势是有寿命的；原地打量他，就是把它花掉再还回去。
- 它还改变防守的行为。接球即刻形成威胁的人，会逼得 closeout 步幅仓促、重心失衡；接球后停住的人，则让五名防守人全部重新站好。
- **接球前决策的身体一半是脚步：** 用内侧脚接球、身体已经面框、双腿已经蓄力、双手举起来当靶子——这样你选定的那个选项不需要多迈一步就能执行。
