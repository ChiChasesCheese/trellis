## Q
What is the "shoulder past hip" moment, why is it a *legal* marker of advantage and not merely a tactical one, and what two things does it obligate you to do?

## A
- **Once your shoulder and torso are past the defender's hip, you have established the driving line.** From that instant he is behind you, and contact he initiates from behind or from the side is a foul on him. The space is yours.
- **Before that moment the space is his.** Lowering a shoulder into a defender whose torso is still in front of you is an offensive foul, no matter how much momentum you have. That is the line the officials are actually watching.
- So it is a **legal** marker: it is the frame at which you stop asking for the lane and start owning it, and every referee's charge/block judgment hinges on where the torsos were at contact.
- **Obligation 1 — get skinny and go straight.** Widening back out around him lengthens your path and hands him the angle to re-establish position in front of you, which resets the whole exchange.
- **Obligation 2 — absorb, don't avoid.** Ride the contact with your body and a braced off arm (a bar, not a push-off) and finish through him. Avoiding contact after you have won the angle is how a drive that was already a foul turns into a missed layup.

## Q zh
"shoulder past hip"（肩过胯）是哪一刻？为什么它是优势的**规则性**标志、而不只是战术性标志？它给你带来哪两项义务？

## A zh
- **一旦你的肩膀和躯干过了防守人的髋部，你就确立了突破线路。** 从那一瞬间起他在你身后，由他从背后或侧面制造的接触是他的犯规。这块空间归你了。
- **在那一刻之前，空间是他的。** 躯干仍在你前面的防守人，你去沉肩撞他，就是进攻犯规，不管你带着多大的冲力。裁判真正盯着的就是这条线。
- 所以它是**规则性**标志：它是你从"请求这条通道"变成"拥有这条通道"的那一帧，而每一次撞人/阻挡的判罚，都取决于接触瞬间两人躯干的相对位置。
- **义务一——把身体收窄，走直线。** 绕开他往外拉宽会拉长你的路径，并把重新站到你前面的角度还给他，整个交锋就重置了。
- **义务二——吸收对抗，而不是躲开它。** 用身体和撑住的非持球臂（是横杠，不是推人）顶着接触完成终结。角度已经赢了却去躲对抗，正是一次本已成立的犯规变成一次投丢上篮的方式。
