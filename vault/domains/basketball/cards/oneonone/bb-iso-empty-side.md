## Q
Compare being isolated on an "empty" side — no teammate in that corner — with being isolated while a teammate stands in the corner beside you. How does the difference change your attack?

## A
- **Empty side:** there is no help defender near that baseline, because there is nobody down there to guard. Your defender is genuinely alone and the entire baseline half of the floor is yours.
- So on an empty side you **drive baseline by default**. The only help must come from across the floor, which is a long rotation you can see starting and beat with a skip pass if it does come.
- **Occupied corner:** your teammate's defender is now the low man. Drive baseline and he is already standing there; drive middle and he must choose between stepping to you and staying with the corner.
- So on an occupied side the value of the drive is in **creating that choice**: attack middle, into the help, and the shot is the corner three or the drop-off, not your layup.
- **Emptying a side is therefore a deliberate act, not sloppy spacing.** Teams clear a corner precisely to delete the nearest helper before an iso or a pick and roll — which is also why the defense will try to send that vacated defender back as an early tag.

## Q zh
比较两种单打：一种是在"empty side"（空侧）——那一侧底角没有队友；另一种是身旁底角站着队友。这个差别如何改变你的进攻方式？

## A zh
- **空侧：** 那条底线附近没有协防人，因为下面根本没人需要防。你的防守人是真正孤身一人，整个底线半场都是你的。
- 所以在空侧你**默认走底线突破**。唯一的协防只能来自场地另一侧，那是一条你能看着它启动的长距离轮转；真来了，用 skip pass（跨场转移）就能打穿。
- **底角有人：** 你队友的防守人现在就是 low man。你走底线，他已经站在那里了；你走中路，他就必须在上前招呼你和留守底角之间做选择。
- 所以在有人的一侧，突破的价值在于**制造那个抉择**：往中路突、撞进协防里，出手权属于底角三分或篮下的 drop-off，而不是你自己的上篮。
- **因此清空一侧是一个有意为之的动作，不是空间站乱了。** 球队清底角，正是为了在 iso 或 pick and roll（挡拆）之前删掉最近的那个协防人——这也是防守方会试图把那名被清走的防守人提前派回来做 tag（招呼）的原因。
