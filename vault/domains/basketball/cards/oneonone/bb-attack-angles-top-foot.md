## Q
Your defender is in a staggered stance with one foot forward. What is the "top foot", why is that the side you attack, and how does he counter?

## A
- **The top foot is the forward, higher foot** — the one nearer the top of the key. A defender staggers his stance deliberately, to force you one direction: usually baseline, or toward your weak hand.
- **Attacking the top foot means driving at the shoulder above that foot.** To stay with you he must open his hips and drag that front foot backwards — a full change of direction — while you are simply continuing to move in a straight line.
- **Attacking the back foot is easy for him:** he is already turned that way, so he just pivots and runs. That is the direction his stance was inviting you into, and every good defender is comfortable there.
- **The mechanics:** put your first step past his top foot, get your near shoulder to his chest, and drive a straight line. You win because a turn costs him more time than a step costs you — that is the whole trade.
- **His counter is to swivel early and re-establish a new top foot in front of you.** That is why the drive must be one decisive step out of a threatening stance, not a build-up of dribbles that gives him time to reset.

## Q zh
你的防守人以前后错开的姿态站立，一只脚在前。什么是 top foot（前脚）？为什么要攻那一侧？他怎么反制？

## A zh
- **top foot 就是靠前、靠上的那只脚**——更接近弧顶的那只。防守人故意错开站位，是为了把你逼向一个方向：通常是逼底线，或者逼向你的弱侧手。
- **攻击 top foot 意味着朝那只脚上方的肩膀突。** 为了跟上你，他必须打开髋部、把那只前脚往后拖——一次彻底的转向——而你只是继续沿直线移动。
- **攻他的后脚对他来说很轻松：** 他本来就朝那个方向转着，只需转身跑起来。那正是他的站姿在邀请你去的方向，任何好防守人在那一侧都很舒服。
- **动作要点：** 第一步就迈过他的 top foot，把近侧肩送到他胸前，然后沿直线突。你赢，是因为一次转向对他的时间成本高于一步对你的成本——全部交易就在这里。
- **他的反制是提前转髋、在你面前重新建立一只新的前脚。** 这就是为什么突破必须是从威胁姿态里迈出的一步果断动作，而不是一串给他时间重新站好的运球铺垫。
