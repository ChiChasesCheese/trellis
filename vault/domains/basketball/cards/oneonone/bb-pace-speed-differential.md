## Q
Why does a player with unremarkable top speed regularly beat quicker defenders off the dribble, while genuinely faster players get contained?

## A
- **Defense is reactive.** The defender is responding to your speed, and he can only match a speed he has already recognized. What beats him is the *change*, not the number.
- **Speed differential is the gap between your slow and your fast.** A player who cruises at 70% and can jump to 100% has a 30-point usable gap; a player who runs at 95% all the time has 5. The second player is faster and easier to guard.
- **Constant speed is easy to guard** because the defender can set his retreat pace once and hold it. He never has to decelerate, and decelerating in a stance is the hardest thing a defender does.
- **Changing pace forces him to change the direction of force** — from backpedalling to planting and pushing. That transition costs him time; you paid nothing for it, because you were the one who chose the moment.
- **The practical rule: arrive at the defender slower than you are able to**, so you still have somewhere to go. If you are already at top speed when you reach him, your only remaining weapon is direction, and he only has to guard one of your two tools.

## Q zh
为什么一个最高速度并不出众的球员能反复用运球过掉更快的防守人，而真正更快的球员却被限制住？

## A zh
- **防守是被动反应的。** 防守人是在对你的速度做反应，而他只能匹配一个他已经识别出来的速度。打败他的是**变化**，不是那个数值。
- **speed differential（速度差）是你的慢和你的快之间的差距。** 一个以 70% 巡航、能提到 100% 的球员，有 30 个点的可用差距；一个全程 95% 的球员只有 5 个点。第二个人更快，也更好防。
- **匀速最好防**，因为防守人只需把后撤速度设定一次就够了。他从不需要减速，而在防守姿态里减速是防守人最难做的事。
- **变速迫使他改变发力方向**——从后撤步变成蹬地。这个转换消耗他的时间；而你没有为此付出任何代价，因为选择时机的人是你。
- **实用准则：到达防守人面前时，跑得要比你能跑的慢**，这样你才还有地方可去。如果你冲到他面前时已经是最高速，剩下的唯一武器只有方向，而他只需要防你两件工具中的一件。
