## Q
You catch in triple threat and jab at your defender. What are the three reactions you are looking for, what does each one give you, and what makes a jab useless?

## A
- **Triple threat is a stance,** not a pose: ball at hip or shoulder in a strong position, knees bent, pivot foot planted, eyes up — shot, drive and pass all equally available, so the defender cannot pre-commit to any one of them.
- **Reaction 1 — he retreats,** dropping his hips and giving ground. He has conceded space: rise into the shot. The jab bought you a clean look, not a drive, and taking the drive instead throws away what you just won.
- **Reaction 2 — he does not move,** staying flat and square with his cushion intact. Nothing changed, so nothing is open. Jab again longer, use a shot fake, or move the ball and cut. Attacking a set, balanced defender straight up is a coin flip.
- **Reaction 3 — he over-reacts,** turning his hips or crossing his top foot toward the jab. His top foot is now committed: drive opposite, or attack the space he just vacated on that same side.
- **What makes a jab useless:** dropping the ball to your knees or standing up out of the stance as you jab. Both announce a drive, so he never has to respect the shot and the jab moves nothing.

## Q zh
你在 triple threat（三威胁）姿态下接球并试探（jab）。你要找的三种反应是什么？各自给了你什么？什么会让 jab 变得毫无用处？

## A zh
- **triple threat 是一个姿态，不是一个造型：** 球置于髋或肩的强位，屈膝，中枢脚踩实，抬头——投、突、传三者同等可用，让防守人无法提前赌其中任何一样。
- **反应一——他后撤，** 沉胯让出空间。他让出了空间：直接起跳出手。jab 给你换来的是一次干净的出手，不是突破；改去突破等于把刚赢来的东西扔掉。
- **反应二——他不动，** 保持平衡、正对你，距离不变。什么都没变，所以什么都没开。再做一次更长的 jab，或者用投篮假动作，或者转移球然后空切。正面强攻一个站稳了的防守人是掷硬币。
- **反应三——他反应过度，** 髋部转过去或者 top foot（前脚）朝试探方向交叉过来。他的前脚已经交待出去了：往反方向突，或者攻击他刚刚在同侧让出来的那块空间。
- **什么会让 jab 失效：** 试探时把球拉低到膝盖，或者直起身子离开姿态。这两样都在宣告你要突破，于是他根本不必尊重投篮，jab 也就什么都调动不了。
