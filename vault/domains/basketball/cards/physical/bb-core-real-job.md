## Q
What is the core's actual job during a basketball movement, and what does that imply about which exercises transfer? Include the posterior chain in your answer.

## A
**The core's job is anti-movement: resisting rotation, extension and side-bend so that force produced by the legs reaches the floor instead of leaking through a soft middle.** On a drive, your hip drives one way and a forearm pushes the other; whatever your trunk gives up is speed you paid for and did not receive.

What transfers, therefore, is bracing under load rather than flexing under no load:
- **anti-rotation and anti-extension work** (carries, planks with load, chops that are resisted rather than swung),
- **loaded single-leg work**, because every cut is a one-legged brace.

Crunches train trunk flexion, which is a movement basketball almost never asks for under load.

**The posterior chain** — glutes, hamstrings, spinal erectors — is the engine behind acceleration and the primary brake in deceleration and landing. It is also the tissue most implicated in protecting the knee, which is why hip-hinge strength shows up in both the performance and the injury conversation.

## Q zh
在篮球动作中，core（核心）真正的工作是什么？这对哪些训练动作能迁移意味着什么？回答中请包含 posterior chain（后链）。

## A zh
**核心的工作是"抗动"：抵抗旋转、抵抗后仰、抵抗侧屈，好让腿产生的力量传到地面，而不是从松软的腰腹漏掉。** 突破时，你的髋往一个方向发力，一条前臂从另一个方向推你；躯干让掉多少，就是你付了钱却没拿到的速度。

因此能迁移的是负荷下的支撑，而不是无负荷下的屈曲：
- **抗旋转、抗伸展类训练**（负重行走、加负荷的平板、有阻力而非甩动的劈砍），
- **负重单腿训练**，因为每一次变向都是一次单腿支撑。

仰卧起坐练的是躯干屈曲，而这是篮球在负荷下几乎从不要求的动作。

**Posterior chain**（臀、腘绳肌、竖脊肌）是加速的发动机，也是减速和落地时的主刹车。它同时是保护膝关节最相关的组织，这就是为什么髋铰链（hip hinge）力量会同时出现在表现和伤病两个话题里。
