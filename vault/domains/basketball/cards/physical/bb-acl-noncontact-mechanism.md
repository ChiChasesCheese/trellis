## Q
Describe the typical non-contact ACL mechanism in basketball, and name the two things in a warm-up that most directly change the risk.

## A
**The mechanism:** a hard deceleration or a single-leg landing where the foot is planted, the knee collapses inward (**valgus**) while the leg is close to straight, and the trunk is off to one side or rotating. Typically it happens landing from a rebound or planting to change direction — not during contact, which is why it looks like nothing happened.

The two features that matter are that the knee is **stiff and near-extended** (little hip and knee flexion to absorb the load) and that it is **collapsing inward** rather than tracking over the foot.

**What changes the risk in a warm-up:**
- **Landing mechanics** — teaching a soft landing that bends at hip and knee, with the knee tracking over the second toe, practised on one leg because that is how the injury happens.
- **Hip and posterior-chain strength**, especially eccentric hamstring and glute work, so the leg can actually control the collapse rather than merely being told not to.

Neuromuscular warm-ups that combine both, done consistently rather than heroically, are the cheapest injury insurance available to an amateur.

## Q zh
描述篮球中典型的非接触性 ACL 损伤机制，并说出热身中最直接改变风险的两件事。

## A zh
**机制：** 一次急停或单腿落地时，脚已经踩定，膝盖在腿接近伸直的状态下向内塌陷（**valgus，膝外翻**），同时躯干偏向一侧或正在旋转。通常发生在抢篮板落地或踩地变向时 —— 不在身体对抗中，所以看上去像什么都没发生。

关键的两个特征是：膝关节**僵硬且接近伸直**（髋膝几乎没有屈曲去吸收负荷），以及它**向内塌陷**而不是沿着脚的方向走。

**热身里能改变风险的两件事：**
- **落地力学** —— 教会髋膝屈曲的柔和落地，膝盖对准第二根脚趾的方向，并且要用单腿练，因为受伤就是那样发生的。
- **髋部与后链力量**，尤其是腘绳肌离心和臀部训练，让腿真的有能力控制住那次塌陷，而不是只被告知"别内扣"。

把两者结合起来的神经肌肉热身，长期坚持而不是偶尔猛练，是业余球员能买到的最便宜的伤病保险。
