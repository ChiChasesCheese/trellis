## Q
An adult amateur lifts to "get stronger for basketball". What are the two on-court outcomes strength training should actually be bought for, and what is the common goal that is a poor purchase?

## A
**Buy these two:**
- **Absorbing contact without losing your line.** Finishing through a forearm, holding a seal, taking a bump on a drive and still arriving at the angle you wanted. This is trunk and hip stiffness plus the ability to brace, not bicep size.
- **Repeatability.** Being the same player in the fourth quarter as in the first. Strength raises the ceiling that each sprint, jump and stop is a percentage of, so a stronger player fatigues more slowly at the same workload.

**The poor purchase: size for its own sake.** Added mass you cannot accelerate makes you slower to change direction and harder on your own joints. Basketball rewards force relative to bodyweight.

Practical shape: heavy, low-rep compound work for force production, plus loaded eccentric and single-leg work for absorption — because on the floor you brake and land on one leg far more often than two.

## Q zh
一名成年业余球员为了"打球更强壮"去举铁。力量训练真正应该买到的是哪两个场上结果？哪个常见目标其实是笔坏买卖？

## A zh
**该买这两样：**
- **吃住身体接触而不丢线路。** 顶着前臂完成终结、卡住位置、突破中被撞一下仍然能到达你想要的角度。这靠的是躯干和髋的刚性以及支撑（brace）能力，不是手臂围度。
- **Repeatability（可重复性）。** 第四节还是第一节那个自己。力量抬高了每一次冲刺、起跳和急停所占比例的那个上限，所以同样负荷下，更强壮的人衰减更慢。

**坏买卖：为了大而大。** 你加速不动的体重，只会让你变向更慢、关节更受累。篮球奖励的是相对体重的力量。

实操形状：大重量、低次数的复合动作练发力，加上负重离心和单腿训练练吸收 —— 因为在场上，你用单腿刹车和落地的次数远多于双腿。
