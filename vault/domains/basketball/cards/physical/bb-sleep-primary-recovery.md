Among everything an amateur can do to recover, {{c1::sleep}} has the largest and best-evidenced effect — larger than ice baths, massage guns or supplements, which are {{c2::marginal}} by comparison. It is the tool that restores the qualities basketball actually depends on: reaction time, decision making and {{c3::motor learning}}, since the skills practised during the day are consolidated overnight. The practical target for an athlete in training is around {{c4::8-9 hours}}, and the most reliable lever is not a gadget but {{c5::a consistent sleep and wake time}}, because a regular schedule stabilizes sleep quality even when total hours are imperfect.

## zh
在业余球员能做的所有恢复手段里，{{c1::sleep}} 的效果最大、证据也最扎实 —— 大过冰浴、筋膜枪或补剂，后者相比之下是 {{c2::marginal}}（边际收益）。它恢复的正是篮球真正依赖的能力：反应时间、决策，以及 {{c3::motor learning}}（运动学习），因为白天练的技术是在夜里被巩固的。训练中的运动员的实际目标大约是 {{c4::8-9 hours}}，而最可靠的抓手不是什么设备，而是 {{c5::a consistent sleep and wake time}}（固定的入睡和起床时间），因为规律作息即使在总时长不理想时也能稳住睡眠质量。
