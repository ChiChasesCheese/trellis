## Q
What is the classic basketball ankle-sprain mechanism, what is the single biggest risk factor, and what actually reduces recurrence?

## A
**Mechanism:** the foot rolls **inward** (inversion) with the ankle pointed down, spraining the ligaments on the outside of the ankle. In basketball it usually happens landing on somebody else's foot under the rim, or cutting on a foot that is already loaded and slightly outside your base.

**Biggest risk factor: a previous sprain.** By a wide margin. A first sprain damages both the ligament and the joint's position sense, so the ankle is later to notice that it is rolling — which is why "it never healed properly" is so common.

**What reduces recurrence:**
- **Balance and proprioception work** — single-leg stability, eyes closed, on unstable surfaces and then in fatigue. This retrains the reflex that catches the roll.
- **Calf and peroneal strength** through full range, so the muscles on the outside of the lower leg can resist inversion.
- **Bracing or taping** for return to play and for high-risk situations, which is genuinely effective but is a supplement to the strength work, not a substitute.
- **Full rehabilitation before returning**, since the recurrence risk is highest in the weeks after coming back early.

## Q zh
篮球中典型的踝关节扭伤机制是什么？最大的风险因素是什么？真正能降低复发的是什么？

## A zh
**机制：** 脚向**内**翻（inversion），踝关节同时下压，扭伤的是踝外侧的韧带。在篮球里通常发生在篮下落地踩到别人的脚上，或者在已经承重、稍微超出支撑面的那只脚上变向。

**最大的风险因素：以前扭过。** 而且遥遥领先。第一次扭伤同时损伤了韧带和关节的位置觉，之后踝关节察觉自己正在翻的时间会变晚 —— 这就是"当年那次一直没好利索"如此常见的原因。

**真正降低复发的：**
- **平衡与本体感觉训练** —— 单腿稳定，闭眼、不稳定平面，然后在疲劳状态下做。这是在重新训练那个能"接住"翻脚的反射。
- **小腿与腓骨肌群的全幅度力量**，让小腿外侧的肌肉能对抗内翻。
- **护踝或贴扎**，用于复出和高风险场合，确实有效，但它是力量训练的补充，不是替代。
- **彻底康复后再复出**，因为复发风险最高的正是过早复出后的那几周。
