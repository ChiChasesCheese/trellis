Athletic qualities split roughly into {{c1::strength}} qualities — slow, high-force expressions like a heavy squat, which build the ability to *produce and absorb* force — and {{c2::elastic}} qualities — fast, reactive expressions like a bounding stride, which use the stretch-shortening cycle to *reuse* force in very short ground contacts. On court, a hard first step and a hard stop are limited mainly by {{c3::strength}}, while a second jump on a rebound and a quick shuffle are limited mainly by {{c4::elastic}} qualities. Training only one leaves you slow in the other, which is why a basketball week needs both.

## zh
运动能力大致分为两类：{{c1::strength}} 类 —— 慢速、高力量的表现，比如大重量深蹲，建立的是*产生和吸收*力量的能力；以及 {{c2::elastic}} 类 —— 快速、反应性的表现，比如跨步跳，靠 stretch-shortening cycle（拉长-缩短周期）在极短的触地时间里*复用*力量。在场上，第一步的爆发和一次急停主要受 {{c3::strength}} 限制，而抢篮板的第二跳和快速横向滑步主要受 {{c4::elastic}} 类限制。只练一边，另一边就会慢，所以一周的篮球训练两者都需要。
