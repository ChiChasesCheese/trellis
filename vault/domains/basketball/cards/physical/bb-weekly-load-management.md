## Q
You play twice a week and lift twice a week, and you have decided to add three more runs because you want to improve faster. What is the principle you are about to violate, and what are the signals that you are accumulating fatigue rather than adapting?

## A
**The principle: adaptation happens during recovery, not during work — so total weekly load, not any single session, is the variable that decides whether you improve.** A large, sudden jump in weekly volume is also the classic setup for overuse injury; load should change gradually relative to what you are already used to.

**Signals that you are accumulating rather than adapting:**
- Performance drifting down over weeks — your usual shots feel heavy, your first step is slower, your vertical is quietly lower.
- **Elevated resting heart rate** in the morning and unusually poor sleep quality.
- Soreness that lasts longer than it used to, or joints (knees, Achilles, shins) rather than muscles complaining.
- Motivation dropping and irritability rising, which arrives earlier than most people expect.
- Skill decaying under fatigue: turnovers and defensive lapses climbing late in games.

The correct move is usually to add one thing at a time and to keep one genuinely easy day in the week rather than filling every slot.

## Q zh
你每周打两次球、举两次铁，现在决定再加三次跑步，因为想进步得更快。你即将违反的原则是什么？哪些信号说明你在累积疲劳而不是在适应？

## A zh
**原则：适应发生在恢复期，不在训练当中 —— 所以决定你会不会进步的变量是每周总负荷，而不是任何单次训练。** 周负荷的大幅骤增，也正是过度使用性损伤的经典前奏；负荷应该相对于你已经习惯的量渐进变化。

**在累积而不是在适应的信号：**
- 表现连续数周下滑 —— 平时的投篮觉得沉，第一步变慢，弹跳悄悄降低。
- 早晨**静息心率升高**，睡眠质量异常变差。
- 酸痛持续的时间比以前长，或者叫的是关节（膝、跟腱、胫骨）而不是肌肉。
- 动力下降、易怒上升，这往往比大多数人预期的来得更早。
- 疲劳下技术衰减：比赛末段失误和防守漏人明显增多。

正确的做法通常是一次只加一件事，并且每周保留一天真正轻松的日子，而不是把每个空档都填满。
