## Q
Distinguish the slip screen, the ghost screen, and "rolling to a spot rather than to a person." What is the common error each one fixes?

## A
- **Slip:** you begin to set the screen and dive *before* contact, because your defender has already left early to hedge, show or switch. The slip attacks the space he vacated, and it is a read, not a call.
- **Ghost (a fake or slipped-out screen):** you approach as if to screen, never make contact, brush past and pop out to the perimeter. It is a shooting screener's weapon against switching defenses — there is nothing clean to switch, and the scramble ends with you open behind the arc.
- **Rolling to a spot:** you roll to a *place* on the floor — the front of the rim, the dunker area — on a fixed line, instead of chasing the ball handler around. He gets a predictable target, and the low man is stretched by geometry rather than by your effort.
- **Why the spot matters even when the pass never comes:** the low man still has to honour a roller heading for the rim, and that obligation is what holds the corner three open.
- **The error all three fix is drifting toward the ball.** Chasing the handler keeps you in his driving lane, behind his defender, and shortens the distance between you and the corner shooter — which lets one defender cover both of you, the exact thing spacing exists to prevent.

## Q zh
区分 slip screen、ghost screen，以及"roll 到一个位置而不是一个人"。它们各自修正了哪个共同的错误？

## A zh
- **slip：** 你开始摆出掩护姿势，但在接触**之前**就溜下去，因为你的防守人已经提前离开去 hedge、show 或换防了。slip 攻击的是他让出的空间，它是一个阅读，不是一个喊出来的战术。
- **ghost（假掩护 / 擦身而过的掩护）：** 你像要掩护一样靠过去，却完全不接触，擦身而过并弹出到外线。这是会投篮的掩护人对付换防体系的武器——没有干净的东西可换，一阵慌乱之后你在三分线外空着。
- **roll 到一个位置：** 你沿着一条固定的线，顺下到场上的某个**地点**——篮筐正前方、dunker spot 区域——而不是追着持球人跑。他得到一个可预期的传球目标，而 low man 是被几何位置拉扯的，不是被你的跑动量拉扯的。
- **为什么即使球从没传来，这个位置也重要：** low man 仍然必须尊重一个奔向篮筐的 roller，而正是这份义务把底角三分撑开着。
- **三者共同修正的错误是往球的方向飘。** 追着持球人跑会让你留在他的突破通道里、待在他防守人的身后，并且缩短你和底角射手之间的距离——于是一名防守人就能同时防住你们两个，而这恰恰是 spacing 存在的意义所要防止的事。
