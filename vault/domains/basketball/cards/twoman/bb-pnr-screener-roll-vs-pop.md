## Q
You have just set a ball screen. What determines whether you roll to the rim or pop out behind the arc?

## A
- **Your shooting range is a precondition, not the read.** If you cannot make the shot, popping simply removes you from the play and gives the defense a free man.
- **Read your own defender's coverage.** If he is in drop or already back in the paint, **roll** — you are attacking a body that is retreating and you create the 2-on-1 with the low man. If he hedges or shows hard above the screen, **pop** — he now has a long way to recover outward and you are behind him.
- **Read the ball.** Roll when the handler has turned the corner and is going downhill: you arrive at the rim as the defense collapses behind him, and the pass is a simple one. Pop when he is being contained above the screen and needs a release valve.
- **Read the floor behind you.** If a help defender has already sunk into your roll lane, rolling delivers the ball into two bodies; popping gives the handler a target that is an actual pass rather than a hope.
- **Against a switch, roll and seal early.** A smaller defender is now on you — pin him before he can front you, rather than popping away from the mismatch you were just handed.

## Q zh
你刚设完一个挡拆掩护。什么决定你是 roll（顺下）到篮下还是 pop（外弹）到三分线外？

## A zh
- **你的投篮射程是前提条件，不是那个阅读。** 如果你投不进，pop 只是把你从战术里移除，白送防守一个自由人。
- **读你自己防守人的防法。** 如果他在 drop（沉退）或已经退回禁区，就 **roll**——你攻击的是一个正在后退的身体，并与 low man 制造 2 打 1。如果他 hedge（外弹）或在掩护上方大幅 show（示防），就 **pop**——他现在要往外回防很长一段距离，而你在他身后。
- **读球。** 当持球人已经 turn the corner（转过弯）下压时就 roll：你在防守朝他收缩的同时抵达篮下，传球很简单。当他在掩护上方被限制住、需要一个泄压出口时就 pop。
- **读你身后的场地。** 如果已经有协防人沉进了你的顺下通道，roll 等于把球送进两个人身体里；pop 则给持球人一个真实存在的传球目标，而不是一个指望。
- **面对换防，早 roll 早封位。** 现在换到你身上的是个更小的防守人——在他能绕前之前先把他钉住，而不是从刚拿到的错位里 pop 走。
