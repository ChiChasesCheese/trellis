## Q
You are the screener in a ball screen. What defensive behaviour tells you to slip the screen, and what different behaviour tells you to re-screen instead?

## A
- **Slip trigger — your own defender leaves early.** He steps up to hedge, show or blitz *before* the screen is set. His body is now above you and there is nothing between you and the rim, so you never make contact: you turn and dive into the space he abandoned.
- **Slip also beats an aggressive switch.** As the two defenders begin to exchange assignments, the handover itself is a gap. Diving into it before the switch completes catches both defenders between two men.
- **Re-screen trigger — the action failed but the defenders are scrambled.** Your man got over or under the screen and re-established in front of the handler, which means both defenders are out of stance and moving. Set it again immediately, before they reset.
- **Re-screen also when your angle was wrong.** If the on-ball defender slid comfortably over the top, the screen was pointed the wrong way — change the angle rather than repeating the same screen and expecting a different result.
- **Both are recognition, not calls.** The screener's eyes belong on the *defenders* the entire time, not on the ball — which is the single habit that separates a screener who creates advantages from one who just stands there.

## Q zh
你是挡拆中的掩护人。哪种防守行为提示你该 slip（提前溜掉）？哪种不同的行为提示你该 re-screen（重新掩护）？

## A zh
- **slip 的触发信号——你自己的防守人提前离开。** 他在掩护站定**之前**就上来 hedge（外弹）、show（示防）或 blitz（包夹）。他的身体现在在你上方，你和篮筐之间空无一人，所以你根本不去接触：转身直接顺下，扑进他放弃的那块空间。
- **slip 同样能打穿激进的换防。** 当两名防守人开始交接对位时，交接本身就是一道缝隙。在换防完成之前扎进去，会让两名防守人同时卡在两个人之间。
- **re-screen 的触发信号——战术失败了，但防守人乱了。** 你的对位人从掩护上方或下方过去、并重新站到了持球人前面，这意味着两名防守人都离开了防守姿态、都在移动中。立刻再设一次，赶在他们重新站好之前。
- **角度错了也要 re-screen。** 如果持球防守人轻松地从掩护上方滑了过去，说明掩护的朝向错了——改变角度，而不是重复同一个掩护还指望不同结果。
- **这两者都是识别，不是喊出来的战术。** 掩护人的眼睛全程都该在**防守人**身上，而不是在球上——这一个习惯，就区分了能创造优势的掩护人和只是站在那里的掩护人。
