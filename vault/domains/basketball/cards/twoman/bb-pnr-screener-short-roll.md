## Q
What is the short roll, which coverages create it, and why is it described as a "4-on-3 launcher"?

## A
- **The short roll is stopping in the middle of your roll** — around the free-throw line or the elbow — and catching there, instead of rolling all the way through to the rim.
- **It exists for coverages that commit two defenders to the ball:** a blitz or a hard hedge. Two are on the handler, so the man catching in the short roll faces **three defenders covering four offensive players**.
- **Catching there and reading is the whole point.** You are now the playmaker in a 4-on-3, with four options in front of you: a lob or dump to the rim, a pass to the weakside corner, a swing to the top, or a straight-line drive if nobody steps.
- **Rolling all the way through against a blitz throws the ball into the teeth of the help.** Catching at the elbow gets it past the trap while the defense is still a man short — the advantage is spent where it is largest.
- **What makes it work is timing on the pass out of the trap.** It has to leave early enough that you are catching in space with the trap behind you; catch it late and a rotating defender is already on you, and the 4-on-3 has become 4-on-4.

## Q zh
什么是 short roll（短顺下）？哪些防守会催生它？为什么说它是"4 打 3 的发射器"？

## A zh
- **short roll 就是在顺下途中停住**——大约在罚球线或 elbow（肘区）——并在那里接球，而不是一路顺到篮下。
- **它是为那些把两名防守人投入到球上的防法而存在的：** blitz（包夹）或 hard hedge（强外弹）。两人黏着持球人，所以在 short roll 位置接球的人面对的是**三名防守人覆盖四名进攻人**。
- **在那里接球并阅读，才是全部要点。** 你现在是 4 打 3 里的组织者，面前有四个选项：吊传或分球给篮下、传给弱侧底角、回传弧顶，或者没人上前时直接直线突破。
- **面对 blitz 一路顺到底，等于把球送进协防的牙口里。** 在肘区接球，是在防守还少一个人的时候把球带过了包夹——优势被花在它最大的地方。
- **让它成立的关键是从包夹里传出来的时机。** 球必须出手够早，让你在空位接住、包夹在你身后；接晚了，轮转过来的防守人已经贴上你，4 打 3 就变成了 4 打 4。
