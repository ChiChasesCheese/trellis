## Q
On a wing ball screen your defender jumps to the top side of the screen and turns his chest to force you baseline, while the screener's man sits between you and the rim on the sideline side. What coverage is this, and what are your reads?

## A
- **This is ICE (also called blue, or down).** The defense refuses to let the pick and roll go to the middle of the floor; it traps you in the box bounded by the sideline, the baseline, your on-ball defender and the drop big.
- **Read 1 — reject the screen.** His body is committed to the topside, so cross back and attack the side the screen came *from*. The big is positioned for the other direction and cannot help both.
- **Read 2 — take the space they are giving.** ICE concedes the baseline drive and the short midrange. If the big is sitting deep, that pull-up is genuinely open and is a fair price for them.
- **Read 3 — use the screener's second action.** Because two defenders are committed to the sideline, a re-screen from the opposite angle, or a slip into the middle, turns the coverage inside out.
- **Read 4 — the weak side is understaffed.** Two defenders are pinned to one corner, so a skip pass across the floor often finds the best advantage on the court without you having to beat anybody.
- **The one thing ICE wants** is for you to pick up your dribble inside that box. Never let the second defender arrive with the ball already dead.

## Q zh
在侧翼挡拆中，你的防守人跳到掩护的上侧、用胸口把你逼向底线，而掩护人的对位内线守在你和篮筐之间、靠边线一侧。这是什么防守？你有哪些阅读？

## A zh
- **这是 ICE（也叫 blue 或 down）。** 防守拒绝让 pick and roll（挡拆）走向中路；它把你困在由边线、底线、你的持球防守人和沉退内线围成的方框里。
- **阅读一——reject（拒绝掩护）。** 他的身体已经交待在上侧，所以体前变向回来，攻击掩护**来的那一侧**。内线的站位是为另一个方向准备的，兼顾不了两边。
- **阅读二——收下他们给的空间。** ICE 让出的是底线突破和短中距离。如果内线退得很深，那记急停确实是空的，而这正是他们愿意付的价钱。
- **阅读三——用掩护人的第二次动作。** 因为两名防守人都压在边线一侧，从相反角度的 re-screen，或者一次往中路的 slip，会把整套防守里外翻转。
- **阅读四——弱侧人手不足。** 两名防守人被钉在一个底角，所以一记跨场的 skip pass 常常能找到全场最好的优势，而你根本不需要过掉任何人。
- **ICE 唯一想要的**，是你在那个方框里把球收起来。绝不要让第二名防守人到位时你的球已经死了。
