## Q
You passed and your teammate is now driving. Why is standing still — even in a technically correct spot — a mistake for the two players away from the action, and what are their specific jobs?

## A
- **A stationary shooter is the easiest player in basketball to help off.** His defender can sink one or two steps into the drive, keep him in peripheral vision, and recover on the catch. Nothing forces a choice, so no advantage is transported.
- **A stationary player is also often invisible.** A driver's vision is a narrow cone and the defense creates blind zones behind it. Relocating a few feet along the arc puts you back into his line of sight, which is why an "open" man frequently never gets the ball.
- **Movement changes the closeout, not just the space.** If you slide two steps while your defender steps in to help, he must now recover to a spot he never left from. That extra half second is the entire shot.
- **The two away players have distinct jobs:** one keeps the weakside wing/45 filled so a skip pass has a target, and one stays deep in the corner to hold the low man in his two-man bind. They swap those jobs by relocating as the ball moves.
- **The failure mode to watch for:** both away players drifting toward the ball to "get open". That collapses the exact spacing they were on the floor to provide, and lets one defender guard both of them.

## Q zh
你传了球，队友正在突破。对于远离球的那两名球员来说，为什么站着不动——哪怕站位在技术上是对的——也是错误？他们各自的具体职责是什么？

## A zh
- **站着不动的射手是篮球里最容易被放掉去协防的人。** 他的防守人可以往突破方向沉一两步，用余光盯着他，等球传出来再回位。没有任何东西逼出抉择，于是优势没有被运输出去。
- **站着不动的人往往还是隐形的。** 突破者的视野是一个很窄的锥形，而防守会在它背后制造盲区。沿弧线转移几英尺，就能把你重新放回他的视线里——这正是为什么"空位"的人常常等不到球。
- **移动改变的不只是空间，还有 closeout。** 如果你在防守人往里协防的同时横移两步，他现在必须回防到一个他从未出发过的位置。多出来的那半秒，就是这次出手的全部。
- **两名远离球的球员职责不同：** 一个负责把弱侧侧翼 / 45 度位填满，让 skip pass（跨场转移）有目标；另一个留在深底角，把 low man 继续困在"一人防两人"的两难里。随着球的转移，他们通过相互轮换来交换这两项职责。
- **要警惕的失败模式：** 两名远离球的球员都往球的方向飘过去"找空位"。那会毁掉他们站在场上本该提供的那份空间，让一名防守人同时防住他们俩。
