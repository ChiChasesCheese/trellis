## Q
Your defender is denying you hard — hand in the passing lane, chest turned toward the ball. Why is this the backdoor trigger, and how do you and the passer execute it?

## A
- **Denial is an overplay.** To take away the pass he has given up the space *behind* him and turned his hips so that he cannot see you and the ball at the same time. Both of those are the backdoor's raw material.
- **The backdoor is not a trick; it is the price the defense pays for denying.** An offense that never cuts backdoor invites denial everywhere, which is why a team that will cut once makes the next twenty catches easier.
- **Execution: walk him up first.** Take two steps toward the ball so his weight moves that way and the cushion shrinks, then plant the outside foot and cut hard on a straight line to the rim, inside hand up as a target.
- **The passer's half:** the pass goes **early** — as you plant, not as you arrive — and it goes **low and away from the defender**, usually a bounce pass past his back hip, because a chest pass travels through the arm he already has extended.
- **The read runs in reverse too.** The threat of the backdoor is exactly what makes a defender back off, which then opens the catch you originally wanted. The two options guard each other, and that pairing is why the cut is worth running even when it does not score.

## Q zh
你的防守人在死命地绕前防你——手插在传球路线上，胸口转向球。为什么这是 backdoor（反跑）的触发信号？你和传球人分别怎么执行？

## A zh
- **贴身抢断式防守（deny）就是一种过度防守。** 为了掐掉传球，他放弃了自己**身后**的空间，并且把髋部转成了无法同时看到你和球的角度。这两点正是 backdoor 的原材料。
- **backdoor 不是花招，它是防守为绕前所付的代价。** 一支从不反跑的球队等于到处邀请对手贴身防守；反过来，肯反跑一次的球队，接下来二十次接球都会变轻松。
- **执行：先把他往上带。** 朝球的方向走两步，让他的重心跟过去、距离收缩，然后蹬住外侧脚，沿直线狠切篮筐，内侧手举起来当靶子。
- **传球人的那一半：** 球要**早**出手——在你蹬脚的瞬间，而不是在你到位之后——而且要传得**低、并远离防守人**，通常是一记从他后侧髋部旁边穿过的击地球，因为胸前传球会正好穿过他早就伸出来的那条手臂。
- **这个阅读反过来同样成立。** 正是 backdoor 的威胁让防守人退开，从而打开你最初想要的那次接球。两个选项互相保护着对方，而这种配对正是这次切入即便不得分也值得跑的原因。
