## Q
Compare what you do as the ball handler against a blitz or hard hedge versus against a switch. What is the universal error?

## A
- **Blitz** (two defenders trapping the ball above the screen) and a **hard hedge** (the big jumps out to stop you, then recovers) both put two men on the ball. That means **three defenders are covering four offensive players** — you have a numbers advantage that does not belong to you.
- **Against a blitz your job is to get off it, not beat it.** Split it only if there is genuine daylight between the two defenders' shoulders; otherwise retreat one dribble to create a passing angle and hit the screener on the **short roll**.
- **The pass must be early.** Once the trap arrives and your dribble is dead, the advantage is gone and the turnover risk is at its highest — and a live-ball turnover here becomes a fast break the other way.
- **Against a hard hedge the same logic applies, but the hedger has to recover.** So after you pass, re-cut behind him, or let the screener slip the next screen while his man is still travelling back.
- **A switch is different in kind.** The ball is not trapped, so there is no numbers advantage — this is a **matchup** advantage. Attack the new defender *immediately*, before help can shade over: a big on you means attack downhill; a guard on your screener means throw it in before the defense can dig.
- **The universal error is confusing the two:** passing out of a good mismatch as if it were a trap, or attacking two set defenders as if it were a switch.

## Q zh
作为持球人，面对 blitz 或 hard hedge，与面对 switch（换防），你的做法有何不同？普遍性的错误是什么？

## A zh
- **blitz**（两名防守人在掩护上方包夹球）和 **hard hedge**（内线大幅扑出来阻挡你、随后回防）都把两个人放在球上。这意味着**三名防守人要覆盖四名进攻人**——你手里有一个本不属于你的人数优势。
- **面对 blitz，你的任务是把球脱手，而不是打穿它。** 只有当两名防守人肩膀之间确有明显缝隙时才 split（从中间穿过）；否则后撤一步运球创造传球角度，把球送给做 **short roll**（短顺下）的掩护人。
- **传球必须早。** 一旦包夹到位、你的球已死，优势就没了，而失误风险达到最高——此处一次活球失误会直接变成对手的快攻。
- **面对 hard hedge 逻辑相同，但外弹的内线必须回防。** 所以传球之后，你可以从他背后再切一次，或者让掩护人在他还在往回跑的时候 slip 下一个掩护。
- **switch 则是另一种性质。** 球没有被包夹，所以没有人数优势——这是**错位**优势。**立刻**攻击换过来的防守人，赶在协防挪过来之前：大个换到你身上就直线下压；后卫换到掩护人身上就在防守收缩之前把球喂进去。
- **普遍性的错误就是把两者搞混：** 把一个好错位当成包夹传出去，或者把两个站稳的防守人当成换防去强攻。
