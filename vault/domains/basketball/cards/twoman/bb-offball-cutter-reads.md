## Q
You are coming off a down screen at the wing. Your defender's path relative to the screen determines your cut. Give the four reads and the principle behind them.

## A
- **He trails / chases tight behind you, running through the screen → curl.** Turn tightly around the screener into the lane. He is stuck behind you with nowhere to go, and the screener's man has to pick you up, which is the switch you wanted.
- **He goes under, ducking below the screener to beat you to the spot → straight cut / pop to the ball.** He has conceded the catch, so come off flat, catch in rhythm and be ready to shoot immediately before he closes.
- **He top-locks or overplays above the screen, denying you the use of it → backdoor.** He has taken away one direction, which means the other is completely free, and he cannot see you and the ball at once.
- **He switches, or the screener's man shows high to cut you off → fade / flare** away from the ball into the space behind the screen, and expect a skip pass rather than a direct one.
- **The principle: read the defender's relationship to the screen, not a memorized route.** The screener reads it with you — if you curl and his man steps up, he re-screens or slips, so the two of you are solving the same problem from opposite sides.

## Q zh
你正借侧翼的 down screen（下掩护）跑出来。你防守人相对于掩护的路线决定了你的跑位。给出四种阅读，以及它们背后的原则。

## A zh
- **他紧贴在你身后穿过掩护尾随追防 → curl（绕切）。** 紧贴掩护人转向禁区。他被卡在你身后无路可走，掩护人的对位人必须接管你——这正是你想要的换防。
- **他从掩护人下方钻过去，抢在你之前到达接球点 → 直上 / pop 迎球。** 他让出了接球，所以走平线出来，有节奏地接住，在他扑到之前立刻准备出手。
- **他 top-lock 或从掩护上方过度贴防、不让你用掩护 → backdoor（反跑）。** 他拿走了一个方向，意味着另一个方向完全空着，而且他无法同时看到你和球。
- **他换防，或掩护人的对位人上提示防截住你 → fade / flare（外弹）**，往远离球的方向切进掩护身后的空档，并预期接到一记 skip pass 而不是直传。
- **原则是：读防守人与掩护的相对关系，而不是背诵一条路线。** 掩护人和你一起读——如果你 curl 而他的对位人上提，他就再设一次掩护或者 slip；你们俩是在从相反的两侧解同一道题。
