A screen is three variables plus the handler's half. {{c1::Angle}} — you point your back and hip toward the spot the ball handler wants to go, so the screen *sends* the defender the wrong way rather than merely standing in his path. {{c2::Timing}} — you arrive and are stationary before the handler starts his move; screening early lets the defender see it and adjust, screening late is a moving screen and an offensive foul. {{c3::Contact}} — you screen the defender's body, not the space he happens to occupy, because a screen he can slide around never happened. And the handler owes {{c4::the setup}} — walking his defender away from the screen first, then coming off it shoulder-to-shoulder so there is no gap to slide through.

## zh
一个掩护由三个变量加上持球人的那一半组成。{{c1::角度（Angle）}}——你把背和髋朝向持球人想去的位置，让掩护把防守人**送**向错误的方向，而不是仅仅站在他的路线上。{{c2::时机（Timing）}}——你要在持球人启动之前到位并站定；掩护设得太早，防守人看得见并能调整，设得太晚就是移动掩护、进攻犯规。{{c3::接触（Contact）}}——你要掩护防守人的身体，而不是他碰巧占据的那块空间，因为一个他能绕过去的掩护等于没发生。而持球人欠的那一半是 {{c4::铺垫（the setup）}}——先把自己的防守人带离掩护，然后擦着掩护人的肩膀过去，不留下任何可以钻过去的缝隙。
