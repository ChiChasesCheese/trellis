## Q
What is the rule "a cut must end", and name what goes wrong when a cut does not end.

## A
- **A cut has a destination and a deadline.** If you cut and the ball does not come, you must clear all the way through to a spot that spaces the floor — usually the opposite corner, or back out to the perimeter — inside about two seconds.
- **Stopping halfway leaves you in the paint,** which does three separate kinds of damage at once.
- **One: your defender is now free help standing in the driving lane** the ball handler was about to use. You personally closed the gap you cut to open.
- **Two: it starts a three-second count,** and more practically it removes a shooter from the arc, so the defense is now defending four players in the space of three.
- **Three: the cut stops pulling anyone.** A cutter who clears all the way through drags his defender across the floor, and that movement is often what opens the weak side even though the pass never came.
- **The corollary is that somebody must fill behind you.** A cut that ends correctly still costs the offense a spot unless the next player rotates up into it, which is why cutting and filling are taught as one rule and not two.

## Q zh
"a cut must end（切入必须有终点）"这条规则是什么？切入没有终点会出什么问题？

## A zh
- **一次切入要有目的地，也要有时限。** 如果你切了而球没来，你必须一路穿过去，到达一个能拉开空间的位置——通常是对侧底角，或者回到外线——大约两秒之内完成。
- **停在半路会把你留在禁区里，** 而这会同时造成三种不同的伤害。
- **其一：你的防守人现在成了免费的协防**，就站在持球人正要用的突破通道里。是你自己堵上了你切入本想打开的那条缝。
- **其二：它开始了三秒计时**，更实际的是它把一个射手从三分线上撤走了，于是防守现在只用三个人的空间就防住了四个人。
- **其三：这次切入不再牵动任何人。** 一路穿过去的切入者会把防守人拖过整个场地，而正是这段移动常常打开弱侧——哪怕球从来没有传过来。
- **推论是：必须有人补到你身后。** 即使切入正确地结束了，除非下一个人轮转上来补位，进攻仍然少了一个点——这就是为什么"切入"和"补位"是被当作一条规则、而不是两条规则来教的。
