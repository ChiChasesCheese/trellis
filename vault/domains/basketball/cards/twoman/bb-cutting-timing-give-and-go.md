## Q
You just passed to the wing. When exactly do you cut, and why is the basket cut called the cheapest advantage in basketball?

## A
- **Cut on the defender's head turn, not on your own pass.** The moment his eyes and chest follow the ball is the moment he stops seeing you. That window lasts about a beat.
- **Give-and-go works because the attention transfer is automatic** — every defender is trained to locate the ball. Delaying even half a second lets him re-find you and re-establish ball-you-man position, and the same cut is now guarded.
- **Cheap because it costs nothing.** No screen, no dribble, no called set, no turnover risk in the cut itself. It exploits a defensive *habit* rather than beating a defender athletically, which is why it works at every level and against faster opponents.
- **It pays even when the ball does not come.** Your cut drags a defender through the lane, which either reopens the gap you left or forces a help defender to pick you up and abandon someone else — an advantage created for free.
- **Two conditions:** the cut must be at full speed, and it must go all the way to the rim. A lazy cut to the elbow is guarded by the same ball-watching defender and clogs the lane instead of clearing it.

## Q zh
你刚把球传到侧翼。你到底该在什么时候切入？为什么 basket cut（空切篮下）被称作篮球里最便宜的优势？

## A zh
- **在防守人转头的那一刻切，而不是在你自己传球的那一刻切。** 他的眼睛和胸口跟着球转过去的瞬间，就是他不再看得见你的瞬间。这个窗口大约只有一拍。
- **give-and-go（传切配合）之所以有效，是因为注意力的转移是自动发生的**——每个防守人都被训练成要找球。哪怕晚半秒，他就能重新找到你、重新建立"球—你—人"的位置，同样的切入现在被防住了。
- **便宜，是因为它不花任何成本。** 不需要掩护、不需要运球、不需要喊战术，切入本身也没有失误风险。它利用的是防守的**习惯**，而不是靠身体天赋过人，所以它在任何级别、面对更快的对手时都有效。
- **即使球没传来，它也有回报。** 你的切入把一名防守人拖过禁区，这要么重新打开你离开时留下的缝隙，要么迫使一名协防人接管你、从而放掉别人——一个白得的优势。
- **两个条件：** 切入必须是全速的，而且必须一路切到篮下。懒洋洋地切到肘区，会被同一个正在看球的防守人防住，而且是把禁区堵上而不是让开。
