## Q
Your teammate drives baseline on your side of the floor. Do you drift or lift? Define both, and give the rule that decides.

## A
- **Drift** means moving *away* from the ball along the arc, deeper toward the corner and the baseline side. **Lift** means moving *up* the arc, from the corner toward the wing or the slot.
- **The rule: move away from the drive, in the direction that lengthens the closeout and keeps you inside the driver's line of sight.**
- **Baseline drive toward you → lift.** Standing in the corner as he arrives means you and he are competing for the same twelve feet, your defender is already there as the low man, and the only pass available is a six-foot flip into traffic.
- **Middle drive away from you → drift** toward the corner. Your defender is stepping in to help, so the extra distance turns his recovery into a long closeout you can attack or shoot over.
- **Both are about the angle as much as the space.** Relocating changes the passing line from one that runs through a defender's body to one that does not — which is often the entire difference between an assist and a deflection.

## Q zh
你的队友在你这一侧走底线突破。你该 drift 还是 lift？定义这两个词，并给出判断的规则。

## A zh
- **drift** 指沿三分弧线**远离**球移动，往底角、往底线一侧更深处走。**lift** 指沿弧线**往上**移动，从底角提到侧翼或 slot（弧顶两侧）。
- **规则：朝远离突破路线的方向移动，且要选那个能拉长 closeout（扑防）距离、并让你留在突破者视野之内的方向。**
- **底线突破朝你而来 → lift。** 他杀到时你还站在底角，等于你俩在争同一块十二英尺的地方，你的防守人作为 low man 本来就在那儿，而唯一可用的传球是一记塞进人堆的六英尺短传。
- **中路突破远离你 → drift** 向底角。你的防守人正在收缩协防，多出来的这段距离会把他的回防变成一次长距离 closeout，你可以攻击它或者直接投过去。
- **这两者关乎角度的程度不亚于关乎空间。** 转移位置会把传球线路从"穿过一个防守人身体"变成"不穿过任何人"——而这往往就是一次助攻和一次被打掉之间的全部差别。
