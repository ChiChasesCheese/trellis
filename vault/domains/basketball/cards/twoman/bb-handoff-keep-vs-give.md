## Q
You are the dribbler in a handoff. What tells you to keep the ball instead of giving it, and what has to happen next in each case?

## A
- **The read is the receiver's defender, not your own.** If he chases over the top and follows the receiver into the handoff, **give it** — the exchange plus your body screens him off and the receiver comes off downhill with a live advantage.
- **Keep it if he cheats.** If he jumps the handoff, shoots the gap in front of the receiver, or top-locks him (getting above him to deny the handoff entirely), he has committed to a ball you never handed over. Now you are attacking downhill with his defender out of the play.
- **If the two defenders switch the handoff early,** decide by matchup: keep it and attack the new man, or give it and let the receiver attack his. Whichever mismatch is worse for the defense wins.
- **A "keep" is not a fake for its own sake — it is a live drive.** If you keep and then stop, you have handed the defense a free reset with half your dribble already spent.
- **Because keep-vs-give is decided at the moment of contact, the receiver must run it identically every time.** His job is to be a genuine threat on every rep, not to guess whether he is getting the ball.

## Q zh
你是手递手中运球的那一方。什么提示你该 keep（自己留住球）而不是 give（把球递出去）？两种情况下接下来必须发生什么？

## A zh
- **要读的是接球人的防守人，不是你自己的对位人。** 如果他从上方追、跟着接球人一起进入手递手，就 **give**——这次交接加上你的身体把他挡住，接球人带着活的优势顺势下压。
- **他偷跑就 keep。** 如果他抢在手递手之前跳出来、从接球人前面钻缝，或者 top-lock（卡到他上方彻底掐死手递手），他就把自己交待给了一个你从未递出去的球。此时你在直线下压，而他的防守人已经出局了。
- **如果两名防守人提前换防了这次手递手，** 就按错位来决定：留住球攻新换过来的人，或者递出去让接球人攻他的人。谁的错位对防守更糟，就选谁。
- **"keep" 不是为了做假动作而做的假动作——它是一次真实的突破。** 如果你 keep 之后又停下来，就等于在自己的运球已经花掉一半时，白送防守一次免费重整。
- **因为 keep 还是 give 是在接触瞬间才决定的，接球人每一次都必须一模一样地跑。** 他的任务是每一趟都构成真实威胁，而不是去猜自己会不会拿到球。
