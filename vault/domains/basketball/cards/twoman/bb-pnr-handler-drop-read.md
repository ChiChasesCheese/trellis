## Q
You come off a ball screen. The screener's man is in drop coverage — sitting back near the paint rather than stepping out — and your own defender goes *under* the screen. What are your options, and which one does the coverage invite?

## A
- **Drop coverage is built to concede the pull-up and protect the rim.** The big retreats, your man chases from behind, and the two of them try to sandwich you into a contested midrange shot with no lane to the basket.
- **Going under hands you the most invited shot in basketball: the pull-up three off the screen.** The big is too deep to contest and your man is behind the screener. If you can shoot it, that is the answer, and it is the answer the coverage chose for you.
- **Option 2 — re-screen immediately.** If you cannot shoot it, come straight back off the same screen. The defender who went under must now fight over the top or repeat the mistake at a worse angle.
- **Option 3 — snake the screen.** Dribble across the screener's body back toward the middle, which cuts your own defender off *behind* the screen and turns the drop big into a 2-on-1: you arrive in the mid-post with the roller behind you and the big having to choose.
- **What not to do: drive straight into the drop big's chest.** That is precisely the shot the coverage is designed to take away, and it is where the block and the charge both live.
- **The discrimination to memorize:** he goes *under* → shoot it or re-screen. He fights *over* → get into the pocket between the two defenders — turn the corner if the big is deep, snake if the big is up.

## Q zh
你借掩护出来。掩护人的对位内线在 drop coverage（沉退防守）——退在禁区附近而不是扑出来——而你自己的防守人从掩护**下方**穿过。你有哪些选择？这套防守在邀请你做哪一个？

## A zh
- **drop coverage 的设计目的就是放你投急停、保护篮筐。** 内线后撤，你的人从背后追，两人试图把你夹成一次没有篮下路线、被干扰的中距离出手。
- **对方从下方穿过，等于把篮球里最"被邀请"的出手送到你手上：借掩护的三分急停。** 内线退得太深干扰不到，你的人还在掩护人身后。如果你投得进，那就是答案，而且是这套防守替你选的答案。
- **选项二——立刻 re-screen（重新掩护）。** 如果你投不进，就直接原路借同一个掩护回来。从下方穿过的防守人现在必须从上方硬挤，或者在更糟的角度上重犯一次错误。
- **选项三——snake（蛇形回切）。** 贴着掩护人的身体往中路方向横运，把你自己的防守人截在掩护**身后**，把沉退的内线变成一个 2 打 1：你到达中位，roller 在你身后，内线必须做选择。
- **不该做的：直接撞进沉退内线的胸口。** 那正是这套防守要拿走的那次出手，也是盖帽和带球撞人同时存在的地方。
- **要背下来的判别：** 他从**下方**穿过 → 投，或者 re-screen。他从**上方**硬挤 → 钻进两名防守人之间的口袋——内线退得深就 turn the corner（转过弯下顺），内线上得高就 snake。
