## Q
Why is a dribble handoff (DHO) described as "a screen with the ball", and what does each of the two players owe the action?

## A
- In a DHO the ball carrier dribbles toward a teammate and **hands** the ball to him as he runs past; the carrier's body then immediately becomes the screen on the receiver's defender. It is a ball screen and a pass occurring at the same instant.
- **That simultaneity is the advantage.** The defense cannot pre-switch it the way it can a called ball screen, because the man they would switch onto does not have the ball until the moment of contact.
- **The handoff man owes:** a stationary, wide base at the exchange; the ball *handed* into the receiver's outside hand rather than passed to him; and an immediate second action — turn and roll, or re-screen — because standing still after the handoff wastes the screen he just set.
- **The receiver owes setup and speed.** He must move away first to send his defender's momentum the wrong way, then come off shoulder-to-shoulder. A receiver who arrives slowly and wide gives his defender a free path around the handoff and the action is dead.
- **Why it is everywhere in flow offense and the pistol series:** it manufactures a ball screen with no called play, out of two players simply meeting on the wing.

## Q zh
为什么 dribble handoff（DHO，运球手递手）被形容为"带着球的掩护"？两名球员各自欠这个战术什么？

## A zh
- 在 DHO 里，持球人运球朝队友移动，在队友跑过时把球**递**给他；随即持球人的身体就成了挡住接球人防守人的掩护。它是一次挡拆和一次传球发生在同一瞬间。
- **这个同时性就是优势所在。** 防守无法像对待喊出来的挡拆那样提前换防，因为他们要换过去防的那个人，直到接触的那一刻才拿到球。
- **递球的人欠：** 交接瞬间站定、站宽的支撑面；球是**递**到接球人的外侧手上，而不是传给他；以及一个立刻的第二动作——转身顺下，或者再设一次掩护——因为递完球站着不动，就浪费了他刚设好的那个掩护。
- **接球的人欠铺垫和速度。** 他必须先往反方向走，把防守人的冲力带偏，然后擦着肩膀过来。慢吞吞、绕得很宽地过来的接球人，等于白送防守人一条绕过手递手的通道，战术就死了。
- **为什么它在 flow offense（流动进攻）和 pistol series 里到处都是：** 它不需要任何喊出来的战术，仅凭两名球员在侧翼相遇，就能制造出一次挡拆。
