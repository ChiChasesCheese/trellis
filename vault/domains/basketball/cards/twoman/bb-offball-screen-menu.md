The off-ball screen menu, each shape defined by what it attacks. A {{c1::down screen (pin-down)}} is set from above, facing the baseline, so a shooter can come up to the wing — it attacks a defender trailing from below. A {{c2::flare screen}} sends the cutter *away* from the ball into open space — it attacks a defender cheating up into the passing lane, and it is paired with a skip pass. A {{c3::back screen}} is set behind a defender who is watching the ball, freeing the cutter to the rim — it attacks ball-watching. A {{c4::cross screen}} is set across the lane so a post player can seal on the opposite block — it attacks a defender playing on the wrong side. And {{c5::screening the screener}} follows one screen with a second one for the original screener — it attacks a defense that has just relaxed because it thinks the action is over.

## zh
无球掩护菜单，每种形状都由它攻击的东西来定义。{{c1::down screen (pin-down)}}（下掩护）从上方设置、面朝底线，让射手能提上到侧翼——它攻击的是从下方尾随追防的防守人。{{c2::flare screen}}（外弹掩护）把切出者送向**远离**球的空档——它攻击的是往传球路线上抢位偷跑的防守人，通常与 skip pass（跨场转移）搭配。{{c3::back screen}}（背掩护）设在一名正在看球的防守人身后，把切入者放向篮筐——它攻击的是盯球不盯人。{{c4::cross screen}}（横掩护）横跨禁区设置，让内线球员在另一侧低位封位——它攻击的是站错侧的防守人。而 {{c5::screening the screener}}（为掩护人做掩护）是在一个掩护之后紧接着为原来的掩护人再设一个——它攻击的是刚刚以为战术结束而松懈下来的防守。
