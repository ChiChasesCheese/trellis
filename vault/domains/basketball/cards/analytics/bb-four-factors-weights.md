Dean Oliver's four factors, in descending order of importance, are {{c1::shooting (eFG%)}}, {{c2::turnovers (turnover rate)}}, {{c3::rebounding (offensive rebound rate)}} and {{c4::free throws (free-throw rate)}}. The weights usually quoted are roughly {{c5::40 / 25 / 20 / 15}} percent, which means shooting matters about as much as the other three combined. Each factor has an offensive and a defensive version, so a full team profile is {{c6::eight}} numbers, not four.
