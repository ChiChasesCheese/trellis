## Q
On-off numbers say your team is 9 points per 100 possessions better with your worst starter on the floor. Give two innocent explanations before you conclude he is secretly great, and say what "fit over talent" means.

## A
**Two innocent explanations:**
- **Who he plays with.** On-off compares the team with him to the team without him — and "without him" is a *different team*, usually one missing a starter and running deeper bench minutes. The gap can belong entirely to his teammates.
- **Who he plays against, and when.** If his minutes are concentrated against second units, or in blowouts, the comparison is not like-for-like.

On-off is therefore a **hypothesis generator**: it says "something here is worth explaining", not "this player is worth 9 points".

**Fit over talent** is the idea the number is often gesturing at: a lineup's value is not the sum of its players but the product of complementary skills. Five ball-dominant scorers share one ball; a limited player who spaces the floor, screens honestly and rotates on time can raise a lineup more than a better player who needs the ball. That is a claim about the *combination*, which is exactly why single-player ratings struggle with it.

## Q zh
On-off 数据显示，你队里最弱的首发在场时，球队每 100 回合要好 9 分。在下结论说他其实很强之前，先给出两个无辜的解释，并说明什么是 "fit over talent"（契合优先于天赋）。

## A zh
**两个无辜的解释：**
- **他和谁一起打。** On-off 比的是"有他的球队"和"没他的球队"，而后者是*另一支球队*，通常少了一名首发、替补上场时间更多。这个差距可能完全属于他的队友。
- **他对谁打、什么时候打。** 如果他的时间集中在对方替补阵容，或者集中在分差很大的时段，这个对比本身就不对等。

所以 on-off 是一个**假设生成器**：它说的是"这里有件事值得解释"，而不是"这名球员值 9 分"。

**Fit over talent** 正是这个数字常常在指向的东西：一套阵容的价值不是球员的加总，而是互补技能的乘积。五个都要球的得分手只能分一个球；一个能拉开空间、认真做掩护、轮转不迟到的有限球员，可能比一个更强但需要持球的球员更能抬高阵容。这是关于*组合*的判断，也正是单人评分处理不好它的原因。
