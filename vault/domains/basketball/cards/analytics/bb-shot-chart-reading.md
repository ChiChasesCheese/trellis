## Q
You are handed your own shot chart for a season. What are the three things worth reading off it, and what is the trap that makes most people misread one?

## A
1. **Volume by zone, before percentage.** Where are the shots actually coming from? A chart that is mostly long twos is a shot-selection problem no percentage can rescue.
2. **Points per shot by zone, not field-goal percentage.** 40% from three (1.20 per shot) beats 45% from midrange (0.90 per shot). FG% silently compares shots that are not worth the same.
3. **Corner threes versus above-the-break threes, separately.** The corner is a shorter shot and converts better; merging them hides whether you are getting to the best three on the floor.

**The trap: small samples.** A zone with 14 attempts tells you essentially nothing about your ability there — the confidence interval is wider than the difference you are trying to detect. Read low-volume zones as *"I do not shoot here"*, which is real information about your habits, rather than as *"I am bad here"*, which the data cannot support.

## Q zh
有人把你整个赛季的 shot chart（投篮分布图）给你。有哪三件事值得读？大多数人会在哪一点上读错？

## A zh
1. **先看各区域的出手量，再看命中率。** 出手到底来自哪里？如果图上大部分是 long two，那是选择问题，任何命中率都救不了。
2. **看各区域的每次出手得分，不要看 FG%。** 三分 40%（每次出手 1.20 分）优于中距离 45%（每次出手 0.90 分）。FG% 会悄悄把价值不同的出手放在一起比。
3. **把 corner three（底角三分）和弧顶三分分开看。** 底角距离更短、命中更高；合并统计会掩盖你到底有没有拿到全场最好的那种三分。

**陷阱：小样本。** 一个区域只有 14 次出手，基本说明不了你在那儿的能力 —— 置信区间比你想分辨的差距还宽。低出手量的区域应该读成 *"我不在这儿出手"*（这是关于你习惯的真实信息），而不是 *"我在这儿很差"*（数据支撑不了这个结论）。
