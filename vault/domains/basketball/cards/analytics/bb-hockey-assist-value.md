## Q
What is a "hockey assist", and what does counting them reveal that the assist column hides? Give the practical consequence for how you play.

## A
A **hockey assist** is the pass *before* the assist — the pass that moved the defense and created the situation in which the assist was easy.

What it reveals: the assist column credits whoever happened to make the **last** pass, which is often the least difficult one in the sequence. The drive that collapsed three defenders and the skip pass that beat the rotation get nothing; the simple swing to the open shooter gets the assist. Counting the pass before it exposes who is actually generating advantage as opposed to who is finishing the paperwork.

The practical consequence: **the extra pass is usually correct even though it costs you the assist**, because the second pass in a rotation arrives when the defense is one step further out of position. Play for the second pass — and when you evaluate a teammate, ask who started the possession's advantage, not who ended it.

## Q zh
什么是 "hockey assist"？统计它揭示了 assist（助攻）栏掩盖的什么？对你自己的打法有什么实际影响？

## A zh
**Hockey assist** 指的是助攻*前面*的那一传 —— 那次真正调动了防守、让后面那记助攻变得轻松的传球。

它揭示了什么：助攻栏只记功给恰好传出**最后**一传的人，而那往往是整串动作里最容易的一次。吸引三个防守人的突破、以及打穿轮转的 skip pass 什么都拿不到；那记把球倒给空位射手的简单横传却拿到助攻。统计"助攻前那一传"，能看清谁在真正制造优势，谁只是在办最后一道手续。

实际影响：**多传那一下通常是对的，哪怕它让你丢掉助攻**，因为轮转中的第二传到达时，防守已经又错位了一步。为第二传而打；评价队友时，问的是谁开启了这个回合的优势，而不是谁结束了它。
