## Q
Your team lost by 12. The four factors say: eFG% roughly equal, turnover rate much worse than the opponent's, offensive rebound rate slightly better, free-throw rate equal. What do you fix first, and why is a turnover more expensive than a missed shot?

## A
**Fix the turnovers** — it is the only factor that is clearly out of line, and it is the second-heaviest factor after shooting.

Why a turnover costs more than a miss:
- A missed shot still ends a possession that *had* a chance to score, and it can be rebounded — your team recovers a meaningful share of them.
- A turnover produces **zero points with certainty** and, worse, it is usually a **live ball**: the opponent gets it moving forward against an unset defense, which is their most efficient possession type. You do not just lose your possession, you upgrade theirs.

The general discipline the four factors teach is **diagnosis order**: read the factors, find the one that is furthest from parity, and fix that — instead of the vaguer and more comfortable conclusion that you should "shoot better".

## Q zh
你的球队输了 12 分。Four factors 显示：eFG% 大致持平，turnover rate 比对手差很多，offensive rebound rate 略好，free-throw rate 持平。你先修哪一项？为什么一次失误比一次投失更贵？

## A zh
**先修失误** —— 这是唯一明显失衡的一项，而且它是仅次于投篮的第二重项。

为什么失误比投失更贵：
- 投失至少结束的是一个*有机会得分*的回合，而且球可能被抢下 —— 你的球队能收回相当一部分。
- 失误是**确定性的零分**，更糟的是它通常是 **live ball（活球）**：对手带着球向前推进，打的是尚未落位的防守，那是他们效率最高的回合类型。你不只是丢了自己的球权，还顺手升级了对方的球权。

Four factors 真正教的纪律是**诊断顺序**：读四项数据，找出离持平最远的那一项，去修它 —— 而不是得出"我们得投得更准"这种更模糊、也更舒服的结论。
