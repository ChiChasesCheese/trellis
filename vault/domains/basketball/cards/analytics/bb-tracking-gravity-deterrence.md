## Q
Name the two mirror-image tracking-era ideas — one offensive, one defensive — that describe value created by shots that were never taken, and explain why the box score cannot see either.

## A
- **Gravity** (offense): the defensive attention a player pulls purely by being a threat. His teammate's layup exists because a defender could not leave him. He may finish the possession with no stat at all.
- **Rim deterrence** (defense): the shots an interior defender prevents from being *attempted*. A driver sees him, pulls up short or passes out; the possession ends in a worse shot that never touched the paint. Blocks are the visible residue of deterrence, not its measure — a great rim protector often has *fewer* blocks precisely because nobody challenges him.

**Why the box score misses both:** it records events, and both of these are non-events. Counting stats can only measure things that happened; gravity and deterrence are about the shots the opponent *did not take* and the help that *did not come*. Tracking data made them measurable because it records where all ten players were, not just what happened to the ball.

## Q zh
说出 tracking（追踪数据）时代两个互为镜像的概念 —— 一个进攻端、一个防守端 —— 它们描述的都是"没发生的出手"所创造的价值。并解释为什么 box score 两个都看不见。

## A zh
- **Gravity（进攻端）**：一名球员仅凭自身威胁就吸走的防守注意力。队友那个上篮之所以存在，是因为有个防守人不敢离开他。而他这个回合的数据可能一片空白。
- **Rim deterrence（防守端）**：内线防守人让对手*根本没去尝试*的那些出手。突破的人看到他，提前收住或者传出来，回合以一次从未碰到禁区的更差出手结束。盖帽是威慑的可见残留，不是它的度量 —— 顶级护框者往往盖帽*更少*，恰恰因为没人去挑战他。

**为什么 box score 都漏掉：** 它记录的是事件，而这两者都是"非事件"。计数统计只能测量发生过的事；gravity 和 deterrence 说的是对手*没有出的手*和*没有来的协防*。追踪数据让它们变得可测，因为它记录的是场上十个人分别在哪，而不只是球发生了什么。
