## Q
A five-man lineup is +18 in 40 minutes together. Someone wants to play it more. What is wrong with the inference, and what would make you believe it?

## A
Forty minutes is roughly 80 possessions. At that size the noise swamps the signal: a few threes going in, one opponent's cold night, or a single garbage-time run can produce +18 in a lineup that is actually below average. **Plus-minus over a small sample measures what happened, not what the lineup is.**

Three specific contaminants:
- **Opponent quality** — bench lineups usually play other bench lineups.
- **Score context** — minutes played with a big lead or deficit are not normal basketball.
- **Shot luck** — opponent three-point percentage in a small sample is nearly random and is not something the lineup controls.

What would make you believe it: a **mechanism** you can name, then evidence consistent with it. "This lineup is good because it puts a shooter in each corner and our best passer at the elbow, which is why our rim frequency goes up when they play" is a hypothesis you can check. The number alone is a reason to look, never a verdict.

## Q zh
某个五人组合在一起打了 40 分钟净胜 +18。有人主张多用这套阵容。这个推论错在哪？什么才会让你相信它？

## A zh
40 分钟大约是 80 个回合。在这个量级上噪声完全盖过信号：几个三分进了、对手手感冰凉一晚、或者一段垃圾时间的连续得分，都能让一套其实低于平均的阵容打出 +18。**小样本下的 plus-minus 度量的是发生了什么，不是这套阵容是什么。**

三个具体污染源：
- **对手质量** —— 替补阵容通常对的是替补阵容。
- **比分情境** —— 大比分领先或落后时打的时间不是正常篮球。
- **投篮运气** —— 小样本里对手的三分命中率近乎随机，而且不是这套阵容能控制的。

什么才让你相信：先能说出一个**机制**，再看证据是否与之一致。"这套阵容好，是因为两个底角各有一个射手、最好的传球手在肘区，所以他们在场时我们的篮下出手频率上升" —— 这是一个可以验证的假设。数字本身只是去看一眼的理由，从来不是判决。
