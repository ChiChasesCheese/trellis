## Q
Separate shot quality from shot making. You hit a contested step-back two with an open teammate in the corner. Grade it, and say what each of the two concepts is actually measuring.

## A
**Grade: a bad shot that went in.** The outcome does not change the decision.

- **Shot quality** is a property of the *shot before it is released*: location, how open you were, whether it came off the catch or off the dribble, and who is shooting it. It is what you control and what repeats.
- **Shot making** is the residual — the part of whether it went in that is the shooter's skill and luck on that particular attempt. It varies enormously game to game and cannot be relied on.

Why the distinction matters in practice: shot quality is stable enough to plan around over a season, while shot making swings wildly over any single night. If you grade your decisions by outcomes, a hot night teaches you a bad habit and a cold night unteaches a good one. Grade the shot you chose; record whether it went in separately.

## Q zh
把 shot quality（出手质量）和 shot making（投进能力）分开。你在有队友底角空位的情况下，投进了一记被干扰的后撤步两分。给这次出手评分，并说明这两个概念各自度量的是什么。

## A zh
**评分：一次进了的坏出手。** 结果不改变决策的好坏。

- **Shot quality** 是*球出手之前*就确定的属性：位置、你有多空、是接球投还是运球投、以及是谁在投。它是你能控制、并且会重复出现的部分。
- **Shot making** 是残差 —— 球进与否当中属于射手个人技术和当次运气的那部分。它逐场波动极大，靠不住。

为什么这个区分在实践中重要：shot quality 稳定到可以按赛季去规划，而 shot making 在任何单场都剧烈摆动。如果你用结果来评判决策，手感火热的一晚会教会你一个坏习惯，手感冰凉的一晚会毁掉一个好习惯。评判你选择的那次出手，进没进另外单独记。
