The **x-out** is what happens after the low man helps on a baseline drive. Step 1: {{c1::the low man leaves the weak-side corner and stops the ball at the rim}}. Step 2: the ball is kicked to that now-open weak-side corner, and {{c2::the next weak-side defender up (the high weak-side wing) sprints down to close out on the corner}}. Step 3: {{c3::the original low man, once the ball is out of his area, sprints up and out to take the man the second rotator just left}} — the two defenders cross paths in an X, which is where the name comes from. The whole thing lives or dies on {{c4::the second rotation being made on the pass, not after the catch}}, and on {{c5::rotating up to the ball rather than back to your own man}}.

## zh
**X-out（交叉轮转）** 是底线突破时 low man 上去协防之后要发生的事。第一步：{{c1::low man 离开弱侧底角，在篮下把球挡住}}。第二步：球被分到那个此刻空出来的弱侧底角，于是 {{c2::弱侧再往上的那名防守人（弱侧高位侧翼）冲下来对底角做 closeout（封堵）}}。第三步：{{c3::最初那名 low man 在球离开他的区域后，冲向上方去接管刚才第二名轮转者放下的人}}——两名防守人的路线交叉成一个 X，名字就是这么来的。整套东西成败取决于 {{c4::第二次轮转要在传球出手的瞬间完成，而不是等对方接到球之后}}，以及 {{c5::朝球的方向往上轮转，而不是往回找自己原来的人}}。
