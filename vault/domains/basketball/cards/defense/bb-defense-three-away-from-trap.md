## Q
Two of your teammates have trapped the ball in the corner. You are one of the three defenders away from the ball. What do the three of you owe, and why is the **first pass out** the only one that matters?

## A
- **You are playing 3-on-4 on purpose,** so you cannot guard men — you guard **passing lanes and the rim**, in that order, and you rotate off what the trapped player can actually see.
- **The three jobs:** one **interceptor** jumps the nearest release valve (the most obvious escape pass) and tries to steal it; one plays the **middle of the floor** as the safety, because the pass that kills a trap is the one to the middle; one takes the **basket** and is the last line if the ball gets out cleanly.
- **The first pass out is everything.** A trap that forces a bad first pass is a turnover or a scramble you can survive. Once the ball escapes to the middle of the floor, the offense is in a genuine 4-on-3 with a live advantage and your rotations are chasing forever.
- **So you gamble on the first pass and stop gambling after it.** Anticipate hard while the ball is trapped; the instant it is out, everyone sprints to the paint and rebuilds from the inside out — you are back to normal defense with the ball ahead of you.
- **What kills presses at the amateur level:** all three away-defenders going for the same steal, or all three retreating and nobody jumping the outlet. Assign the roles before the possession, not during it.

## Q zh
你的两名队友已经在底角把球包夹住了。你是远离球的三名防守人之一。你们三个欠什么？为什么**第一记外传**是唯一重要的那一传？

## A zh
- **你们是故意在打 3 防 4，** 所以你们守的不是人——而是**传球路线和篮筐**，按这个优先级，并根据被包夹的人实际能看到什么来轮转。
- **三项分工：** 一人做 **interceptor（抢断者）**，扑最近的接应点（最明显的那条逃生传球路线）去抄球；一人守**球场中路**做安全阀，因为能破掉包夹的正是传向中路的那一记；一人守**篮筐**，在球干净地传出去时做最后一道防线。
- **第一记外传就是全部。** 一次逼出糟糕第一传的包夹，换来的是失误或一段你能扛住的混乱。而一旦球被传到球场中路，进攻方就进入了真正带优势的 4 打 3，你的轮转会一路追到底。
- **所以在第一传上赌，第一传之后就不再赌。** 球还被包住时要大胆预判；球一出来，所有人冲向油漆区、由内向外重建阵型——你就回到了球在你身前的正常防守。
- **业余层面击垮全场紧逼的原因：** 三名远离球的防守人都去抢同一个球，或者三个人都往回退、没人扑接应点。角色要在回合开始**之前**分配好，而不是在回合当中。
