## Q
The offense sets a side pick-and-roll for their guard on the right wing, with the screen coming from the middle of the floor. Your bench calls **ICE** (also called **blue** or **down**). Describe what the on-ball defender and the big each do, what the coverage is trying to achieve, and the one shot you are accepting.

## A
- **On-ball defender:** jump to the **top side of the screen** — put your body between the handler and the screener so he physically cannot use it — and turn him **toward the baseline**. You are refusing the screen rather than navigating it.
- **Big:** slide down into the paint on the ball side, sitting at roughly the level of the block, walling off the drive and turning the two of you into a corridor with the sideline and baseline as extra defenders.
- **The goal:** the sideline pick-and-roll is where ICE works, because the sideline is already a defender. Pushing the ball down there shrinks the floor to a corner and denies the offense the middle of the court, which is where every pick-and-roll wants to go.
- **What you accept:** the **baseline mid-range pull-up or floater** from the handler, and a skip pass out of the corner if you over-help. Against a non-shooting big or a downhill guard who cannot shoot that shot, this is a bargain.
- **Where ICE fails:** at the top of the key (there is no sideline to use), and against a handler who simply **rejects** the screen and drives middle — so the on-ball defender's top-side position must be there *before* the screener arrives, not after.

## Q zh
对方在右侧翼给后卫做侧翼挡拆，掩护来自球场中路一侧。你的替补席喊 **ICE**（也叫 **blue** 或 **down**）。描述防球人和大个各自要做什么、这套防守想达成什么、以及你主动接受的是哪一种出手。

## A zh
- **防球人：** 抢到**掩护的上方一侧（top side）**——把身体卡在持球人和掩护人之间，让他根本用不上这个掩护——并把他逼向**底线**方向。你是在拒绝这个掩护，而不是绕过它。
- **大个：** 沿球侧下沉到油漆区，大约站在低位方框（block）的高度，封住突破路线，让你们两人和边线、底线一起围成一条走廊——边线和底线相当于两名额外的防守人。
- **目的：** ICE 在侧翼挡拆上最好用，因为边线本身就是一名防守人。把球压到那里，把场地缩成一个角落，同时不让进攻方进入球场中路——而每一次挡拆想去的都是中路。
- **你接受的出手：** 持球人从底线方向的中距离 pull-up 或抛投，以及你协防过头时从底角传出的 skip pass（大范围横传）。面对不会投篮的大个，或者投不进这种球的冲击型后卫，这笔买卖很划算。
- **ICE 会失效的地方：** 弧顶（那里没有边线可用），以及面对干脆 **reject（拒绝掩护）** 直接往中路突的持球人——所以防球人必须在掩护人到位**之前**就抢好上方站位，而不是之后。
