## Q
You are the first defender back on a 3-on-2 break, with a second teammate arriving a step behind you. The ball handler is coming down the middle at speed. Where do you go, where does your teammate go, and what is the rule that decides it?

## A
- **Rule: protect the rim before you protect the ball.** The deepest man back goes to the front of the rim first, not out to meet the handler. A layup is worth far more than a pull-up, and stopping the ball 25 feet out just means the pass goes over you into the layup you abandoned.
- **So: you sink to the rim, teammate takes the ball.** The second man back is the one who steps up to the level of the free-throw line to stop the handler; the first man back holds the paint and takes whichever of the two wings gets the ball first.
- **Then the two of you play a small zone.** The top defender bluffs at the ball and retreats; the bottom defender slides to whichever side the pass goes and the top defender drops to take the other. You are stalling for the third defender, not trying to get a stop by yourselves.
- **The goal is not a stop, it is a delay.** Every second you buy converts a 3-on-2 into a 3-on-3, and a 3-on-3 is just offense against a set defense. Fouling early or lunging at the ball destroys the delay.
- **"Build the wall":** as more defenders arrive they fill the paint shoulder to shoulder facing the ball, and only then start expanding out to shooters. Defenders match the ball's advance from the inside out — never the reverse.

## Q zh
你是 3 打 2 快攻中第一个退回来的防守人，第二名队友慢你一步正在赶到。持球人正高速从中路推进。你该去哪里？队友去哪里？决定这一切的规则是什么？

## A zh
- **规则：先护篮筐，再护球。** 退得最深的人先站到篮筐正前方，而不是迎出去防持球人。一次上篮的价值远高于一记急停跳投；在离篮 25 英尺处把球挡住，只意味着传球越过你，变成你刚刚放弃的那次上篮。
- **所以：你沉到篮下，队友去防球。** 第二个退回来的人上到罚球线高度去阻挡持球人；第一个退回来的人守住油漆区，并接管两侧翼中先拿到球的那一个。
- **然后你们两人打一个小型区域。** 上方的防守人对球虚晃一下再撤；下方的防守人跟着传球滑到那一侧，上方的人下沉去接管另一侧。你们是在给第三名防守人拖时间，不是想靠两个人完成防守。
- **目标不是防死，而是拖延。** 你每买到一秒，就把 3 打 2 变得更接近 3 打 3，而 3 打 3 不过是对着站好位的防守打阵地战。过早犯规或扑向球，会毁掉这份拖延。
- **"Build the wall（筑墙）"：** 随着更多防守人退到位，他们肩并肩填满油漆区、面向球，然后才开始向外扩张去防射手。防守人要**由内向外**去匹配球的推进——绝不能反过来。
