## Q
The ball is swung to your man on the wing and you are two passes late getting there — a closeout (defender sprinting out at a player who has just caught the ball). Case A: he is a knockdown three-point shooter who is shaky off the dribble. Case B: he is a non-shooter who drives hard right every time. How does your closeout differ, and what stays the same in both?

## A
- **Same in both:** cover ground while the ball is in the air, not after the catch; arrive **under control** rather than fast; and never leave the floor on a shot fake.
- **Case A (shooter):** close out **higher and shorter** — get your top hand into his shooting window and take the shot away first. You accept that he may drive; you are betting a contested drive is worth less than his open three.
- **Case B (driver):** close out **short, low and off to his right** — stop a step early, in a wide stance, with your feet already angled to wall off his strong hand. You concede the shot on purpose because his shot is the cheapest thing he does.
- **The general rule:** a closeout is a choice about *which* shot you give up, not an attempt to give up nothing. Deciding before the ball is in the air is what makes it look effortless.
- **Personnel beats habit:** the same distance and the same hand are wrong against half the league. Know your matchup's shot before the possession starts.

## Q zh
球被转移到侧翼的你的人手上，而你晚了两传的距离才赶到——这是一次 closeout（封堵：防守人冲向刚接到球的进攻人）。情况 A：他是稳定的三分射手，但运球突破一般。情况 B：他不会投篮，但每次都强行往右突。两种情况下你的 closeout 有什么不同，又有什么是共通的？

## A zh
- **两种情况共通的：** 在球还在空中时就跑完距离，而不是等他接到球才动；到位时要**能刹得住**，而不是一味冲快；以及绝不因为一个投篮假动作就起跳。
- **情况 A（射手）：** closeout 要**更高、距离更近**——把上手伸进他的出手窗口，先把投篮拿掉。你接受他可能突破；你赌的是一次被干扰的突破比他的空位三分更便宜。
- **情况 B（突破手）：** closeout 要**短、重心低，并偏向他的右侧**——提前一步刹住，大开立，脚已经摆成封死他强手的角度。你是故意把投篮让给他，因为投篮是他最不值钱的一项。
- **通则：** closeout 是在选择**放弃哪一种出手**，而不是试图什么都不放弃。在球起飞前就做完这个决定，才会让它看起来毫不费力。
- **对人优先于习惯：** 同样的距离、同样的手，对一半的对手都是错的。开始这个回合之前就要知道你对位人的出手方式。
