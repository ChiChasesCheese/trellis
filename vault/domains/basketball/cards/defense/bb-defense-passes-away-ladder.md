Off-ball position is set by how many passes your man is from the ball. **One pass away**: {{c1::deny — a hand and a foot in the passing lane, chest turned so you can see the ball, close enough to touch him}}. **Two passes away**: {{c2::drop off into help — sink toward the middle of the floor and give up the direct pass, because that pass takes long enough for you to recover}}. **Three passes away / weak-side corner**: {{c3::you are effectively guarding the paint, not a man — your feet are in the lane and you only find him when the ball starts to swing}}. The whole ladder is one idea: {{c4::the further your man is from the ball, the more of the floor you owe the team and the less of him you owe}}.

## zh
无球站位由你的人离球有几传决定。**一传之外（one pass away）**：{{c1::deny（掐球路）——一只手和一只脚插进传球路线，胸口转开好让你看得见球，近到伸手能碰到他}}。**两传之外（two passes away）**：{{c2::退到 help（协防）位置——向球场中路收缩，主动放掉那记直接传球，因为那记传球飞行时间够你回追}}。**三传之外／弱侧底角**：{{c3::你实际上守的是禁区而不是人——脚站在油漆区里，只有当球开始转移时才去找他}}。整条阶梯只讲一件事：{{c4::你的人离球越远，你欠球队的场上区域就越多，欠他本人的就越少}}。
