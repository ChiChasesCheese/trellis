## Q
Your team makes contact on every shot — everybody finds a body and holds it — and you are still giving up offensive rebounds. What is the missing half of a box out, and what does the full sequence look like?

## A
- **A box out is two acts: contact, then pursuit.** Making contact only buys you about a second of separation. Teams that stop there are watching the ball land somewhere neither player is standing.
- **The sequence:** (1) on the shot, find your man and **hit him first** — a short step into his body before he can start his run; (2) **feel** him with your backside and forearms while your eyes go to the flight of the ball; (3) **release and go get it** at the moment you can read where it will come down.
- **Never hold and wait.** Holding on longer is both a foul risk and a way to lose the ball to a third player. The block is a springboard, not a destination.
- **Rebound with two hands, high, out of your stance.** Chin it on the way down and land wide — the possession is not over until nobody can dig it out.
- **The unit test:** count how many of your defensive rebounds were caught **outside** the paint. If almost none were, your team is boxing out and never pursuing.
- **The failure mode to name out loud:** "hit and stand." It looks like effort and it is why long rebounds go the other way.

## Q zh
你们队每次出手都有身体接触——每个人都找到人并顶住了——可还是在丢前场篮板。box out（卡位）缺失的另一半是什么？完整的顺序是什么样的？

## A zh
- **一次 box out 是两个动作：先接触，再追球。** 只做接触，大约只能买到一秒的间隔。停在这一步的球队，只会眼睁睁看着球落在两个人都不在的地方。
- **顺序：** (1) 出手瞬间找到你的人，**先撞上他**——在他起跑之前用一小步顶进他身体；(2) 用臀部和前臂**感觉**他的位置，同时眼睛去追球的飞行；(3) 一旦读出球会落在哪里，就**脱离并去把球拿下来**。
- **绝不要顶住等球。** 顶得太久既有犯规风险，也容易把球让给第三个人。低位方框是跳板，不是终点。
- **抢篮板要双手、举高、从蹲姿中弹起。** 落地时把球护到下巴（chin it）并且双脚打开——直到没人能把球掏走，这个回合才算结束。
- **检验方法：** 数一数你们的防守篮板有多少是在油漆区**之外**抢到的。如果几乎没有，说明你们只在卡位、从不追球。
- **要点名的失败模式：** "顶完就站着"（hit and stand）。它看起来很拼，而它正是长篮板总被对手拿走的原因。
