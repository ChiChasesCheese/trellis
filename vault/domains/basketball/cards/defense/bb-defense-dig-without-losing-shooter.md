## Q
The post catches on the block anyway and turns to face. You are guarding a 40% three-point shooter one pass away on the wing. Your coach wants you to **dig** (a quick jab at the ball from the help side). How do you dig without giving up an open three, and when should you not dig at all?

## A
- **Dig on the dribble, not on the catch.** The moment he puts the ball on the floor to make his move, his eyes go down and his handle is exposed — that is the window. Digging at a two-handed chinned ball just gives him a passing read for free.
- **Dig with your feet, not your body.** One or two hard steps in, a swipe at the ball from below, and you are already retreating. Your hips never fully turn toward the post; you stay on the ball-you-man line so the recovery is a step, not a sprint.
- **Come from the top side.** Digging from above him keeps you between your shooter and the ball; digging from the baseline side puts the post *between* you and your man and the kickout is uncontestable.
- **Dig on the blind side.** If he has turned baseline, the dig comes from the middle where he cannot see it. A dig he can see is not a dig, it is an invitation to pass.
- **When not to dig at all:** when your man is an elite shooter and the post is not an elite scorer. Then the honest answer is to stay attached and make the post beat you one-on-one — a contested post-up is a worse shot than an open corner three, and trading them is a losing trade.
- **The principle:** every help action is priced by who you are helping off. Digging off a non-shooter is nearly free; digging off a shooter has to buy something real.

## Q zh
低位球员还是在方框（block）接到了球并转身面框。你在一传之外的侧翼防一名 40% 命中率的三分射手。教练要你去 **dig**（从协防侧快速探手掏一下球）。你怎样 dig 才不会送出空位三分？什么时候干脆不该 dig？

## A zh
- **在他运球时 dig，不要在他接球时 dig。** 他把球放到地上开始做动作的那一刻，眼睛向下、球暴露出来——那才是窗口。对着一个双手抱在下巴处的球去掏，只是白送他一次传球读位。
- **用脚 dig，不要用身体。** 一两步硬步切进去，从下往上撩一下球，然后立刻往回退。你的胯永远不完全转向低位；你始终留在 ball-you-man 线上，让回防只是一步，而不是一段冲刺。
- **从上方（top side）来。** 从他上方去 dig，能让你始终处在你的射手和球之间；从底线一侧去 dig，会让低位球员挡在你和你的人中间，那记外传你根本干扰不到。
- **从他的盲侧 dig。** 如果他已经转向底线，dig 就从他看不见的中路方向来。一次被他看见的 dig 不叫 dig，那叫邀请他传球。
- **什么时候完全不该 dig：** 当你的人是顶级射手而低位球员并不是顶级得分手时。这时诚实的答案是贴住不动，逼低位单打赢你——一次被干扰的低位单打，价值低于一记空位底角三分，用后者换前者是亏本买卖。
- **原则：** 每一次协防的价格，取决于你是从谁身上收缩过来的。从不会投篮的人身上 dig 几乎免费；从射手身上 dig，必须换回真正值钱的东西。
