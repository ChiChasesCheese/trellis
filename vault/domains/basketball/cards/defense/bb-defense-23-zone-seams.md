## Q
You are the left forward (bottom-left) in a 2-3 zone. The ball goes from the left wing to the **short corner** — the spot along the baseline just outside the paint. Who takes it, what happens behind that action, and which three spots is a 2-3 always conceding?

## A
- **You take it.** The bottom defender on the ball side covers the short corner; the centre does **not** chase out there, because the moment the centre leaves the middle, the offense scores at the rim off a duck-in.
- **Behind you, the zone shifts as a body.** The centre slides ball-side to cover the block you just left, the weak-side bottom defender rotates all the way across to the middle of the paint, and the top guards drop toward the elbows. A zone moves in a chain — one man out means four men shift.
- **The three permanent seams of a 2-3:**
  1. **The high post / free-throw line** — nobody's area, and a catch there splits the whole zone because both guards have their backs to it.
  2. **The short corner and the baseline behind the bottom line** — a long baseline pass forces the zone to shift further than it can.
  3. **The corners**, especially the weak-side corner on a skip pass, because the closeout distance is the longest on the floor.
- **The trigger for every movement is the flight of the ball, not the catch.** A zone that shifts on the catch is one pass behind for the entire possession, which is exactly how a good offense converts a zone into open threes.
- **The zone's real weakness is not any seam** — it is that nobody has a man to box out, so a 2-3 must box out by *area* and still gives up more offensive rebounds than man defense.

## Q zh
你在 2-3 联防里担任左侧底线（左下）防守人。球从左侧翼传到 **short corner**（短角：底线上刚出油漆区的那个位置）。谁去防？这个动作背后会发生什么？2-3 永远会放掉哪三个位置？

## A zh
- **由你去防。** 球侧的底线防守人负责 short corner，中锋**不能**追出去，因为中锋一离开中路，进攻方就会在篮下 duck-in（内切要位）得分。
- **在你身后，整个联防像一个身体一样滑动。** 中锋滑向球侧补上你刚离开的低位方框，弱侧底线防守人一路横移到油漆区中央，上方两名后卫下沉到肘区。联防是链式移动——一个人出去，四个人跟着挪。
- **2-3 永远存在的三条缝：**
  1. **高位／罚球线** —— 那是没人负责的区域，球一旦在那里被接到就会把整个联防劈开，因为两名上线后卫都是背对它的。
  2. **Short corner 以及底线防守人身后的底线区域** —— 一记长距离底线传球会逼联防移动超出它能移动的范围。
  3. **两个底角**，尤其是 skip pass（大范围横传）之后的弱侧底角，因为那是全场最长的 closeout（封堵）距离。
- **每一次移动的触发信号是球的飞行，而不是接球。** 等接球才移动的联防，整个回合都会慢一传，而这正是好进攻把联防变成空位三分的方式。
- **联防真正的弱点不是任何一条缝** —— 而是没有人有固定的人可以 box out（卡位），所以 2-3 只能按区域卡位，前场篮板依然比盯人防守丢得多。
