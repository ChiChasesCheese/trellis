## Q
A driver beats his man baseline on the right side. Your team's rule is "the **low man** helps." At that instant, who is the low man, what exactly is he responsible for, and what is he specifically not allowed to do?

## A
- **Who:** the low man is the **weak-side defender closest to the baseline** — usually the defender guarding the opposite corner or the weak-side block. He is defined by position on the floor, not by his jersey number or his man.
- **His job:** step across into the driver's path **early**, from the weak-side low position, and take the charge or wall up vertically. He is the designated rim protector on a baseline drive, and he goes on the *drive*, not on the gather.
- **He must be early.** A low man who arrives as the ball is being gathered gets a blocking foul or a dunk. Rotation is a decision made when the driver's shoulder passes the defender's hip — half a second earlier than instinct wants.
- **What he may not do:** he may not "stunt and stay." A half-committed low man gives up the layup *and* the corner three, which is the worst possible outcome. Rotations are binary — you go, or you do not go and trust the on-ball defender.
- **The unglamorous part:** the low man giving up the corner three is not a mistake — it is the *plan*. The mistake belongs to whoever fails to make the next rotation and cover him.

## Q zh
一名突破手从右侧底线方向过掉了防守人。你们队的规则是"**low man** 上来协防"。在那一瞬间，谁是 low man？他具体负责什么？他明确不许做什么？

## A zh
- **谁是 low man：** 弱侧**离底线最近的那名防守人**——通常是防对面底角或弱侧低位的人。他由场上位置定义，不是由球衣号码或他对位的人定义。
- **他的任务：** 从弱侧低位**提前**横切进突破路线，制造带球撞人，或者垂直起跳用身体墙封。底线突破时他就是指定的护筐人，而且他要在**突破发生时**就动，不是等对方收球才动。
- **他必须早。** 等球被收起来才赶到的 low man，换来的是一次防守犯规或一记扣篮。轮转的决定应该在突破手的肩膀越过防守人胯部时就做出——比直觉想动的时间早半秒。
- **他不许做的事：** 不许"虚晃一下又不上"（stunt and stay）。一个只上了一半的 low man，同时丢掉上篮**和**底角三分，这是最糟的结果。轮转是二元的——要么上，要么不上并且信任防球人。
- **不好看但正确的部分：** low man 放掉底角三分不是错误，那本来就是**计划**。错误属于那个没有完成下一次轮转、没能补上他的人。
