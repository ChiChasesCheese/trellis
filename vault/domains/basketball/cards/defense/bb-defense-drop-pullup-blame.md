## Q
Your team is in **drop coverage** — your big retreats toward the rim on ball screens instead of stepping out. The opposing guard comes off the screen at the top of the key and calmly shoots a pull-up three. Whose mistake was it, and what should change on the next possession?

## A
- **It is almost never the big's mistake.** Drop is *designed* to give up that shot in exchange for taking away the rim and the roller. The big did his job by staying home.
- **It is the guard's mistake, if anyone's.** In drop, the on-ball defender's job is to **fight over the top of the screen** and get back into the handler's hip before he can rise. Going under the screen against a shooter hands him the exact shot the coverage already concedes.
- **The screen navigation detail:** get skinny and shoulder-through on the screener's high side, and take the shortest recovery path — you are chasing to contest from behind, not to get in front again.
- **If he keeps making it, drop is the wrong coverage for him** — that is the honest answer. Move him to a **hedge** (big steps out, then recovers), an **ICE** if the screen is on the wing, or flat-out **blitz** him. Changing coverage is how you make a shooter earn it a different way.
- **The trap to avoid:** do not have the big "help a little" out of drop. A big who is neither at the level of the screen nor protecting the rim gives up the three *and* the roll.

## Q zh
你的球队打 **drop coverage**（大个在挡拆时后撤护筐，而不是挤出去）。对方后卫从弧顶掩护后出来，从容地投进一记 pull-up 三分。这是谁的错？下一个回合该改什么？

## A zh
- **这几乎不是大个的错。** Drop 本来就是**设计好**要放掉这记出手，换来掐死篮筐和顺下的大个。他守住位置，就是完成了任务。
- **要说是谁的错，是防持球那名后卫的。** 在 drop 里，防球人的任务是**从掩护上方挤过去（fight over the top）**，在持球人起跳前贴回他的胯位。对着射手从掩护下方绕过（go under），等于直接把这套防守本来就让出的那记出手双手奉上。
- **过掩护的细节：** 侧身收肩、从掩护人的高位挤过去，走最短的回追路线——你是要从后面追上去干扰，不是要重新回到他身前。
- **如果他一直投进，那说明 drop 对他就是错的防守**——这是诚实的答案。对他改成 **hedge**（大个挤出去再回追）；如果挡拆发生在侧翼就用 **ICE**；或者干脆 **blitz**（包夹）他。换防守方式，就是逼射手换一种方式去得分。
- **要避开的陷阱：** 不要让大个在 drop 里"稍微帮一点"。一个既不在掩护高度、又没护住篮筐的大个，三分和顺下会同时丢掉。
