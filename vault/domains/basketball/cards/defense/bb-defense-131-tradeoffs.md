## Q
A team switches to a **1-3-1 zone** — one at the top, three across the free-throw line, one on the baseline. What is this shape trying to do to you, and where do you attack it?

## A
- **What it is built for:** pressure and deflections in the *upper* two-thirds of the floor. The top man and the two wings live in the passing lanes, so a 1-3-1 is really a trapping shape — it invites a pass to the wing, then traps it between the top man and the wing defender with the middle man tagging the high post.
- **The single baseline defender is the whole compromise.** One player is responsible for both corners and the entire baseline, running sideline to sideline. That is impossible against sharp ball movement.
- **So you attack it in three places:** (1) the **corners** — get the ball there and the baseline defender is already late on one side; (2) the **short corner / baseline behind the three-man line**, which is the softest square on the floor; (3) the **high post**, because a catch at the free-throw line has the whole zone facing the wrong way and can hit either corner.
- **The other cost is rebounding.** A 1-3-1 leaves the defensive glass covered by essentially two players in position, which is why it is often used by teams that need turnovers more than they need possessions.
- **When it works:** against a team with one primary handler, poor spacing, or no shooter who can punish the corners — and as a *change-up* for a few possessions, because the value of any zone is largely surprise.

## Q zh
对手改打 **1-3-1 联防**——一人在弧顶，三人横在罚球线一线，一人在底线。这个阵型想对你做什么？你该从哪里进攻它？

## A zh
- **它的设计目的：** 在场地**上方**三分之二区域制造压迫和抄球。顶点和两名侧翼都活在传球路线上，所以 1-3-1 本质上是一个包夹阵型——它引诱你把球传到侧翼，然后由顶点和侧翼防守人合围，中间那人负责 tag（贴防）高位。
- **只有一名底线防守人，这就是它全部的妥协。** 一个人要负责两个底角和整条底线，从边线跑到边线。面对干脆的传导球，这不可能做到。
- **所以要从三个地方打它：** (1) **底角**——球一到那里，底线防守人在某一侧已经晚了；(2) **short corner／三人横线身后的底线区域**，那是全场最软的一格；(3) **高位**，因为在罚球线接到球时整个联防都面朝错误的方向，而你可以直接传向任一底角。
- **另一项代价是篮板。** 1-3-1 实际上只有两名球员在有利位置守防守篮板，这就是为什么它常被那些更需要抢断而不是更需要球权的球队使用。
- **它什么时候有效：** 对手只有一个主要持球人、拉不开空间、或者没有能惩罚底角的射手时——以及作为几个回合的**变招**，因为任何联防的价值很大程度上来自出其不意。
