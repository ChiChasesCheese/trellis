## Q
You reach in at the ball handler, miss, and he goes by you for a layup. A teammate says "stop gambling." Make the actual case for containment over the steal attempt — including when gambling *is* correct.

## A
- **The payoffs are lopsided.** A successful strip wins you one possession. A failed reach gives up a layup *and* pulls a helper out of position, which usually costs a second breakdown on the same play. You are risking roughly two possessions to win one.
- **Reaching moves your weight.** The hand goes out, the shoulders follow, the hips open — you have surrendered your first slide before he has even moved. Most reaches fail on the balance cost, not the timing.
- **Containment is a team promise.** Your four teammates positioned themselves assuming the ball stays in front of you. When you gamble you also silently cancel their plan.
- **Play the ball only on a defined trigger:** a live dribble exposed away from the body, a picked-up dribble (now you swarm and deny), a lazy chest pass you already read, or a handler in traffic with his head down.
- **When gambling is correct:** you are already beaten and behind him, you are down late and need possessions rather than stops, or the opponent's handler is genuinely loose with the ball and the math flips.
- **Default:** hands active and low (mirroring the ball, digging at it) but feet doing the work. The steal is a byproduct of good position, not a substitute for it.

## Q zh
你伸手去掏持球人的球，掏空了，他直接过掉你上篮。队友说"别赌了"。请真正说清楚为什么 containment（把人控在身前）优于抢断——并说明什么时候赌反而是对的。

## A zh
- **收益是不对称的。** 抢断成功让你多赢一个回合。伸手失败则送出一次上篮，**同时**把一名协防人扯离位置，通常还会在同一回合造成第二次崩盘。你是在拿大约两个回合去赌一个回合。
- **伸手会带走你的重心。** 手伸出去，肩膀跟着走，胯打开——他还没动，你的第一步滑步就已经交出去了。大多数伸手失败输在重心，而不是时机。
- **Containment 是你对全队的承诺。** 四名队友的站位都假设球会留在你身前。你去赌的时候，等于悄悄取消了他们的计划。
- **只在明确的触发条件下去够球：** 运球时球离身体过远、他已经收球（此时全队围上去封死出球）、你已经读到的一记随手胸前传球、或者持球人在人堆里低着头。
- **什么时候赌是对的：** 你已经被过掉、人在他身后了；比赛末段落后需要的是球权而不是防守成功；或者对手持球人确实护球很松，这笔账翻过来了。
- **默认做法：** 手保持活跃并放低（跟着球移动、去掏 dig），但靠脚干活。抢断是好站位的副产品，不是好站位的替代品。
