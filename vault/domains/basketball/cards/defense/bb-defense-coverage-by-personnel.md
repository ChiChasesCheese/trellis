## Q
Pick the pick-and-roll coverage for each pairing and say why. (A) A downhill guard who cannot shoot off the dribble, screening with a rim-running big. (B) A shooting big who pops to three, screening for a shot-making guard. (C) A guard who is the opponent's only real creator.

## A
- **(A) Downhill guard + rolling big → drop coverage.** Drop's whole concession is the pull-up jumper, and this guard cannot take it. Your big sits at the level of the paint, walls off the drive, and takes away the roll; the guard's best available shot is the one he is worst at.
- **(B) Shooting big + shot-making guard → switch, if the sizes allow.** You cannot drop (the guard shoots over it) and you cannot hedge with a big who has to recover to the three-point line. Switching kills the screen entirely; the price is a mismatch, which is a smaller price than two open shooters. If sizes do not allow a switch, **ICE** it to the sideline and live with the mid-range.
- **(C) The only creator → blitz or hard hedge, at least on some possessions.** Get the ball out of his hands and make the other four beat you. You are deliberately conceding a 4-on-3 because the three other players converting it is a better outcome than him operating freely.
- **The general rule:** choose the coverage whose concession lands on the opponent's *worst* skill. Every coverage gives something up; scheme choice is only the question of what you would rather give up to whom.
- **And re-choose during the game.** Coverage is not an identity. If the pull-up is falling, change it — a scheme that is being punished and is not changed is a coaching decision, not bad luck.

## Q zh
为以下每组人员搭配挑选挡拆防守方式并说明理由。(A) 一名不会运球急停投篮的冲击型后卫，与一名只会顺下的大个做挡拆。(B) 一名外弹投三分的射手型大个，为一名能投的后卫做掩护。(C) 一名后卫是对手唯一真正的组织进攻点。

## A zh
- **(A) 冲击型后卫 + 顺下大个 → drop coverage。** Drop 让出的正是急停跳投，而这名后卫恰恰投不进。你的大个守在油漆区高度，封住突破、掐掉顺下；后卫能拿到的最好出手，正是他最不擅长的那一种。
- **(B) 射手型大个 + 能投的后卫 → 只要身高体型允许，就 switch（换防）。** 你不能 drop（后卫会直接投过它），也不能让一个还得回追三分线的大个去 hedge。换防彻底废掉这个掩护；代价是错位，而错位比两个空位射手便宜得多。如果体型差距太大不能换，就 **ICE** 把球逼向边线，接受中距离。
- **(C) 唯一的组织点 → blitz 或强 hedge，至少在部分回合这么做。** 把球从他手里逼出去，让另外四个人来赢你。你是故意放出一个 4 打 3，因为让另外三人去把它转化成得分，好过让他从容操作。
- **通则：** 选择那种"所让出的东西"正好落在对手**最差技能**上的防守方式。每一种防守都会让出点什么；scheme choice 无非是在问：你更愿意把什么让给谁。
- **而且要在比赛中重新选。** 防守方式不是身份认同。如果对方急停跳投一直进，就换掉它——一套正在被惩罚却不做调整的防守，是一个教练决定，不是运气不好。
