## Q
On film, your team's help defender always stops the ball, and yet the possession still ends in an open corner three about half the time. Why is the **second** rotation — not the first — the one that actually breaks, and what are the three habits that fix it?

## A
- **The first rotation is easy to make because it is obvious.** A man is driving at the rim; the help is visible, urgent, and rewarded with applause. The second rotation is invisible: nobody is scoring on the second rotator yet, so nothing in the moment tells him to move.
- **It is also physically harder.** The first helper moves *toward* the ball (a short, downhill sprint). The second rotator has to cover a longer distance *away* from the action, often uphill toward a corner, and arrive under control for a closeout.
- **And it is a trust problem.** The second rotator has to abandon his own man before he can see the pass that will punish him — he is moving on a prediction, not on evidence. Most amateurs wait for proof and are then one full pass late for the rest of the possession.
- **Habit 1 — talk before the help.** The helper calls his own rotation out loud ("help right!") so the next defender starts moving at the same instant, not after.
- **Habit 2 — rotate on the pass, never on the catch.** The pass's flight time is your entire budget. If you leave on the catch you will be closing out into a shooter who is already up.
- **Habit 3 — rotate to the ball, not to your man.** After a full rotation you almost never end up back on your original matchup; the team matches up again on the next dead ball or on the way back. Chasing "your" man is what leaves the open shooter.

## Q zh
看录像时，你们队的协防人总能把球挡下来，可这个回合还是有大约一半以底角空位三分结束。为什么真正崩掉的是**第二次**轮转而不是第一次？有哪三个习惯能修好它？

## A zh
- **第一次轮转容易做，因为它显而易见。** 有人正在往篮下突，协防的必要性看得见、很紧急，做了还有掌声。第二次轮转是隐形的：此刻没有人在第二轮转者头上得分，所以当下没有任何东西提醒他动。
- **它在身体上也更难。** 第一名协防人是**朝球**移动（一段短的、顺势向下的冲刺）。第二轮转者要**背离**球的方向跑更长的距离，常常是往底角方向逆着跑，还得能刹住做 closeout（封堵）。
- **它还是一个信任问题。** 第二轮转者必须在看到那记会惩罚他的传球**之前**就放弃自己的人——他是靠预判在动，不是靠证据。大多数业余球员要等看到证据，然后整个回合都慢一整传。
- **习惯 1——协防之前先出声。** 协防的人自己把轮转喊出来（"help right!"），让下一名防守人在同一瞬间启动，而不是在他之后。
- **习惯 2——在传球出手时轮转，绝不在接球后。** 传球的飞行时间就是你全部的预算。等到对方接住球才走，你的 closeout 只会撞上一个已经起跳的射手。
- **习惯 3——朝球轮转，不要回去找自己的人。** 一次完整轮转之后，你几乎不可能回到原本的对位；全队会在下一次死球或回防途中重新 match up（重新对位）。去追"自己的"人，正是空位射手的来源。
