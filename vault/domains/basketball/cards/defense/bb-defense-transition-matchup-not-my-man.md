## Q
Your team gets everybody back in transition, the paint is protected, and then three seconds later a wide-open corner three goes up while two of your defenders are guarding the same player. What went wrong, and what is the rule that prevents it?

## A
- **What went wrong: people went looking for "their" man.** After a shot, players scatter into different lanes; the matchups you had in the half court no longer describe where anyone is. Two defenders both chasing their assignments will collide, and someone is left with nobody.
- **The rule: match up, then sort out.** The first defender back takes the **most dangerous player**, not his own. Everyone else picks up the nearest unguarded threat working from the rim outward. You trade matchups back on the next dead ball, or by calling a switch as you settle.
- **It has to be spoken.** "I've got ball," "I've got the trailer," "I've got two" — transition defense is the loudest thirty feet in basketball because nobody can see the whole floor while running backwards.
- **Count, don't identify.** The first thing to establish is *numbers* — how many of them are ahead of how many of us. Numbers dictate whether you stop the ball or protect the rim; names come later.
- **Accept the temporary mismatch.** A guard briefly on a big in transition is fine — the big has to establish position and post up, which costs the offense the entire advantage of the break. A "correct" matchup found two seconds late costs a layup.

## Q zh
你们全队都退回来了，油漆区也守住了，三秒后却被投出一记大空位底角三分，同时你们有两名防守人守着同一个人。哪里出了问题？什么规则能防止它？

## A zh
- **问题出在：大家都在找"自己的"人。** 出手之后，球员会散到不同的跑动路线上，阵地战里的对位关系已经不再描述任何人的实际位置。两名防守人各自去追自己的对位，必然撞车，而某个人身边一定没人。
- **规则：先 match up（就近对位），再理顺。** 第一个退回来的人接管**威胁最大的那个人**，而不是自己原来的人。其余人从篮筐往外，依次接管最近的无人防守的威胁。等下一次死球，或者在阵地站定时喊一次换防，把对位换回来。
- **这必须喊出来。** "I've got ball（我防球）"、"I've got the trailer（我防跟进的大个）"、"I've got two（我防 2 号位）"——转换防守是篮球场上最吵的三十英尺，因为没有人能一边倒退跑一边看清全场。
- **先数人，不要先认人。** 第一件要确认的事是**人数**——他们有几个人在我们几个人前面。人数决定你是去挡球还是去护筐；名字是之后的事。
- **接受暂时的错位。** 后卫在转换中短暂对上大个完全可以接受——大个必须先要位、打低位，这会让进攻方失去快攻的全部优势。而一个晚了两秒才找对的"正确对位"，代价是一次上篮。
