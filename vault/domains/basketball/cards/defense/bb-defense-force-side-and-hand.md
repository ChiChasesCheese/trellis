## Q
You are guarding a right-handed guard at the top of the key with no screen coming. The scout says force him left (his weak hand). Where do you set your body, what does the rest of your team owe you once you do, and what single mistake makes the whole plan worse than having no plan?

## A
- **Position:** put your **nose on his right shoulder** — body slightly shifted to his strong-hand side — so the only comfortable path is left. You are not standing in front of him; you are standing on one side of him.
- **Stance:** feet staggered with the **top foot** (the one toward his strong hand) forward, so the first slide you have to make is the one you are already loaded for.
- **What the team owes you:** everyone's help position is now built on the assumption he goes left. The weak-side defenders shade to that side; the big sets up on that side of the rim. A force call is a **team** call, not a personal one.
- **The fatal mistake:** forcing him left and then getting beaten *right*. Now four teammates are helping to the wrong side and a beaten defender has no one behind him. A defender who is wrong about direction is more dangerous than a defender with no direction.
- **Rule:** it is always better to lose the ball handler in the direction the team expected than to win the guess and get beaten the other way.

## Q zh
你在弧顶防一名右手为主的后卫，没有挡拆过来。赛前情报说逼他往左（他的弱手）。你的身体该摆在哪里？一旦你这么做，队友欠你什么？哪一个错误会让整套计划比根本没有计划更糟？

## A zh
- **站位：** 把**鼻子对准他的右肩**——身体略微偏向他强手那一侧——让他唯一舒服的路线是往左。你不是正面站在他前面，而是站在他的一侧。
- **姿势：** 双脚前后错开，**上脚**（靠他强手那只脚）在前，这样你需要做的第一步滑步正是你已经蓄好力的那一步。
- **队友欠你的：** 现在所有人的协防站位都建立在"他会往左"这个前提上。弱侧防守人向那一侧靠，内线在篮筐的那一侧准备。**force（逼向某侧）是全队的约定，不是你个人的选择。**
- **致命错误：** 逼他往左，结果被他从**右边**过掉。这时四名队友的协防全在错误的一侧，而被过掉的人身后没有任何人。方向判断错误的防守人，比没有方向的防守人更危险。
- **准则：** 按全队预期的方向被持球人突破，永远好过你猜对了却被从另一边过掉。
