## Q
Your team traps the ball handler and keeps getting split down the middle or beaten by a quick pass out. Explain why a trap is best understood as a **timing device**, and list the triggers that say "trap now."

## A
- **A trap does not win the ball, it wins the clock.** The point is to force the offense to spend four or five seconds solving a problem, so they enter their offense late with a shrinking shot clock and take a worse shot. Steals are the bonus, not the plan.
- **That means the trap must arrive at a moment the handler cannot immediately escape.** Trapping a live dribble in open space is how you get split — he simply dribbles between you.
- **The triggers to trap:**
  - **A picked-up dribble.** The best one. He cannot go anywhere; you swarm and everyone else denies.
  - **The sideline and any corner**, especially the half-court line corner, where two lines act as extra defenders.
  - **The moment after a catch**, before he has surveyed the floor — a trap on a turned back is a trap he has to solve blind.
  - **A weak handler** receiving the ball, or the fourth or fifth pass of a possession when the offense is already tired of moving it.
- **Mechanics:** two defenders arrive **chest to chest, feet inside his feet, no gap between your hips**, hands up in a mirror rather than reaching. A trap with daylight between two bodies is not a trap.
- **When not to trap:** never against a good handler in the middle of the floor with two feet of space, and never as a habit — a trap that is expected is a 4-on-3 you handed over for free.

## Q zh
你们的包夹总是被从中间劈开，或者被一记快速外传破掉。请解释为什么 trap（包夹）最好被理解为一个**计时装置**，并列出"现在可以包夹"的触发条件。

## A zh
- **包夹赢的不是球，而是时间。** 目的是逼进攻方花四五秒去解决一个问题，让他们在进攻时限所剩不多时才开始组织，从而投出更差的一球。抢断是附赠品，不是计划。
- **这意味着包夹必须在持球人无法立刻逃脱的时刻到达。** 在开阔地带对着还在运球的人包夹，就是被劈开的原因——他直接从你们中间运过去。
- **包夹的触发条件：**
  - **对方已经收球。** 这是最好的时机。他哪儿也去不了；你们围上去，其余人全部 deny（掐球路）。
  - **边线和任何角落**，尤其是中场线的角落，那里两条线相当于两名额外的防守人。
  - **刚接到球的瞬间**，在他看清全场之前——对着背身的人包夹，等于逼他闭着眼解题。
  - **控球差的人**拿到球时，或者一个回合传到第四、第五次、进攻方已经传烦了的时候。
- **技术要点：** 两名防守人要**胸贴胸、脚在他的脚内侧、两人胯部之间不留缝隙**，双手举起镜像跟着球，而不是去掏。两个身体之间有光透过来的包夹，就不算包夹。
- **什么时候不要包夹：** 绝不对场地中央、有两英尺空间的优秀持球人包夹；也绝不要养成习惯——一次被预判到的包夹，等于免费送出去的 4 打 3。
