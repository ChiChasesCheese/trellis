Every pick-and-roll coverage is a trade — it protects one thing by conceding another. **Drop** (the big retreats toward the rim): protects {{c1::the rim and the roller}}, concedes {{c2::the pull-up jumper over the screen}}. **Hedge** (the big steps out hard, then recovers): protects {{c3::the pull-up three by forcing the handler wide and backwards}}, concedes {{c4::the short roll — the big is briefly guarding nobody, so 4-on-3 opens behind him}}. **Switch**: protects {{c5::everything at the moment of the screen — no gap opens at all}}, concedes {{c6::the mismatch afterwards, a small on a big in the post or a big on a guard in space}}. **Blitz** (two defenders trap the handler): protects {{c7::against an elite individual scorer by getting the ball out of his hands}}, concedes {{c8::a 4-on-3 for the other three defenders every single time}}.

## zh
每一种 pick & roll（挡拆）防守都是一笔交易——它保住一样东西，代价是放掉另一样。**Drop**（大个后撤护筐）：保住 {{c1::the rim and the roller}}（篮筐和顺下的大个），放掉 {{c2::the pull-up jumper over the screen}}（掩护上方的急停跳投）。**Hedge**（大个横向挤出去再回追）：保住 {{c3::the pull-up three by forcing the handler wide and backwards}}（把持球人逼向外线和后方，从而掐掉急停三分），放掉 {{c4::the short roll — the big is briefly guarding nobody, so 4-on-3 opens behind him}}（short roll：大个有一瞬间谁也没守，他身后就出现 4 打 3）。**Switch（换防）**：保住 {{c5::everything at the moment of the screen — no gap opens at all}}（掩护发生的那一刻的一切——根本不出现空当），放掉 {{c6::the mismatch afterwards, a small on a big in the post or a big on a guard in space}}（之后的错位：小个在低位被大个打，或大个在外线被后卫拉开打）。**Blitz**（两人包夹持球人）：保住 {{c7::against an elite individual scorer by getting the ball out of his hands}}（面对顶级单打手时把球从他手里逼出去），放掉 {{c8::a 4-on-3 for the other three defenders every single time}}（每一次都留给另外三名防守人一个 4 打 3）。
