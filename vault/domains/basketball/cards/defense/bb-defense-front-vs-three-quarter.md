## Q
A strong post scorer is working to seal you on the left block while the ball is on the left wing. You can **front** him (get completely between him and the passer), **three-quarter** him (a forearm and leg across his high side, chest on his ribs), or play behind. How do you choose, and what does each choice demand from your teammates?

## A
- **The decision is made before the catch.** Post defense is a battle for position that ends when the ball arrives; once he catches it on the block with two feet planted, you have already lost most of the possession.
- **Front** when he is a genuinely better scorer than you can contest, or when he is on the block with deep position. It denies the entry completely. **Cost:** you cannot see the ball or box out, so you have surrendered the lob — a weak-side defender must be sitting in the lane ready to cover the lob and to rebound over your back.
- **Three-quarter (high side)** is the default: you take away the direct entry but keep an arm and eyes on him. Use it when he is *above* the block or when the ball is above the free-throw-line extended, where the lob angle is bad for the passer.
- **Three-quarter baseline side** when the ball is at the top and you want to force the entry away from the middle, or when your help is on the middle side.
- **Behind** only against a weak post scorer, or when you want him to catch it far out and burn clock. Playing behind is a decision to guard the shot instead of the catch.
- **The teammate cost is the real deciding factor:** fronting is a five-man commitment. If your weak side will not cover the lob, fronting is not available to you no matter how good it feels.

## Q zh
一名强力低位得分手正在左侧低位方框（block）卡你的位，球在左侧翼。你可以 **front**（绕前，完全站在他和传球人之间）、**three-quarter**（四分之三防守：前臂和一条腿卡在他高位一侧，胸口贴住他的肋部），或者站在他身后。你怎么选？每种选择又要求队友做什么？

## A zh
- **这个决定要在接球之前做完。** 低位防守是一场抢位置的较量，球一到就结束了；一旦他在低位双脚站定接到球，这个回合的大部分你已经输了。
- **Front（绕前）** 用于他确实比你能干扰的水平更高，或者他已经在低位要到很深的位置时。它彻底切断了传球入口。**代价：** 你看不见球也无法卡位，等于交出了 lob（吊传）——必须有一名弱侧防守人守在禁区里，随时准备处理吊传，并越过你的背去抢篮板。
- **Three-quarter（高位一侧）** 是默认选择：切断直接传入，同时保持一只手和视线在他身上。适用于他站位**高于**低位方框，或球在罚球线延长线以上——那里传吊传的角度对传球人很差。
- **Three-quarter 底线一侧** 用于球在弧顶、你想把传球入口逼离中路时，或者你的协防在中路一侧时。
- **站在身后** 只用于对手低位威胁不大，或者你希望他在很远的地方接球去耗时间。站在身后是一个决定：守他的出手，而不是守他的接球。
- **真正的决定因素是队友的代价：** front 是五个人的承诺。如果你的弱侧不会去补 lob，那么无论 front 感觉多好，它对你来说都不是一个可用选项。
