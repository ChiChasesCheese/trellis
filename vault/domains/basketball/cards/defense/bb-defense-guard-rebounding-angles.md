## Q
You are a guard. A right wing three goes up. Your instinct is to leak out toward half court. Where should you actually go, why does the ball's likely landing spot say so, and what is the "45-degree rule"?

## A
- **The 45-degree rule:** a shot taken from an angle tends to carom **back out at a similar angle on the opposite side**. A right-wing miss most often comes off to the left side of the floor; a straight-on shot comes back straight, long or short. So the guard on the **weak side** of the shot is the one standing where the ball is going.
- **Threes rebound long.** The further out the shot, the further out the miss — most three-point misses land at or outside the free-throw line, in territory bigs cannot reach because they are pinned under the rim boxing out.
- **Which means the long rebound is a guard's ball, structurally.** Nobody is assigned to it. The team that decides its guards will chase it wins several possessions a game without any extra athleticism.
- **What to actually do:** on the shot, step **into** the floor toward the weak-side elbow area rather than turning and leaking out. Two steps in, then read the flight; you can still get out in transition after the ball is secured.
- **The trade you are making:** leaking out buys you occasional easy baskets and costs you offensive rebounds against your own team. In a half-court game, the possession is worth more than the sprint — leak out only when your team is clearly the better transition team or the shot is a certain miss you cannot reach.

## Q zh
你是后卫。右侧翼一记三分出手了。你的本能是提前往中场溜。你实际上应该去哪里？球最可能的落点为什么这么说？什么是 "45 度规则"？

## A zh
- **45 度规则：** 从一个角度出手的球，倾向于以相近的角度**弹向对面一侧**。右侧翼的打铁最常弹到球场左侧；正面出手的球则弹回正前方，或长或短。所以站在出手点**弱侧**的那名后卫，正好站在球要去的地方。
- **三分球的篮板弹得远。** 出手点越远，打铁弹得越远——大多数三分不中会落在罚球线附近或更外面，那片区域内线够不到，因为他们正被压在篮下卡位。
- **也就是说，长篮板在结构上就是后卫的球。** 没有人被指派去负责它。哪支球队决定让后卫去追这个球，每场比赛都能白赚好几个球权，不需要任何额外的身体天赋。
- **具体该做什么：** 出手瞬间，**朝场内**弱侧肘区方向迈步，而不是转身外溜。先进两步，再读球的飞行；球被控制住之后你照样跑得出快攻。
- **你在做的取舍：** 提前外溜偶尔换来轻松得分，代价是把前场篮板送给对手。在阵地战里，球权比那次冲刺更值钱——只有当你们明显是转换更强的一方，或者这球必定不中而你也够不到时，才提前外溜。
