## Q
You are denying your man one pass away on the wing. He fakes toward the ball, you turn your head to check where the ball is, and he cuts backdoor for a layup. Diagnose the two separate errors and give the habit that fixes each.

## A
- **Error 1 — you turned your head.** Turning to find the ball means that for half a second you can see the ball or your man, never both. The fix is the **open (pistol) stance**: chest angled to split the difference, ball in one edge of your vision and man in the other, so you track both with your eyes and never move your head.
- **Error 2 — you were flat and level with him.** In denial you must be **up the line**, a step toward the ball, so a backdoor cut has to travel *around* you. If you stand shoulder-to-shoulder, the backdoor is a straight line and you are already beaten.
- **On the backdoor itself:** open to the cut and run with him — do not try to trail him on the ball-side; the sprint you lose is unrecoverable.
- **The habit that prevents all of it — jump to the ball.** The instant a pass leaves the passer's hands, take a hard step toward the ball's new location before it arrives. You are re-establishing your position while the ball is in flight, which is the only free time defense ever gets.
- **The underlying principle:** off the ball, your position is a function of where the ball *is going*, not where it is. Defenders who move on the catch are permanently a beat late.

## Q zh
你在侧翼一传之外 deny（掐球路）防你的人。他假装往球的方向走，你转头去确认球在哪儿，他反跑 backdoor 上篮。请指出这里两个各自独立的错误，并给出各自的纠正习惯。

## A zh
- **错误 1——你转头了。** 转头去找球，意味着有半秒钟你要么看得见球、要么看得见人，绝不可能两者兼顾。解决办法是 **open stance（开放式站姿，也叫 pistol stance）**：胸口斜对，让球在视野的一边、人在另一边，用眼睛同时追两个目标，头一动不动。
- **错误 2——你和他站平了。** 在 deny 时你必须 **up the line**，也就是往球的方向站前一步，让 backdoor 反跑必须**绕过**你。如果你和他肩并肩站着，反跑就是一条直线，你在他起步前就已经输了。
- **反跑本身的处理：** 顺势打开身体，和他一起跑——不要试图从球侧尾随他，那一步跑差是补不回来的。
- **能一并预防这一切的习惯——jump to the ball（球一出手就往球移动）。** 传球刚离开传球人的手，就朝球的新位置抢先跨一大步，在球到达之前完成移动。你是在球的飞行时间里重建站位，而那是防守唯一能白拿的时间。
- **底层原则：** 无球防守时，你的位置取决于球**要去哪里**，而不是球在哪里。等到接球才动的防守人，永远慢一拍。
