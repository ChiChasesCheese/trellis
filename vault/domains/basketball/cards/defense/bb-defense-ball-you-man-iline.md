## Q
Your man is standing in the weak-side corner and the ball is on the opposite wing. A coach yells "get on the line, get up the line." What line is he talking about, where exactly should your feet be, and what tells you how far off your man you are allowed to be?

## A
- **The line is the ball-you-man line** (often called the **I-line**): the imaginary straight line from the ball to your man. Your job is to stand *on* that line, closer to the ball than to your man — "on the line" means on it, "up the line" means shifted toward the ball along it.
- **Feet:** open stance, chest angled toward the ball, both feet pointing roughly at the strong-side elbow so one shoulder faces the ball and the other faces your man. You never square up to either one.
- **How far off:** as far as you can be and still recover before the ball arrives after **two** passes — because on a swing you get the flight time of the pass to close out. That is why "two passes away" is a distance rule, not a location rule.
- **The check:** if a driver from the strong side would reach the rim without you being in his path, you are not deep enough. If your man could catch and shoot before your closeout arrives, you are too deep. You live between those two errors.
- **Why amateurs lose here:** they stand next to their man in the corner because it *feels* responsible. That defender is guarding one person; the correct position guards two.

## Q zh
你的人站在弱侧底角，球在对面侧翼。教练大喊 "get on the line, get up the line"。他说的是哪条线？你的脚具体该在哪里？什么决定你可以离你的人多远？

## A zh
- **这条线是 ball-you-man 线**（常称 **I-line**）：从球到你的人之间那条假想直线。你的任务是站**在**这条线上，且靠球比靠人更近——"on the line" 指站在线上，"up the line" 指沿着线往球的方向挪。
- **脚：** open stance（开放式站姿），胸口朝向球，两只脚大致指向强侧肘区，让一侧肩膀对着球、另一侧对着你的人。你永远不正对其中任何一个。
- **能离多远：** 远到在**两**次传球后球到达之前你还能回位为止——因为球转移过来时，你有传球的飞行时间去做 closeout（封堵）。这就是为什么"两传之外"是一条距离规则，而不是一个固定位置。
- **自检：** 如果强侧的突破手能一路到篮下而你完全不在他的路线上，说明你收得不够深；如果你的人接球就能出手而你的 closeout 还没到，说明你收得太深。你就活在这两个错误之间。
- **业余球员在这里输在哪：** 他们贴着自己的人站在底角，因为那样**感觉**很尽责。那个防守人只守住了一个人；正确的站位守住了两个。
