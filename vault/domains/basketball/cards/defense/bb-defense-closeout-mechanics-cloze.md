A closeout (sprinting out at a player who has just caught the ball) is two movements, not one: {{c1::sprint the first two-thirds of the distance while the ball is still in the air}}, then {{c2::chop your feet into short, choppy steps for the last third so you can change direction}}. Arrive with {{c3::a wide base, weight back on the heels-to-midfoot, and the hand nearest his shooting pocket high}} — the high hand takes the vision away without a jump. The stance you land in matters more than the speed you arrived with, because a defender who arrives fast but {{c4::upright, with feet together and weight forward}} is beaten by a single dribble in either direction.

## zh
Closeout（封堵：冲向刚接到球的进攻人）是两段动作，不是一段：{{c1::球还在空中时就冲完前三分之二的距离}}，然后 {{c2::最后三分之一改成短促的碎步，这样才能变向}}。到位时要 {{c3::大开立，重心压在脚跟到脚掌中段，离他出手点最近的那只手举高}}——举高的手不用起跳就能挡住他的视线。你落位时的姿势比你赶到的速度更重要，因为一个冲得很快但 {{c4::身体直立、双脚并拢、重心前倾}} 的防守人，一次运球就会被从任意一侧过掉。
