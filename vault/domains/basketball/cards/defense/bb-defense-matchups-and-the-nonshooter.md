## Q
Before tip-off you assign five matchups. The opponent has one player who genuinely cannot shoot from outside. Where do you put your best defender, and how does that one non-shooter change your entire defensive shape?

## A
- **Your best defender does not automatically guard their best scorer.** Guarding a non-shooter is the most valuable job on the floor, because that defender gets to leave — he becomes a free helper who plays the paint, tags rollers, and takes every low-man rotation while nominally guarding somebody.
- **So the usual answer: your best team defender guards the non-shooter, and your best on-ball defender guards the primary scorer** — those are often two different people. The IQ goes where the roaming happens; the feet go where the ball is.
- **The non-shooter turns your five-man defense into a 4+1.** Everyone else can guard tighter and gamble less, because there is a permanent extra body in the paint. Your PnR coverage can also be more conservative (drop is safer) since you have a free tagger for the roll.
- **The catch: you must actually help off him, all game, on purpose.** Amateur teams identify the non-shooter and then still guard him honestly, which throws away the entire advantage of the scout.
- **And you must be willing to keep helping when he makes one.** He will hit one open three at some point. If that single shot makes you guard him for the remaining twenty minutes, you have let a 25% shooter shoot you out of your defense — which is precisely what that shot was worth to them.
- **Mirror image on offense:** their coach knows this too, so expect them to use him as a screener and a dribble-handoff hub to keep your free helper occupied.

## Q zh
开赛前你要分配五个对位。对手有一名球员确实不会投外线。你把最好的防守人放在哪里？这一个不会投篮的人又如何改变你整套防守形态？

## A zh
- **你最好的防守人不该自动去防他们最好的得分手。** 防守那个不会投篮的人，是场上最有价值的活儿，因为那名防守人可以离开自己的人——他变成一个自由协防人，负责守禁区、tag（贴防）顺下的大个、承担每一次 low man 轮转，名义上却还有人可守。
- **所以通常的答案是：把最好的团队防守人放去守不会投篮的人，把最好的一对一防守人放去守主要得分手**——这往往是两个不同的人。防守智商放在需要游动的位置，脚步放在球所在的位置。
- **这名不会投篮的人把你的五人防守变成了 4+1。** 其他人都可以贴得更紧、赌得更少，因为油漆区里永远多一个人。你的挡拆防守也可以更保守（drop 更安全），因为你有一个免费的 tagger 去处理顺下。
- **前提是：你必须整场、有意识地真的从他身上收缩过来协防。** 业余球队常常认出了这个不会投篮的人，却依然老老实实地贴防他，把情报带来的全部优势原样扔掉。
- **而且他投进一个之后，你必须继续这么做。** 他总会在某个时刻投进一记空位三分。如果这一球让你在接下来的二十分钟里老实贴防他，那就是让一个 25% 命中率的射手把你打出了自己的防守体系——那一球对他们的价值恰恰就在这里。
- **进攻端的镜像：** 对方教练也懂这一点，所以要预料到他们会让他去做掩护、当作 dribble-handoff（运球交接）的枢纽，好把你那个自由协防人拴住。
