## Q
You are dribbling on a 2-on-1 break with a teammate filling the lane to your left. One defender is back, retreating to the front of the rim. What is the rule, what is the most common way amateurs waste this, and what does your teammate owe you?

## A
- **The rule: attack the defender, not the rim, and make him commit before you pass.** Drive straight at his body on a line that forces him to choose. A defender who has not committed is still guarding both of you, and any pass you throw before that is a pass into a live defender.
- **Read his hips and his eyes.** If he turns toward you, the pass is already there. If he stays on your teammate, you finish — and you finish all the way to the rim, not with a floater from twelve feet that lets him guard two people.
- **Pass late and low.** The bounce pass on a 2-on-1 exists because it travels under a retreating defender's hands and arrives at the finisher's speed.
- **The classic waste: passing too early.** A pass thrown from the three-point line lets one defender recover to the ball and turns a 2-on-1 into two contested attempts. The second-most common waste is a jump-stop pull-up that surrenders the numbers entirely.
- **What your teammate owes you: width and a lane, then a straight-line cut to the rim.** He runs *outside* the arc until the defender commits, keeping the two of you far enough apart that one man cannot touch both — then he cuts, he does not drift.
- **Underlying principle:** a numbers advantage is only an advantage while one defender is responsible for two people. Everything you do should extend that state, and every early pass ends it.

## Q zh
你在 2 打 1 快攻中运球推进，队友沿左侧跑道跟上。只有一名防守人退回来，正撤到篮筐正前方。规则是什么？业余球员最常见的浪费方式是什么？你的队友欠你什么？

## A zh
- **规则：攻击防守人，不是攻击篮筐；在传球之前先逼他做选择。** 沿着一条逼他二选一的路线直冲他的身体。一个还没做出选择的防守人，同时守着你们两个人；在那之前传出的任何球，都是传进一个活着的防守人手里。
- **读他的胯和他的眼睛。** 如果他转向你，那记传球已经成立；如果他继续守你的队友，你就自己终结——而且要一路杀到篮下，不要在十二英尺处来一记抛投，让他一个人守住两个人。
- **传得晚、传得低。** 2 打 1 里的击地传球之所以存在，是因为它能从退防人的手底下穿过去，并且以终结者跑动的速度送到位。
- **典型的浪费：传得太早。** 从三分线外传出的球，会让那名防守人回追到球，把 2 打 1 变成两次被干扰的出手。第二常见的浪费是急停跳投，直接把人数优势整个交出去。
- **队友欠你的：拉开宽度、跑出通道，然后直线切向篮筐。** 在防守人做出选择之前，他要跑在三分弧线**外侧**，让你们两人之间的距离大到一个人碰不到两个——然后他要**切进去**，而不是飘着。
- **底层原则：** 人数优势之所以是优势，只因为一名防守人要为两个人负责。你做的每件事都该延长这个状态，而每一记过早的传球都会终结它。
