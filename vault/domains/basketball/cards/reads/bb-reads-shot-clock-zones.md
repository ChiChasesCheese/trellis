On a 24-second shot clock, think in four zones rather than counting down. **24 to 18** is {{c1::transition and early offense — only a genuinely great shot is allowed here, because the alternative is a whole possession of half-court offense}}. **18 to 7** is {{c2::the possession proper: run the action, get a paint touch, and take any good shot the advantage produces}}. **7 to 4** is {{c3::urgency — the bar drops sharply, you attack rather than reverse the ball, and a decent shot is now a good shot}}. **Under 4** is {{c4::get a shot up with someone on the offensive glass — a shot-clock violation is strictly worse than a bad shot because it scores zero and gives up the rebound too}}. The one rule tying them together: {{c5::the quality bar for a shot falls as the clock falls, so the same shot is wrong early and right late}}.

## zh
在 24 秒进攻时限下，要按四个区间去想，而不是倒着数秒。**24 到 18 秒**是 {{c1::转换与早期进攻——这里只允许真正上乘的出手，因为不出手的代价只是打一整回合阵地战}}。**18 到 7 秒**是 {{c2::回合的主体：跑战术、拿到一次 paint touch（禁区触球），并投出优势创造出的任何好机会}}。**7 到 4 秒**是 {{c3::紧迫期——门槛急剧下降，此时要进攻而不是再把球转移一遍，一次尚可的出手现在就算好球}}。**4 秒以内**是 {{c4::必须把球投出去，并且有人在前场篮板位置——24 秒违例严格劣于一次坏球，因为它得零分，还把篮板一并送掉}}。串起这四段的唯一规则：{{c5::随着时钟走低，出手的质量门槛也随之下降，所以同一记出手在早期是错的、在末段是对的}}。
