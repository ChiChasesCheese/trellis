## Q
Your teammate keeps setting screens that "don't work" even though he is in the right place with a solid base. Explain timing as a separate skill — what early, on time, and late each do to a screen and to a cut.

## A
- **On a screen, timing is a two-body problem.** The screen must arrive at the instant the defender is committed to a path he cannot abandon. Set it **early** and the defender simply walks around it before the cutter moves; set it **late** and it is a moving screen or the cutter has already gone.
- **The tell for on time:** the screener arrives and *stops* just before the cutter arrives, so the cutter is a step behind the screen, not level with it. The cutter's job is to wait — most failed screens are cut too soon, not set too slow.
- **On a ball screen, timing belongs to the handler:** he must set the defender up by attacking away from the screen first, then use it. A handler who dribbles straight into a screen lets his defender pre-position through it.
- **On a cut, being early is the same as being guarded.** A cutter who arrives before the passer can see him has removed himself from the play; the pass window is a fraction of a second wide and it opens when the passer's eyes and the cutter's break line up.
- **Being late has one legitimate use:** as a deliberate change of pace. Walking a defender to sleep and then cutting is a timing decision, not a mistake. What is never useful is being late *by accident* — moving at a constant speed makes every one of your actions readable.
- **The practical fix:** count the beat. Screeners arrive on the cutter's second step; cutters leave when the passer's head turns. Timing is trainable precisely because it is a cue, not a feeling.

## Q zh
你的队友总是在设"没起作用"的掩护，尽管位置正确、下盘也稳。请把 timing（时机）当作一项独立技能来解释——早、准时、晚分别会对一个掩护、一次空切造成什么。

## A zh
- **掩护的时机是一个两具身体的问题。** 掩护必须在防守人已经投入某条他无法放弃的路线的那一瞬间到达。设得**太早**，防守人在切入者启动前就绕过去了；设得**太晚**，就是移动掩护，或者切入者已经跑掉了。
- **"准时"的标志：** 掩护人到位并**停住**，恰好在切入者到达之前，让切入者比掩护慢一步，而不是和掩护齐平。切入者的任务是等——大多数失败的掩护是切得太早，而不是设得太慢。
- **在挡拆里，时机属于持球人：** 他必须先朝远离掩护的方向攻一下把防守人骗开，然后再用掩护。直接运向掩护的持球人，等于让自己的防守人提前穿过去站好位。
- **空切时，早等于被守住。** 在传球人还看不到你之前就到位的切入者，等于把自己从这个回合里删掉了；传球窗口只有零点几秒宽，而它在传球人的眼睛与切入者的启动对上时才打开。
- **晚只有一种正当用法：** 作为刻意的变速。把防守人溜到松懈再切，是一个时机决策，不是失误。永远没用的是**意外**地晚——匀速移动会让你所有的动作都被读出来。
- **可操作的纠正：** 数拍子。掩护人在切入者第二步时到位；切入者在传球人转头时启动。时机之所以可训练，正因为它是一个信号线索，而不是一种感觉。
