A shot is judged before it leaves your hand, on six questions. **Shooter:** {{c1::is this shot inside the range and the shot type this particular player actually makes at a good rate}}. **Openness:** {{c2::is there a hand in the shooting window, and is the closeout already there or still arriving}}. **Balance and rhythm:** {{c3::are the feet set and is it in rhythm off the catch or the dribble, rather than rushed or drifting}}. **Situation:** {{c4::does the offense have rebounders in position and defenders back, or does a miss become a break the other way}}. **Clock:** {{c5::how much shot clock is left — early in the clock the bar for a shot is higher, late it collapses}}. **Score and game state:** {{c6::whether you need points quickly or need to consume time}}. A shot that clears all six is a good shot {{c7::whether or not it goes in}}.

## zh
一次出手在离手之前就该被评判，靠六个问题。**射手：** {{c1::这一球是否落在这名球员真正能以不错命中率投进的射程和出手类型之内}}。**空位程度：** {{c2::出手窗口里有没有手，closeout（封堵）是已经到位还是还在路上}}。**平衡与节奏：** {{c3::双脚是否站定，接球或运球后是否在节奏上，而不是仓促或飘着出手}}。**局面：** {{c4::进攻方是否有人在篮板位置、有人回防，还是一次不中就变成对方的快攻}}。**时钟：** {{c5::还剩多少进攻时限——时限早期对出手的门槛更高，末段则几乎归零}}。**比分与比赛状态：** {{c6::你现在是需要快速拿分，还是需要消耗时间}}。六项全部通过的出手就是好的出手，{{c7::无论它进不进}}。
