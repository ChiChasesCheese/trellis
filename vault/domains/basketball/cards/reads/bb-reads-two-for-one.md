## Q
There are 48 seconds left in the quarter, you have the ball, and the shot clock is off (it is longer than the game clock allows). Your point guard says "two-for-one." What is he asking for, what is the arithmetic, and when is chasing it a mistake?

## A
- **The ask:** shoot early enough that your opponent must use their possession *and* give the ball back to you before the quarter ends — so you get two possessions to their one.
- **The arithmetic:** you need to shoot with roughly **30 or more seconds** left. That leaves the opponent up to 24 seconds of shot clock, plus the time your possession consumes, and still returns the ball to you with something like 5 to 8 seconds — enough for one more attempt.
- **So the shot has to go up by about the 30-second mark**, which means the action starts immediately off the inbound or rebound, not after a walk-up and a set call.
- **The cost:** your first shot is taken under a self-imposed time limit, so its quality drops. You are trading one worse shot for one extra shot.
- **When it is a mistake:** when the rushed shot is a genuinely bad one (a contested pull-up rather than an early-offense drive), when your team is poor at defending the possession in between, or when the extra possession would go to your worst decision-maker anyway. A two-for-one that produces two bad shots is worse than one good one.
- **The mirror on defense:** knowing they want a two-for-one, you can slow their entry, defend without fouling, and then deliberately hold your own possession so they get nothing back — clock awareness is a two-sided skill.

## Q zh
本节还剩 48 秒，球在你手上，而且进攻时限已经关闭（时限比比赛时钟允许的还长）。你的控卫喊 "two-for-one"。他在要求什么？算术是怎样的？什么时候去追它反而是错的？

## A zh
- **他要的是：** 足够早地出手，逼对手必须用掉他们的一个回合**并且**在本节结束前把球还给你——于是你用两个回合对他们一个。
- **算术：** 你需要在大约还剩 **30 秒或更多**时出手。这样对手最多能用掉 24 秒进攻时限，加上你这个回合消耗的时间，球回到你手上时仍有大约 5 到 8 秒——够再来一次出手。
- **也就是说球必须在 30 秒刻度前后出手**，这意味着进攻要在发球或抢下篮板后立刻开始，而不是先运过半场再喊一个战术。
- **代价：** 你的第一次出手是在自我施加的时间限制下投的，质量必然下降。你是在用一次更差的出手，换一次额外的出手。
- **什么时候是错的：** 当那记仓促出手确实很糟时（被干扰的急停跳投，而不是早期进攻的突破）；当你们防不住中间那个回合时；或者当那次额外球权反正会落到你们决策最差的人手里时。产生两记坏球的 two-for-one，比一记好球更糟。
- **防守端的镜像：** 知道他们想要 two-for-one，你就可以拖慢他们的推进、不犯规地防守，然后刻意把自己这个回合拖满，让他们什么也拿不回来——时钟意识是双向的技能。
