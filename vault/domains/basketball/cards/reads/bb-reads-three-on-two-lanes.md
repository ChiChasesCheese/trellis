## Q
You have the ball in the middle on a 3-on-2 break, with runners on both wings. Two defenders are back in a tandem — one high near the free-throw line, one low at the rim. What is each of the three attackers' job, and what is the read that actually decides the possession?

## A
- **Middle man (you): attack the top defender to the free-throw line, then stop or pass.** Your job is to occupy the high defender so he cannot also cover a wing. Getting to the free-throw line area with a live dribble is the whole point — from there you can pass to either side and he cannot be in both.
- **The two wings: run wide to the corners, then cut to the blocks.** Width is what makes one defender unable to reach two. If a wing drifts to the elbow, both wings and the ball live in an area two defenders can cover.
- **The read is the *bottom* defender, not the top one.** The low defender must eventually pick a side. Whichever side he takes, the ball goes to the other — you are not reading the man in front of you, you are reading the man behind him.
- **The trailer changes the math.** The fourth man arriving behind the play is the reason a 3-on-2 becomes a 4-on-2: if both defenders sink, the trailer's pull-up or drag screen is uncontested. Recognizing him is what separates good breaks from rushed ones.
- **The mistake that gives it back: driving into the low defender.** Attacking all the way to the rim in a 3-on-2 lets the bottom defender guard the ball and the pass at once, and you have converted a 3-on-2 into a 1-on-1.

## Q zh
你在 3 打 2 快攻中从中路持球，两翼各有一名跑动的队友。两名防守人以一前一后（tandem）退防——一人在罚球线附近的高位，一人在篮下。三名进攻者各自的任务是什么？真正决定这个回合的读位是什么？

## A zh
- **中路持球人（你）：攻到罚球线，逼住高位防守人，然后急停或传球。** 你的任务是把高位那人钉住，让他没法同时兼顾一侧翼。带着活球推进到罚球线区域就是全部意义所在——从那里你可以传向任意一侧，而他不可能同时在两边。
- **两个侧翼：跑宽到底角，然后切向低位方框。** 宽度才是让一名防守人够不到两个人的原因。如果侧翼飘到肘区，那么两个侧翼加球都挤在两名防守人能覆盖的区域里。
- **要读的是后面那名防守人，不是前面那个。** 底下那名防守人最终必须选一边。他选哪边，球就去另一边——你读的不是你面前的人，而是他身后的人。
- **Trailer（跟进的第四人）会改变这道算术题。** 从后方跟上的第四人，正是 3 打 2 变成 4 打 2 的原因：如果两名防守人都往里收，trailer 的急停跳投或 drag screen（跟进挡拆）就完全没人管。认出他，是好快攻和赶时间的快攻之间的区别。
- **把优势还回去的错误：直冲底下那名防守人。** 在 3 打 2 里一路杀到篮下，会让底位防守人同时守住球和传球路线，你把 3 打 2 变成了 1 打 1。
