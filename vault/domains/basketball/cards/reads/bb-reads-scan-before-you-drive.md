## Q
You catch the ball on the right wing and you know you can beat your man baseline. Before you put the ball on the floor, what two specific defenders should you have already identified, and what does each one tell you?

## A
- **The low man** — the weak-side defender nearest the baseline, usually guarding the opposite corner or weak-side block. He is the one who will step into your path at the rim. **What he tells you:** how far you can go, and who your kickout is. If the low man is guarding the weak-side corner, that corner shooter is the shot your drive creates, before you have even dribbled.
- **The tagger** — the defender responsible for bumping a roller or a cutter in the middle of the lane, usually the highest weak-side defender at the nail (the middle of the free-throw line). **What he tells you:** whether the middle is open, and whether your big's roll or dive will be met.
- **Read them by position, not by name.** Both roles are defined by where a defender is standing at that instant. They change every time the ball moves, which is why the scan happens on the catch and again on the first dribble.
- **The practical version:** on every catch, look weak side *first*. Most amateurs look at their defender, drive, and only then discover the help — which means their pass is a reaction rather than a plan.
- **What the scan buys:** you drive knowing which pass you are making if the help commits, and which finish you are taking if it does not. That is the difference between a drive with two outcomes and a drive with one.

## Q zh
你在右侧翼接到球，并且知道自己能从底线方向过掉防守人。在你把球放到地上之前，你应该已经认出哪两名具体的防守人？他们各自告诉你什么？

## A zh
- **Low man** —— 弱侧最靠近底线的防守人，通常在防对面底角或弱侧低位。他就是那个会在篮下横切进你路线的人。**他告诉你：** 你能突到多深，以及你的外传对象是谁。如果 low man 防的是弱侧底角，那么那名底角射手就是你这次突破要创造的出手——在你运球之前就已经确定了。
- **Tagger** —— 负责在禁区中路顶住顺下大个或空切者的那名防守人，通常是弱侧位置最高、站在 nail（罚球线中点）的那个。**他告诉你：** 中路是否敞开，以及你的大个顺下时会不会被拦。
- **靠位置认，不要靠名字认。** 这两个角色都由某名防守人在那一瞬间站在哪里来定义。球每移动一次它们就变一次，所以这次扫描要在接球时做一遍，第一次运球时再做一遍。
- **可执行的版本：** 每一次接球，**先**看弱侧。大多数业余球员先看自己的防守人，突进去，然后才发现协防——这意味着他们的传球是反应，而不是计划。
- **这次扫描买到了什么：** 你突破时已经知道——协防上来我传哪一球，协防不上来我用哪种终结方式。这就是"有两种结果的突破"和"只有一种结果的突破"之间的区别。
