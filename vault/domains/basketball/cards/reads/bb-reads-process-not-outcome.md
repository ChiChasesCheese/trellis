## Q
You pull up for a contested step-back with fifteen seconds on the shot clock and it goes in. Teammates celebrate. Later you take the same shot four times and miss all four, and now it is "a bad shot." How should you actually audit your own shot selection, given that outcomes are this noisy?

## A
- **Judge the decision at the moment it was made, with the information you had.** The contested step-back with fifteen seconds left was a bad shot when it went in, too — a made bad shot is a bad shot that got paid.
- **Outcome noise is enormous at individual-shot scale.** A 35% shot and a 45% shot look identical over ten attempts; you will never learn shot selection from your own make-miss record in a season of pickup basketball.
- **So count decisions, not results.** Watching your own film, tally: shots taken with a defender's hand in the window; shots taken with time still available and a better option one pass away; open shots inside your range that you passed up. Those three counts move much faster than percentages and they are all under your control.
- **The dangerous feedback loop:** making a low-value shot rewards the habit that produced it, and a good percentage over a small sample hides two or three genuinely terrible decisions per game.
- **The practical rule:** after any shot, ask "would I take that again knowing nothing about whether it went in?" If yes, it was a good shot regardless of the result; if no, celebrate the make and stop taking it.

## Q zh
进攻时限还剩十五秒时你投出一记有干扰的后撤步，球进了，队友一片欢呼。之后你同样出手四次，四次不中，现在它成了"坏球"。既然结果的噪声这么大，你到底该如何审视自己的出手选择？

## A zh
- **要用做决定那一刻你所掌握的信息去评判这个决定。** 那记还剩十五秒、有人干扰的后撤步，在它进的时候同样是坏球——一记投进的坏球，只是一记被付了钱的坏球。
- **在单次出手的尺度上，结果噪声极大。** 35% 的球和 45% 的球在十次出手里看起来完全一样；靠自己一个赛季野球的进/不进记录，你永远学不会出手选择。
- **所以去数决定，而不是数结果。** 看自己的录像时统计三件事：出手窗口里有防守人手的出手次数；还有时间、且一传之外有更好选择时仍然出手的次数；射程之内却被你放掉的空位出手次数。这三个计数比命中率收敛得快得多，而且全都在你的掌控之内。
- **危险的反馈回路：** 一记低价值的出手投进，会奖励制造出这一球的那个习惯；而小样本上的漂亮命中率，会掩盖每场两三个真正糟糕的决定。
- **可操作的规则：** 每次出手之后问自己："如果完全不知道它进没进，我还会再投一次吗？"如果会，那无论结果如何它都是好球；如果不会，就为这一球高兴一下，然后别再投了。
