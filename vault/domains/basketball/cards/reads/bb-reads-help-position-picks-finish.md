## Q
You are driving the middle of the lane. Match each help-defender picture to the finish it should produce: (A) the helper is late and still sliding across; (B) the helper is set, early, feet planted outside the restricted area; (C) the helper stays home on the weak-side shooter; (D) the helper stunts at you and retreats.

## A
- **(A) Late and still sliding → go through him, two feet, off the inside hand.** A moving helper cannot legally take a charge and cannot jump vertically; a two-foot gather lets you absorb contact and finish or draw the foul.
- **(B) Set and early → do not go into him.** Now you take the floater or the drop-off. Your options are the one-foot runner before you reach him, or a pass to your big rolling behind him — the helper who beat you to the spot has left the man he came from.
- **(C) Helper stays home → the rim is yours; finish high off one foot at speed.** No one is coming; the only mistake is slowing down to look for a pass that is not needed.
- **(D) Stunt and retreat → keep the ball and re-attack.** He has moved his weight and recovered, so his hips are wrong for a second slide. This is the moment for a change of direction rather than a pass, because his rotation is already spent.
- **The unifying idea:** you do not choose the finish and then hope; the help defender's **position and momentum at the moment you gather** already chose it. The finishing package (inside hand, Euro, floater, two-foot stop, drop-off) is a menu you read off, not a move you decide in advance.

## Q zh
你正从中路突入禁区。请把每一种协防画面对应到它应该产生的终结方式：(A) 协防人晚了，还在横向滑步过来；(B) 协防人早已站定，双脚扎在合理冲撞区之外；(C) 协防人不动，继续贴防弱侧射手；(D) 协防人朝你虚晃一下又退回去。

## A zh
- **(A) 晚到、还在滑动 → 直接从他身上过去，双脚起跳，用内侧手终结。** 移动中的协防人既不能合法制造带球撞人，也来不及垂直起跳；双脚急停收球能让你吃住对抗完成终结或造犯规。
- **(B) 早已站定 → 不要撞上去。** 此时该用抛投或分球。你的选项是在够到他之前的单脚抛投，或者传给从他身后顺下的大个——那个抢先站到位的协防人，已经离开了他原本防的人。
- **(C) 协防人不动 → 篮筐是你的；全速单脚起跳、高点终结。** 没有人会来；唯一的错误是减速去找一记根本不需要的传球。
- **(D) 虚晃后退回 → 把球留住，再攻一次。** 他已经动了重心又回来了，胯的方向不适合做第二次滑步。这一刻该用变向而不是传球，因为他的那次轮转已经花掉了。
- **统一的思路：** 你不是先选好终结方式再祈祷；是协防人在**你收球那一刻的位置和动量**替你选好了。终结技术包（内侧手、Euro step、抛投、双脚急停、分球）是一张你照着读的菜单，不是提前决定的动作。
