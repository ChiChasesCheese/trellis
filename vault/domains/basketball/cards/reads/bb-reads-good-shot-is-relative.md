## Q
Two players on your team take the identical shot: an open above-the-break three, ten seconds into the shot clock, nobody within six feet. For one it is a good shot and for the other it is a bad one. Explain what makes the difference, and how a team communicates it without insulting anybody.

## A
- **A shot's value is the player's expected points on it, not the shot's location.** An open above-the-break three is worth about 1.1 points for a 37% shooter and about 0.75 for a 25% shooter — the first is one of the best shots on the floor, the second is worse than almost any layup attempt.
- **So "good shot" is a property of the shooter-shot pair.** The same square of hardwood produces different expected values for different people, and honest teams say so out loud.
- **It is also about opportunity cost.** A weak shooter taking that three ends a possession that could have produced a much better shot for someone else. The question is never "is this shot okay" but "is this the best shot available in the next few seconds."
- **How teams communicate it:** define **shot diets** rather than rank players. Everybody gets a short list of shots that are *theirs* — corner threes and rim finishes for one player, pull-ups and floaters for another — so the message is "take yours," not "you are not allowed."
- **The reverse error is just as expensive:** a good shooter passing up his own open three to swing it once more. Refusing your shot moves your team's expected points down as surely as a bad shooter taking one.

## Q zh
你们队的两名球员投出完全相同的一球：弧顶三分空位，进攻时限走了十秒，六英尺内没有防守人。对其中一人这是好球，对另一人这是坏球。请解释差别来自哪里，以及球队如何在不冒犯任何人的前提下把这件事说清楚。

## A zh
- **一次出手的价值是这名球员在这球上的期望得分，而不是出手位置本身。** 一记空位弧顶三分，对 37% 命中率的射手约值 1.1 分，对 25% 命中率的球员约值 0.75 分——前者是全场最好的出手之一，后者比几乎任何一次上篮尝试都差。
- **所以"好球"是"射手—出手"这一组合的属性。** 同一块地板对不同的人产生不同的期望值，坦诚的球队会把这一点讲出来。
- **它也是机会成本问题。** 一名投篮差的球员出手这记三分，结束了一个本可以为别人创造出更好机会的回合。问题从来不是"这球行不行"，而是"这是接下来几秒里能拿到的最好一球吗"。
- **球队怎么沟通：** 定义每个人的 **shot diet（出手食谱）**，而不是给球员排名。每个人拿到一份属于**自己**的简短出手清单——某人是底角三分加篮下终结，另一人是急停跳投加抛投——于是这句话就变成"投你自己的那种球"，而不是"你不许投"。
- **反向的错误一样昂贵：** 好射手放掉自己的空位三分再多转一次。放弃属于你的出手，和差射手强投一样，会切实拉低球队的期望得分。
