## Q
A teammate cuts to the rim, you deliver an accurate chest pass right to his hands, and he has to catch, gather, and finish through a defender who recovered. Nothing was technically wrong. What did you actually get wrong, and what does "pass a teammate open" mean?

## A
- **You passed to the player instead of to the spot.** By the time your pass reaches where he *is*, he is somewhere else and the defense has caught up. The pass has to lead him to where he **will be** at the moment of the catch.
- **Passing to the spot also means passing to the right *side* of him** — away from the recovering defender, to the hand he will finish with, at the height he can use without gathering. A pass that forces an extra step or a dribble erases the advantage the cut created.
- **"Passing a teammate open" is the whole idea:** the pass itself can create separation. A ball delivered a half-step ahead of a cutter, or to a shooter's outside hip so he steps *into* the shot, means he catches in a better position than he was in when you released it.
- **On a shooter, this means passing to the shooting pocket, on time and on rhythm** — a pass that arrives while his feet are still moving into the catch turns a set shot into a rushed one, and that is on the passer.
- **Timing beats accuracy.** An on-time pass to a slightly imperfect place beats a perfect pass thrown a beat late, because the window closes faster than the target moves.
- **The habit:** throw to where the play is going. Every pass is a prediction, and passing to a stationary target is the one form of accuracy that does not help.

## Q zh
队友空切到篮下，你送出一记精准的胸前传球，正好到他手上，可他还得接球、收球，然后顶着一名已经回位的防守人去终结。技术上似乎没有任何错误。你实际错在哪里？"把队友传空位（pass a teammate open）"又是什么意思？

## A zh
- **你传给了人，而不是传给位置。** 等你的球到达他**当时所在**的地方，他已经在别处了，而防守已经追上。传球必须把他引向接球那一刻他**将会在**的位置。
- **传给位置也意味着传到他身体正确的那一侧**——避开正在回追的防守人，送到他将要用来终结的那只手上，送到他不用重新收球就能使用的高度。一记逼他多走一步或多运一次的传球，会把空切创造出来的优势抹掉。
- **"把队友传空位"就是这整个概念：** 传球本身可以创造空间。一记送到切入者前方半步的球，或者传向射手外侧胯位、让他**迎着**球踏进出手的球，都会让他接球时的位置比你出手那一刻更好。
- **对射手而言，这意味着传到他的出手口袋，准时且踩在节奏上**——在他双脚还在往接球位置移动时到达的球，会把一次站定出手变成一次仓促出手，而这笔账算在传球人头上。
- **时机胜过精度。** 一记准时但位置稍微不完美的传球，胜过一记晚了一拍的完美传球，因为窗口关闭的速度比目标移动的速度快。
- **要养成的习惯：** 传向这个回合**正在去往**的地方。每一记传球都是一次预测，而传给静止目标是唯一一种帮不上忙的"精准"。
