## Q
"Make the extra pass" is standard advice. Describe a situation where the extra pass **destroys** the advantage instead of extending it, and give the rule for telling the two cases apart.

## A
- **An advantage has a half-life.** It is created by one defender being responsible for two people, and it decays every tenth of a second as the defense rotates. Passing extends the advantage only when the pass moves the ball **faster than the defense can rotate**; otherwise it just hands the ball to a closed-out shooter.
- **The destroying case:** you are open on the wing off a drive-and-kick, the closeout is late, and you pass to a teammate on the same side of the floor to "get a better one." Your defender never had to help — you gave up an open shot in exchange for a shot the defense had time to reach.
- **The rule — a pass must change the picture, not just the passer.** A pass across the middle of the floor or to the opposite side forces new defenders to move. A pass to the man next to you moves nobody, so it can only cost you.
- **Second test: are you passing up a shot you would take at practice?** An open shot inside your range at the end of a rotation is the shot the advantage was created to produce. Refusing it is not unselfishness; it is giving back what four teammates just earned.
- **The exception that proves it:** the extra pass to the **corner** works precisely because it is a long closeout across the last rotation, and to a spot the defense arrives at last. Not all extra passes are equal — the geometry decides, not the generosity.

## Q zh
"多传一次球"是标准建议。请描述一个多传一次反而**摧毁**优势而不是延续优势的情形，并给出区分这两种情况的规则。

## A zh
- **优势有半衰期。** 它诞生于"一名防守人要负责两个人"，并随着防守轮转每十分之一秒都在衰减。只有当传球移动球的速度**快过防守轮转的速度**时，传球才在延长优势；否则它只是把球交给一个已经被 closeout（封堵）到位的射手。
- **摧毁优势的情形：** 突分之后你在侧翼空位，对方 closeout 已经晚了，而你为了"找一个更好的机会"把球传给同一侧的队友。你的防守人根本不需要协防——你用一记空位出手，换来了一记防守有时间赶到的出手。
- **规则——一记传球必须改变画面，而不只是换个持球人。** 穿过球场中路或传向另一侧的球，会逼新的防守人移动；传给身边那个人的球谁也没调动，所以它只可能让你付出代价。
- **第二个检验：你放弃的是不是训练里你会毫不犹豫出手的那一球？** 一次轮转末端、在你射程之内的空位出手，正是这次优势被创造出来要生产的产品。拒绝它不是无私，而是把四名队友刚挣来的东西还回去。
- **恰恰印证规则的例外：** 传向**底角**的那记额外传球之所以有效，正因为它是跨过最后一次轮转的超长 closeout 距离，而且传向防守最后才能赶到的位置。并非所有的额外传球都等价——决定它的是几何，不是慷慨。
