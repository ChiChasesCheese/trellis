## Q
A player catches the ball standing still, pivots, then steps past his defender with the non-pivot foot and finishes. Everyone screams travel. Walk through why the step-through is legal, and name the one thing that would make it a violation.

## A
**Why it is legal.** On a stationary catch the player establishes a **pivot foot**. He may move the *other* foot anywhere, as many times as he likes, as long as the pivot foot stays in contact with the floor. A step-through is exactly that: the free foot steps past the defender while the pivot foot never moves. Nothing about the number of steps matters, because only one foot is being counted.

**What makes it a violation:** the pivot foot lifting *and returning to the floor* before the ball is released. Lifting it is allowed — that is how you start a dribble or a shot — but once it comes up, the ball must leave your hands (pass or shot) before that foot lands again. On a drive to the basket the same principle applies: the free foot may go, the pivot foot may not re-plant.

The confusion comes from counting *steps* instead of tracking *the pivot foot*, which is what the rule is actually about.

## Q zh
一名球员原地接球，做了一个转身，然后用中枢脚以外的那只脚跨过防守人完成上篮。全场都在喊走步。请讲清楚 step-through（跨步）为什么合法，并说出唯一会让它变成违例的那件事。

## A zh
**为什么合法。** 原地接球时，球员确立了一只 **pivot foot（中枢脚）**。只要中枢脚保持与地面接触，他可以把*另一只*脚移动到任何地方、想移动几次都行。Step-through 正是如此：自由脚跨过防守人，而中枢脚从未移动。走了几步完全不重要，因为被计数的只有一只脚。

**什么会让它变成违例：** 中枢脚抬起后，在球出手之前*又落回地面*。抬起本身是允许的 —— 开始运球或起跳投篮就是这么做的 —— 但一旦抬起，球必须在那只脚再次落地之前离手（传或投）。突破上篮时原则相同：自由脚可以走，中枢脚不能重新落地。

混乱来自去数*步数*，而不是盯住*中枢脚* —— 而后者才是这条规则真正说的事。
