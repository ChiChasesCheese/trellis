The **gather** is the moment a player moving with a live dribble {{c1::gains control of the ball}} — in NBA terms, when the hand comes under or beside the ball and the dribble ends. The step count does not start until the gather is complete, which is why a player may take {{c2::two}} steps after it before releasing the ball for a pass or shot. A step taken *during* the gather is therefore {{c3::not counted}}, and this is the single reason so many legal drives look like travels to spectators counting from the wrong instant.

## zh
**Gather（收球）** 是指处于活球运球中的球员 {{c1::gains control of the ball}}（获得对球的控制）的那一刻 —— 按 NBA 的说法，就是手掌来到球的下方或侧面、运球结束的时候。步数要等 gather 完成之后才开始算，所以球员在此之后可以走 {{c2::two}} 步再把球传出或投出。发生*在* gather 过程中的那一步因此 {{c3::not counted}}（不计入），这就是为什么那么多合法的突破，在从错误时刻开始数步的观众眼里像走步。
