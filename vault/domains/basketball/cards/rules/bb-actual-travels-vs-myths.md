## Q
Sort these four into "travel" and "not a travel", with the reason: (a) two long steps into a layup after the gather; (b) the Euro step; (c) hopping onto both feet after picking up the dribble, then lifting one foot and stepping; (d) shuffling the feet before starting the dribble on a catch.

## A
- **(a) Two long steps into a layup — not a travel.** After the gather you get two steps. Length is irrelevant; the rule counts steps, not distance.
- **(b) Euro step — not a travel.** It is the same two steps, taken in different directions. Changing direction between step one and step two is not part of the rule.
- **(c) Jump stop, then step — travel.** Landing on both feet simultaneously after the dribble means **neither foot is a pivot foot**. Lifting one and stepping is therefore a violation; from that landing you may pivot on neither foot, only shoot or pass.
- **(d) Shuffling before the dribble — travel.** On a catch, the pivot foot must stay planted until the ball has left your hand to begin the dribble. Moving it first is the most common *genuinely uncalled* travel at amateur level.

The pattern: the moves that look most spectacular are usually legal, and the small sloppy ones nobody watches usually are not.

## Q zh
把下面四种情况分成"走步"和"不是走步"，并说明理由：(a) gather 之后两大步上篮；(b) Euro step（欧洲步）；(c) 收球后双脚同时跳停，然后抬起一只脚迈出；(d) 接球后开始运球前先蹭动双脚。

## A zh
- **(a) 两大步上篮 —— 不是走步。** Gather 之后你有两步。步幅无关紧要；规则数的是步数，不是距离。
- **(b) Euro step —— 不是走步。** 它就是那同样的两步，只是迈向不同方向。第一步和第二步之间改变方向，规则里根本没提。
- **(c) 跳停后再迈步 —— 走步。** 运球后双脚同时落地，意味着**两只脚都不是 pivot foot**。因此抬起一只脚迈出就是违例；从这个落地姿势你不能用任何一只脚做中枢，只能投或传。
- **(d) 运球前蹭脚 —— 走步。** 接球后，中枢脚必须保持不动，直到球离手开始运球。先动脚是业余层面最常见的、*真正没被吹*的走步。

规律是：看起来最花哨的动作通常合法，而没人盯着的那些小马虎通常不合法。
