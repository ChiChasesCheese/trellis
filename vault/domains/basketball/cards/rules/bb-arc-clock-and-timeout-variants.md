## Q
Beyond defensive three seconds, name three NBA/FIBA differences that change how you play, and say what each one changes.

## A
- **Three-point distance and court size.** The NBA arc is farther out and the court is longer and wider; FIBA's arc is closer. Closer arc plus a smaller floor means **less room between the line and the paint** — corner spacing is tighter, help is a shorter trip, and the value gap between a long two and a three is slightly narrower.
- **Timeout rules.** In the NBA a coach calls timeouts; under FIBA rules a coach requests one and it is granted only at a dead ball. That single difference removes the "call timeout while being trapped" escape hatch — under FIBA you have to actually break the press, so press-break execution matters more.
- **Advancing the ball after a timeout.** Both leagues allow moving the ball to the frontcourt in late-game situations, but the conditions and the time windows differ. Know your competition's exact wording, because it decides whether a late deficit is one possession or two.
- **Fouls and free throws.** Team-foul bonus thresholds and the reset per period differ, which changes when deliberate fouling becomes profitable.

The habit that matters more than memorizing any of them: **read the rulebook of the competition you are actually playing in** before the season starts.

## Q zh
除了防守三秒，再说出三条会改变打法的 NBA/FIBA 差异，并说明各自改变了什么。

## A zh
- **三分线距离与球场尺寸。** NBA 的三分线更远，球场更长更宽；FIBA 的弧线更近。弧线更近加上场地更小，意味着**三分线与禁区之间的空间更少** —— 底角 spacing 更挤，协防的路程更短，long two 与三分之间的价值差距也略微变窄。
- **暂停规则。** 在 NBA 是教练直接叫暂停；按 FIBA 规则，教练只能提出申请，并且只在死球时才被准予。仅这一条就取消了"被夹击时叫暂停"这个逃生口 —— 打 FIBA 你必须真的把压迫破掉，所以 press break 的执行力更重要。
- **暂停后的球权推进。** 两边都允许在末段把球推进前场，但条件和时间窗口不同。要清楚你所在赛事的确切措辞，因为它决定了末段落后算一个球权还是两个。
- **犯规与罚球。** 全队犯规进入罚球线的阈值、以及每节的重置方式不同，这改变了故意犯规何时才划算。

比背下任何一条都更重要的习惯：**赛季开始前，去读你实际参加的那项赛事的规则手册。**
