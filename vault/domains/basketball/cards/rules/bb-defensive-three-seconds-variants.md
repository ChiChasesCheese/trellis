## Q
Defensive three seconds exists in the NBA and not in FIBA or NCAA play. Name the tactical consequence for both offense and defense when you cross between them.

## A
**The rule:** in the NBA a defender may not remain in the paint for three seconds unless he is actively guarding an opponent. FIBA and NCAA have no such restriction — a defender may sit in the lane indefinitely.

**Consequence for the defense (in FIBA/NCAA you gain):** you may park your best rim protector in the middle of the paint as a permanent free safety. Drop coverage becomes much stronger, help arrives from a shorter distance, and driving lanes are pre-clogged. It is a straightforward reason zone defenses and packed paints are more viable outside the NBA.

**Consequence for the offense (in FIBA/NCAA you lose):** you cannot count on an empty paint. Isolation and straight-line drives are worth less; **you have to move the help defender**, which raises the value of ball reversals, off-ball screening, the short corner, and shooting well enough to make the anchor come out.

Crossing the other way — playing NBA rules — the paint clears itself, which is a large part of why NBA-style drive-and-kick spacing scales the way it does.

## Q zh
Defensive three seconds（防守三秒）只存在于 NBA，FIBA 和 NCAA 都没有。在两者之间切换时，对进攻和防守各有什么战术后果？

## A zh
**规则：** 在 NBA，防守人不得在禁区内停留三秒，除非他正在贴防某名对手。FIBA 与 NCAA 没有这条限制 —— 防守人可以无限期地待在禁区里。

**对防守的后果（打 FIBA/NCAA 时你赚到）：** 你可以把最好的护框者钉在禁区中央，当成常驻的自由人。Drop coverage 因此强得多，协防的距离更短，突破路线事先就被堵住。这就是为什么区域防守和"堆禁区"在 NBA 之外更可行的直接原因。

**对进攻的后果（打 FIBA/NCAA 时你吃亏）：** 你不能指望禁区是空的。Isolation 和直线突破价值下降；**你必须把协防者调动开**，这抬高了倒球、无球掩护、short corner（底线短角）以及投得够准把内线锚点拽出来的价值。

反过来打 NBA 规则时，禁区会自己清空 —— 这也是 NBA 式 drive-and-kick 空间之所以那么好用的重要原因。
