## Q
A shot bounces high off the rim and sits spinning on it. Your teammate taps it in; the referee waves it off. Separately, a missed shot hits the backboard and drops toward the rim and you slap it away. Which is which, and when does a ball above the ring become fair game?

## A
- **Tapping the ball while it rests on the rim: basket interference.** The cylinder above the ring is protected space for as long as the ball is on or inside it. Nobody, offense or defense, may touch the ball or the rim then. The basket is cancelled.
- **Slapping a shot descending toward the rim after hitting the backboard: goaltending**, provided it is above ring level and still has a chance to score. Rebounding it off the glass is legal; touching it on its downward path to the basket is not.

**When it becomes fair game:** once the ball is no longer within the cylinder and is no longer on the rim — that is, once it has bounced clear or fallen away with no chance to go in. A ball rattling in the cylinder is untouchable; a ball that has come off the rim and out is a live rebound.

The distinction rewards patience: at the rim the correct habit is to wait a beat rather than to grab, because the ball almost always leaves the protected space on its own.

## Q zh
一次投篮从篮圈上高高弹起，然后旋转着停在篮圈上，你队友把它拨进去，裁判判无效。另一种情况：一次投失打到篮板后向篮圈下落，你把它扇掉。这两种分别是什么违例？篮圈上方的球什么时候可以碰？

## A zh
- **球停在篮圈上时去拨它：basket interference。** 只要球在篮圈上或篮圈上方的圆柱体内，那块空间就是受保护的。此时无论进攻还是防守都不得触碰球或篮圈。进球被取消。
- **球打板后正向篮圈下落时把它扇掉：goaltending**，前提是它位于篮圈水平面之上且仍有进筐可能。从篮板上抢下篮板是合法的；在它下落向篮筐的路径上触碰它则不合法。

**什么时候可以碰：** 当球既不在圆柱体内、也不在篮圈上时 —— 也就是它已经弹开或落下、不再有进筐可能。在圆柱体里晃荡的球碰不得；已经从篮圈上弹出来的球，是一次活的篮板。

这个区分奖励的是耐心：在篮下，正确的习惯是等一拍再动手，因为球几乎总会自己离开那块受保护的空间。
