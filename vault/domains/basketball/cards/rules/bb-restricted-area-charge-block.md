## Q
What is the restricted area, what does it change about the charge/block decision, and what are the two conditions a defender must satisfy to draw a charge anywhere on the floor?

## A
**The restricted area** is the marked arc under the basket. A secondary (help) defender standing inside it cannot draw an offensive foul from an airborne driver — the contact is called a block. Its purpose is to stop defenders from parking under the rim and turning every drive into a collision.

**Two conditions for a charge**, restricted area aside:
1. **Legal guarding position established first** — the defender is facing the opponent with both feet on the floor, *before* the offensive player leaves his feet or commits to his path.
2. **Position maintained without illegal movement** — he may stay still, move backwards, or move laterally, but he may not move *into* the driver. Late arrival is the classic failure.

Two details worth carrying: the restricted-area exception does **not** protect a driver against the defender who was guarding him all along (a primary defender), and a defender who is still sliding sideways when the contact happens has not established anything.

## Q zh
什么是 restricted area（合理冲撞区）？它改变了 charge/block（撞人/阻挡）判罚的什么？防守人在场上任何位置要造进攻犯规，必须满足哪两个条件？

## A zh
**Restricted area** 是篮下画出来的那道弧线。站在它里面的**协防**球员，无法从腾空突破的球员身上造出进攻犯规 —— 这种接触会被吹阻挡。它的目的就是阻止防守人赖在篮下，把每一次突破都变成撞车。

**造撞人的两个条件**（先不论合理冲撞区）：
1. **先取得 legal guarding position（合法防守位置）** —— 防守人正面对着对手、双脚站在地上，而且是*在*进攻球员起跳或确定路线*之前*。
2. **保持位置且没有违法移动** —— 他可以站着不动、后退、或横向滑动，但不能*朝着*突破者移动。到位太晚是最经典的失败。

两个值得记住的细节：合理冲撞区的例外**不**保护突破者免于一直在防他的那名主防人；而在接触发生时仍在横向滑步的防守人，什么位置也没有取得。
