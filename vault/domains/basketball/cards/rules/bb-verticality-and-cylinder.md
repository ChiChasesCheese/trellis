## Q
Define a player's cylinder and the principle of verticality, then say exactly what a help defender must do to make contact at the rim legal — and what forfeits the protection.

## A
**The cylinder** is the imaginary column of space a player occupies, bounded by the width of his own stance and reaching from the floor to as high as he can extend. He is entitled to that space.

**Verticality** is the right to rise straight up inside it. A defender who has established a legal position and jumps **vertically, with arms extended straight up**, is protected: if an airborne shooter jumps into him, the contact is the shooter's responsibility, not a foul on the defender.

**What forfeits it:**
- Arms leaning, chopping down, or reaching **forward into the shooter's cylinder** rather than staying up.
- Moving *toward* the shooter — drifting under him or leaning into the space he is landing in.
- Not having a legal position to begin with, or arriving late while still moving.

The practical coaching cue follows directly: at the rim, give up the block attempt and **go straight up with the hands high**, because it is the only version of the contest the rule protects.

## Q zh
定义球员的 cylinder（圆柱体）和 verticality（垂直原则），然后说清楚协防者要怎么做，篮下的身体接触才算合法 —— 以及什么会让他丧失这项保护。

## A zh
**Cylinder** 是球员所占据的那段假想空间柱，横向以他自己的站姿宽度为界，纵向从地板一直到他能伸展到的最高处。这块空间是他有权拥有的。

**Verticality** 是他在这个柱体内笔直上升的权利。一名已经取得合法位置、并且**垂直起跳、双臂笔直上举**的防守人受到保护：如果空中的投篮者撞进他身上，责任在投篮者，而不是防守犯规。

**什么会让他丧失保护：**
- 手臂倾斜、下劈，或者**向前伸进投篮者的 cylinder**，而不是保持上举。
- *向着*投篮者移动 —— 钻到他身下，或者倾进他落地的那块空间。
- 一开始就没有合法位置，或者还在移动中才赶到。

由此直接得出实战口诀：在篮下放弃盖帽企图，**双手高举、笔直向上** —— 因为规则保护的只有这一种对抗方式。
