## Q
Goaltending and basket interference are two different violations that people use as one word. Separate them: what does each one actually describe, and can an offensive player commit either?

## A
**Goaltending** is about a shot **on its way to the basket**: touching the ball while it is on its downward flight toward the rim, above the level of the ring, with a chance to go in. It is a defensive act by nature — a blocked shot that arrived too late.

**Basket interference** is about the ball **at the basket**: touching the ball or the rim while the ball is on the rim or within the imaginary cylinder above it, or reaching up through the net from below.

**Yes — offensive players commit both.** An offensive player who tips a teammate's shot while it sits on the rim, or who dunks a ball already inside the cylinder above the ring, commits basket interference; the basket is waved off and the ball goes to the other team.

The penalty is the mirror: defensive violation awards the shot's points, offensive violation cancels the basket. Remembering "goaltending is about the *flight*, interference is about the *rim*" separates them cleanly.

## Q zh
Goaltending（干扰投篮）和 basket interference（干扰球）是两个不同的违例，很多人把它们当一个词用。请区分：各自到底描述什么？进攻球员会犯这两种吗？

## A zh
**Goaltending** 说的是**正飞向篮筐的球**：在球处于向下飞行、位于篮圈水平面之上、且有进筐可能时触碰它。它天然是防守行为 —— 一次来得太晚的封盖。

**Basket interference** 说的是**已经到达篮筐处的球**：在球停在篮圈上、或处于篮圈上方那个假想圆柱体内时触碰球或篮圈，或者从下方伸手穿过球网。

**是的 —— 进攻球员两种都会犯。** 进攻球员在球停在篮圈上时拨了队友的投篮，或者把已经进入篮圈上方圆柱体内的球扣进去，就是 basket interference；球不算，球权交给对方。

罚则正好是镜像：防守方违例判给这次投篮应得的分数，进攻方违例取消进球。记住"goaltending 管的是*飞行*，interference 管的是*篮圈*"，就能干净地分开两者。
