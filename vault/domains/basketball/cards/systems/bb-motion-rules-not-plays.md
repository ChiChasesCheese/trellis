## Q
Your team says it runs "motion offense", but everyone stands still until someone calls something. What actually makes an offense a motion offense rather than a play, and what are the three base rules that generate it?

## A
A **play** is a fixed sequence of positions run regardless of what the defense does; a **motion offense** is a small set of *conditional rules* every player applies to whatever the defense gives, so the same offense produces a different possession every time.

The three base rules:
- **Pass and cut** — after you pass, you cut to the basket. Standing still after a pass is the single most common way an amateur motion offense dies.
- **Screen away** — if you are not the cutter, you go screen for a teammate away from the ball.
- **Fill** — someone immediately fills the spot the cutter or screener vacated, so the floor keeps its spacing instead of collapsing to one side.

Because the rules are conditional, there is nothing for a scout to memorize: the defense has to guard reads, not a diagram.

## Q zh
你的球队说自己打的是 motion offense，但所有人都站着不动，等人喊战术。到底是什么让一套进攻成为 motion offense 而不是一个 play？生成它的三条基本规则是什么？

## A zh
**Play（战术）** 是一段固定的走位顺序，不管防守怎么打都照跑；**motion offense（动态进攻）** 则是每个人对防守给什么就用什么的一小组*条件规则*，所以同一套进攻每回合跑出来都不一样。

三条基本规则：
- **Pass and cut（传切）** —— 传完球就往篮下切。传完球站着不动，是业余球队的 motion offense 最常见的死法。
- **Screen away（反跑掩护）** —— 如果你不是那个切入的人，就去给远离球侧的队友做掩护。
- **Fill（补位）** —— 立刻有人补上切入者或掩护者空出的位置，让 spacing（空间）保持住，而不是把人都堆到一侧。

因为规则是条件性的，对手无从背诵：防守要防的是 reads（阅读），不是一张战术图。
