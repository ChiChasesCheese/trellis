## Q
Do the expected-value arithmetic that killed the long two — a jumper from just inside the arc. Then name the situations in which it is still the right shot.

## A
**The math.** A shot from just inside the line and a shot from just outside it convert at nearly the same rate, because two feet of distance changes accuracy very little. Call both roughly 36%. The long two pays 2, the three pays 3:

- long two: 0.36 × 2 ≈ **0.72 points per shot**
- above-break three: 0.36 × 3 ≈ **1.08 points per shot**
- shot at the rim: roughly **1.2 points per shot**

The long two is therefore the worst shot on the floor by a wide margin — not because it is hard, but because it is priced wrong.

**When it is still correct:**
- The shot clock is expiring and it is the best available look.
- You are an unusually good midrange shooter and the gap is genuinely open, against a defense that concedes it.
- Late game when two points is enough and a lower-variance shot is preferable to a longer one.
- As a counter: if the defense runs you off the line every time, the pull-up two is what it is giving you.

## Q zh
把杀死 long two（三分线内一步的中远投）的期望值算一遍，然后说出它仍然正确的情况。

## A zh
**算术。** 三分线内一步和线外一步的命中率几乎一样，因为差这两英尺对准度影响很小。两者都按大约 36% 算。Long two 值 2 分，三分值 3 分：

- long two：0.36 × 2 ≈ **每次出手 0.72 分**
- 弧顶三分：0.36 × 3 ≈ **每次出手 1.08 分**
- 篮下出手：大约 **每次出手 1.2 分**

所以 long two 是全场差距最大的劣质出手 —— 不是因为它难，而是因为它定价错了。

**它仍然正确的时候：**
- Shot clock 快到点，而这是能拿到的最好机会。
- 你是特别好的中距离射手，空档是真的空，而防守就是放这一口。
- 比赛末段只需要两分，且比起更远的出手，方差更小的更可取。
- 作为反制：如果防守每次都把你赶离三分线，那 pull-up 两分就是它主动给你的。
