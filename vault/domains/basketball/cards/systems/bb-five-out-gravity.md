## Q
Define gravity, and explain how a player who never touches the ball on a possession can be the reason it scores.

## A
**Gravity** is the amount of defensive attention a player pulls away from the ball purely because of the threat he represents — almost always shooting, sometimes cutting.

The mechanism: a drive is stopped by a *second* defender, not the first. If the corner shooter's defender cannot leave him — because that leaves a corner three, the most efficient shot on the floor — then the drive arrives at the rim unhelped. The layup was created by a man standing still forty feet away.

Two consequences worth internalizing:
- Gravity is invisible in a box score. A shooter can go 0-for-2 and still be the most valuable offensive player on the floor that possession.
- It explains why an average shooter standing in the right spot outproduces a better scorer standing in the wrong one, and why **five-out** spacing works at all.

## Q zh
定义 gravity（引力），并说明为什么一个整个回合都没碰球的人，可以是这球能进的原因。

## A zh
**Gravity** 指的是一名球员单凭自身威胁（几乎总是投射，有时是空切）就把多少防守注意力从球身上拽走。

机制是这样：突破是被*第二个*防守人挡下的，不是第一个。如果底角射手的防守人不能离开他 —— 因为一离开就送出 corner three（底角三分），全场效率最高的出手 —— 那么这次突破到篮下时就没有协防。这个上篮，是由十几米外一个站着不动的人创造的。

有两点值得记住：
- Gravity 在 box score（技术统计）里是看不见的。一个射手可以 0 投 2 中，同时仍是那个回合场上最有价值的进攻球员。
- 它解释了为什么站对位置的普通射手，产出高过站错位置的更强得分手，也解释了 **five-out** 空间为什么能成立。
