## Q
Princeton puts its most skilled big at the high post (elbow / free-throw line) instead of on the block. Name the two things that one placement changes for the defense.

## A
1. **It pulls the rim protector out of the paint.** His defender has to guard him 15-18 feet from the basket. The defense's best help defender is now standing where he cannot help, which is what makes every backdoor cut and every basket cut behind him live.
2. **It creates a passing hub with vision of both sides.** From the elbow the big can see the weak-side cutter, the strong-side backdoor lane and the top of the key at once, so the ball can enter the middle and come back out on the opposite side faster than the defense can rotate.

The block placement fails at both: the big sits in the space the cuts need, and he can see only half the floor.

## Q zh
Princeton 把技术最好的内线放在 high post（罚球线/肘区），而不是低位 block。这一个位置改变，给防守带来了哪两件事？

## A zh
1. **把护框者从禁区里拉出来。** 他的防守人必须在离篮筐 15-18 英尺的地方贴防。对方最好的协防者现在站在帮不上忙的位置上，这正是每一次 backdoor cut 和从他背后走的 basket cut 能成立的原因。
2. **造出一个能看到两侧的传球中枢。** 从肘区，这名内线可以同时看到弱侧的空切者、强侧的 backdoor 通道和弧顶，所以球可以从中路进去、再从另一侧出来，速度比防守轮转更快。

放在低位则两件事都做不到：内线占住了切入需要的空间，而且只能看到半个球场。
