## Q
What is a "delay" entry in early offense, and why is a possession that starts before the defense is set worth measurably more than the same action run against a set defense?

## A
**Delay** is the trailing big catching the ball at the top of the key with the floor already spread. It is not a play — it is a decision point with four continuations: dribble handoff, drag screen, keep and attack, or reverse the ball to the weak side.

It is worth more because a defense that has not finished getting back has not yet done three specific things:
- **matched up** — someone is guarding the wrong man, or two people are guarding one,
- **built help positions** — the low man is not in place, so a drive has no second defender waiting,
- **set the rim** — nobody is anchored in front of the basket.

Every second you spend organizing hands those three back. That is why *flow* — continuing straight from transition into an action without a call — outscores calling a set, and why calling one should be the last resort rather than the default.

## Q zh
早期进攻里的 "delay" 入球是什么？为什么在防守站好之前开始的回合，比同样的动作打阵地防守值钱得多？

## A zh
**Delay** 是指跟进的内线在弧顶接球，此时场上空间已经拉开。它不是一个战术，而是一个决策点，有四种延续：dribble handoff（运球交接）、drag screen（跟进掩护）、自己保留并进攻、或把球倒向弱侧。

它更值钱，是因为一支还没退防完的球队还没做完三件具体的事：
- **对位** —— 有人盯错了人，或者两个人盯同一个，
- **建立协防位置** —— low man（底线协防人）还没就位，突破进去没有第二个人等着，
- **落位护框** —— 没人在篮前扎住。

你每花一秒去组织，就是把这三件事还给对手。这就是为什么 *flow*（从转换直接接进动作、不喊战术）比喊 set 效率更高，也是为什么喊战术应该是最后手段，而不是默认选项。
