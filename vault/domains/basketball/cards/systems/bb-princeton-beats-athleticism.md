## Q
Why does a Princeton-style offense travel well against a team that is simply more athletic than yours? Give the mechanism, not the folklore.

## A
It turns their advantage into the thing that beats them, and it removes the game state they are best at.

- **Aggression becomes the weakness.** Athletic defenders deny harder and help faster. Denial is punished by the backdoor cut; early help is punished by the pass out of the middle to the vacated side. The harder they play, the more layups you get.
- **It removes transition.** Long possessions with few live-ball turnovers mean almost no run-outs — and transition is where an athletic team's edge is largest.
- **It lowers the possession count.** Fewer possessions shrink the sample, so the better team's per-possession edge has less time to express itself. That does not make you better; it makes an upset more likely.
- **It converts the game from a speed test into a decision test**, which is the one dimension an older, smarter team can actually win.

## Q zh
为什么 Princeton 风格的进攻，打身体条件明显强过你的球队时特别好用？请讲机制，不要讲传说。

## A zh
它把对方的优势变成击败他们的东西，同时抽掉了他们最擅长的比赛状态。

- **侵略性变成弱点。** 运动能力强的防守人抢得更凶、协防更快。抢传球路线会被 backdoor cut 惩罚；提前协防会被从中路传向空出那侧的球惩罚。他们打得越凶，你拿到的上篮越多。
- **它抽掉了 transition（转换进攻）。** 回合长、活球失误少，就几乎没有快攻机会 —— 而转换正是身体条件优势最大的地方。
- **它压低回合数。** 回合少意味着样本小，强队的每回合优势没有足够时间兑现。这不会让你变强，但会让爆冷更可能发生。
- **它把比赛从速度测验变成决策测验**，而这正是一支更老练、更聪明的球队真正能赢的维度。
