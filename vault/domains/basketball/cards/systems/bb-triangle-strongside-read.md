## Q
The entry pass goes into the strong-side post in the triangle. The post player is now the decision maker. What is he reading, and what decides whether he finishes or the offense continues?

## A
He reads **two things in order**, and the second one decides the possession.

1. **His own defender's position** — behind him, on the high side, on the low side, or fronting. That tells him which turn is available if nobody helps.
2. **Where the first help defender is coming from.** This is the read that matters:
   - Help from the **corner** → the corner shooter is open on the kick-out.
   - Help from the **top** → the two players on the weak side are now defended by one man; the skip pass is on.
   - **No help** → he finishes, because a single-covered post catch is already the best shot on the floor.

The triangle continues rather than resets because the wing and the corner are already in position to receive and re-space, so a pass out of the post is the next action, not a failure.

## Q zh
在 triangle 里，球从强侧传进了低位。现在低位球员是决策者。他在阅读什么？什么决定了他自己终结还是让进攻继续？

## A zh
他**按顺序读两件事**，第二件决定这个回合。

1. **自己防守人的位置** —— 在身后、上侧、下侧，还是绕前（fronting）。这告诉他，如果没人协防，他能用哪个转身。
2. **第一个协防者从哪来。** 这才是关键的 read：
   - 协防来自 **corner（底角）** → 底角射手在 kick-out（分球）后是空的。
   - 协防来自 **top（弧顶）** → 弱侧两个人现在只剩一个人防，skip pass（横传）成立。
   - **没有协防** → 他直接终结，因为单防下的低位接球本身已经是全场最好的出手。

Triangle 是继续而不是重开，因为侧翼和底角本来就站在能接球、能重新拉开空间的位置上 —— 从低位传出来是下一个动作，不是失败。
