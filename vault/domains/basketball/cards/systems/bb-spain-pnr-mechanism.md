## Q
Spain pick and roll adds one back screen to an ordinary ball screen. Which defender does that screen attack, and why does it break a coverage that would otherwise be fine?

## A
It attacks **the screener's defender** — the big who would normally sit in drop coverage and absorb the roll.

As the roll starts, a shooter back-screens that big from behind. He now has to solve two problems in the same half-second:
- Fight over the back screen to stay with the roller, arriving late to the rim, or
- Switch onto the back-screener, who immediately **pops** to the three-point line.

Meanwhile the on-ball defender is still trailing the original screen. That leaves **two defenders responsible for three live threats** in a small area: the handler's pull-up, the roller's dive or lob, and the back-screener's pop. Whichever the defense takes away, it hands you one of the other two — which is why the correct offensive skill here is reading, not memorizing the first option.

## Q zh
Spain pick and roll 只是在普通的 ball screen 上加了一个 back screen。这个掩护针对的是哪名防守人？为什么它能打崩一个本来没问题的防守策略？

## A zh
它针对的是 **掩护者的防守人** —— 那个本来会站 drop coverage（沉退）去吃挡拆顺下的内线。

在顺下启动的同时，一名射手从背后给这名内线做 back screen。他必须在同一个半秒内解决两个问题：
- 从 back screen 上方挤过去继续跟顺下，代价是到不了篮下；或者
- 换防到做 back screen 的人身上，而那个人立刻 **pop（外弹）** 到三分线。

与此同时，防持球人的那位还在追第一个掩护。结果是**两名防守人要在一小块区域里负责三个活的威胁**：持球人的 pull-up、顺下者的直冲或 lob、以及 back-screener 的外弹三分。防守拿掉哪一个，就等于把另外两个送给你 —— 所以这里真正的进攻技术是阅读，而不是背第一选择。
