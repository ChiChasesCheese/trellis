## Q
In the Princeton offense the backdoor cut is not called — passer and cutter run it simultaneously off a shared cue. What defensive behaviour is the trigger, and what exactly are the two players looking at?

## A
The trigger is **denial overplay**: the defender has stepped into the passing lane, put a hand or forearm on it, and turned his head to watch the ball.

- **The cutter** feels the forearm on the passing line and sees the defender's head turn away from him — the moment the defender is watching the ball instead of the man, the path behind him is empty.
- **The passer** sees the defender's back or shoulder number instead of his chest. A defender square to the ball can recover; a defender turned sideways in the lane cannot.

Both cues appear at the same instant, which is why no call is needed. The pass is thrown early, ahead of the cutter and away from the recovering defender, and it is a **backdoor cut** — a punishment for aggression, not a set play you run on a schedule.

## Q zh
在 Princeton offense 里，backdoor cut（空切）是不喊的 —— 传球人和切入者靠同一个信号同时启动。触发它的防守行为是什么？两个人具体在看什么？

## A zh
触发信号是 **denial overplay（过度贴防、抢传球路线）**：防守人已经踩进了传球路线，用手或前臂压住这条线，并且把头转过去盯球。

- **切入者**能感觉到前臂压在传球线上，同时看到防守人的头转开了 —— 防守人在看球不看人的那一瞬间，他身后就是空的。
- **传球人**看到的是防守人的背号或肩膀，而不是胸口。正面对球的防守人还能回追；侧身站在路线里的防守人来不及。

两个信号同时出现，所以不需要喊。球要提前出手，传到切入者前面、远离回追防守人的一侧。这就是 **backdoor cut**：它是对防守侵略性的惩罚，不是按表跑的固定战术。
