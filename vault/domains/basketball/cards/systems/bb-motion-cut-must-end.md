The cardinal spacing rule of motion offense is that {{c1::every cut ends}} — the cutter either finishes at the rim or clears all the way through to a new spot. A cutter who stalls in the lane becomes {{c2::a second defender for his own team}}, because he drags a help defender into the exact space a drive needs. The spot he left is immediately {{c3::filled}} by the player behind him, which is what keeps the floor at its working width of about {{c4::15 feet}} between teammates.

## zh
Motion offense 的空间第一原则是 {{c1::every cut ends}}（每次切入都要有结束）—— 切入者要么在篮下终结，要么一路穿过去落到新的位置。切到一半停在禁区里的人等于 {{c2::a second defender for his own team}}（给自己队多添一个防守人），因为他把一名协防者拽进了突破正需要的那块空间。他空出来的位置由身后的队友立刻 {{c3::filled}}（补上），这才让场上队友之间维持大约 {{c4::15 feet}} 的有效间距。
