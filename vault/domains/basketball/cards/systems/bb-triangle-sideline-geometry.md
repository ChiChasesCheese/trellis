The triangle's strong side is a sideline triangle formed by {{c1::the post, the wing and the corner}}, spaced roughly {{c2::15-18 feet}} apart, while the remaining two players run a {{c3::two-man game}} on the weak side. The geometry is the point: from any of the three strong-side spots there are always {{c4::two}} passing options, so a defense that denies one of them has, by definition, opened the other.

## zh
Triangle（三角进攻）的强侧是由 {{c1::the post, the wing and the corner}}（低位、侧翼、底角）构成的边线三角形，彼此间距大约 {{c2::15-18 feet}}，剩下两名球员在弱侧打 {{c3::two-man game}}（二人配合）。几何形状本身就是重点：站在强侧三个位置的任何一个，永远有 {{c4::two}} 条传球选择，所以防守只要掐掉其中一条，按定义就一定放开了另一条。
