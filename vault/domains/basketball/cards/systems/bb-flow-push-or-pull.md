## Q
You grab a defensive rebound and push. At half court it is 3 offensive players against 3 defenders back. Push or pull it out — and what is the actual decision rule?

## A
**Push the advantage, not the ball.** Equal numbers is not by itself a reason to stop, and it is not by itself a reason to go.

Push when at least one of these is true:
- a defender is still behind the play, so it is really 3-on-2 in the half you are attacking,
- the matchups are scrambled — a guard is on your big, or nobody has picked up the ball,
- **the rim is unprotected**: nobody is anchored in front of the basket yet.

Pull it out when the rim is protected, all three defenders are matched and set, and pushing would trade an organized possession for a contested early shot.

Two modifiers override both: late in a close game the shot clock and the score decide (an early two when you need three is a wasted possession), and if you are the weaker team, the extra early possessions are worth more to you than the tidiness is.

## Q zh
你抢下防守篮板往前推。到中场时是 3 打 3，对方三个人都退回来了。推还是拉出来打 —— 真正的决策规则是什么？

## A zh
**推的是优势，不是球。** 人数相等本身既不是停下的理由，也不是继续的理由。

只要下面任一条成立就推：
- 还有一名防守人落在球后面，也就是你攻的那半场其实是 3 打 2，
- 对位乱了 —— 后卫在防你的内线，或者根本没人接管持球人，
- **篮筐没人保护**：还没有人在篮前扎住位置。

篮筐被守住、三个人都对上位并站好、而且推进只会把一个有组织的回合换成一次被干扰的仓促出手时，就拉出来打。

两个修正条件优先于上面：比分接近的末段由 shot clock 和分差决定（需要三分时投进两分等于浪费回合）；而如果你是弱队，多打出的这些早期回合，比阵地上的整齐更值钱。
