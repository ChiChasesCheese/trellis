## Q
What is an "empty side" (empty corner) ball screen, and precisely which defender does clearing that corner remove from the play?

## A
An **empty side** action is a ball screen run on a side of the floor where the offense has deliberately left nobody below the wing — the corner is vacated, usually by lifting that player to the opposite side.

What it removes: **the low man / tagger**. In normal spacing, the corner defender on the ball side is the one who steps in to tag the roller and to wall up a baseline drive, because he is closest. Empty the corner and there is no defender there to do it. The nearest help now has to travel across the paint from the weak side — a longer trip that arrives late, and one that leaves a shooter behind it.

The consequence is that the two defenders in the pick and roll must guard it **two-on-two, with no rescue**. That is exactly the situation a good handler wants: whichever of them commits, the other assignment is open, and there is no third defender to clean it up.

## Q zh
什么是 "empty side"（空侧 / 空底角）挡拆？清空那个底角，具体拿掉了哪一名防守人？

## A zh
**Empty side** 指的是在某一侧打挡拆，而进攻方刻意让这一侧从侧翼往下没有任何人 —— 底角空出来，通常是把那名球员提到另一侧去。

它拿掉的是 **low man / tagger（底线协防人）**。在正常空间下，球侧底角的防守人就是那个上来点顺下、并封住底线突破的人，因为他离得最近。把底角清空，那里就没有人能做这件事。最近的协防必须从弱侧横穿禁区赶过来 —— 路程更长、到得更晚，而且他身后会留下一个射手。

结果是挡拆里的两名防守人必须**二对二地守，没有救援**。这正是好的持球人想要的局面：他们两个谁扑上去，另一个的对位就是空的，而且没有第三个人来收拾残局。
