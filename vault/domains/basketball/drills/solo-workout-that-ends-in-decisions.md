# Drill: A solo workout that ends in decisions

Ninety minutes alone in a gym, no partner, no rebounder. Build a session whose
last third cannot be completed on autopilot — every rep in it has to be
chosen, not repeated. The point is not shot volume; it is arriving at the
shots you actually get in games, in the state you are actually in when you get
them.

**Structure to run**
1. *Form and rhythm, 10 min.* Close range, one-motion checkpoints, no counting.
2. *Blocked block, 15 min.* One shot type from one spot until the pattern is stable — this is the only blocked work in the session.
3. *Random block, 25 min.* Rotate spot and shot type every rep. Never two identical reps in a row. Track makes per 25 shots, not per spot.
4. *Decision block, 25 min.* Every rep starts with a self-imposed cue you cannot predict — a coin, a phone timer, a shuffled card. The cue tells you which read to play: shoot the catch, one-dribble pull, drive to the rim, or shot fake and re-space.
5. *Fatigue finisher, 10 min.* Same decision block with a conditioning cost attached to each rep.

**Constraints to state and honor**
- The randomiser must be genuinely outside your control — deciding the read yourself turns this back into blocked practice.
- Shot count in the decision block is whatever the reads produce. Do not top it up to reach a round number.
- Every rep begins with a real catch (self-toss off the backboard or a rebound), never a stationary hold.
- Score by decision quality, and record the shot type you actually took beside the one the cue called for.

**Grading points (what a strong session hits)**
- The blocked block is the smallest block, not the biggest ([[bb-practice-design-blocked-random]], [[bb-blocked-vs-random-transfer]]).
- The decision block genuinely varies the read, not just the spot ([[bb-random-practice-cue-design]]).
- Shots taken sit on the value ladder you claim to believe in — rim, free throw, corner three, above-break three ([[bb-four-factors]]).
- Fatigue reps are treated as data about your real game shot, not as a bonus round.
- The session's log survives a bad day: percentages recorded, but decision counts recorded first ([[bb-workout-tracking-that-survives]]).
