# Drill: Closeout and second-rotation shell

Four on four, offence in a four-out shape, defence in shell. The offence is
restricted to swing passes and one drive per possession; the defence has to
close out, help, and — this is the whole point — cover the *second* rotation,
the one that follows the help. Most amateur defences survive the first
rotation and get scored on by the third pass.

**Constraints to state and honor**
- The offence takes exactly one drive per possession, chosen at random by the coach or the passer.
- No defender may leave his feet on a closeout. A closeout that concedes a straight-line drive is a failed rep even if the shot misses.
- Every possession ends with a box-out and a secured rebound, or it does not count.
- Run in silence for two minutes, then two minutes with mandatory talk — compare.
- Score the drill on rotation timing: the helper's arrival relative to the ball, and the second rotation's arrival relative to the pass.

**Grading points (what a strong rep hits)**
- Sprint-then-chop closeout with hands high on a shooter, low on a driver ([[bb-shell-defense-variations]]).
- Position adjusts while the ball is in the air, not after the catch.
- The low man is identified before the drive starts, by everyone, not just by him.
- The x-out is run to the shooter's outside shoulder so the extra pass has no angle.
- Box-out is contact first and pursuit second; guards rebound rather than leaking out.
- Talk measurably changes the second-rotation timing — if it does not, the talk was noise.
