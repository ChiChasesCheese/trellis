# Explaining The Gather Step To Basketball Fans

A referee and a trainer walking the footwork out on the floor, in slow motion,
which is the only way this rule ever becomes clear. Most of the moves adult
players call travels are legal, and most players cannot say where the count
starts — this fixes both in about ten minutes.

**Extract on watch:**
- Where the gather begins, and why the count does not start before it.
- The two steps that are legal after the gather, demonstrated rather than described.
- Why the step-through looks like a travel and is not.
- The genuine violations that go uncalled, so you know what you are getting away with.
