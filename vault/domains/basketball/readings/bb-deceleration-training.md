# Get SHIFTY By Training Your Deceleration the Right Way 🔬

Basketball athleticism is sold as vertical leap and is mostly braking: the
player who can stop can change direction, and the player who can change
direction gets separation. This makes the case with the training implications
attached, which is what makes it useful rather than just interesting.

**Extract on watch:**
- Why deceleration, not acceleration, is usually the limiting quality on court.
- The eccentric loading demand a hard stop puts on the leg, and how it is trained.
- How braking capacity feeds directly into a change-of-direction move.
- What to add to a week that already has playing and lifting in it.
