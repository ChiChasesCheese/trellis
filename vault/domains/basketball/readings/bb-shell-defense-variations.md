# 3 Shell Defense Variations For Lockdown Defense | Baskeball Team Defense Drills

Shell is the drill that teaches team defence as a shape rather than five
individual matchups, and the variations here are the ones that force the
second rotation — the one amateurs never practise and always lose. Use it as
the template for the closeout drill in this vault.

**Extract on watch:**
- Ball-you-man positioning that changes as the ball moves, not after it arrives.
- Who the low man is in each variation, and what he gives up to help.
- The closeout that takes the shot without conceding the straight-line drive.
- Why the drill is scored on rotation timing rather than on stops.
