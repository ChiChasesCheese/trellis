# How the Princeton Offense Fits Into Today's Basketball

The Princeton offence is usually filed under nostalgia; this argues it as a
live solution to a live problem — what to do when the other team is more
athletic than you are. For an adult amateur that is most nights, which makes
this the most directly applicable system video in the vault.

**Extract on watch:**
- The high-post hub, and why passing through it changes every defender's job.
- The backdoor cut as a punishment for overplaying rather than as a set play.
- How the offence forces a choice between denying passes and protecting the rim.
- Which pieces of it survive into modern spacing, and which do not.
