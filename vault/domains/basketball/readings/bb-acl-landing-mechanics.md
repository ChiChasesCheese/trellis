# ACL Injury Prevention: Jumping and Landing Technique

From a hospital that operates on these knees for a living, which is the right
place to take this from. Most non-contact ACL injuries happen in a position
you can see on video and change with a warm-up, and this shows both the
position and the correction.

**Extract on watch:**
- The knee-in landing position that carries the risk, and how to spot it on yourself.
- Landing soft through hip and knee instead of stopping the fall at the knee.
- Single-leg control, since that is how the injury actually happens in a game.
- What a warm-up has to contain to be worth the time it costs you.
