# The Basics of Spain Pick & Roll | Ball Screen Offense

Spain is the clearest example of what a set actually is: a shape assembled to
put one defender in an impossible position. Learn to see the mechanism and you
stop memorising sets and start recognising the problem each one poses.

**Extract on watch:**
- Where the back screen goes, and which defender it is aimed at.
- Why the screener's defender cannot both help on the roll and recover to the screener's screener.
- The three scoring outlets the action produces, and which one the defence chooses for you.
- How the same idea gets hidden inside Horns and other entries.
