# Basketball Transition to Flow to Set Offense from John Tauer

Early offence is where the cheapest advantages live, and this is a coach
showing the seam between running, flowing, and finally calling something. The
useful idea is that the three are one continuum, not three separate offences
with pauses between them.

**Extract on watch:**
- What has to happen in the first few seconds for flow to be available at all.
- Drag and delay entries that continue transition instead of stopping it.
- The decision to push or pull it out, and who owns that decision.
- Why calling a set should be the last resort rather than the default.
