# One Motion Shot Vs Two Motion Shot: How to Shoot a Basketball

Most amateur shooting advice pushes one template; this splits the jump shot
into two legitimate families and explains what each buys you. That framing
matters tactically, because the family you shoot from decides how much space
you need to get a shot off against a closeout.

**Extract on watch:**
- The structural difference between the two shooting patterns, not just how they look.
- Which one trades release height for release speed, and what that costs against a longer defender.
- Why changing families mid-season is expensive, and when a smaller repair beats a rebuild.
- The cue you can self-check on video without a coach standing next to you.
