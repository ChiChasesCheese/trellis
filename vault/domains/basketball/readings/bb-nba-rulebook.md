# RULE NO. 10: Violations and Penalties | NBA Official

The primary source, and the one rules argument at an adult gym is usually
settled by two paragraphs nobody has read. This is the violations chapter —
travelling, the three-second counts, goaltending and basket interference — in
the league's own wording rather than somebody's summary of it.

**Extract on read:**
- The definition of travelling and where the count starts relative to the gather.
- Offensive and defensive three seconds, and what ends each count.
- The goaltending and basket-interference language, including the cylinder above the rim.
- Which of these the league words differently from FIBA, so you know what travels between competitions.
