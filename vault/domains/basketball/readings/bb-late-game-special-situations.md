# Fran Fraschilla's Late Game Plays and Special Situations

The scripted end of the game is the part amateurs never rehearse, and this is
a coach laying out how those minutes are actually planned. Watch it once for
the principles and again for the counters — the second option in a good ATO is
usually the one that scores.

**Extract on watch:**
- How the choice of action changes between down two and down three.
- Inbounds structure: screening the screener, and the inbounder as a scoring threat.
- What a timeout buys beyond rest — the matchup or switch you are trying to force.
- Why the obvious first option exists mainly to move a defender off the real one.
