# 5 Change of Pace SECRETS (With Drills)

Speed differential beats top speed, and this is the clearest demonstration of
why: the advantage comes from the gap between your slow and your fast, not
from the fast. Useful for an adult player because it is a skill that keeps
improving long after your vertical stops.

**Extract on watch:**
- How a defender's recovery step is timed to your speed, and what breaks that timing.
- Decelerating as the move rather than as the thing before the move.
- Where the ball has to be for a change of pace to still threaten the rim.
- The drills that isolate the quality, so you can build them into a solo workout.
