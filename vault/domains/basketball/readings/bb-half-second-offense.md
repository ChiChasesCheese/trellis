# What is this 0.5 offense all about for the Phoenix Suns? Monty Williams explains.

A head coach explaining, in his own words, the rule that governs an entire NBA
offence: shoot, pass or drive inside half a second of the catch. Short, and
worth it because it reframes team offence as a decision-latency problem rather
than a play-calling one.

**Extract on watch:**
- What the half-second budget is actually protecting — the advantage decaying while you hold the ball.
- Why holding the ball to "make a good decision" usually destroys the decision.
- How a rule like this replaces memorised plays for players who already know how to read.
- The connection between quick decisions and the extra pass that beats a rotation.
