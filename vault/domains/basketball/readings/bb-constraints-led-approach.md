# The Constraints Led Approach for Youth Basketball: Game-Like Drills That Build Skill & IQ Faster

Constraints are how you make a player discover a read instead of being told
it, and this shows the design pattern with drills attached. Ignore the "youth"
in the title — the method is identical for an adult, and the small-sided games
here drop straight into a pickup run.

**Extract on watch:**
- What a constraint is: a rule change that makes the correct read the easy one.
- Examples worth stealing — dribble limits, scoring zones, touch requirements.
- Why 3-on-3 exposes more decisions per minute than 5-on-5.
- How to tell a constraint that teaches from one that just makes the game harder.
