# The Gap Theory (Spacing)

Spacing is the variable every other idea on this map depends on, and it is
easier to see on film than to argue about in words. Watch this early: once you
can name the gap a defender is standing in, most of what a good offence does
stops looking like magic and starts looking like arithmetic.

**Extract on watch:**
- What a "gap" actually is, and how to spot the defender who is guarding two players at once.
- The distance between teammates that keeps a help defender from covering both.
- How one player standing in the wrong place removes a drive that was there a second ago.
- Why creating the advantage and transporting it are two separate problems.
