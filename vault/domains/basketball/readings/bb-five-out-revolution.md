# The 5-Out Basketball Revolution

A film-driven account of how five-out spacing took over, told as a chain of
consequences rather than a trend. It is the best single explanation of why the
long two disappeared and why an average shooter standing in the right place is
worth more than a good scorer standing in the wrong one.

**Extract on watch:**
- What emptying the paint does to help defenders' choices.
- Gravity: the shooter who scores nothing on a possession and still creates the shot.
- Why the empty side exists and what it removes from the defence.
- The trade five-out accepts on the offensive glass, and whether it is worth it.
