# Official FIBA Basketball Rules | FIBA Referees

FIBA's own rules portal, which matters because most basketball outside the
United States — and most international competition — is played under these,
not the NBA's. Several differences are tactical, not cosmetic, and they change
what the defence is allowed to do to you.

**Extract on read:**
- No defensive three seconds: help can sit in the paint, which changes drive geometry.
- Arc and court dimensions, and what that does to corner spacing.
- Timeout and possession-arrow rules, and how they alter late-game management.
- Where FIBA's goaltending and travelling wording differs from the NBA's.
