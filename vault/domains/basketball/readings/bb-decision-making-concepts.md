# Basketball Decision Making Concepts and Game Examples

Basketball IQ is usually described as something you either have or do not;
this treats it as a set of nameable reads with game footage attached to each
one. That is the version you can actually practise, and the framing the drills
in this vault are graded against.

**Extract on watch:**
- Naming the defender whose decision you are attacking before you make your move.
- Reading the second defender rather than the one in front of you.
- Why the same shot is a good decision for one player and a bad one for another.
- How to phrase a read as a question you can ask yourself in a live rep.
