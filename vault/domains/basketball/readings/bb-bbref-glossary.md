# Glossary | Basketball-Reference.com

The dictionary for every statistic you will meet, with the formula beside each
one. Keep it open the first few times you look at a box score or an on-off
table: knowing that a rate is per-possession rather than per-game is usually
the whole difference between reading a number and misreading it.

**Extract on read:**
- Pace, possessions, and how offensive and defensive rating are actually computed.
- eFG% and TS% side by side, and which one accounts for free throws.
- The rate stats (usage, rebound percentage) and why per-game versions mislead.
- Which listed metrics are descriptive and which are estimates with error bars.
