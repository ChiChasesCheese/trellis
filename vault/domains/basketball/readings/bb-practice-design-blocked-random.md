# How to Apply Blocked, Serial and Random Basketball Practice Design

The biggest gap between an amateur's training and a professional's is not
volume, it is the schedule — and this explains the three schedules and when
each is correct. Watch it before you design another solo workout, because it
predicts which of your reps will survive contact with a game.

**Extract on watch:**
- What blocked, serial and random practice are, in practical session terms.
- Why blocked reps feel productive during the session and transfer poorly afterwards.
- The point in learning a skill where you should switch schedules.
- How to build randomness into a session without making it chaos.
