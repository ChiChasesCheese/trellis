# Introduction to Oliver's Four Factors

A careful walk through the four factors with the formulas written out and the
weighting treated as an estimate rather than scripture — which is exactly the
attitude to take into any basketball number. It is the piece to read before you
start using eFG% and turnover rate to argue about why your team lost.

**Extract on read:**
- Each factor's definition and formula: shooting, turnovers, rebounding, free throws.
- The commonly quoted weights, where they came from, and how much to trust them.
- That every factor has an offensive and a defensive twin — eight numbers, not four.
- Why shooting dominates, and what that implies about which problem to fix first.
