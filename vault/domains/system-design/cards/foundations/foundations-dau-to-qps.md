Estimation shortcuts: a day is {{c1::~86,400 ≈ 10⁵ seconds}}, so average QPS ≈ {{c2::daily requests ÷ 10⁵}} (e.g. 100M requests/day ≈ 1,000 QPS), and peak QPS is typically {{c3::2–5× average}}.


## zh
