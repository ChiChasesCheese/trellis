# Amazon's Leadership Principles

The published list the behavioural half of an Amazon-shaped assessment is scored
against. Read it as a rubric rather than as corporate copy: each principle names
a behaviour an interviewer will look for evidence of, and the wording tells you
which evidence counts — "leaders are right, a lot" is asking about calibration
and updating, not about being correct. Prepare stories against the list, not
against a generic "tell me about yourself".

**Extract on read:**
- Map each of your six to eight prepared stories to several principles rather
  than one story per principle ([[cc-transfer-amazon-star-with-a-number]]).
- The principles that most often go unprepared: Dive Deep, Are Right A Lot,
  Have Backbone — all of which need a disagreement story with a measurable outcome.
- The language itself, which is worth borrowing when you narrate a decision.
