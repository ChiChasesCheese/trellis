## Q
The interviewer suggests an approach you believe is slower or wrong. What do you do?

## A
**Disagree once, concretely, with a cost — then commit.**

> "Re-sorting the candidates on every event is O(n log n) each time; a heap keeps it O(log n). Happy to write the sort first if you'd rather see it simple."

Then take their call and move. Stalling to win the argument costs more than being wrong, and the interviewer may be steering you toward the next part on purpose. If you take their path and it breaks, say so plainly and fix it — no I-told-you-so, and no silent resentment in the code.

## Q zh
面试官提出一个你认为更慢或不对的方案。你怎么做？

## A zh
**具体地、带代价地反对一次 —— 然后服从。**

> 「每来一个事件就重排候选是每次 O(n log n)；用 heap 是 O(log n)。如果你想先看简单版，我可以先写排序。」

然后照他们的决定往下走。为赢下争论而僵住，代价比错更大，而且面试官可能是有意把你引向下一部分。如果按他们的路走坏了，直说并修好 —— 不说「我早说了」，也不要在代码里憋着情绪。
