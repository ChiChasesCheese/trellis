## Q
You have been stuck on one part for five minutes with no measurable progress. Give yourself a rule.

## A
**Timebox to one re-read, then leave.** Re-read that part's statement and its worked example once, slowly — most stalls are a misread rule, not a bug, and the second reading finds it.

If that does not resolve it: write the failing case down in a comment, move to the next part (parts are usually scored independently), and come back with the remaining parts banked. Continuing to stare is the most expensive thing you can do with the clock, because it also consumes the calm you need for the parts you *can* finish.

## Q zh
你已经在某一部分卡了五分钟，毫无可衡量的进展。给自己定一条规则。

## A zh
**限时一次重读，然后离开。** 慢慢地把这一部分的题面和样例再读一遍 —— 大多数卡壳是读错了规则而不是有 bug，第二遍会读出来。

如果还不行：把失败用例写进注释，去做下一部分（各部分通常独立计分），等其余部分入袋后再回来。继续盯着看是对时钟最昂贵的用法，因为它同时耗掉了你完成**能做完**的部分所需的冷静。
