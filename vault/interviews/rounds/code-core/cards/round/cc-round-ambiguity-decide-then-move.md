## Q
You have spent four minutes deciding between two readings of a rule and cannot break the tie. What is the correct move?

## A
**Decide, mark, and move — an ambiguity is a two-minute budget, not a research project.**

The expected cost of guessing wrong is one flipped operator plus a re-run; the cost of stalling is the part you did not write. Concretely: pick the reading that matches the worked example (or the stricter one, which fails loudly rather than silently), leave the assumption in a comment, keep it behind one flag, and note it as the first thing to revisit if a later part contradicts you. Deciding badly and moving beats deciding well too late.

## Q zh
你已经在一条规则的两种读法之间纠结了四分钟，还是打不破平局。正确的动作是什么？

## A zh
**决定、标注、往前走 —— 歧义的预算是两分钟，不是一个研究项目。**

猜错的期望代价是翻转一个运算符外加重跑一次；僵住的代价是你没写出来的那一部分。具体做法：选与样例一致的读法（若无样例则选更严格的那个，它会响亮地失败而不是悄悄地错），把假设写进注释、藏在一个 flag 后面，并记下"若后面的部分与之矛盾，这是第一个要回头看的地方"。选得不好但继续前进，胜过选得好但太晚。
