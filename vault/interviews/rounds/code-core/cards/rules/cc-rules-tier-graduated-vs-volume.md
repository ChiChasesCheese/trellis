With bands `1-5 @ 700` and `6+ @ 650` and a quantity of 6, **graduated** pricing charges each band on the units inside it — {{c1::5 x 700 + 1 x 650 = 4150}} — while **volume** pricing finds the single band containing the whole quantity and charges every unit at that rate — {{c2::6 x 650 = 3900}}. Same table, different totals, and the statement's word ({{c3::"graduated"/"incremental" versus "volume"}}) is the only thing that tells them apart.

## zh
在 `1-5 @ 700` 和 `6+ @ 650` 两个区间下，数量为 6 时：**graduated**（阶梯）计价对每个区间内的单位分别计费 —— {{c1::5 x 700 + 1 x 650 = 4150}}；而 **volume**（总量）计价先找到包含整个数量的那一个区间，再按该费率对每一单位计费 —— {{c2::6 x 650 = 3900}}。同一张表，不同的总额，而题面里的那个词（{{c3::"graduated"/"incremental" 对 "volume"}}）是区分它们的唯一依据。
