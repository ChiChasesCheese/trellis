## Q
A problem fits none of the headings in your notes. Is that a filing failure?

## A
**No — it is a proposal to grow the map, and often the most valuable output of a round.** A heading you did not have is a skill you were not practising.

- Add the heading *before* the lesson, so the lesson has somewhere to live and the next problem of that kind becomes a second entry instead of another orphan.
- Keep a heading narrow enough to name the single skill it trains. When one accumulates more than a handful of rules, split it — "I'm weak here" has to point at something specific enough to drill.
- Resist the opposite move: filing an ill-fitting problem under a heading that nearly matches. That is how a note becomes unfindable and a real gap stays invisible ([[cc-transfer-playbook-find-the-general-leaf]]).

## Q zh
一道题在你的笔记里找不到任何标题可归。这算归档失败吗？

## A zh
**不算 —— 它是一份「扩充地图」的提案，而且往往是一轮里最有价值的产出。** 你没有的标题，就是你没在练的技能。

- 先加标题，*再*写经验，这样经验才有地方住，下一道同类题就成了第二条记录，而不是又一个孤儿。
- 标题要窄到能说清它训练的那**一个**技能。当某个标题攒下超过几条规则时就拆开 —— 「我这里弱」必须指向具体到能拿来练的东西。
- 抵抗相反的动作：把不合身的题塞进一个「差不多」的标题下。笔记正是这样变得找不回来，而真正的缺口也就此隐形（[[cc-transfer-playbook-find-the-general-leaf]]）。
