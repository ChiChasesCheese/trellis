## Q
What does a quant-shaped assessment ask for, and how does that change what "finished" means compared with a software round?

## A
**Short numeric answers to probability and expectation questions under a tight per-question clock**, often mixed with a little coding and a market-intuition question.

- The answer is usually a number or a closed form entered directly, so there is **no partial credit for a nearly-right derivation** — the opposite of a multi-part coding assessment.
- Recognizing the standard setup beats manipulation skill: linearity of expectation, conditioning on the first step, symmetry, indicator variables.
- "Finished" means a number **plus a sanity check** — units, bounds, and one degenerate case — not code that runs.
- When the question is ambiguous, state the assumption with the answer. An answer with a stated assumption is gradeable; one without is indistinguishable from a guess.

## Q zh
量化型笔试要的是什么？相比软件轮，这如何改变「做完了」的含义？

## A zh
**在每题很短的时限内，给出概率与期望问题的简短数值答案**，通常还混有少量编程和一道市场直觉题。

- 答案通常是一个直接填入的数字或闭式表达式，所以**几乎正确的推导拿不到部分分** —— 与多 part 编程笔试恰好相反。
- 认出标准套路胜过推导技巧：期望的线性性、对第一步取条件、对称性、示性变量。
- 「做完了」意味着一个数字**外加一次合理性检查** —— 量纲、取值范围、一个退化情形 —— 而不是一段能跑的代码。
- 题意含糊时，把假设和答案一起写出来。带明示假设的答案是可评分的；不带的和瞎猜没有区别。
