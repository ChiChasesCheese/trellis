## Q
If the transferable rule becomes the card, what happens to the problem itself?

## A
**It stays as evidence and as a drill — never as a card.**

- **Evidence**: the concrete story that makes the rule stick — "disputing a *non-fraud* charge pushed a ratio account over the line" — attached to the rule rather than standing on its own.
- **Drill**: re-runnable practice with a clock and a grading list, restated abstractly enough that it is about the shape, not about one company's question.
- Keep the input, the expected output and the exact failing case. Those are what make a re-attempt honest instead of a memory test.
- What you do **not** keep is the statement as a card. Recalling "what did that question ask" trains nothing you can use in a round you have not seen.

## Q zh
如果可迁移的规则变成了卡片，那题目本身怎么办？

## A zh
**它留作证据和练习 —— 永远不作为卡片。**

- **证据**：让规则粘得住的那个具体故事 —— 「对一笔*非欺诈*的 charge 提起争议，反而把比例型账户推过了线」—— 挂在规则下面，而不是独立存在。
- **练习**：可重跑的、带时钟和评分清单的训练，抽象到只关乎形态、不关乎某家公司的题目。
- 保留输入、期望输出和确切的失败用例。正是它们让重做是一次诚实的重做，而不是一次记忆测验。
- **不**保留的是题面本身作为卡片。回忆「那道题问了什么」，训练不出任何能用在你没见过的轮次里的东西。
