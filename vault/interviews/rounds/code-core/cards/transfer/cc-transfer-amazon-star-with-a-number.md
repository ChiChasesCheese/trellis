## Q
A behavioural question asks for a time you disagreed with a decision. What structure and what content make the answer score?

## A
**STAR with a measurable outcome, told in the first person singular.**

- **Situation / Task**: two sentences — just enough to make the stakes legible.
- **Action**: what *you* did. "We" hides exactly the signal being scored.
- **Result**: a number. Latency, cost, defect count, hours saved, adoption. "It went well" is unscoreable.

- Prepare six to eight stories and map each to several principles, rather than one story per principle: the same project is ownership, bias for action or customer obsession depending on which part you tell.
- Include one genuine failure and what you changed afterwards. A candidate with no failure story reads as inexperienced or unreflective, and both are cheaper to fix than they look.

## Q zh
一道行为题问你一次与某个决定意见相左的经历。什么结构、什么内容才能得分？

## A zh
**用 STAR，带一个可量化的结果，用第一人称单数讲。**

- **情境 / 任务**：两句话 —— 刚好让利害关系清晰。
- **行动**：*你*做了什么。「我们」恰好盖住了对方要评的那个信号。
- **结果**：一个数字。延迟、成本、缺陷数、省下的工时、采用率。「效果不错」无法评分。

- 准备六到八个故事，每个映射到多条准则，而不是一条准则配一个故事：同一个项目，讲哪一段就分别是主人翁精神、行动偏好或客户至上。
- 放进一个真实的失败，以及你之后改变了什么。没有失败故事的候选人读起来要么经验不足、要么不善反思，而这两者都比看上去更容易修正。
