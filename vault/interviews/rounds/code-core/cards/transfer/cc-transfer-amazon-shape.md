## Q
The Amazon-shaped assessment is more than a coding section. Name the components, and say what the practical consequence is for how you prepare.

## A
**Typically two algorithm-style coding problems, a work-simulation section, and behavioural items tied to the company's published leadership principles.**

- **Coding**: standard data-structure problems with fixed test cases. Speed and correctness, not spec interpretation.
- **Work simulation**: scenario-based items about prioritization, escalation and acting under ambiguity.
- **Workstyle / behavioural**: attitudinal items plus short written or recorded answers about your own experience.

Practical consequence: the non-coding sections are graded and are part of the debrief, so spending all of your preparation on algorithms leaves points on the table in exactly the sections candidates treat as filler ([[cc-transfer-amazon-work-simulation]]).

## Q zh
Amazon 型笔试不止有编程部分。说出它的组成，以及这对你的准备意味着什么。

## A zh
**通常是两道算法风格的编程题、一个工作模拟部分，以及与该公司公开的领导力准则挂钩的行为题。**

- **编程**：带固定测试用例的标准数据结构题。考的是速度和正确性，不是对规格的解读。
- **工作模拟**：围绕优先级排序、升级上报、在模糊中行动的情景题。
- **工作风格 / 行为**：态度类题目，加上关于你自身经历的简短书面或录音回答。

实际后果：非编程部分同样计分，并会进入复盘材料，所以把全部准备时间投在算法上，恰好在候选人当作凑数的那些部分丢分（[[cc-transfer-amazon-work-simulation]]）。
