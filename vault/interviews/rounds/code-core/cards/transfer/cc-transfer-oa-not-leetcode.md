## Q
A candidate drills 200 algorithm puzzles and fails a bespoke-spec assessment. Diagnose the mismatch, and say what actually transfers.

## A
**The bespoke shape tests spec fidelity, not pattern recall.** The data structures involved are usually a dict, a sort and occasionally a heap; the difficulty lives in thresholds, reversals, tie-breaks, money arithmetic and byte-exact output.

- Transfers: typing speed, stdlib fluency, and complexity intuition ([[cc-performance-budget-from-n]]).
- Does not transfer: the reflex to recognize a named technique and start typing. Here the first ten minutes are reading and modelling, and typing early is the failure.
- Drill instead: parsing and validation, integer money, boundary semantics, deterministic output, and finishing a multi-part program against a clock.
- Keep both kinds of practice. An assessment that turns out to be algorithmic is a pleasant surprise; the reverse is a failed round.

## Q zh
一位候选人刷了 200 道算法题，却挂在定制题面型笔试上。诊断这处错配，并说明真正能迁移的是什么。

## A zh
**定制题面考的是对规格的忠实度，不是套路的记忆。** 涉及的数据结构通常就是一个字典、一次排序、偶尔一个堆；难点在阈值、冲正、平局裁决、金额算术和逐字节精确的输出。

- 能迁移的：打字速度、标准库的熟练度、复杂度直觉（[[cc-performance-budget-from-n]]）。
- 不能迁移的：认出一个有名字的技巧就开始敲的反射。这里的前十分钟属于阅读和建模，过早敲键盘本身就是失败。
- 该改练的：解析与校验、整数金额、边界语义、确定性输出、以及在时钟下写完一个多 part 的程序。
- 两种练习都留着。结果发现是算法题算是意外之喜；反过来则是一轮失败。
