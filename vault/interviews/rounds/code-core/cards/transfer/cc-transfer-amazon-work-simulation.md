## Q
The work-simulation section presents workplace scenarios with ranked or forced-choice options. How do you approach it without either gaming it or being naive?

## A
**Answer as the role you are applying for, consistently.** These items probe judgement under ambiguity: what to prioritize, when to escalate, how much evidence to gather before acting.

- Prefer options that gather the cheapest relevant evidence, act on what is reversible, and escalate what is not.
- **Consistency across items matters more than any single answer** — contradictory choices are the detectable signal, and the section is long enough to detect them.
- Do not over-optimize for a guessed rubric. A maintained, defensible policy scores better than clever inference about what the grader wants.
- Budget real time for it. Rushing this section to buy minutes for coding trades a graded section for a marginal one.

## Q zh
工作模拟部分给出职场情景，让你排序或二选一。怎样既不投机取巧、也不天真地应对？

## A zh
**以你所申请的那个岗位的身份，一致地作答。** 这些题目考察模糊情境下的判断：优先做什么、何时上报、行动前收集多少证据。

- 优先选那些「以最低成本收集相关证据、对可逆的事直接行动、对不可逆的事上报」的选项。
- **跨题目的一致性比任何单题答案更重要** —— 自相矛盾的选择才是可被检测到的信号，而这部分的题量足够检测出来。
- 不要为了猜到的评分标准过度优化。一以贯之、站得住脚的策略，得分好过对评分者意图的聪明推断。
- 给它留出真实的时间。为了给编程挤时间而赶完这一段，是拿一个计分部分去换一个边际部分。
