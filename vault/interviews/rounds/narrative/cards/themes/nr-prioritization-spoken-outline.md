## Q
Give the beat-by-beat order of a two-minute answer to "Tell me about a time you had to prioritise under time pressure with incomplete information", independent of which real story you tell.

## A
1. **The squeeze, in numbers.** The deadline, the size of the work, the people available: "four days, roughly two weeks of work, two of us."
2. **The ambiguity.** What you did not know and could not wait to find out.
3. **Your criterion.** The rule you ranked by, said as a rule: "anything that could corrupt data or block the release first; cosmetic and internal-only last."
4. **The cut, by name.** The two or three things you decided not to do, and the one you nearly cut but kept, with the reason.
5. **The conversation.** Who wanted the dropped work, how and when you told them, and what they said.
6. **What happened, including the cost.** Did the release go out, and did anything you cut come back to bite? Close with what you would rank differently now.

Spend the most time on beats 3 and 4: the criterion and the cut are what is being scored. If the true story has no real cut in it, it is a hard-work story; choose a different one.
