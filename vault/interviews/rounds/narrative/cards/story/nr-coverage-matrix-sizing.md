A workable story bank is about {{c1::6-8}} stories, because you map stories onto **{{c2::signals}}** rather than onto questions — and one story can answer several different questions by {{c3::re-entering it at a different point}} (the same outage becomes a failure story, a conflict story, or an ambiguity story depending on which decision inside it you open on).

Two coverage rules: no story should be your only answer for {{c4::more than two signals}}, or a bad reaction to it takes out a third of your bank — and at least one story must be one where {{c5::the outcome was genuinely bad}}, since a bank of six wins reads as curated.
