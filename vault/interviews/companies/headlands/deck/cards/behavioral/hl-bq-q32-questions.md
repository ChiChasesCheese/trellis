## Q
面试结尾被问 "你有什么问题要问我吗？"，怎么用 3–5 分钟？
Any questions for me?

## A
- 选三个："What are the biggest pieces of the research platform, and which is hardest to keep fast?"；"Tell me about a recent performance problem — your code, a library, or the OS?"；"What do you look for in a session like this, and what are the next steps?"
- 让话题围着他转："What did your first **six months** look like?"
- 收尾："Thank you — **[something he actually said]** confirmed my interest."（方括号里换成他真的说过的一件事。）
