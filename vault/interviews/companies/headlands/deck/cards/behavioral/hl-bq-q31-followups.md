## Q
"加入后先看什么"之后的追问：你会先测量什么？你会问他们什么？
How would you measure first? What would you ask them?

## A
- **How would you measure first?** 一个研究员任务**每阶段的时间**和**每秒行数**，**在优化之前**。
- **What would you ask them?** "What's the **slowest step** for a researcher today?"
