## Q
"写过多少 C++"之后的追问：能解释 RAII 吗？多久能上手？
Can you explain RAII? How long to be productive?

## A
- **Can you explain RAII?** 我知道这套模型：**RAII、移动语义（move semantics）、智能指针（smart pointers）**；会的就答，到边界就停；不会的说 "I know it to X; below that I'd check the reference."
- **How long to be productive?** "I'd expect to be **slower than your team for a while**"，并说出学习计划。
