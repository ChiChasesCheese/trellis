## Q
JD 要求 "Preferred degree in any computing related area"，你的评级、证据和说法是什么？

## A
评级：**强**。

- 证据：NYU 计算机硕士（MS Computer Science，2023–2025）。
- 说法："I have an MS in computer science from NYU."
- 不展开本科（建筑管理）；被问时说 "I switched into CS for my master's and have been writing production code since."
