## Q
面试被问「Why are you leaving PayPal?」，怎么答才稳？

## A
1. **Forward-looking**: "I want a **deeper stack** — compiler, runtime, hardware — a team being **built from zero**, and testing as **the main job**."
2. **Credit PayPal**: "I own a **money pipeline end to end** there, and I learned on-call and root-causing."
3. **Stop**.

> 中文提示：20 秒。【待补：真实原因，一句话】如果真实原因是签证、团队变化等，说真话、简短，再转回 what I want next。不批评人和流程；薪资不是理由。
