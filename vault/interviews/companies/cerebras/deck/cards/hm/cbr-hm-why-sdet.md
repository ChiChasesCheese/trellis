## Q
从软件工程师转 SDET，面试被问「Why SDET / why release integration?」，怎么答？

## A
1. **Pattern**: "My strongest work has been **verification**: parity tests, quality gates, health checks, root-cause analysis."
2. **Make it the job**: "Here it's **the main job**, not the 20% around feature work."
3. **It's engineering**: "The posting says **software engineering applied to quality** — not just executing test cases. I'll be **writing frameworks and tools** in Python."
4. **Where I add value fast**: "Triage, failure classification and gates are things I've done in production on-call."

> 中文提示：30 秒。别说"SDET 更简单""拿不到 SWE offer"。如果追问"会不会觉得是降级"：说"It's where my best work has been, and here the stack is deeper than anything I've tested."
