## Q
Cerebras 经理面：30 秒自我介绍，按说出口的顺序怎么讲？

## A
1. **Who**: "I'm a software engineer at **PayPal**, on Braintree's **fee and settlement platform**."
2. **Strength**: "What I'm best at is **proving a change is safe before it ships**."
3. **Three proofs**:
   - "I migrated Ruby and Kotlin logic to Java behind **behavioral-parity tests**,"
   - "built a **config-driven quality-gate** framework that decides when data is safe to read,"
   - "and as **on-call owner**, a **daily health check** I built caught a fee bug that **threw no error**."
4. **Why here**: "I want that to be **my whole job**, on a stack much **deeper** than mine — which is why release integration at Cerebras."

> 中文提示：约 85 个英文词，30 秒。三个证据对应 JD 的回归验证、发布门、跨组件排查。说完就停，等他挑一个追问。
