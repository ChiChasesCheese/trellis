## Q
如果见到 Cerebras 的 CEO 或创始人，问哪三个问题？各想听出什么？

## A
- **问法 1**："Speed is the headline. When a customer chooses Cerebras over a GPU cloud, what's the second reason — and what does that ask of software quality?"
  - 想听：可靠性、交付能力、模型上线速度是不是被当作竞争力。
- **问法 2**："New models keep arriving. How fast do you want to go from a model's release to serving it reliably, and where is that pipeline weakest?"
  - 想听：新模型上线的节奏和瓶颈层，直接决定 RIT 的工作量。
- **问法 3**："If RIT works the way you hope, what's different about Cerebras in a year?"
  - 想听：高层是否真的重视这个新团队。
- **不问**：股价、OpenAI 交付进度、薪资。
