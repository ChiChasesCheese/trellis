## Q
**项目结束后想在哪**｜经理面与行为面

“Where do you want to be at the end of the LEaD program?”

## A
面试官想听的是你对轮岗的目的有想法，而且这个想法和公司的需要对得上，不是随便一个“成为高级工程师”。

- **落点：** 最贴近数据和风险基础设施的那个分部，再次端到端负责一个系统。这是你已经证明过会做的事。
- **为什么轮岗有用：** 轮完前台、中台、后台之后，你选分部时手上有真实的视野，而不是凭招聘材料猜。
- **别说的：** 不说具体想去哪个 pod 赚钱，也不说“想转交易”；这是技术岗位，重点放在数据和风险系统上。
- **与前两问的衔接：** 动机里讲的“一个平台、很多使用方、必须算准”，在这里就是你想继续做的那类系统。

**英文答法。**
- By the end I'd like to be in the division **closest to data and risk infrastructure**.
- **Owning a system end to end again** — but with the context of having seen front, middle and back office.
- That context is what I'm missing today, and it's what lets me pick the right problems.

**追问。**
- **What would you bring in the first rotation?** Two years of **money pipelines where every number gets audited**; I treat a silent wrong number as worse than a loud failure.
- **What if the rotation puts you outside your comfort zone?** That is the point; I've done it before as an intern, learning Kafka, Spring and Spark in one summer.
- **Why not stay in payments?** I've gone deep on one platform; the next step is depth in finance technology.
