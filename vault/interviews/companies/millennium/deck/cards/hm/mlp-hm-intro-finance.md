## Q
**自我介绍与为什么转金融**｜经理面与行为面

“Tell me about yourself. You come from payments — why finance technology?”

## A
这是几乎每一轮的开场题。用的不是某一个故事，而是简历主线加一个能证明兴趣的项目。

- **主线：** 在 PayPal 的 Braintree 支付平台做后端，一年半全职加一个暑期实习，自己负责 American Express（美国运通）的结算和手续费管线，并且是它的值班负责人。
- **兴趣的证据：** 今年自己做了多市场回测平台（backtesting，用历史数据检验交易策略）Quant-Stroller，目的是弄清一个“好回测”在真实交易成本和样本外检验后还剩多少。
- **面试官在查：** 对金融的兴趣是不是具体。说“我对金融感兴趣”不够，要说你做过什么、学到了什么。
- **结论：** 回测的答案是“比想象的少得多”，这正是风险纪律在这类公司重要的原因。

**英文答法。**
- I'm a backend engineer at PayPal on Braintree's payments platform: about **a year and a half** full-time, plus a summer internship before that.
- I own the **American Express settlement and fee pipeline** end to end, and I'm the on-call authority for it.
- Outside work I built **Quant-Stroller**, a multi-market backtesting platform: **point-in-time data, purged cross-validation, deflated Sharpe, transaction-cost models**.
- I wanted to know how much of a good backtest **survives realistic costs** — much less than you'd hope, which is why risk discipline matters.

**追问。**
- **Why leave PayPal?** I've shipped and run one platform end to end; the next step is **depth in finance technology**, not another year of the same platform.
- **How many years of experience?** About a year and a half full-time plus a summer internship on the same team — two years of production work on one payments platform.
- **You've never worked in a fund.** Fair. I bring two years of **correctness-critical money pipelines**; the vocabulary I'll pick up in weeks, the habit of treating every number as auditable is already there.
