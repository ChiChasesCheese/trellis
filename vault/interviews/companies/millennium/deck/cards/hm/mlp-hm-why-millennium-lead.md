## Q
**Why Millennium，why LEaD**｜经理面与行为面

“Why Millennium, and why this program in particular?”

## A
Millennium 是多经理（multi-manager）对冲基金：几百个相互独立的交易团队（pod）共用一套技术和风控基础设施。LEaD 是 12 到 18 个月的轮岗项目，一批新人一起入职，有导师带。三个“为什么”都要挂在具体事实上。

- **问题类型相同：** 一个平台服务大量独立使用方，还必须算得准，这和你在 PayPal 做的一条费用管线服务所有商户是同一种形状。
- **技术文化：** 团队自己选适合的工具，技术和使用者紧密合作，你想离使用者近，而不是隔三层。
- **方向：** 自有的 AI 基础设施和智能体（agent）工具，叠在一个严肃的数据体系上；你自己做过 LLM 产品和市场研究数据平面，想在数据和风险都是真的地方做。
- **为什么是 LEaD：** 你拥有一个系统两年，缺的是前台、中台、后台技术怎么在基金里拼起来的视野；轮岗加同批同伴是最快的诚实办法。

**英文答法。**
- Three reasons. First, the **problem class**: hundreds of independent trading teams on **one shared, correct platform** — the same shape as my fee pipeline serving every merchant.
- Second, teams **choose the tools that fit the challenge**, close to the people who use them.
- Third, **proprietary AI infrastructure** on a serious data estate; I've built LLM products and a research data plane, and I want real data and real stakes.
- For LEaD: I've owned one system for two years; what I lack is how **front, middle and back office** fit together. A 12-to-18-month rotation with mentors is the fastest honest way.

**追问。**
- **Relocate to Miami?** Yes; I'd relocate for the program start.
- **Compensation expectations?** In line with the program's band for my experience; I'm focused on the rotation and the team, happy to discuss numbers later.
