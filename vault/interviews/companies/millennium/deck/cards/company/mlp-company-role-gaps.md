## Q
**对不上的地方怎么说**｜公司与岗位

"Your experience is below our two-year line" / "You haven't used Java much" / "You've never worked in finance."

## A
三个弱点各有一个诚实的说法：**事实 加 补偿**，不辩解。

- **年限：** 2025 年 2 月起全职，加上同一团队的一个暑假实习：两年左右同一支付平台上的生产经验。
- **Java：** 简历里有 Java 和 Spring Boot，实习用过；基础（HashMap、集合、并发）能口头答；不主动提 C++。
- **金融：** 没有基金工作经验，但有两年处理钱的、要被审计的数据管线，加一个自己做的金融研究平台。

**英文答法。**
- "I've been **full time since February 2025**, plus a summer internship on the same team: about two years of production work on one payments platform."
- "I've used **Java and Spring Boot**, and I'm comfortable with the fundamentals."
- "I haven't worked inside a fund, but I've built **correctness-critical money pipelines** and a research platform to learn the domain."
