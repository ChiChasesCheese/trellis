# 06 · 反问清单

## A. R1（对 SWE，最后 2–3 min，挑 2 个）

1. "Which of the four technology divisions have you worked with, and what does a LEaD engineer's first rotation typically look like?"（官方页说四个分部但没点名——让他说）
2. "What's the tech stack in Miami day to day — I've seen Java, Kafka and Python mentioned publicly; is that right for the teams LEaD rotates through?"
3. "What separates the LEaD engineers who do best in the program from the rest?"
4. 流程题（必问一个）："What are the next steps after this round, and roughly what's the timeline?"

不问：薪资、level、具体 pod、RTO 细节。不提读过他的公开文章。

## B. 回复 recruiter 邮件时（排期同一封）

- "Is there anything specific I should prepare for the live-coding portion — preferred language or the kind of problem (algorithmic vs. practical)?"
- "Could you share the structure of the later rounds so I can plan?"
- 不问薪资。

## C. R2+（HM / tech leader / Steve Johnson）

**项目 / 轮岗**
- "How are rotations assigned — by the engineer's preference, by division need, or both?"
- "What did a recent LEaD project look like end to end — who was the user, and what shipped?"
- "At the end of the program, how does the transition into a division work — is it a match process, and who decides?"

**技术 / 数据 / AI（贴 JD）**
- "The JD mentions applying LLMs to business problems. Which workflows have actually gone to production, and what did the evaluation look like?"
- "For the data side — the technology page mentions 900K+ daily data files — what's the ingestion and quality-check stack, and who owns it?"

**文化**
- "Candidates describe Millennium as high-autonomy and high-accountability. What does that look like for someone in their first year?"
- "How do LEaD engineers get feedback — cohort reviews, mentor 1:1s, or both?"

## D. 收尾一句（每轮）

> "I'm genuinely interested — the rotation across front, middle and back office is exactly the breadth I'm after. Thank you for the time."
