# fit · Why Millennium / Why LEaD / Why leave（English scripts）

> 数字与引语来自 `01-company-brief.md` 与 `catalog/raw/official_lead.md`。不说 $600B；不报薪资数字。

## 1. Why Millennium?（45 s）

> Three reasons. First, the problem class: a multi-manager platform means hundreds of independent trading teams on top of one shared technology and risk infrastructure — that's the same "many tenants, one correct platform" shape I've been building at PayPal, where one fee pipeline serves every merchant and has to be exact. Second, the tech culture as you describe it publicly — teams choose the tools that fit the challenge, and technology is built in close partnership with the people who use it — I'd rather be near the user than three layers away. Third, the direction: proprietary AI infrastructure and agentic tools on top of a serious data estate. I've built LLM-powered products on my own and a research data plane for markets; I want to do that where the data and the stakes are real.

## 2. Why LEaD specifically?（30 s）

> Because I'm early enough in my career that breadth compounds. I've owned one system end to end for two years; what I don't have is exposure to how front, middle and back office technology fit together in a fund. A 12-to-18-month rotation with mentors and a cohort is the fastest honest way to get that, and Miami is a yes for me.

## 3. Why leave PayPal?（30 s，不抱怨）

> I've designed, shipped and run one platform end to end and I'm the on-call authority for it. The next step for me is depth in finance technology, not another year of the same platform. Millennium is the most direct version of that move, and the LEaD structure is built for exactly this stage.

## 4. Interest in finance — 用证据（30 s）

> It's not abstract for me. This year I built a multi-market backtesting platform: point-in-time data, purged cross-validation, deflated Sharpe, transaction-cost models, and a paper-trading loop with pre-trade risk checks. I did it because I wanted to know how much of a "good backtest" survives realistic costs and out-of-sample tests — the answer is: much less than you'd hope, which is exactly why risk discipline matters at a firm like this.

（对应 Steve Johnson 官方建议第 3 条 "build and back test a simple trading strategy in Python"。）

> About a year and a half full-time at PayPal, plus a summer internship on the same team before that — so two years of production work on one payments platform.

## 6. Relocation / onsite（10 s）

> Miami onsite is a yes; I'd relocate for the program start.

## 7. Compensation expectations（15 s）

> I'd expect it to be in line with the program's band for my experience; I'm more focused on the rotation and the team right now. Happy to discuss numbers once we're at that stage.
